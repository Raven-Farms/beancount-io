# Replace "(s)" count strings in Settings plan and Plaid flows with plural messages

**Severity:** minor (i18n). **Estimate:** 45–60 minutes for 10 keys × 15 locales. **Owner:** `dashboard` (user-settings, plaid). **A3:** the billing card any paying user opens reads "20 ledger(s) · Up to 10 collaborator(s) per ledger".

## Live reproduction

Production `https://beancount.io`, 2026-10-15, local main `575a8798`. QA-owned headless Chromium, en-US, authenticated QA owner, read-only. Open `/settings/general`. The Subscription card reads "2,000,000 AI tokens / month · **20 ledger(s)** · Unlimited directives · **Up to 10 collaborator(s) per ledger**", and the Organization upgrade tile reads "100 ledger(s)" and "Up to 50 collaborator(s) per ledger".

Source-traced (not reproduced live, since no bank connection was touched): the Plaid flows use the same pattern.

## Root cause

These messages put counts in "(s)" workarounds, which `dashboard/AGENTS.md` forbids ("Counts are plural messages, not `Label: {count}` workarounds"):

- `features/user-settings/locales/en.ts`: `userSettings.includedLedgers` ("{count} ledger(s)"), `userSettings.collaboratorsPerLedger` ("Up to {count} collaborator(s) per ledger").
- `features/plaid/locales/en.ts`: `plaid.management.toast.syncAllCompleteDescription` (two counts: transactions and banks), `plaid.management.toast.syncAllSkipped`, `plaid.accountMapping.toast.acceptedAllDescription`, `plaid.transactionReview.deleteConfirmDescription`, `plaid.transactionReview.hiddenSelectedNotice`, `plaid.transactionReview.missingAccountsAlert`, `plaid.transactionReview.toast.missingTargetAccountsDescription`, `plaid.transactionReview.toast.transactionsDeletedDescription`.

## Fix

Convert each key to a plural family in all 15 locales and call `t(key, { count })`. For `syncAllCompleteDescription`, which has two independent counts, split it into two plural fragments (or two messages) rather than pluralizing on only one count. Let `src/i18n/__tests__/plural.test.ts` and `src/test/translations.test.ts` enforce the categories.

Acceptance: `/settings/general` reads "20 ledgers" and "Up to 10 collaborators per ledger" (and "1 ledger" for a 1-count plan). Plaid toasts and alerts inflect for 1 and many in English and Russian. Translation/plural tests and dashboard gates pass.
