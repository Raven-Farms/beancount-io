# Let keyboard users reach the clipped Other column in statement tables

**Severity:** minor (accessibility; WCAG 2.1.1 Keyboard; axe `scrollable-region-focusable`). **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. **Owner:** `dashboard` (reports, shared `HierarchyList`). **A2:** at the default desktop width, the Income Statement's non-USD amounts sit outside the card, and some cards give keyboard users no way to scroll to them.

## Live reproduction

Production `https://beancount.io`, 2026-10-15, local main `575a8798`. QA-owned headless Chromium, **1440×1000**, en-US. Public `open_ledger/example` and a synthetic ledger. Read-only, reproduced on fresh loads.

1. Open `/ledger/open_ledger/example/income-statement`. The Income List, Net Profit, and Expenses List cards sit side by side, and each `.hierarchy-scroll` region measures `scrollWidth 640 > clientWidth 556`. The whole **Other** column lies past the right edge: the Income header `IRAUSD`/`VACHR` and Net Profit's `13 VACHR`. Only `Account | USD` is visible.
2. The Net Profit region contains **no focusable element** (0 links/buttons and no `tabindex`), so Tab never enters it and arrow keys can't scroll it. A keyboard-only user can't see the `13 VACHR` net profit at all. The same applies to Cash Flow's "Net Cash from Investing Activities" region.
3. Overflow (`scrollWidth − clientWidth`) of each Income Statement table by viewport: 768 → 176px, 1024 → 292px, 1280 → 164px, 1440 → 84px, 1920 → 0. Only very wide screens show the Other column without scrolling.
4. axe-core 4.13 reports `scrollable-region-focusable` on `/income-statement` and `/cash-flow`, light and dark.

## Root cause

`dashboard/src/features/reports/balance-sheet/hierarchy-list.tsx` (shared `HierarchyList`, wrapped by `hierarchy-list-card.tsx`) renders the statement `<table className="w-full min-w-[40rem]">` (640px; ~line 478) inside an `overflow-x: auto` `.hierarchy-scroll` div. The div has no `tabIndex`, role, or name, and relies on contained links to make it reachable. Summary-only cards (`summaryRows` with plain unlinked rows) have no links.

## Fix

When the region overflows, make it a keyboard-scrollable, named region: `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"`, and `aria-label` from the card title (e.g. "Net Profit table"), with a visible focus ring. Alternatively, let the grid shrink to its card at ≥1024px so the Other column isn't clipped on desktop. Keep w3/m32's table semantics and w4/081's "+N more" disclosure.

Acceptance: at 1440×1000 on `open_ledger/example`, a keyboard user can Tab to every statement table and scroll to (or directly see) Net Profit's `13 VACHR`. axe `scrollable-region-focusable` passes on Income Statement, Cash Flow, Balance Sheet, and Trial Balance. The 390px layout is unchanged or improved. Dashboard gates pass.
