# Accept Beancount's tolerance residuals in the Cash Flow conservation check, since At Cost is unusable on the public example

**Severity:** major (regression from w4/198, `83523377`, 2026-10-10). **Estimate:** 50–60 minutes including fixture-based regression tests. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`features/reports/cash-flow/lib/model.ts`). **A2/A3:** the flagship public ledger `open_ledger/example` shows "Cash flow cannot be reconciled" for every full year and for all time, which is the first thing a visitor sees on that report.

## Live reproduction

Production `https://beancount.io`, 2026-10-15, local main `575a8798`. QA-owned headless Chromium, 1440×1000, en-US, conversion **At Cost**, interval Monthly. Public `open_ledger/example`, read-only.

| `/cash-flow?…` | result |
| --- | --- |
| (no time), `time=2015`, `time=2016`, `time=2017` | "Cash flow cannot be reconciled — The selected conversion or filters do not reconcile with recorded cash movements. **Try At Cost** or adjust the report filters. Export is unavailable…" (At Cost is already selected) |
| `time=2016-01` … `time=2016-12` (each month alone) | statement renders, e.g. 2016-03: operating 2,657.88, opening 7,087.14, closing 5,882.46 |

Independent API control: the page's own `getLedgerIntervalTotals` reads for all five roots (`conversion: "at_cost"`, `interval: "monthly"`), summed per interval and unit:

- `time=2016`: non-zero USD in 9 of 12 buckets, e.g. 2016-01-31 `+0.00057`, 2016-02-29 `+0.00426`, 2016-03-31 `-0.0099`, 2016-05-31 `-0.00638`, 2016-12-31 `+0.01663`.
- `time=2016-02`: sums to exactly 0. Diffing the February bucket between the two reads, the only difference is `Equity:Conversions:Current: -0.00426 USD`. That entry exists only in the single-month read.

Blast radius (all-time view, At Cost): `open_ledger/crypto-example` also shows the unreconciled state. `stock-example`, `real-estate-example`, `microsoft`, `kroger`, `spotify`, `freelancer-invoicing`, and `minimax` render. Crypto residuals need verifying under the same mechanism before the fix is closed. A private multi-year QA-account ledger (about 4,700 entries) shows the same state on its all-time Cash Flow, so this isn't limited to demo data.

## Root cause

Beancount accepts each transaction within a tolerance (here half a cent on fractional fund purchases at `{cost}`), so at-cost movements over a period don't sum to exactly zero. The clamp (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/time-clamp.ts`, beancount's `summarize.clamp_opt`) books the accumulated residual to `Equity:Conversions:Current` once, at the end of the selected period. A one-interval read therefore conserves, but in a multi-interval read every bucket except the last carries its own residual.

w4/198 added `buildCashFlowStatement`'s per-interval check (`model.ts:290–299`: `sumBalanceRecords(...)` then `if (!isZeroAmounts(differences)) throw new CashFlowReconciliationError`). The check demands **exact** zero per interval and unit, so it rejects these legitimate tolerance residuals. It was meant to reject non-conserving *valuation* (At Market Value moving securities, w4/198's NWRB repro) and to keep At Cost as the working control.

## Fix

Keep w4/198's protection against valuation-induced differences but stop failing on tolerance residuals. Either:

- (a) compare each interval's per-unit difference to a tolerance bound derived from the ledger (e.g. Σ per-transaction tolerance, or the unit's display-precision half-unit times the interval's transaction count), and fold an accepted residual into a non-cash "conversion/rounding" line rather than into opening cash; or
- (b) have the interval-totals producer emit the per-interval conversion residual (as Fava's `Conversions` handling does), so each bucket conserves exactly.

Don't widen the tolerance enough to admit At Market Value differences such as NWRB's 60 USD. Also fix the guidance copy: when the selected conversion already is At Cost, don't tell the user to "Try At Cost". Suggest a filter change or name the residual instead.

Acceptance: `open_ledger/example` Cash Flow at At Cost renders and exports for all time, 2015, 2016, and 2017, and its opening + net change = closing per unit. w4/198's stock-example At Market Value and Units repros still show the reconciliation state. Regression tests use a fixture with sub-cent cost rounding across ≥2 monthly intervals (must pass) and the NWRB valuation fixture (must fail). Dashboard gates pass.
