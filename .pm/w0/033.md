# Income and Expenses account pages color by raw sign: salary reads red and spending reads green

**Minor · mobile · 30–45m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-10-15, `main` at `22aec290`, development build `io.beancount.ios` (JS `1.20261002.50`) on iPhone 17e / iOS 26.5, light theme, English, default text size. Authenticated production session at `https://beancount.io/` with the dedicated QA account. Public ledger `open_ledger/example`. Report-only run.

## Repro

1. Select `open_ledger/example` and open the **Accounts** tab.
2. Tap **Expenses** ($254,609.05). At **6M** the chart card reads **+$41,945.63 (+19.72%)** in **green**, over a green rising line. Every row is green: **Cafe Modagor +$33.71**, **Farmer Fresh +$86.31**, **Hoogle +$2,089.10** (payroll taxes).
3. Back, then tap **Income** (−$357,944.58). The card reads **−$61,416.45 (−20.71%)** in **red**, over a red falling line, and each payroll row is red: **Hoogle −$4,639.70**.
4. Control: in **Transactions** the same Cafe Modagor entry is **−$33.71** in plain text and Hoogle payroll is **+$2,550.60** in green. The Accounts tab shows Income and Expenses balances in the plain color.

Earning salary is shown in the app's error color and spending in its success color, the opposite of what the colors mean everywhere else.

**Expected:** amounts stay exactly as the ledger holds them (the Accounts tab rule). Color follows the change's effect on net worth: income earned or an expense refunded reads as favorable, an expense incurred as unfavorable.
**Actual:** the color is the raw sign of the change, which is only right for Assets and Liabilities.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/components/account-entry-row.tsx:131–136`: `changeColor = row.change > 0 ? theme.success : row.change < 0 ? theme.error : theme.black60`.
- `mobile/src/common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:637–639`: `lineColor = (scrubUp ?? restingUp) ? theme.success : theme.error`. The same color paints the change row (`:346`, `:459–460`), the area gradient and the scrub dot. `BalanceChartCard` (`account-detail-screen.tsx:385–395`) passes no category information.
- The app already documents the rule this violates: `mobile/src/components/account-list/select-account-list.ts:29–52` (`CATEGORY_SIGN`) says balances are shown as the ledger holds them, and color marks only a value "running *against* its category". Account detail never consults it.

## Fix

Derive the account's root category in `AccountDetailScreen` and pass a net-worth direction: `+1` for Assets and Liabilities, `−1` for Income, Expenses and Equity. Pass it to `AccountEntryRow` and, via `BalanceChartCard`, to `InteractiveLineChartD3`. Choose colors from `change × direction` (and from `(last − baseline) × direction` for the chart line, change row and scrub state). Do not change any displayed number or sign. Home's Net Worth, Assets and Liabilities cards keep the current direction (`+1`). Add tests for an Expenses series (rising reads unfavorable), an Income series (falling reads favorable), a refund row, and an unchanged Assets case.

## Blast radius and controls

- Affected: every Income, Expenses and Equity account page, roots and leaves alike (they share `AccountDetailScreen`), including scrubbing.
- Unaffected: Home charts (balance-sheet series) and the Accounts tab.
- Working controls: Reports negates income and expense once for display (`CATEGORY_SIGN`); [w4/038](../w4/done/038.md) records that the Budget screen's variance colors invert correctly for income budgets (`select-budget-card-stats.ts`).
