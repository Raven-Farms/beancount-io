# Dashboard dates formatted with date-fns ignore the reader's language: "Feb 13, 2026" in German, Russian and Chinese

**Minor · dashboard · 20–30m · A2.** Seen live on 2026-10-17 during w0/m1/t005. The Plaid bank card reads "Verknüpft am Feb 13, 2026", "Привязан Feb 13, 2026" and "链接于 Feb 13, 2026".

Two sites call date-fns `format(…, "MMM d, yyyy")` with no locale, so the month is always English:
- `dashboard/src/features/plaid/pages/plaid-connections/components/bank-item.tsx:148` (the linked-on date)
- `dashboard/src/features/receipt/components/receipt-success-card.tsx:52` (the parsed receipt date)

The dashboard already formats dates by language elsewhere: `common/lib/format/format-datetime.ts` uses `detectLanguage()` with `toLocaleDateString`.

**Fix:**
- Use the locale-aware formatter, or pass the active date-fns locale.
- Add a guard test that fails on a month-name `format()` pattern without a locale.
- Verify in de, ru and zh.

**Dedupe:** w0/m1 (catalog copy, not date formats). No date-format item is open.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-17** — Both date-fns dates now read in the reader's language.

What changed (`dashboard`):
- The Plaid bank card's linked-on date (`plaid/…/bank-item.tsx`) and the parsed receipt date (`receipt/components/receipt-success-card.tsx`) format with `"PP"` and the active date-fns locale from `useDateLocale()`, the pattern `feed-card.tsx` already used. `PP` keeps English exactly as it was ("Feb 13, 2026"). Other languages get their own medium date: German "13. Feb. 2026", Russian "13 фев. 2026 г.", Chinese "2026-02-13".
- No other `format()` call prints names: every remaining pattern is numeric (`yyyy-MM-dd`, the chart keys).

Tests:
- New guard `src/test/date-format-locale.test.ts` parses every production source file. It fails on a date-fns `format` (aliases followed) whose literal pattern prints month or weekday names, or a localized `P` format, without a `locale` option. Its own cases check that `MMM`, `PP` and `EEEE` are flagged, and that a `locale`, a numeric pattern or quoted literal text are not.
- `receipt/components/__tests__/receipt-success-card-date.test.tsx` renders the card under German, Russian, Chinese and English date locales.
- Both fail on the old code (the English case passes either way, as intended).

Verified with date-fns' real de, ru and zh-CN locales in the component test, not in a browser.

Gates: dashboard `format:check`, `lint`, `test` and `build` pass.
