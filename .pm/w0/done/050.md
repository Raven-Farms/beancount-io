# German and Chinese use two words for "ledger" in the dashboard: "Ledger-Limit … weitere Ledger" beside "Hauptbuch", "分类账" beside "账本"

**Minor · dashboard · 20–30m · A2.** Seen live on 2026-10-17 during w0/m1/t005 on Settings → General.

`page.dashboard.ledgerLimitReached` reads:
- German: "Sie haben Ihr Ledger-Limit erreicht. Upgraden Sie, um weitere Ledger zu erstellen." (`dashboard/src/features/ledger-list/pages/dashboard-page/locales/de.ts:81`)
- Chinese: "你已达到分类账限制。升级以创建更多分类账。" (`…/zh.ts:78`)

The rest of each UI says "Hauptbuch" / "账本", including the plan list beside it. The importer's Chinese progress message also says "分类账" (`features/importer/locales/zh.ts:411`).

**Fix:**
- Use the catalog's established term in these messages.
- Add a small per-locale glossary assertion (de "Hauptbuch", zh "账本") to the locale tests, so the alternate word cannot return.

**Dedupe:** w3/193 (Statistics label consistency) and w0/m1 (copied English). Neither covers the ledger term. Mobile's German has a similar "Bücher"/"Hauptbücher" mix, which is separate work.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-17** — German says "Hauptbuch" and Chinese "账本" throughout the dashboard, and a glossary test keeps the other word out.

What changed (`dashboard` catalogs):
- **Chinese:** `page.dashboard.ledgerLimitReached` ("你已达到账本数量上限。升级以创建更多账本。") and the importer's progress message ("…添加到账本") no longer say 分类账.
- **German, plan limit:** `page.dashboard.ledgerLimitReached` reads "Sie haben Ihr Hauptbuch-Limit erreicht. Upgraden Sie, um weitere Hauptbücher zu erstellen."
- **German, beyond the note** (the new test found these):
  - `journal.entryDeleteDescription` ("…aus dem Hauptbuch…").
  - The four star toasts in `reports/locales/de.ts`, which were also mistranslated. "Ledger spielte erfolgreich die Hauptrolle" meant "the ledger played the lead role" for "Ledger starred", and "Fehler beim Entsperren des Hauptbuchs" meant "error unlocking". They now use the button's own term ("Hauptbuch mit Stern versehen", "Stern vom Hauptbuch entfernt", and the two failure messages).
  - Seven API-key strings in `user-settings/pages/api-keys/translations.ts`, which my first scan missed because they sit outside `locales/`. "Hauptbuchdaten lesen.", "Alle Hauptbücher", …; the scope description also moved from "du" to the catalog's "Sie".

New `src/test/locale-glossary.test.ts` reads the flattened catalogs: German messages must not contain "Ledger" as a word or in a compound, and Chinese must not contain 分类账. Proper names allowlisted in `IDENTICAL_BY_DESIGN` (Open Ledger) are exempt. Restoring the old Chinese message fails it.

Not covered: mobile's German "Bücher"/"Hauptbücher" mix, as the note says.

Gates: dashboard `format:check`, `lint`, `test` and `build` pass.
