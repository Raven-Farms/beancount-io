# Marking a merchant Recurring labels two identical monthly payments "Irregular", because "too few payments" and "no pattern" collapse into one label

**Minor · mobile · 20–30m · A2.** Native mobile QA, 2026-10-15, `main` at `22aec290`, development build `io.beancount.ios` (JS `1.20261002.50`) on iPhone 17e / iOS 26.5, light theme, English. Authenticated production session at `https://beancount.io/` with the dedicated QA account, on a synthetic ledger the account owns. The Recurring switch is a device-local preference; it was turned back off, and the merchant returned to its original state (override cleared).

## Repro

1. Open **Merchants** on a ledger where a payee has exactly two equal payments one calendar month apart. Here: **LANDLORD LLC**, `−$1,800.00` on **2026-07-03** and **2026-08-03** ("Rent").
2. Open the merchant. **Recurring** is off (automatic detection needs three dates).
3. Turn **Recurring** on.
4. The header gains a cadence chip reading **Irregular**. Back on **Merchants**, the pinned **Recurring** block lists **LANDLORD LLC — Irregular**, with no next date or amount.

**Expected:** when the history is too short to infer a cadence, the chip says so neutrally (for example "Not enough history yet" or "Cadence unknown"), or no cadence chip appears until a third payment exists. "Irregular" should mean that the payments were analysed and do not follow a pattern.
**Actual:** two perfectly regular payments are described as irregular.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts` returns `null` both when the series is too short (`MIN_EVENT_DATES = 3`, `:33`; early returns at `:163` and `:187`) and when the gaps are too dispersed (the MAD/median check). Callers cannot tell the two apart.
- `selectors/resolve-recurring.ts:13` and `:33`: "Manual mark without a detectable cadence surfaces as `irregular`" (`cadence: detection?.cadence ?? "irregular"`).
- `selectors/merchant-sections.ts:117–138` maps that to `merchantsCadenceIrregular` ("Irregular", `translations/en.ts:82`).

## Fix

Make detection report why it found no cadence, e.g. `{ verdict: null, reason: "tooShort" | "irregular" }`, or a separate `hasEnoughHistory(series)` check. Resolve a manual mark with too-short history to a new `"unknown"` cadence, labelled with a translated neutral string in all 13 locales, and keep `"irregular"` for analysed, dispersed series. Unit-test `resolveRecurring` for: a manual mark with 2 monthly dates (unknown), a manual mark with dispersed dates (irregular), and 3 monthly dates (monthly via detection).

## Blast radius

The merchant header chip (`merchant-cadence-chip`) and the pinned **Recurring** block on **Merchants**; both read `cadenceLabelKey`. Detection results and the stored override are unchanged. The toggle's restore path works: turning the switch off cleared the override (`toggleRecurringOverride`, `common/vars/merchant-recurring-overrides.ts:73–88`).

**Resolved 2026-10-17** — A manual Recurring mark now tells "too short to tell" apart from "no pattern".

What changed:
- New `hasEnoughHistory` (`detect-recurrence.ts`) applies detection's own threshold: three distinct, valid event dates.
- `usePayeeRecurrence` returns the set of payees that meet it.
- `resolveRecurringVerdict` takes that signal as a required argument. A mark with no detected cadence resolves to `"irregular"` only when the history was long enough to analyse, and to the new `"unknown"` otherwise.
- `cadenceLabelKey` maps `"unknown"` to `merchantsCadenceUnknown` ("Not enough history", 13 locales). The merchants directory and merchant detail both pass the signal.
- Automatic detection and the stored override are unchanged.

Verified on iPhone 17e, disposable `puncsky/mcp-agent-eval`: LANDLORD LLC (two −$1,800.00 rent payments, Jul 3 and Aug 3) shows **Not enough history** when Recurring is on. Turning it off removed the chip and cleared the local override.
