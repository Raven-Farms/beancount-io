# Persian: letter-spaced captions break Arabic-script joins, the Payee "Suggestions" caption stays left, and two inputs were never checked in RTL

**Minor · mobile · 45–60m · A2.** Seen on 2026-10-17 while verifying w0/045 on iPhone 17e in فارسی.

- On Payee, the caption **پیشنهادها** renders split as "پیشنهاد ها". The caption style uses `letterSpacing` (`mobile/src/components/text-input-screen/index.tsx:66`), and letter spacing breaks cursive joining in Arabic script. The same caption also sits at the physical left while the rows below mirror.
- `mobile/src/` has 14 `letterSpacing` sites, mostly uppercase section captions: notifications, transaction detail, merchants, ledger drawer, multi-postings list, commit detail, and others.
- w0/045 left two inputs unverified in RTL: Open Account's sub-path input and Add Budget's right-aligned amount.

**Fix:**
- Add a shared caption helper that drops `letterSpacing` for Arabic-script locales, and apply it to the 14 sites.
- Align the Payee caption to the leading edge.
- Verify the two inputs after a Persian restart.
- Guard that no `letterSpacing` bypasses the helper.

**Dedupe:** w0/045 (input alignment) and w4/066 (account bars). Neither covers letter spacing or captions.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-17** — Persian captions keep their joins, the Payee caption and card follow the leading edge, and both inputs from w0/045 were checked in RTL and fixed.

What changed (`mobile`):
- **Letter spacing.** New `letterSpacing()` in `src/common/rtl.ts` keeps the tracking in a left-to-right layout and drops it in a right-to-left one. All 13 non-zero `letterSpacing` sites use it: the captions, plus the tracked amount displays. Guard `src/common/__tests__/letter-spacing.test.ts` fails on any literal other than `0`.
- **Payee screen** (`components/text-input-screen`): the caption is aligned with `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN`. The suggestions ScrollView pads its content container instead of itself, because an iOS ScrollView padded itself shifts its content toward the trailing edge in RTL. That cut the card off at the right, and it predated this change.
- **Open Account:**
  - The account field row is pinned left-to-right, so the root reads first ("Assets: | Bank:Checking") instead of the name backwards.
  - Its sub-path input is left-aligned, so typed text sits beside the root.
  - The "Account" label is on the leading edge.
- **Add Budget:** the amount input uses a new `trailingInputTextAlign()`, so in Persian it sits at the trailing (left) edge with the other values instead of mid-row.

Verified on iPhone 17e after switching to فارسی and restarting:
- **Payee:** "پیشنهادها" joined, on the right, aligned with a card that now has even margins.
- **Open Account:** "Assets: | Bank:Checking" with the label on the right.
- **Add Budget:** "500" on the trailing edge.
- **Drawer:** the caption untracked.
- **English after switching back:** Payee unchanged, the tracked "SUGGESTIONS" caption on the left.

The QA ledger was only viewed; nothing was submitted. The app was returned to English and its previous ledger.

Tests: `rtl.test.ts` covers `letterSpacing` and `trailingInputTextAlign` in both directions; the guard fails when a literal is reintroduced. Mobile gates pass (prettier, tsc, eslint, knip, gqlops, unit: 2,338).

Follow-up found while verifying (not fixed here; app-wide): Persian UI copy left at `natural` alignment renders on the physical left in several places. Examples: Settings section titles, Add Budget's footnote, the drawer's "Browse ledgers" row label, and the read-only notice's link. That contradicts `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN`'s note that UI copy needs nothing; an explicit `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` moved each label checked here to the right. Worth its own item: decide the default (a shared `Text` default, or the rule in the docs) and apply it.
