## Objective

Delete the ledger service's buffering `GET /ledgers/:owner/:repo_name/archive/:archive` now that nothing calls it, along with its contract entries and generated clients, so the full-buffer code path cannot come back into use.

## Context

- Route: `backend-cluster/ledger/src/api/files.ts:312-347` (operationId `getLedgerArchive`), plus its error class and tests.
- Contract and generated clients that list it: `backend-cluster/idl/beancount-ledger.openapi.json`, `backend-cluster/idl/beancount-ledger-cli/src/generated/api.ts`, and backend-v2's `src/foundation/fava/Api.ts` (`getLedgerArchive`). Follow `backend-cluster/idl/AGENTS.md` for how the spec and clients are regenerated.
- `assertSafeArchiveName` lives in both packages (`ledger/src/shared/safe-archive-name.ts`, `backend-v2/src/features/ledger/utils/safe-archive-name.ts`); the ledger copy may become unused.

## Steps

1. Confirm with a repository-wide search that, after t001, nothing calls the ledger route or `getLedgerArchive`.
2. Remove the route, its error type and tests; remove any ledger helper left unused (`yarn lint:deadcode`).
3. Update the ledger OpenAPI spec and regenerate the clients as the IDL guide says; run the ledger's route/spec parity checks.

## Files

- backend-cluster/ledger/src/api/files.ts (and its tests)
- backend-cluster/ledger/src/shared/safe-archive-name.ts (if unused)
- backend-cluster/idl/beancount-ledger.openapi.json and generated clients
- backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/foundation/fava/Api.ts

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `GET /ledgers/{owner}/{repo}/archive/main.zip` on the ledger service returns 404.
- [x] The spec and generated clients have no `getLedgerArchive`; ledger and backend-v2 typecheck, lint (including dead-code) and tests pass.

## Out of scope

- Any other ledger route.

## Outcome

- Removed the route, its `ArchiveDownloadError`, and the ledger's now-unused `shared/safe-archive-name.ts` (backend-v2 keeps and tests its own copy). `src/server/__tests__/archive-route-retired.test.ts` builds the app and holds `main.zip` / `main.tar.gz` at 404 with no Gitea request; it fails with the old route restored.
- Deleted the path from `idl/beancount-ledger.openapi.json` and regenerated backend-v2's `src/foundation/fava/Api.ts` (`yarn generate-fava-client`, which drops only `getLedgerArchive`) and the CLI's gitignored `src/generated/api.ts` (`yarn codegen`).
- The ledger service has no runnable route/spec parity check: `parity/` is retired, and its `COVERAGE.md` is an archived record that "is not updated going forward", so its historical `getLedgerArchive` row and suite were left as written.
