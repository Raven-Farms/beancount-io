# Dashboard dates formatted with date-fns ignore the reader's language: "Feb 13, 2026" in German, Russian and Chinese

**Minor · dashboard · 20–30m · A2.** Seen live on 2026-10-17 during w0/m1/t005. The Plaid bank card reads "Verknüpft am Feb 13, 2026", "Привязан Feb 13, 2026" and "链接于 Feb 13, 2026".

Two sites call date-fns `format(…, "MMM d, yyyy")` with no locale, so the month is always English:
- `dashboard/src/features/plaid/pages/plaid-connections/components/bank-item.tsx:148` (the linked-on date)
- `dashboard/src/features/receipt/components/receipt-success-card.tsx:52` (the parsed receipt date)

The dashboard already formats dates by language elsewhere: `common/lib/format/format-datetime.ts` uses `detectLanguage()` with `toLocaleDateString`.

**Fix:**
- Use the locale-aware formatter, or pass the active date-fns locale.
- Add a guard test that fails on a month-name `format()` pattern without a locale.
- Verify in de, ru and zh.

**Dedupe:** w0/m1 (catalog copy, not date formats). No date-format item is open.
