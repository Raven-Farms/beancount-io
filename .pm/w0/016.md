# Raise the contrast of the "Related Pages" heading

**Severity:** minor (accessibility; WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), AA; axe `color-contrast`). **Estimate:** 10–15 minutes. **Owner:** `dashboard` (`common/components/related-links.tsx`). **A2:** this heading appears at the bottom of most ledger pages.

## Live reproduction

Production `https://beancount.io`, 2026-10-15, local main `575a8798`. QA-owned headless Chromium, 1440×1000, authenticated owner on a synthetic ledger. axe-core 4.13 flags the 12px "Related Pages" `<h2>` on Holdings, Statistics, Query, and Import:

- light: `#858781` on `#ffffff` = **3.63:1**
- dark: `#74766d` on `#0b0e0b` = **4.21:1**

Both are below 4.5:1 for normal-size (12px, medium-weight) text. Computed color: `oklab(0.44 … / 0.7)`, i.e. `text-muted-foreground` at 70% opacity. On other routes axe didn't evaluate the node (it was below the scanned area or obscured), so treat it as the same element site-wide.

## Root cause

`dashboard/src/common/components/related-links.tsx:30`: `<h2 className="text-xs font-medium text-muted-foreground/70">`. The `/70` opacity takes the already-muted token below AA.

## Fix

Drop the `/70` opacity (plain `text-muted-foreground` passes elsewhere in the app), or use a token that clears 4.5:1 in both themes. Keep the size and weight.

Acceptance: axe `color-contrast` reports no violation for the Related Pages heading in light and dark on Holdings, Statistics, Query, and Import. Dashboard gates pass.
