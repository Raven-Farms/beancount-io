## Objective

Prove the stage-2 flows in production against the deployed revision, with real subscriptions, at no cost: scheduling, undo, cancellation, resume, the emails and the limit display. This is the user's requirement of 2026-10-18.

## Context

- **Credentials and coupons:** same rules as m39/t015. `QA_EMAIL` and `QA_PASSWORD` come from `beancount-io/.env` and are never printed or committed. Use two new single-use, `duration: forever`, 100%-off production coupons, created with live access or by the operator under t006. Codes never enter the board, a commit or a log.
- **Checkout collects a payment method by default even when the total is $0.** Block the task if no QA payment method is available; do not change Checkout for everyone.
- **What production cannot show:** a scheduled change only takes effect at period end, and a 100% coupon never produces a failed charge. So the phase transition and `past_due` handling stay covered by t012's fixtures. Production covers everything up to the effective date.

## Steps

1. Sign in as the QA account, starting on Free with no subscription.
2. **Flow C — coupon 1, Growth · Yearly:**
   - Schedule a downgrade to Premium · Yearly. The sheet shows the effective date, $0 today, the limits against usage and the plan features. The Scheduled panel appears, and the confirmation email arrives.
   - Undo it with **Keep current plan**.
   - Schedule the switch to monthly with a higher tier to see the split choice, then undo it.
   - Cancel from the cancel sheet: it offers the downgrade alternative and records the optional reason, and the scheduled state reads "ends <date>".
   - Resume.
   - At every step, settings, quotas and `bea --json cloud status` still say GROWTH.
3. **Flow D — coupon 2, Premium · Monthly:**
   - End the Flow C subscription immediately first.
   - Check out Premium · Monthly. The cancel sheet offers **Switch to yearly**.
   - Schedule a cancellation, then make a plan change: the change withdraws the cancellation.
   - Check that the limit errors read correctly: adding a collaborator beyond Premium's limit on a QA ledger is refused with the resource-limit message. Remove anything you created.
4. **Cleanup:** end both subscriptions immediately, using live access or the operator. Deactivate unused codes, remove test ledgers and collaborators, and confirm the QA account reads Free.
5. Record public-safe evidence in the milestone README: date, revisions, each step's pass or fail, the emails received (subjects only) and the cleanup. No codes, emails, or customer or subscription identifiers.
