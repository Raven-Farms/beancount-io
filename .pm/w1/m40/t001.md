## Objective

`changePlan` applies a downgrade, a switch to monthly and the "monthly at renewal" half of a split choice as a Subscription Schedule. The current phase runs to the period end and the next phase holds the new price. It returns `SCHEDULED` with `effectiveAt`, and charges nothing today.

## Context

- m39/t001 previews these kinds with `effectiveAt` and nothing due; m39/t002 refuses them until this task lands.
- Tier comes from the subscription's current price (`backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/stripe/service/subscription-plan.ts`). When the phase starts, the `customer.subscription.updated` webhook changes the limits with no extra code.
- Stripe: `subscriptionSchedules.create({ from_subscription })`, then update its phases.
- Rule from PRFAQ005 question 14: gains now, losses at period end; nothing is refunded.

## Steps

1. Create a schedule from the subscription, or update the existing one. Keep the current phase until the period end, then the new price.
2. Return `SCHEDULED` with the effective date. Refuse legacy prices as targets.
3. Confirm the webhook path resolves the new tier at the phase change, and that renewal caches update only the matching binding (w1/m37 rule).
