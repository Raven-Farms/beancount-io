## Objective

A subscriber whose renewal charge fails hears about it by email, and a yearly subscriber is reminded before the renewal charge. Stripe's own emails cannot do this for Beancount alone: the account bills other products too, its failed-payment email's card-update link points at the company homepage, and the only fix there is an irreversible, account-wide switch. The user decided on 2026-10-19 that the backend sends Beancount-only emails instead (t006 records the settings).

## Shipped

- **Failed renewal** — `invoice.payment_failed` (added to the beancount.io webhook endpoint on 2026-10-19) for a renewal (`billing_reason: subscription_cycle`) whose invoice bills one of our prices:
  - first failed attempt: "Your {plan} renewal didn't go through", with the amount, the next retry date and an "Update your card" button to Billing settings, where the failed-renewal banner opens the billing portal;
  - no retry left (`next_payment_attempt` null): "Your {plan} plan has ended", saying the account is on Free, nothing is deleted, and how to choose the plan again;
  - the retries in between send nothing.
- **Yearly renewal reminder** — on `invoice.upcoming` (7 days before), for a yearly plan that is not set to end and has no scheduled change landing at that renewal (that change has its own reminder): "Your {plan} plan renews on {date}", with the upcoming invoice's amount, discounts included. A renewal that charges nothing sends none.
- Each email is sent once per invoice (failures) or per renewal (reminder) through `sent_billing_emails`; a failed send gives the claim back and the webhook answers with an error so Stripe redelivers, as the plan-change reminder already did.
- The claim-and-send, plan-name, date and money formatting now live once in `operations/billing-email.ts`, shared with the plan-change emails.
- The template is `renewal.template.ts` (English only, like every other template), with preview entries and a README row.
