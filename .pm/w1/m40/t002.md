## Objective

A subscription has at most one pending change:

- `subscriptionStatus` exposes `scheduledChange { priceId, effectiveAt }`.
- A new change replaces a scheduled one.
- Any change withdraws a pending cancellation, and cancelling releases a scheduled change.
- `cancelScheduledPlanChange` releases the schedule and leaves the current plan untouched.
- The preview says which pending change a new choice replaces.

## Context

- Cancellation today sets `cancel_at_period_end` (`stripe-service.ts`), and `resumeSubscription` clears it.
- PRFAQ005 question 14 (last two rows) and question 16.
- Billing operations are GraphQL-only and browser-session-only, with parity, op-class and rate-limit entries (see m39/t001).

## Steps

1. Add `scheduledChange` to status, and the `replacesScheduledChange` and `withdrawsCancellation` flags to the preview.
2. Make change, cancel and resume mutually exclusive in the service layer, replacing or releasing the other pending change explicitly.
3. Add `cancelScheduledPlanChange` with its authorization action and gates, and update the dashboard schema copy.

## Files

- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/stripe/`, the authorization catalog, the API gates and `dashboard/api-gateway.graphql`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each combination ends with exactly one pending change, visible in status and in the preview: scheduled plus a new change, cancellation plus a change, and a scheduled change plus cancellation.
- [ ] Releasing a schedule leaves the current subscription and price unchanged.
- [ ] Gates pass, and the operation has no REST or MCP eligibility.
