# The change sheet's "Then $X" ignores ongoing discounts

Found by w1/m39/t015's production run. The plan-change preview reports `nextAmount` as the target price's list amount (`plan-change-preview.ts`, `listPrice`), and the sheet renders it as "Then $959.88/year, next on …". A subscriber with an ongoing discount pays less at that renewal: a percent-off or amount-off coupon whose duration is `forever`, or a `repeating` one that is still running. The QA run's 100%-off-forever code made the real next charge $0, and the sheet still said $959.88.

**Fix:** compute the next charge after the discounts that will still apply at that date. Either preview the renewal invoice (`invoices.createPreview` for the next period, with the change applied), or apply the subscription's discounts to the list price when their duration covers the next charge. Say "before discounts" when it cannot be known. Pin it with a percent-off-forever, an amount-off and an expired-repeating case.
