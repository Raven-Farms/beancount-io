## Objective

Premium yearly subscriptions and switches started from the dashboard use the $143.88 price. Subscribers on the $99.99 yearly or $9.99 monthly Premium prices still resolve to Premium and see their price labelled as legacy. The pricing page keeps selling exactly what it sells today until it switches to $143.88, outside this repository.

## Context

- **Pricing-page compatibility** (milestone README): the pricing page creates Checkout with `createSubscriptionSession` for the $99.99 Premium yearly price. Refusing that price would break Premium yearly checkout on `beancount.io/pricing`.
- Backend `SUBSCRIPTION_CONFIG` (`backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/stripe/service/stripe.ts`) lists every recognized price. Dashboard `dashboard/src/features/user-settings/pages/general/stripe-config.ts` holds one monthly and one yearly price per tier, and `dashboard/src/common/lib/subscription/tier-config.ts` hard-codes monthly price strings.
- PRFAQ005 question 21 decision 3 and question 11.

## Steps

1. Add the new price to `SUBSCRIPTION_CONFIG` as Premium yearly. Mark the $99.99 and $9.99 prices as legacy:
   - The dashboard never offers a legacy price.
   - `changePlan` refuses one as a target.
   - Tier resolution still recognizes both.
   - `createSubscriptionSession` keeps accepting the $99.99 price.
2. In the dashboard, point Premium yearly at the new price. Keep one checked-in table of list amounts next to the price IDs, replacing the hard-coded strings.
3. Expose whether the current price is legacy, so the summary card can label it.
4. Add an inbox note on this workstream: retire the $99.99 price from `createSubscriptionSession` once the pricing page sells $143.88.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A dashboard Premium yearly preview or Checkout uses the new price, and `changePlan` refuses a legacy target.
- [ ] `createSubscriptionSession` still accepts every price the pricing page sells, the $99.99 Premium yearly price included.
- [ ] Subscriptions on either legacy price still resolve to PREMIUM for quotas, profile and settings.
- [ ] A test pins the dashboard price table to the backend configuration. It checks that every yearly per-month figure is the yearly amount ÷ 12, rounded to cents, and that every tier saves 20%.

## Out of scope

- Moving existing subscribers to the new price, and changing the pricing page.
