## Objective

Apply the corrected billing model to the existing user population, rather than leaving repairs dependent on each user opening settings. Preserve purchased live subscriptions and make the rollout's coverage measurable.

## Context

t004 supplies the preview/apply/rollback tool; this task owns the existing-users rollout and its completeness evidence. Existing users include live-only, historical-test-only, mixed-history, expired/canceled, no-billing-history and ambiguous/missing-binding cases. No specific production row has been verified. Reports and rollback snapshots containing real identities stay outside tracked files.

## Steps

1. Inventory existing users with stable pagination and classify all relevant billing histories. No-history users remain unchanged; never infer a paid binding from email alone or grant live access from a test subscription.
2. Run the tested t004 tool in preview mode across the complete selected cohort; record aggregate scanned/eligible/unchanged/repairable/unresolved counts, checkpoint/cursor, deployed revision and dry-run-to-apply change fingerprint.
3. After reviewing the preview and verifying focused ownership/repair tests and typecheck, apply in bounded resumable batches with per-user transactions, rollback records and concurrent-webhook conflict protection. Re-read authoritative live subscriptions before writes; preserve billing terms, discounts and Stripe objects.
4. Resume from a checkpoint, rerun for idempotence, and verify representative repaired users through profile, settings and quota paths. Unresolved ownership/provider failures remain explicit and unwritten; do not count them as backfilled.
5. Record a public-safe aggregate result and separately retain private operational evidence. If credentials/deployment are unavailable, block actual execution with an exact unblock condition; successful tooling alone does not complete this ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Stable pagination accounts for the full selected existing-user cohort and aggregate classifications sum to the scanned count.
- [ ] Preview performs no writes; reviewed apply batches are bounded, resumable, conflict-safe and backed by rollback records outside git.
- [ ] Live subscribers receive their verified plan/period; test-only and no-history users gain no paid access; ambiguous/missing ownership remains unresolved without guessing.
- [ ] No actual Stripe subscription, coupon, charge, account or ledger is created/deleted/changed by the backfill.
- [ ] Rerunning is a no-op for repaired users; representative profile/settings/quota read-backs agree; real execution evidence exists or the ticket remains explicitly blocked.
