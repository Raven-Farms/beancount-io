# Billing shows Stripe's raw subscription status

Found by w1/m39/t012's review. The billing details card (`dashboard/src/features/user-settings/pages/general/subscription-billing-section.tsx`) renders `subscription.status` verbatim in its badge. A reader sees `active`, `past_due` or `unpaid` in every locale, Chinese and Persian included.

**Fix:** map each Stripe status the card can show to a `userSettings.subscriptionStatus.<status>` key in all 15 dashboard locales: `active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, `unpaid`, `incomplete`, `incomplete_expired`, `canceled` and `paused`. Fall back to a generic label for an unknown status. Pin one locale's rendering in the billing-section tests.
