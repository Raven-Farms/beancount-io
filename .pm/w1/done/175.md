# In-place bank approval for plan changes (Stripe.js)

When a plan change needs the customer's bank to approve the payment (`changePlan` → `REQUIRES_ACTION`), the dashboard's change sheet (w1/m39/t007) sends the customer to Stripe's hosted invoice page and asks them to refresh afterwards. PRFAQ005 question 15 wants the approval to complete inside the sheet.

That needs Stripe.js in the dashboard: either the `@stripe/stripe-js` package or Stripe's script loaded under the dashboard's CSP. Adding a dependency needs the user's approval (root `AGENTS.md`).

**When approved:** confirm `changePlan`'s `clientSecret` with `stripe.confirmCardPayment` in the sheet, keep the hosted invoice page as the fallback when Stripe.js cannot load, and cover approve and decline with tests.

## Done — 2026-10-19

The user approved `@stripe/stripe-js` on 2026-10-19.
- A plan change whose payment the bank must approve is now approved inside Billing. The sheet steps aside while Stripe shows the bank's challenge, because an open modal dialog would trap focus and block the pointer on it. Then:
  - approved: the plan, subscription and AI allowance are refetched, and the change reads as done;
  - declined: "Your bank didn't approve the payment. Nothing changed.", with **Try again** and **Use another card**;
  - still pending, or Stripe.js cannot run (blocked script, no client secret, Stripe unreachable): today's hosted-invoice link and **Refresh**.
- A PaymentIntent that is `requires_action` goes through `handleNextAction`. One that is `requires_confirmation` goes through `confirmPayment` with `redirect: "if_required"`, which returns to `/settings/billing` only when the bank's method redirects.
- Stripe.js loads only through a dynamic import, as its own lazy chunk outside the initial graph. `src/test/stripe-js-lazy.test.ts` fails on any static import of the package.
- The dashboard sends no Content-Security-Policy, either in source or in production's response headers, so nothing needed allowing. Whoever adds a CSP must allow `https://js.stripe.com` (script, frame), `https://hooks.stripe.com` (frame) and `https://api.stripe.com` (connect).
- New analytics event `payment_action_completed`. A decline in the sheet also reports `payment_action_failed`.
