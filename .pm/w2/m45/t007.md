## Objective

Make the siblings hand investment work to the trade skill, and make the suite's inventory and catalogs describe it.

## Context

- `beancount-migrate` says holdings and lots are "surfaced as follow-up work, not converted". Point that follow-up at the trade skill.
- `beancount-add` and `beancount-options` skip trades with lots, and name the trade skill.
- `beancount-close` reports period-end positions, and `beancount-ask` answers holdings questions. Both can cite the realized-gains recipe.
- A new skill needs an installer inventory entry in `scripts/skills/beancount-skills.py`, and the suite count in `skills/README.md`, `skills/AGENTS.md`, `docs/skills/installation.md`, and the root `README.md`. A widened options skill needs its description and catalog rows updated instead.

## Steps
