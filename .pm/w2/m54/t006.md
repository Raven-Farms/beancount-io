## Objective

Make the moment a payment succeeds feel good: a short confetti burst on the confirmation's "done" state and on an in-sheet upgrade that charged — the same transactions t005 counts.

## Context

- The user approved adding a small confetti library (2026-10-20). Prefer `canvas-confetti`: small, maintained, and it takes `disableForReducedMotion`.
- Lazy-load it the way `pages/general/bank-approval.ts` lazy-loads `@stripe/stripe-js`, and keep it out of the entry chunk; `src/test/stripe-js-lazy.test.ts` shows how to guard a lazy-only import.
- Reuse t005's once-per-transaction memory so a refresh never replays it.

## Steps

1. `yarn add canvas-confetti` (and its types) in the dashboard workspace.
2. Add `celebrate(transactionId)` that checks `prefers-reduced-motion`, dynamically imports the library, fires one short burst, and records the transaction.
3. Call it where t005 records a purchase.
4. Add a lazy-import guard test like the Stripe.js one.

## Files

- `dashboard/package.json`, `dashboard/yarn.lock`
- `dashboard/src/features/user-settings/lib/celebrate.ts` (new)
- the confirmation and change-sheet call sites
- `dashboard/src/test/` lazy-import guard

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Plays once per paid transaction; not while confirming, on timeout, on $0, or with reduced motion.
- [ ] The library lands in its own lazy chunk (`yarn build` output), not the entry chunk.
- [ ] Dashboard gates pass.

## Out of scope

- Sound, custom shapes or brand art.
