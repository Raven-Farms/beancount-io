## Objective

Turn one mainstream broker activity export (CSV or investment OFX) into proposed lot-carrying entries through the import pipeline's dedup discipline: buys, sells with booking-produced gains, dividends, fees — reviewed as one batch, idempotent on re-import.

## Context

- `beancount-import` handles bank/card CSV, OFX/QFX, QIF, and PDF statements; investment activity (lot buys/sells) is not among its shapes today, and `beancount-trade` refuses statement files by design.
- Every imported row carries an `import-id` per `beancount-import/references/dedup.md`; sells must reduce lots the ledger actually holds or stop and ask.

## Steps

1. Pick the first format from real export shapes (document which and why), build a synthetic export fixture, and derive the row-to-lot mapping empirically with `bea check`.
2. Write the procedure into the owning skill decided by the mapping's home (import stage vs trade batch), including the unheld-lot and unknown-commodity stops.

## Files

- Owning skill text/references, `references/dedup.md` if a new source kind is named

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The synthetic export imports to entries whose lots, cash, and gains match hand-computed figures, re-import adds zero entries, and a sell of an unheld lot stops before any write.

## Out of scope

- Options activity (beancount-options), margin/short activity.
