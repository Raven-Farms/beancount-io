## Result

Ran m38's routing set twice per client against a scratch suite with all three proposed names (`beancount-options-trades`, `beancount-close-month`, `beancount-query`), relabeled to match, and compared with the two current-name after runs. Recorded in `skills/docs/routing-rehearsal.md`, "Proposed renames". The seven `close`/`option` collision prompts route 7/7 under both names on both clients in every run; close/options positives 6/6 and the ask/query groups are identical. Outcome per the second acceptance criterion: the rename shows no routing benefit and the "no regression elsewhere" clause is not cleanly met, so the milestone is blocked with this evidence rather than continued (see the milestone README).
