## Objective

Give `beancount-ask` tested recipes that list open lots per account/commodity with units, cost basis, market value when price directives exist, unrealized gain, and allocation share. Without a price the recipe reports the gap instead of a number.

## Context

- `skills/beancount-ask/references/bql-recipes.md` covers spending and book-value net worth only; its net-worth recipe already warns it is cost basis, not market value.
- `beancount-trade`'s `references/realized-gains.md` covers closed lots and states open lots are not covered; cite it for realized figures instead of duplicating.
- Every recipe there is tested against a fixture ledger before it ships (the file's own convention).

## Steps

1. Derive the BQL empirically against a synthetic two-account, multi-lot fixture with and without `price` directives; record the exact queries and outputs.
2. Write the recipes (holdings, allocation, unrealized) with the missing-price behavior stated, and cross-point ask/trade at each other.

## Files

- `skills/beancount-ask/references/bql-recipes.md`, ask/trade `SKILL.md` pointers as needed

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Each recipe runs verbatim against its fixture and produces the hand-computed figures; a commodity with no price is listed as unpriced, never valued at cost.

## Out of scope

## Result

Six tested recipes added to `beancount-ask` (`references/bql-recipes.md` §8): `holdings-lots` (open lots at cost), `holdings-unpriced`, `holdings-lots-market` (per-lot market + unrealized), `holdings-prices`, `holdings-market-total`, `holdings-allocation`. Derived empirically against `bea 0.3.1`: `value(position)` on an unpriced commodity silently returns raw units (10 ACME → 10), so every market figure is guarded by `currency(value(position)) = cost_currency`, unpriced holdings are named first, and allocation binds the engine-computed total as a literal so the engine does the division. Trade's realized-gains reference now points open-lot questions here. New executed test `test_holdings_recipes_value_priced_lots_and_name_unpriced_ones` (suite: 120 OK); `ci-check.py` passes.
