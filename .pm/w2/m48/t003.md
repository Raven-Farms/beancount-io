## Objective

Turn ADR 020's evaluation fixtures for these two rows into synthetic eval cases with assertions on the required behavior. Bring the existing reconcile and close evals up to date with the new coverage section.

## Context

- `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-reconcile/evals/evals.json` holds 16 cases with fixtures under `evals/files/`. `beancount-close/evals/evals.json` holds 7. `scripts/skills/ci-check.py` validates `evals.json`, fixture paths, and every `*ledger.beancount` fixture with `bea check`.
- ADR 020 fixtures in scope: "Statement set omits one of three active accounts"; "Two equal payments, one statement row; split settlement; differing account labels"; and "a commodity [that] uses more than two decimals" (the precision part of that row; item identity and age belong to the P1 exception register).
- Use synthetic data only. No real financial documents.

## Steps

1. Close: three active accounts with statements for two. The prompt does not mention the third account; the agent must name it as unverified, and the headline must not call the close verified.
2. Close: a June close with a statement covering June 1–20 only, which must report "partly covered".
3. Reconcile: two equal payments in the ledger against one statement row, reported as an ambiguity with both positions.
4. Reconcile: one statement row that settles two ledger postings, which must be asked about.
5. Reconcile: one CSV holding rows for two accounts under different labels, where the other account's rows are excluded and named.
6. Reconcile: rows that do not take the printed opening balance to the printed ending balance, reported as an extraction problem.
7. Reconcile: a commodity printed with more than two decimals, where precision is preserved and no 0.01 tolerance appears.
8. Update the expectations of existing cases whose proposal now opens with the coverage section, without weakening any existing assertion.
