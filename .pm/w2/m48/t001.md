## Objective

Between Normalize and Match, have `beancount-reconcile` establish and show what the supplied statement covers, and check the statement against itself, before it classifies any discrepancy. Along the way, stop the matcher from resolving ambiguity on the user's behalf: two equal postings against one row, or one row against a split settlement, becomes a question rather than a label.

## Context

- `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-reconcile/SKILL.md` has five phases: Discover, Normalize, Match, Propose, Verify. Normalize extracts the statement period, the ending balance, and the transaction lines. Nothing counts or totals the rows, records which rows were dropped, or compares the statement's own opening, rows, and ending.
- `references/matching.md` requires amount equality for any pairing, uses a ±3-day window, and classes "one line matching two+ postings" as **duplicate**. ADR 020's fixture "two equal payments, one statement row; split settlement; differing account labels" requires preserving the ambiguity and the source-row identity instead.
- ADR 020's release gate for this row: "Detect missing accounts and misleading counterparty columns; never improve match rates by silently excluding rows." Its GL note: preserve commodity precision, choose tolerances per commodity and policy, and report excluded or below-threshold differences, rather than copying the upstream two-decimal rounding and 0.01 tolerance.
- `<!-- recipe: … -->` blocks in `SKILL.md` and `references/statement-formats.md` are executed by `skills/scripts/test_beancount_skills.py` against isolated ledgers, so the ledger-side count and total belongs in a recipe, next to the existing `tie-out` recipe.
- Reconcile stays one account and one statement period per run. A statement period may arrive as several files (two half-month exports, for example).

## Steps

1. Define the coverage section and place it first in the proposal, before the diff report:
   - **Account identity:** the statement's own account label or number against the target. Ask when they disagree.
   - **Currency:** the target currency, with rows in any other currency excluded and listed.
   - **Period:** the statement start and end. With several files, any gap or overlap between them.
   - **Source rows:** the count and the signed total in ledger sign.
   - **Excluded rows:** each with its reason (pending, another account, another currency, a subtotal or summary line, the same row in two files).
   - **Ambiguous mappings:** a counterparty column against the description, or which column carries the amount. Ask before using either.
2. Add a statement-arithmetic check. When the statement prints an opening balance, opening plus the included rows must equal the printed ending balance. Compute it with the engine's decimal arithmetic, as the tie-out recipe does, and quote the output. A failure is an extraction problem: report it and stop before Match.
3. Add a `coverage-ledger-side` recipe: the target account's posting count and signed total for the statement window (`date >= start AND date < end + 1 day`), one row per currency. Show it beside the source figures.
4. In `references/matching.md`, replace "one line matching two+ postings → duplicate" with an ambiguity rule. Two or more equal postings for one row are reported with their file positions, and the user says whether one is a duplicate or belongs to an adjacent period. A row equal to the sum of two near-dated postings, or the reverse, is asked about and never paired automatically.
5. Normalize amounts at the precision the commodity is printed with, and never round to two decimals. A tolerance comes only from the user, per commodity, and every difference below it is listed. Say so in `references/statement-formats.md`.
6. Update the example proposal to open with the coverage section.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every proposal opens with the coverage section, with every field above, and included plus excluded rows equal the source rows.
- [ ] A statement whose included rows do not take its printed opening balance to its printed ending balance is reported as an extraction problem before any match.
- [ ] Rows for a second account in the same file are excluded and named, and never matched to the target.
- [ ] `coverage-ledger-side` exists as an executable recipe and runs in `test_beancount_skills.py`.
- [ ] `matching.md` no longer labels an equal-amount multiplicity a duplicate without asking.

## Out of scope

- Carrying open reconciling items across periods (ADR 020's P1 exception register).
- Reconciling several accounts in one run.
