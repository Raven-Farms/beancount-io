# Ledger rows in Home's Latest Updates do nothing when tapped

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. After any write, the top rows are usually "Committed to <ledger> · Ledger". They look tappable but give no navigation and no feedback. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: ledger activity items carry *relative* links (`/ledger/<owner>/<repo>/commit/<sha>` or `/ledger/<owner>/<repo>`), but the row only acts on `link.startsWith("http")`. The app already routes these paths through `resolve-app-link.ts`, which maps `commit/<sha>` to `/commit-detail`.

Code-level at `main` `5793e4e95`. Not exercised natively: the card sits below the fold on Home, and the automation can't scroll.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/components/feed-card.tsx:65-71` (`isExternal` gate, no else branch)
- The links are built in `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/gitea/feed/service/activity-transformer.ts:163-177`.
- The router mapping is `mobile/src/common/app-links/resolve-app-link.ts:115-126`.

## Fix

For relative `/ledger/…` links, resolve them with `resolveAppLink` and open the target in-app: commit detail, or select the ledger. Disable the row's press state, and drop its button role, only when a link resolves to nothing.

**Acceptance:**
- Tapping a "Committed to puncsky/mcp-agent-eval" row opens that commit's detail.
- A ledger-level row opens the ledger.
- External Blog and Release rows still open Safari.
