# A merchant spelled two ways gets recurrence from only one spelling: a false red "Overdue", or no detection at all

**Severity:** moderate. Payee spellings drift (bank imports change case), and the Recurring section then flags a bill paid on time as Overdue, or misses a perfectly regular bill. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/046 merged case variants into one Merchants row (`otherSpellings`), but recurrence detection, the "enough history" check and the manual-override lookup still use only the *displayed* spelling's series. 046's resolution notes that "the recurrence chip stays keyed by the displayed spelling"; this files the consequence.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `698f8aba5`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-mrc` was ten monthly `Expenses:Entertainment:Streaming 15.49 USD` entries on the 15th, Jan–Oct 2026, with payee "QaFlix" for Jan–Jun and "QAFLIX" for Jul–Oct.
- Merchants → All shows "QaFlix · RECURRING · 10 transactions · Oct 15, 2026".
- Recurring shows **"QaFlix · Monthly · $15.49 · Overdue"** in red. It was paid Oct 15; only the "QaFlix" series (ending June) is analysed.

The reviewer's selector runs show the alternating-month variant gets no recurrence at all, and that a later majority flip of the display spelling loses a saved Recurring override.

## Where (`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/`)

- `selectors/merchant-sections.ts:57-61`

## Fix

Merge the series of every spelling in `[payee, ...otherSpellings]` before detection and the history check. Key overrides by the case-folded merchant key, not the display spelling, and migrate any existing override.

**Acceptance:**
- The fixture shows "Monthly · Next Nov 15", not Overdue.
- The alternating-spelling variant is detected.
- An override survives a display-spelling flip.
- Add selector tests.
