# The Budget chart is sized for the full screen width, so it overflows its card by 32 pt and the first bar and label are cut off

**Severity:** minor. On every Budget card the first period is partly hidden behind the y-axis, and its label is clipped (for example "MAR" shows as "IAR"). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `BudgetBarChart` computes its columns from `ScreenWidth - contentPadding * 2 - LEFT_PADDING`. The chart actually sits inside the Budget list's 16 pt gutter *and* the card's own 16 pt `chartWrap` gutter, so the plot is 32 pt wider than its viewport. `ScrollableAxisChart` scrolls to the end on layout, which hides the first 32 pt even when every bar should fit.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `24a2d3f2c`, on the disposable QA ledger (restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`), with a quarterly budget (four bars). On YTD and ALL alike, the first column is visibly narrower than the other three, and its label reads "IAR".

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/common/d3/budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx:74` (`availableWidth`) and `:83-87` (`columnWidth`, `plotWidth`).
- `budget-screen.tsx:50-51` (`list: { paddingHorizontal: gutter }`) and `budget-group-card.tsx:147-148` (`chartWrap: { paddingHorizontal: gutter }`) add 32 pt the calculation doesn't know about.

## Fix

Measure the container with `onLayout` (or accept a `width` prop from the card) instead of deriving it from `ScreenWidth`. Check the other `ScreenWidth`-based charts (`bar-chart-d3.tsx:44`, `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:100`, `interactive-line-chart.tsx:504`) against their actual containers.

**Acceptance:** a Budget card with four or fewer periods shows every column at full width with no horizontal scroll, and the first label is unclipped, at default size and at AX2.

## Dedupe

w3/124 and w21/018 are the chart's accessibility label, and w21/096 the weekly label format. Searched `.pm/**` for ScreenWidth, availableWidth, budget-bar-chart, clipped and "cut off".

**Also (2026-10-21):** account detail's balance chart has the same mismatch. `account-detail-screen.tsx:107` pads it by 10 pt, but `interactive-line-chart.tsx:504` sizes it as `ScreenWidth - 32`, so the plot sits flush left with 12 pt spare on the right (flush right in RTL).
