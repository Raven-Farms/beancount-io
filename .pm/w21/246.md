# At AX2 the Transactions tab's header reads "Transacti…"

**Severity:** minor. Reproduces at AX2 (`accessibility-large`), the ceiling in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `LedgerDrawerHeader` centres the title in the space left between two fixed 88 pt action groups (about 190 pt on iPhone 17e). The title scales with Dynamic Type, has `numberOfLines={1}`, and can't shrink, so the longest tab title truncates. Longer translations of other titles would too.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `4ba42392a`: at AX2, Transactions' header reads **"Transacti…"**, while Home, Accounts, Reports and Files fit.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/components/ledger-drawer/ledger-drawer-header.tsx:36-43` (`navTitle`: `flex: 1`, `fontSize: fontSizes.xl`, no `maxFontSizeMultiplier`) and `:165` (`numberOfLines={1}`). The side groups are `width: iconActionSize * 2`.

## Fix

Let the title shrink to fit (`adjustsFontSizeToFit` with a `minimumFontScale` around 0.7), or cap its `maxFontSizeMultiplier` the way iOS caps navigation titles. Check the longest title in each locale.

**Acceptance:** at AX2, every tab title reads in full in English, and in Deutsch ("Transaktionen").

w21/111 is the drawer's own header at AX2, w21/027 (done) Budget card titles, and w3/208 and w3/219 other truncation. Searched `.pm/**` for "Transacti…", "header title" and AX2 together with title.
