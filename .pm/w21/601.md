# The transaction headline and list row pad share and point counts to ".00" while Money flow doesn't: "+10.00 GOOG" over "+10 GOOG"

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transactions-screen/utils/transaction-display-utils.ts:37` (`formatAmount`) sets the minimum decimals from `currencyMinorUnits(currency)`, which is 2 for any unknown code. Money flow (`select-transaction-detail.ts:114-116`) keeps the recorded digits for non-currencies and holdings at cost, per w21/445's rule.

Native (iPhone 17e at `dbe9b0144`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): `Assets:QaGoog 10 GOOG {150.00 USD}` / `Income:QaRsu -1500.00 USD`. The Transactions row and the detail headline read "+10.00 GOOG", while Money flow reads "+10 GOOG · cost $150.00". Under `tsx`, 1500 PTS reads "+1,500.00 PTS" over "+1,500 PTS", and 0.5 VTI reads "+0.50 VTI" over "+0.5 VTI".

## Fix

Use the same digits rule as Money flow in `formatAmount` (recorded digits for non-currencies and holdings at cost).

**Acceptance:** that row and headline read "+10 GOOG". Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w21/445 and w21/506 fixed only the Money flow rows; w21/571 is about splits. Searched `.pm/**` for formatAmount and currencyMinorUnits together with the headline.
