# Add Budget offers only operating currencies, falling back to USD, so a ledger without operating_currency always budgets in USD and reads $0 spent

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `add-budget-screen.tsx:220-221, 292-294` and `currency-options.ts:7-15` take `currencies` from `ledger-meta-utils.ts:64` (`operating_currency` only), with a USD fallback. Open Account already offers every ledger currency through `openAccountCurrencyChoices(currencies, meta.currencies)`, and the dashboard's `CurrencyCombobox` lists all of them. Under `tsx`: a EUR ledger with no `operating_currency` offers only `USD`, and its EUR spending reads `0` against the USD budget. On a USD ledger, a JPY trip account can't be budgeted in JPY.

Code-level at `main` `f628d4727`. Not run natively, because it needs a ledger without `operating_currency`.

## Fix

Build the options with `openAccountCurrencyChoices(currencies, ledgerMeta.currencies)`, plus the w21/234 "Other…" entry. Default to the account's commodity or the first ledger currency, never a blind USD.

**Acceptance:** on the EUR fixture, Add Budget defaults to EUR, and the budget tracks the EUR spending.

## Dedupe

w21/121 (update lock), 085 and 234 (Open Account) and blocked/228 (New Transaction) differ. Searched `.pm/**` for budgetCurrencyOptions, "operating currency" and non-operating.
