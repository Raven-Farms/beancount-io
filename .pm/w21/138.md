# Pull-to-refresh on Home doesn't refresh the bell's error badge

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. After a change made outside the app (web, CLI, an agent) while the app stays in the foreground, pulling Home updates the charts and Recent Transactions but not the bell. A newly broken ledger shows no badge, and a fixed one keeps its old count. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `onRefresh` refetches ledger meta, the balance sheet, the valuation basis and prices, and signals the cards, but never refetches `getLedgerErrors`, which the bell counts. w4/042 fixed this for Notifications' own pull-to-refresh, and w21/024 fixes it on foreground; Home was left out.

Code-level at `main` `5793e4e95`. During this run's probes, external fixture writes repeatedly left Home showing stale rows until a relaunch, which is consistent with this.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/home-screen.tsx:177-192`
- `mobile/src/components/ledger-drawer/ledger-drawer-header.tsx:106` (`useLedgerErrors`, `cache-and-network`)

## Fix

Add the ledger-errors refetch to Home's `onRefresh` `Promise.all`, or have `useLedgerErrors` subscribe to the same refresh signal the cards use.

**Acceptance:** append an unbalanced entry externally and pull Home: the bell shows 1. Remove it and pull again: the badge clears.
