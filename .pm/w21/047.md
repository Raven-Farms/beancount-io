# Home's price-age warning reads a stale persisted price list after relaunch, because `useLedgerPrices` is still cache-first (residual of w21/006)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15–20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: w21/006 made every watched query revalidate on mount (`cache-and-network`) so a cold start stops showing the last session's data. `useLedgerPrices` was missed: it pins both of its queries to `cache-first` through a constant. Prices changed outside the app (on the web, by the CLI, by a price feed or by git) never reach Home's valuation caption or the "Holdings and prices" sheet, even across relaunches. The value next to them is fresh, so the screen contradicts itself.

Environment: `main` at `d01c4df74`, dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0). QA account, synthetic disposable ledger `mcp-agent-eval` (restored byte-for-byte afterwards).

## Repro (observed)

1. Earlier in the session, while the app was open, the ledger briefly held `2026-04-02 price QAX 1.2345 USD`. That fixture was later removed outside the app.
2. Outside the app, add a QAX buy/sell (6 QAX remaining at cost 1.50) and `2026-10-07 price QAX 2.10 USD`. The server's `#prices` now holds only the Oct 7 row.
3. Cold-launch the app twice. Home reads **$17,114.11 · Cost $17,110.51 · Unrealized +$3.60**, which is 6 × 2.10, so the value uses the Oct 7 price. The caption above it says **"At market value · 1 price not updated since Apr 2, 2026"**. Tapping it opens Holdings and prices: **6 QAX — Price Apr 2, 2026 ⚠ Not updated**, citing a price that no longer exists.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/common/hooks/use-ledger-prices.ts:11`: `const LEDGER_PRICES_FETCH_POLICY = "cache-first"`, passed to both `useLedgerPricesQuery` and `useLedgerManagedPricesQuery`. It overrides `APOLLO_DEFAULT_OPTIONS`, and with the persisted cache it never asks the network on mount. The comment's premise ("`invalidateLedger` evicts it after every write") covers only writes made in the app. w21/006's guard, `fetch-policies.test.ts:88`, greps for the literal `fetchPolicy: "cache-first"`, which the constant hides.

## Fix

Drop the explicit policy so the hook inherits the client default (`cache-and-network`, then `cache-first`). That costs one revalidation per mount, the same trade w21/006 made for the larger journal reads. Extend `fetch-policies.test.ts` to catch the constant form, for example by asserting no `"cache-first"` string literal in hook sources outside the allow-listed post-invalidation read in `ledger-file-editor-screen`.

**Acceptance:** after a price is changed outside the app, a cold relaunch shows the new price date in the caption and the sheet without a pull-to-refresh. The extended policy test fails on the current hook.
