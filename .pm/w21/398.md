# A budget on a closed account keeps running forever: a former job's income target shows red every month and outranks real overspends on Home

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budgetForInterval` and `groupBudgetEntries` (`budget-selectors.ts:378-452`) and `select-budget-panel.ts` never consult close dates, although `useAccountCloseDates` exists. Under `tsx`, `Income:Salary:OldCo` (−3000 monthly, closed 2026-06-30) shows red bars from July onward and the card reads "Below target". On Home it ranks above `Expenses:Food` at 470 of 500. The only ways out are deleting its history or zeroing it, and zeroing hits w21/394.

Code-level at `main` `f628d4727`.

## Fix

End each budget history at its account's close date, so no budget applies after it, and hide the card once the window starts after the close, on the Budget page and on Home.

**Acceptance:** a budget whose account closed in June shows no budget after June and leaves Home's panel.

## Dedupe

Only w21/045 (New Transaction's picker) matched. Searched `.pm/**` for "closed account", "close date" and useAccountCloseDates with budget.
