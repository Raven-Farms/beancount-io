# A holding priced in its cost currency still shows "at cost (no price)" on the Accounts root and account detail, while Home shows it priced

**Severity:** moderate, the same impact as w21/124: Accounts tells the user to add a price that already exists, and drops the stale-price warning Home shows. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile (and the dashboard, separately). A3: w21/124's fix classifies a converted holding by a price against its cost currency, but only when the caller passes a cost map. The trial-balance (Accounts root) and account-report (account detail) callers pass none, so `selectValuation` falls back to operating-currency prices.

Code-level at `main` `6421904e1`. A reviewer's Node run on a USD ledger with 1000 USD cash plus `10 VEUR {50.00 EUR}` and `2026-06-01 price VEUR 52.00 EUR`:
```
Home (Assets):        priced [VEUR] · "At market value · 1 price not updated since Jun 1, 2026 · 1 not in total"
Accounts root:        atCostNoPrice [VEUR] · "At market value · 1 at cost (no price) · 1 not in total"
dashboard Overview:   VEUR basis "cost", no price date
```
With a recent price (2026-10-15), Home drops the stale warning, but the Accounts root still says "1 at cost (no price)".

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/components/account-list/select-trial-balance.ts:189` and `screens/account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-balance-series.ts:66` call `selectValuation` without `cost`.
- `common/valuation.ts:295-305` then falls back to operating-currency prices.
- The dashboard's `net-worth-valuation.ts:237` looks up operating-currency prices only.

## Fix

Give both callers their cost currencies, from an `at_cost` read or from the non-operating currencies in the market map. Port the same cost-currency lookup to the dashboard's `describeNetWorthValuation` (a separate dashboard change).

**Acceptance:** with the fixture, the Accounts root and VEUR's detail read "At market value · 1 price not updated since Jun 1, 2026", matching Home. Unit cases are added.
