# The Budget chart's VoiceOver summary says missed income targets are "over target", and its span has no years ("NOV–SEP")

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:236-256` counts `favorable === false` as "over", which for an income target means it fell short. The `budgetChartSummary` text (`en.ts` around `:769-772`) always says "over target". The span joins month labels with no year.

Code-level at `main` `3a3c7e4fb`. In the reviewer's tsx run, with an income target of -4000 a month and 3000 received in Nov 2024, Dec 2025 and Sep 2026, it reads "Budget versus actual for NOV–SEP. Actual $9K of $12K budgeted across 3 periods, 3 over target." A 32-month ALL span reads "MAR–OCT". Not exercised with VoiceOver.

## Fix

Word the count by direction ("missed target" for income, "over target" for expenses), or neutrally ("off target"), in all 13 locales. Add the year to the first and last span labels when the span crosses a year, as `selectedPeriodLabel` does.

**Acceptance:** the fixture reads "3 below target" (or equivalent) with years in the span. Unit cases are added.
