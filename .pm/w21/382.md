# The drawer's "Couldn't load ledgers" retry bar has nearly invisible text (1.01:1 contrast)

**Severity:** moderate (the only error notice and retry in that state). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the drawer footer's retry (`ledger-drawer.tsx:1029-1041`) uses `styles.emptyAction` (fill `theme.primary`) with `styles.stateText` (`theme.black80`) for its text (`:269-281`).
