# A payee whose entries are only transfers is never detected as recurring, and marking it Recurring says "Not enough history" despite many entries

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. Card payments, savings transfers and ATM withdrawals are classic recurring items. Marking one manually leaves a Recurring row with "Not enough history" and no amount or Next date. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the payee series reads only Expenses and Income postings, so a payee with only balance-sheet legs never enters it, `enoughHistory` is false, and a manual mark always resolves to "unknown".

Code-level at `main` `698f8aba5`. The reviewer ran the selectors on "ATM Withdrawal" (`Assets:Cash 100.00 USD` from Checking, every Saturday 2026-09-05 to 10-17, 7 entries). The row reads "7 transactions" with no badge. Switching Recurring on gives the chip "Not enough history", and the Recurring section shows that with no amount or Next.

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/payee-series.ts:48` (the Expenses/Income filter) and `selectors/resolve-recurring.ts:37`.

## Fix

For payees with no Expenses or Income legs, build the series from their transfer legs (for example the absolute amount of the debited balance-sheet account), or at least use the transaction dates for cadence, so detection and the history check work.

**Acceptance:** the fixture is detected as weekly with $100.00 and Next Oct 24, and a manual mark shows the cadence.
