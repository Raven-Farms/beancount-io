# Multi-posting entry: a sub-cent leftover shows as a red "+$0.00" that blocks Done, and an amount typed as ".5" is refused

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: two amount edge cases on the multi-posting form leave the user unable to see, or type, what the form wants.

1. **An invisible leftover.** Posting 1 is `10.004`. On the last posting, Auto is off and `-10` is typed.
   - The exact balance check sees 0.004 and `validatePostings` returns `unbalanced`.
   - The Remainder row rounds the exact string through `Number` and `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency` to two decimals, so it reads **"+$0.00"** in the error colour.
   - Done toasts "invalid balance", and the user can't see the 0.004 they need to fix.
2. **A leading decimal point.** The iOS decimal pad lets you type `.` first. `.5` or `.99` turns the field red with the amount-format message, and Done refuses with `invalidAmount`. `0.5` works.

Environment: `main` at `c2a3c7866`. Code-level, and the parsing and rounding were evaluated under Node:
- `/^-?\d+(\.\d+)?$/.test(".5")` → false
- `|10.004 − 10|.toFixed(2)` → "0.00"

Case 2 was reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0), dev build 1.20261002.50. On New Transaction, tap the first posting's amount and type `.` then `5` on the decimal pad. The field shows a red **".5"**, the Remainder shows **"—"**, and the message reads **"Use digits and one decimal point, like 1234.56"**. Nothing was saved. Case 1 was not driven natively: it needs the sign toggle and turning Auto off.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts:51` (`parseExactAmount`): the final pattern needs a digit before the dot. The doc comment lists only `1.` and `-` as the partial forms to reject.
- `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx` (the remainder label near `:673` and the `✓` row at `:827`) formats the exact remainder at the default two decimals (`common/number-utils.ts` `groupThousands`). The save gate stays exact.

## Fix

- Accept `^-?\.\d+$` by normalising it to `0.` plus the digits.
- Render the remainder at the scale of the typed amounts, i.e. the largest `amountScale` among the postings, so "+$0.004" shows. Or snap the label and the check to the same precision. Showing a rounded zero while refusing as unbalanced is the inconsistency to remove.

**Acceptance:**
- `.5` parses to `0.5`.
- With 10.004 and −10, the Remainder reads "+$0.004".
- Existing `postings-utils` tests still pass.
