# Notifications' "Recent changes" shows only the last 30 commits; older history can't be reached from the app

**Severity:** minor (a capability gap). QA, agent and import-heavy ledgers pass 30 commits quickly. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

Code-level at `main` `2e850e737`: `mobile/src/screens/notifications-screen/notifications-screen.tsx:227` fixes `{ branch: "main", page: 1, limit: 30 }`. There is no `fetchMore` or "load more", and no other screen lists commits. Commit detail for older commits is reachable only through a Home feed link. The dashboard has "Load older commits" (w3/468, w4/246).

## Fix

Add a "Show older" footer that calls `fetchMore({ page: n + 1 })` and ends when a page comes back short. Or relabel the section as "Last 30 changes".

**Acceptance:** a ledger with 45 commits can reach the oldest from Notifications.
