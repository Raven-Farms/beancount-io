# A monthly bill's "Next" date drifts one to three days past its day of the month

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: the Merchants Recurring section shows each bill's next expected date, and sorts and flags overdue bills by it. Monthly bills mostly fall on a fixed day of the month, and the app predicts them a day or more late.

Environment: `main` at `ab6f2adf7`. Verified by running the shipped `mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts` unchanged under Node 24 (`--experimental-strip-types`), with `today = 2026-10-20` and amount −30 USD per event:

| Payment dates | Predicted `nextExpected` | Real next date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07-25, 08-25, 09-25 | **2026-10-26** | 2026-10-25 |
| 2026-01-10, 02-10, 03-10, 04-10 | **2026-05-11** | 2026-05-10 |
| 2025-11-01, 12-01, 2026-01-01, 02-01 | **2026-03-04** | 2026-03-01 |
| 2026-05-31, 06-30, 07-31, 08-31 | **2026-10-01** | 2026-09-30 |

The native list could not show these rows in this run, because the Merchants list was stale after relaunch (see `w21/006`). `merchant-row.tsx:202-210` renders `nextExpected` as "Next {date}" in the Recurring section.

## Root cause

`detect-recurrence.ts`, at the end of `detectRecurrence`: `nextExpected = lastDay + round(medianGap)`. For a monthly cadence the median of 28–31-day gaps is usually 31, or 30.5 rounded up. So the prediction lands on the 1st–4th day *after* the bill's day of the month, and month-end bills roll into the next month. `isOverdue` builds its grace period on the same drifted date, so a late bill is flagged late too.

## Fix

For `monthly`, `quarterly` and `yearly` cadences, step by calendar months (1, 3 or 12) from the last event and keep its day of month, clamped to the target month's last day, so the 31st becomes 30 Sep. Keep the day-count step for `weekly` and `biweekly`. Grace stays a fraction of the median gap. The detector already does UTC day math (`parseLedgerDay` / `formatLedgerDay`), so add a small "add months, clamp day" helper beside them.

**Acceptance:** the four rows above predict 10-25, 05-10, 03-01 and 09-30. Weekly and biweekly predictions are unchanged. Overdue is computed from the corrected date. Add these cases to `src/__tests__/detect-recurrence.test.ts`.
