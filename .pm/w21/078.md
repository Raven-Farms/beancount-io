# Transactions' relative date filters (1M/3M/YTD) freeze "today" when they are applied, so after midnight new entries stay hidden while the badge still counts the filter

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: apply 1M on 2026-10-21, and the journal query asks for `2026-09-21 - 2026-10-21`.

1. Leave the app open past midnight, or resume it the next day without touching the filter sheet.
2. Add an entry dated 2026-10-22, from mobile or anywhere else.

The list never shows the new entry, even though the filter means "the last month through today". Pull to refresh refetches the same frozen range. 3M and YTD behave the same way. Only reopening and re-applying the filters moves the window.

Environment: `main` at `524a980c7`. Code-level. The simulator follows the host clock, so crossing midnight can't be driven on demand.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/transactions-screen.tsx:127-131`:
- `filterQuery = useMemo(() => toFilterQuery(filters, new Date()), [filters])` captures the date once per filter change.
- The badge on the line above (`countActiveFilters(filters, new Date())`) re-evaluates on every render.
- `use-foreground-refresh.ts` refetches on resume, but with the memoised variables.

## Fix

Key the memo on the local calendar day as well. For example, a `useToday()` hook that updates on AppState `active` and at local midnight, passed to `toFilterQuery` and `countActiveFilters` alike. Then a resumed or long-open app advances relative ranges, and the refetch carries the new window.

**Acceptance:** a hook or unit test where advancing the mocked clock past midnight, plus an AppState `active` event, changes the 1M query's end date to the new day.
