# Incoming dashboard links to `/errors`, `/commits`, `/cash-flow`, `/trial-balance` and `/holdings` open the ledger's Home instead of the matching screen

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `869713dae`, proven under `tsx`): `common/app-links/resolve-app-link.ts:227` sends any unknown ledger sub-path to `href: "/"`. The app has matching screens: Notifications for ledger errors and recent changes, and Reports for cash flow and statements. Each of `https://beancount.io/ledger/<owner>/<ledger>/errors`, `/commits`, `/cash-flow`, `/trial-balance` and `/holdings` resolves to Home.

Not run natively: `simctl openurl` links can't be accepted on this simulator.

## Fix

Map `/errors` and `/commits` to Notifications, and `/cash-flow` and `/trial-balance` to Reports (with the matching card), keeping Home as the fallback for the rest.

**Acceptance:** those links resolve to those screens. Unit cases are added in the resolver tests.
