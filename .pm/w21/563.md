# Reports' Income and Expenses transaction cards look only at the 200 newest transactions of any account, so a busy month hides the salary and says "No transactions yet" (w21/011 on Reports)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `c1689c201`, proven under `tsx`): `reports-screen/components/account-transactions-card.tsx:32` (`FETCH_WINDOW = 200`, used as `limit` at `:96`) fetches the newest 200 transactions ledger-wide, with no paging and no check against `total`. Then `selectors/select-account-transactions.ts:63-90` keeps only those under the card's root. Both the rows and the "No transactions yet" vs "No transactions in this range" choice (`:128-131`) come from that window. With a salary on Oct 1 and 250 newer grocery entries, the full set has 2 Income rows, the window has 0, and `hasAnyAccountTransaction` returns false.

Partly native (iPhone 17e, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): with one 3,000.00 salary on 2026-10-01 and 210 grocery entries on Oct 2–20, Reports ▸ 1M's chart and Cash flow show the salary. The Income card sits below the fold, and the driver can't scroll to it, so its empty state wasn't seen on device.

## Fix

Query by account (the `getLedgerAccountJournal` / `with_children` path that account detail uses) with the report's date range, or page until the range is covered. This is the approach w21/011 took for Home's Spending.

**Acceptance:** with that fixture, Reports' Income card lists the Oct 1 salary. A test covers a window where the root's rows are older than 200 others.
