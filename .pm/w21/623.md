# Update Budget with a date before the current entry saves but changes nothing, with no warning

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `6f6046fab`, proven under `tsx`): `add-budget-screen/add-budget-screen.tsx:220, 357-362` accept any date without comparing it to the current entry, and `groupBudgetEntries` (`budget-selectors.ts:267-272`) keeps the newer-dated entry as current. With a current `2026-10-21` 500 budget, Update ▸ Date Oct 1 ▸ 300 ▸ Done (a natural way to say "apply this to the whole month") saves. But the history shows "Oct 21 500 (current)" above "Oct 1 300", the card still reads 500, and October to date is a 300/500 mix.

## Fix

Warn ("A newer entry on Oct 21 will override this") or default and limit the Update date to on or after the current entry. Offer "edit the current entry" for corrections.

**Acceptance:** that flow either warns before saving or makes 300 current. A test covers it.
