# When the ledger has nothing this month, Home's 1M/3M/6M count back from the last entry instead of today (account detail fixed this in w21/240)

**Severity:** moderate. It happens every month until the month's first entry, and Home disagrees with account detail. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `filterSeriesByRange` (`common/series-util.ts:180-185`) anchors on the series' last point. The ledger service ends the series at the month-end of the last transaction or price (`interval-series.ts:201-214`), and Home's three series are never carried to today (`home-screen.tsx:84-95`). w21/240 added `carryBalanceToToday` for account detail only.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `6d8c869b2`):** on the disposable QA ledger, whose last entry is 2026-08-29, Home ▸ Net Worth ▸ **1M reads "$17,108.51 · +$1,904.40 (+12.53%)"**, an August change shown as the last month's, while account detail on 1M for a quiet account reads +$0.00 since w21/240. Under `tsx` (last entry 2026-09-28), Home 1M shows Aug→Sep `+$500.00 (+5.26%)` against account detail's Sep→Oct `+$0.00`.

## Fix

In `HomeScreenImpl`, carry each series to today with `carryBalanceToToday(series, today)`, using the `useToday` value Home already has. Like w21/240, this changes nothing for an active ledger.

**Acceptance:** a ledger with no entries since August reads +$0.00 on Home's 1M.
