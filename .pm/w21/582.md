# In Ukrainian and Russian, the file editor's "Unsaved Changes" alert has two buttons that both say "Cancel": the one that throws the edits away is translated as Cancel

**Severity:** major in those locales (a tap meant to keep edits discards them). **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the alert (`ledger-file-editor-screen/index.tsx:880-895`) shows `cancel` (keep editing) next to the destructive `ledgerEditorDiscardChanges`. At `main` `ef83e5894`:
- uk: `ledgerEditorDiscardChanges: "Скасувати"` (`uk.ts:600`), identical to `cancel: "Скасувати"` (`uk.ts:226`).
- ru: `ledgerEditorDiscardChanges: "Отменить"` (`ru.ts:601`) next to `cancel: "Отмена"`. With the body "Отменить изменения?", both read as "cancel".

## Fix

uk: "Відкинути". ru: "Не сохранять" (or "Отбросить"). Add a locale test that the destructive label differs from `cancel`.

**Acceptance:** in uk and ru, the alert's two buttons are distinct, and the destructive one says discard.
