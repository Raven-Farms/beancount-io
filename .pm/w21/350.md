# A failed refresh after a write erases the saved offline cache, so the next offline launch shows "Failed to load" everywhere

**Severity:** moderate. This defeats the offline cold start right after a successful write. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `invalidateLedgerData` (`mobile/src/common/apollo/invalidate-ledger.ts:132-137`) evicts every ledger-scoped `ROOT_QUERY` field and runs `gc()` inside `refetchQueries.updateCache`. The persistor saves on every cache write (`cache-persist.ts:60`, `trigger: "write"`), so the evicted cache is saved too. If the refetch then fails, nothing writes the data back, and the empty cache is what stays on disk. Screens already mounted keep their last result, which hides the loss until the next launch. Screens that weren't mounted (account detail, Budget) lose their cache even when the refetch succeeds.

Code-level at `main` `7280ccceb`. The reviewer ran the real `invalidateLedgerData` against a real `InMemoryCache`, with the network failing after the write. `ROOT_QUERY` went from holding the balance sheet to `{"__typename":"Query"}`, and the `Ledger` entity was collected. Not run natively.

## Repro

1. On a flaky network, add a transaction. The save commits, but the follow-up refresh fails.
2. Kill the app and relaunch it offline.

Home, Accounts and Reports show "Failed to load" instead of the cached numbers, and the Home budget panel has no offline fallback left.

## Fix

Refetch first and evict only after it succeeds. Alternatively, pause the persistor for the duration of the invalidation and resume it on success, or mark the fields stale instead of evicting them.

**Acceptance:** after a write whose refresh fails, an offline relaunch still shows the cached Home, Accounts and Reports.

## Dedupe

w21/066 is the same mechanism for the drawer's ledger list only. w21/227 is the 1 MiB persist cap. Searched `.pm/**` for evict, "refetch fails", invalidation and offline.
