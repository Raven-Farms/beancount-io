# A failed refresh on Budget replaces every card's cached chart with "Failed to load budgets" and hides Actual, Variance and the meter

**Severity:** minor (a routine foreground refresh while offline triggers it). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budget-screen/hooks/use-budget-actuals.ts:88-89, 148` sets `settled = !error && …`, where `error` includes refetch errors that arrive with data still present. Then `budget-group-card.tsx:215, 350` builds stats from `[]` and shows the error branch. Under `tsx`, `selectBudgetCardStats(group, [])` returns actual, variance, status and progress all `null`. There's no stale banner on Budget. Code-level at `main` `966ff79aa` (selector behavior proven under `tsx`; Apollo 3.14.1 keeps the old `data` and sets `error` on a failed refetch, `useQuery.js:236-253`). Not run natively: the simulator can't cut the network here.

## Fix

Treat an error with data present as stale: keep the stats and chart, and show the stale banner (the w21/239 pattern).

**Acceptance:** after a failed refresh, the cards keep Actual, Variance and the chart under a stale banner. A unit case covers error-with-data.
