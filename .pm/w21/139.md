# VoiceOver reads a Merchants row as just the payee, dropping its count, Recurring badge, cadence, typical amount and "Overdue"

**Severity:** moderate (accessibility). A VoiceOver user can't find overdue subscriptions or tell merchants apart by activity. A row showing "Netflix · Recurring · Monthly · ~$15.99 · Overdue" is read as "Netflix, button". **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the row's explicit `accessibilityLabel={merchant.payee}` replaces every child Text.

Code-level at `main` `aa4a9793a`. Looking the row up by testID natively hung the automation bridge, because every row shares `testID="merchant-row"`.

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/merchant-row.tsx:288-292`. The visible text it drops is built at `:179-212` and rendered at `:217-283`.

## Fix

Compose the label from the visible parts: payee, the "N transactions" plural, Recurring with cadence, typical amount, the Overdue or "Next {date}" status, and the last date, all from the same translated strings. Optionally move the secondary parts into `accessibilityValue`. Give rows unique testIDs (`merchant-row-<payee>`).

**Acceptance:** VoiceOver on a recurring overdue row reads payee, count, "Recurring, monthly", the typical amount and "Overdue". Add a unit test for the label builder.
