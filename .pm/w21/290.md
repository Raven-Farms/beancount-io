# Budgets in BTC or ETH are rounded to cents and judged with a $0.005 tolerance, so a 33% overspend reads "On target"

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. Only budgets in fractional commodities are wrong, but for those the status, colour, meter and Home ranking all are. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: prorated budgets are rounded to cents, and `varianceStatus` uses a fixed `VARIANCE_EPSILON = 0.005` in every unit. w4/130 fixed the dashboard with a scale-relative tolerance and said explicitly not to copy mobile's "fixed half-cent assumption into arbitrary commodities".

Code-level at `main` `6421904e1`. A reviewer ran the mobile and dashboard selectors under Node on a monthly budget of `0.003 BTC` (today 2026-10-21):
```
Sep (whole month), actual 0.004:   mobile status=on,  progress 133.3   | dashboard status=above, 133.3
Oct (in progress), actual 0.0015:  mobile budget=0, status=on          | dashboard budget=0.003, status=below, 50.0
Home panel (Oct): budget 0, noBudget true, shown "0.00 BTC of 0.00 BTC", ranked first
```

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:436` (`Math.round(total * 100) / 100`).
- `:456-463` (`VARIANCE_EPSILON`, `varianceStatus`).
- Consumed by `select-budget-card-stats.ts:119-126` and `home-screen/selectors/select-budget-panel.ts:96-100`.

## Fix

Drop the cents rounding, or round to the budget amount's recorded scale. Use a relative tolerance like the dashboard's `budgetVariance`, and format budget amounts at their recorded scale.

**Acceptance:** the September case reads "Above target", and the October one "Below target" with a 0.003 BTC budget. USD cases are unchanged. Unit cases are added.
