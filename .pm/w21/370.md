# Reports' category shares divide by the net total, so with a refund in the period the percentages add up to more than 100% (75% + 50%)

**Severity:** moderate. Share labels and bars overstate every category whenever any category is net-refunded, which is common. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `breakdownShare(value, total)` (`reports-screen/selectors/select-breakdown-rows.ts:45-48`) divides by the signed section total from `select-ranged-account-tree.ts:244`. It is used at `category-breakdown.tsx:301` and printed at `:351`.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `d5c763aa2`):** I set up a disposable fixture: October `Expenses:QaFood` 300, `Expenses:QaShop` 200 and `Expenses:QaElec` −100 (a TV return). Reports ▸ 1M ▸ Expenses card showed **$400.00, QaFood $300.00 · 75%, QaShop $200.00 · 50%**, and QaElec −$100.00 with no share: 125% in total. The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`. The reviewer's `tsx` run of the real selectors also gives 96% + 5% = 101% on the Airbnb-style income figures from w3/216.

## Fix

Divide by the sum of the positive top-level rows; keep the headline as the net total. Return `null` when there are no positive rows.

**Acceptance:** with the fixture, the cards read Food 60%, Shopping 40%, and the refund row has no share.

## Dedupe

w21/039 is the null share for a negative row (it kept clamped percentages for positive rows); w3/216 is about minus signs. Searched `.pm/**` for breakdownShare, pctLabel, "share of", percent, "more than 100" and denominator.
