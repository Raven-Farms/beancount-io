# A receipt with a $0 or unreadable total, and the auto leg, still seed a real "0.00" that typing appends to (w21/159 follow-up)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/159 empties a typed leg's seed, but two paths still put real "0.00" text in an amount field:
- **`createPrefilledPostings`:** a receipt whose total parses as zero or null seeds `amountInput` `["0.00", "0.00"]`. Typing then gives "0.0012.50", and the save fails as `invalidAmount`.
- **The auto leg:** it shows "0.00" as text. On iOS, focus selects it, so a first keystroke replaces it. But a second tap places the cursor and appends ("0.0012.50" via `updatePostingAmount`).

Native check (iPhone 17e, `main` `3e830700b`): the auto leg's "0.00" is real, selectable text with Cut/Copy handles, not a placeholder. Under Node, `createPrefilledPostings` with a zero total and `updatePostingAmount(…, "0.00" + "12.50")` produce the values above.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts:143, 158` (the auto-leg seed) and `:198-210` (`createPrefilledPostings`). The comment at `:155-157` says "never typed into".

## Fix

When the total is zero or null, seed `amountInput: ""` with amount null. Show `""` in the auto leg while its amount is zero, so the placeholder appears.

**Acceptance:** a zero-total receipt prefill shows empty fields, and typing 12.50 into the auto leg in any cursor position gives 12.50.
