# A cancelled subscription stays "Overdue" at the top of Merchants' Recurring for up to about three years

**Severity:** moderate. Stopped bills sit in red above live ones, hiding the real missed bills the section is meant to surface. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `isOverdue` is `today > nextExpected + grace` with no upper bound, nothing marks a series as ended, and `compareRecurring` sorts overdue first. A payee stays detected until fewer than three of its payments remain in the 37-month window.

Code-level at `main` `de881e646`. A reviewer ran `buildMerchantSections` under Node with today 2026-10-21, on "Hulu" paid monthly from 2023-11-08 to 2024-03-08 (then cancelled) and "Netflix" paid monthly through 2026-10-15:
- Recurring lists `Hulu, monthly, next 2024-04-08, OVERDUE` above `Netflix, next 2026-11-15`, and Hulu still reads Overdue on 2026-12-07.
- A switch from monthly to one annual payment (12 monthly payments in 2025, then 2026-01-10) gives `monthly, next 2026-02-10, isOverdue: true`.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:340-341`.
- `selectors/payee-series.ts:25` (37-month window).
- `selectors/merchant-sections.ts:107-112` (`compareRecurring`).

## Fix

Add a "lapsed" state once today passes `nextExpected + k × gap` (for example, 3 periods). Show "Last paid Mar 8, 2024" and keep it out of the pinned top, or sort it last with its own label.

**Acceptance:** Hulu reads "Last paid …" and sorts below Netflix, and a real one-cycle miss still reads Overdue. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w21/167 (a future payment hides Overdue) and w21/233 (today frozen at mount) are different. Searched `.pm/**` for overdue, cancelled, lapsed, nextExpected and compareRecurring.
