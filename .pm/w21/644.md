# Changing your username silently clears the Transactions filters (a gap in w21/226's rename handling)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `ab4fddb8d`, proven under `tsx`): `common/vars/rename-ledger-owner.ts:12-20` (called from `profile/profile-screen.tsx:289`) moves the selected ledger, recent accounts and recurring marks to the new owner, but not the Transactions filters. The filters are stored with the ledger id they were applied to (`transactions-screen/filters/var.ts:25`), and `select-filter-query.ts:19-23` drops them when the id differs. After `alice` becomes `alice2`, the `Expenses:Food` filter on `alice/home` no longer applies to `alice2/home`. The full journal shows, and the chip is gone with no message.

Not run natively (no username changes on the QA account).

## Fix

Re-key the stored filters' ledger id in `rename-ledger-owner`, as for the other scoped state.

**Acceptance:** after a rename, the filter still applies. A unit case covers it.

## Dedupe

Gap in w21/226 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for rename-ledger-owner together with filters.
