# Money flow drops a lot's date and label, so sales from different lots look identical ("−5 VTI · cost $100.00 · price $120.00")

**Severity:** low (the lot date decides short- or long-term gain). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `select-transaction-detail.ts:236` passes `formatPerUnit(p.cost)` (number and currency only) and drops `cost.date` and `cost.label`.

Native (iPhone 17e at `dbe9b0144`, disposable ledger, restored): a sale `-5 VTI {100.00 USD, 2023-01-15} @ 120.00 USD` reads "−5 VTI · cost $100.00 · price $120.00", and VoiceOver reads the same. Under `tsx`, a sale from the "lot-b" lot (2025-06-01) reads identically.

## Fix

Append the lot date or label when present ("cost $100.00 · 2023-01-15" or "· lot-b"), as w21/059's Fix asked.

**Acceptance:** the two sales are distinguishable, on screen and to VoiceOver. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w21/059's resolution shipped without the date and label its Fix asked for. Searched `.pm/**` for formatPerUnit and "lot date".
