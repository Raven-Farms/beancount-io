# A failed star in Browse ledgers shows its error toast over whatever screen the user moved to

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the star toggle's rejection calls the global toast without checking the screen is still mounted, unlike `open()` in the same file. `DiscoveryStore.dispose()` aborts loads but not the star mutation.

Code-level at `main` `10857997b`: `mobile/src/screens/ledger-selection/index.tsx:342-343` (`store.toggle(item).catch(showError)`), `:162-163` (`showError`), `:139-146` (the mounted check `open()` uses), and `discovery-store.ts:146-165`. Scenario: tap a star on a slow connection and go back to Home. When the mutation fails, "Couldn't complete that action" appears over Home. Not driven natively, because the QA run doesn't star.

## Fix

Check `mounted.current` before toasting, or drop the rejection after `dispose()`.

**Acceptance:** a star failure after leaving shows no toast. A unit case covers it.
