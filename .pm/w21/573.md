# Home's Recent Transactions says "No transactions yet" with an Add CTA when every transaction is dated after today (w21/471 regression)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `home-screen/components/recent-transactions-card.tsx:66` filters to `0001-01-01 - <today>`, and `:104-117` treats an empty result as a brand-new ledger. With only pre-entered rent on Nov 1 and Dec 1 (today is Oct 22), the card reads "No transactions yet" with "Add transaction". Meanwhile the net-worth chart already reflects the rent, and the Transactions tab lists both rows (under their November and December date headers; that tab has no Upcoming marker, per w21/261). Code-level at `main` `966ff79aa` (proven under `tsx`). Not run natively: the disposable ledger has past entries.

## Fix

When the window is empty but the ledger has later entries, say "Nothing up to today · N upcoming" (with a link to Transactions) instead of the first-run copy.

**Acceptance:** a future-only ledger shows the upcoming message without the welcome CTA. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Regression of w21/471; see w21/261 and w21/513. Searched `.pm/**` for "No transactions yet" and recentTransactionsWindow.
