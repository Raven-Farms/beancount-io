# The holdings sheet labels a price the user typed "Live price" and holds it to the managed feed's 3-day (or 1-day) staleness rule

**Severity:** minor. A manual price entered to override a stale or broken feed is labelled as the feed's and re-flagged as stale the next day. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: mobile marks a holding "managed" whenever any managed source matches the commodity and currency, regardless of which price is actually used. The user's later or same-day price is still labelled "Live price" and judged by the feed's thresholds instead of the 7-day manual floor.

Code-level at `main` `6263a73fd`. The 1-day re-flag during a feed outage comes from reading the code.

## Scenario

`include "https://beancount.io/prices/BTC-USD"`; 0.01 BTC held; `2026-10-21 price BTC 1.00 USD`. The Accounts row reads "$0.01 at market", so the user's price is the one used, but Home's holdings sheet reads "Live price · Oct 21".

## Where

`mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:214, 297-309` and `mobile/src/components/valuation-sheet/index.tsx:157`.

## Fix

Decide "managed" from the source of the price actually used: compare the winning price's date and value, or have the ledger report the source. Apply the managed thresholds only then.

**Acceptance:** a user-entered price that wins is labelled "Price Oct 21" and uses the 7-day floor. A feed price that wins stays "Live price".
