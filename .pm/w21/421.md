# A journal row that moves two currencies (an FX conversion) shows only the operating-currency leg in account detail

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/100's `otherChanges` only applies when the shown-currency change is zero (`select-account-journal.ts:425-428`; day total `:345-348`); its resolution recorded the gap but no item tracked it. Under `tsx`, a Wise conversion `{USD:-110.00, EUR:100.00}` gives the row `-$110.00` and the day header `-$110.00`, with the +€100 nowhere. In units view, exchanging 10 VTI for 5 VOO shows `+5` and drops the −10 VTI leg.

Code-level at `main` `3e4352ec8`.

## Fix

Always show the other currencies' amounts next to the shown one (marked "Not in total"), not only when the shown amount is zero, and include them in `sumOtherChanges` for the day header.

**Acceptance:** the Wise conversion row reads "−$110.00 · +€100.00 not in total".

## Dedupe

w21/100 covers only the zero case; w21/057 is Transactions' headline. Searched `.pm/**` for "both currencies", otherChanges, "FX conversion" and multi-currency.
