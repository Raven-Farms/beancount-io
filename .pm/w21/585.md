# Receipt photos are never deleted from the device: every scan's full-resolution image stays in the app cache after upload, cancel and sign-out

**Severity:** minor (privacy). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `bb5156667`):
- `receipt-capture-screen/camera-view.tsx:130-140` (camera) writes each shot to the app's `Caches/Camera/`.
- `receipt-capture-screen.tsx:81-90` (library) re-encodes picks into `Caches/ImagePicker/`.
- The upload (`use-receipt-workflow.ts`, about `:97-160`) never removes the local file, and the sign-out cleanup (`common/server-url-actions.ts:31-63`) doesn't sweep those folders. Nothing under `src/` deletes files.

The images are full receipts (card digits, merchant, amounts, sometimes names). They survive sign-out and account switches until iOS clears the cache under storage pressure, while the code says the uploaded copy is temporary. On this simulator, no receipt has been scanned (the run doesn't spend parse quota), so `Library/Caches` has neither folder yet. The leak wasn't observed natively.

## Fix

Delete the shot once the upload settles (success, error or cancel) and on unmount. Sweep `Camera/` and `ImagePicker/` in the sign-out cleanup.

**Acceptance:** after a scan, and after sign-out, neither cache folder holds receipt images. A test covers the cleanup calls.
