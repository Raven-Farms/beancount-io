# The settings wheel picker can open on its first option again, so an untouched Confirm saves "Light"

**Severity:** major (regression of w3/101). **Estimate:** 30–60 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: the shared wheel `Picker` backs Theme, Language, Open Account's root and currency, and Add Budget's interval and currency. When it opens on row 0 instead of the saved value, an untouched **Confirm** silently writes row 0. In Theme that is "Light". In Language, `en` is first, so a non-English user who opens the picker and confirms is switched to English and the app restarts.

Environment: `main` at `ab6f2adf7`, dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e, **iOS 27.0** runtime, English, Theme = System. w3/101's closeout recheck passed on iOS 26.5.

## Repro (3 of 3 on this path)

1. Fresh app. Home → tap a Recent Transactions row → tap a Money flow posting (account detail) → Back → Back.
2. Drawer → **Settings** (Theme reads **System**) → **Theme**.
3. The wheel opens with **Light** centered and emphasized. Light/Dark/System sit from the selection band downward, the same layout as with Light saved.
4. Tap **Confirm** without touching the wheel: Settings now reads **Theme Light**. (Restored to System afterwards by tapping the System row, then Confirm.)

Controls: after a cold launch straight to Drawer → Settings → Theme, and on a second opening within the same Settings visit, the wheel correctly opens on **System**. The defect is intermittent and depends on the path, so a test that only mounts the picker once can pass.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/components/picker/index.tsx`:

- The wheel is positioned only by the `contentOffset={{ x: 0, y: initialScrollY }}` prop on the `AnimatedScrollView` (around line 395). iOS does not reliably apply `contentOffset` to a scroll view mounted inside an animating modal; it is an initial-only prop.
- `scrollY` is seeded from `initialScrollY` (line 210, re-seeded on open at 213-221). But when the native view stays at y = 0, the `useAnimatedScrollHandler` `onScroll` (lines 266-275) reports `contentOffset.y = 0` and overwrites the seed. `handleDone` (line 279) then saves `items[wheelIndexAtOffset(0)]`, which is row 0.

w3/101 fixed the case where no scroll event arrived (the seed covers it). It did not cover the native offset never being applied, where the wheel *displays* row 0 and reports it.

## Fix

Position the wheel imperatively, and don't depend on `contentOffset`. When the modal becomes visible, and again in `onLayout` / `onContentSizeChange` of the scroll view, call `scrollViewRef.current?.scrollTo({ y: initialScrollY, animated: false })`, as `selectIndex` already does for taps. Keep the `scrollY` seed. Optionally ignore scroll events until that initial positioning has landed, so a spurious y = 0 report can't replace the committed value.

**Acceptance:** on the repro path, Theme opens on System and an untouched Confirm keeps System. Language opened from a non-English locale opens on that locale, and an untouched Confirm triggers no restart. Open Account's root and currency open on their current values. A component test drives the picker through open → close → reopen with a non-first `selectedValue` and asserts both the `scrollTo` call and the confirmed item.

## Dedupe

w3/101 (done, `7538fb0d3`) fixed untouched Confirm via the `scrollY` seed; its native recheck ran on iOS 26.5. w3/162 (done) covers emphasis following the pending row. This regression is the display/offset half, observed on iOS 27.0, with a different failing mechanism from the one w3/101 fixed. No open item.

## Not verified

Whether iOS 26.x is affected, Android, and Language and Open Account directly. Their exposure follows from the shared component, not from on-device repro.
