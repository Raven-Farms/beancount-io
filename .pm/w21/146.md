# New Transaction: with 4 or more postings, the number pad covers "Add posting", the Remainder line and the save errors, and with 5 the last amount field

**Severity:** moderate. While typing amounts, the user can't see whether the entry balances or why it won't save, and the screen can't scroll to reveal it. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the form's ScrollView has no keyboard inset, and its content is shorter than the viewport, so it can't scroll. The decimal pad has no return key, so only a tap on blank space dismisses it.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `aa4a9793a`, on the disposable ledger, with nothing saved (I discarded the form). Home → + → Enter new transaction → Add posting (4 postings: Checking, Groceries, Cash, "Select account") → tap the first amount. The number pad's top edge sits just under the 4th posting row. **"Add posting" and "Remainder ✓ $0.00" are hidden.** With a 5th posting, the reviewer's geometry puts its amount field half under the pad.

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:680, 712-715` (the ScrollView, with `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"` but no inset).

## Fix

Add `automaticallyAdjustKeyboardInsets` (or a `KeyboardAvoidingView`), and scroll the focused amount and the Remainder line into view on focus. Optionally add a keyboard accessory "Done" for the decimal pad.

**Acceptance:** with 5 postings and the pad up, the focused amount, the Remainder line and any save error can be scrolled into view above the pad.
