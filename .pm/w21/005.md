# The account journal's first load is a bare spinner, not a skeleton

**Severity:** minor (polish). **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: opening an account is one of the most frequent drill-downs. Its chart card arrives as a skeleton and then real content, but the Transactions list below it is an empty area with a centered spinner. That is the one pattern `mobile/AGENTS.md` ("Loading states — skeleton preloaders, not spinners") rules out, and the layout jumps when the rows land.

Environment: `main` at `ab6f2adf7`, dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0), light, English. Repro on the QA account's ledger and on any public `open_ledger/*` ledger.

## Repro

1. Accounts → tap any account, e.g. `Liabilities:US:Chase:Slate`.
2. The balance card has already rendered, but under **Transactions** there is only a centered `ActivityIndicator` until the journal query resolves. Captured: the screenshot taken about 3.5 s after the tap still showed it.

Controls: Transactions (`TransactionsListSkeleton`) and Merchant detail (`MerchantDetailSkeleton`) render skeleton rows on first load and keep a spinner only for the load-more footer.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/account-detail-screen.tsx:464-468`: `ListEmptyComponent` returns `<ActivityIndicator>` while `isInitialLoading`. It has done so since the screen was introduced in `80bf63f4c` (2026-07-06), and nothing replaced it when the skeleton convention landed.

## Fix

While `isInitialLoading`, render a skeleton that mirrors the account-journal layout: a date-section header with its daily-total slot, then rows with icon, name, amount and the "Balance: …" caption. Build it from `LoadingTile` with varied widths and the loaded rows' fixed line heights, as `ListItemSkeleton` / `TransactionsListSkeleton` do, so nothing shifts when the data lands. Keep the footer spinner for paging, and keep pull-to-refresh showing current rows under the `RefreshControl`. Verify in light and dark.

**Acceptance:** opening an account shows skeleton rows (not a spinner) until the journal arrives, with no vertical jump when they're replaced. The load-more footer and the error and empty states are unchanged. A render test asserts the skeleton (not an `ActivityIndicator`) in `ListEmptyComponent` during the first load.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for ActivityIndicator, spinner and skeleton. w4/042 (Notifications skeleton gating) and w3/m1 (budget skeletons) are other screens. No item covers the account journal.
