# At AX2 the drawer clips its "Ledgers" header, "+ New" and every ledger name to the top half of the text

**Severity:** moderate (accessibility). At the size `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md` requires mobile to support, the ledger switcher's names can't be read. Only the top half of "example" and "mcp-agent-eval" is visible, and "Ledgers" and "+ New" are cut off the same way. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the drawer's text styles pin a point `lineHeight` (18–24). Dynamic Type scales the `fontSize` (roughly ×1.8 at AX2) but not a fixed `lineHeight`, so each line box is shorter than its glyphs. In rows with `numberOfLines={1}`, the overflow is cut.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `67a640a38`, with `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large` (AX2), in light mode. Opening the drawer from Files:
- "Ledgers" and "+ New" show only their top halves under the "Beancount.io" brand row.
- The PUNCSKY owner header is intact.
- The "example" and selected "mcp-agent-eval" rows show the top half of each name. The selected row's green outline cuts through the text.
- "Browse ledgers", "Merchants" and the profile row wrap and render fully.

For comparison, Home, Accounts, Transactions, Reports, Files, transaction detail and New Transaction all render readable text at AX2.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/components/ledger-drawer/ledger-drawer.tsx` styles with a fixed `lineHeight`:
- `sectionLabel` (`:154`, 20)
- `newButtonText` (`:170`, 20)
- `ownerHeaderText` (`:217`, 18)
- `listItemName` (`:231`, 24), rendered with `numberOfLines={1}` at `:846`
- `caption` (`:239`), `stateTitle` (`:263`), `stateText` (`:270`) and `menuItemText` (`:309`)

## Fix

Drop the fixed `lineHeight` from these styles, or scale it with `PixelRatio.getFontScale()` the way the rest of the app's typography does. Let ledger rows grow (keep `minHeight`, no fixed height), and allow the name two lines, or a `maxFontSizeMultiplier` like the brand row's 1.3.

**Acceptance:** at AX2 and AX3, "Ledgers", "+ New" and every ledger name render in full inside the drawer, with the selected row's outline around the whole name. The default size is visually unchanged.
