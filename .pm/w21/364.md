# Home's budget panel shows weekly and daily budgets as $0 spent, because it reads the month's last (future) interval

**Severity:** moderate-to-high. Every weekly or daily budget reads "0% used, Below target" on Home on almost every day of the month, even when it is overspent. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since w21/184, `budgetPanelWindow` (`budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:121`) runs to the end of the month. `use-budget-panel.ts:101, 126` then treats the *last* interval returned as the one in progress. The ledger returns one row per interval, including empty future ones (`interval-series.ts:340`). For a weekly budget that is the month's last week, and for a daily one the month's last day.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `c8be51ed8`):** I set up a disposable fixture: `Expenses:QaWeekly`, a weekly budget of 50 USD, and 80 USD spent on 2026-10-20. Home's row (`budget-row-Expenses:QaWeekly-USD`) read **"$0.00 of $21.43, 0% used, Below target"**. The Budget page for the same account showed **Actual $80.00, Variance +$58.57, "Above target"**. The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`. The reviewer's `tsx` run (`TZ=America/Los_Angeles`, today 2026-10-21) shows the panel reading `2026-10-26 → 2026-10-31` instead of `2026-10-19 → 2026-10-25`. Pacific/Auckland on 2026-11-02 also gives 0.

## Fix

Keep the month-long `time` as the cache key. For each group, pick the item whose date falls in `currentPeriodRange(group.interval, today)`, not the last item. Add unit cases for weekly and daily budgets in mid-month and across a year boundary.

**Acceptance:** with the fixture, Home shows $80.00 spent and "Above target", matching the Budget page.

## Dedupe

This is a regression from w21/184 (done). w21/337 is the monthly pre-entered-entries symptom, and w21/102 and w21/233 differ. Searched `.pm/**` for budgetPanelWindow, "budget panel" with weekly, daily and week, "last interval" and "in progress".
