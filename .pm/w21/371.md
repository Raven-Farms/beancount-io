# Reports' "Other" row nets refunds against real spending, so it can read "$10.00 · 0%" while hiding $500 of travel

**Severity:** moderate. Real spending hides behind a near-zero total. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `topNWithOther` (`select-breakdown-rows.ts:31-32`) adds up the *signed* values of the folded rows, as used by the breakdown cards at `reports-screen.tsx:236, 257`. Under `tsx`, with eight positive categories plus Travel +500 and Electronics −490 folded, the cards show **"Other 10.00 · 0%"**, with Travel 500 and Electronics −490 visible only after tapping Other. The Cash flow chart on the same data keeps refunds as their own flow (the w21/112 convention), so the two cards disagree. If the folded rows net to zero, Other reads $0.00.

Code-level at `main` `d5c763aa2`; the reviewer ran the real selectors. Not run natively.

## Fix

Fold only positive rows into Other. Keep net-negative rows visible, or group them in a separate "Refunds" row, as the Sankey does.

**Acceptance:** in that scenario, Other shows at least $500.00 (Travel), and the −$490 return is visible on its own.
