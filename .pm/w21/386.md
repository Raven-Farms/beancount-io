# The Arabic thousands separator (٬) is read as a decimal comma, so "۱٬۲۳۴" (1,234) is saved as 1.234

**Severity:** major (a wrong amount is written with no warning). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/230's `toAsciiNumerals` (`multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts:72`) maps U+066C ARABIC THOUSANDS SEPARATOR to an ASCII `,`. `parseExactAmount` (about `:98`) then treats a single `,` as the decimal comma (the w21/031 rule). Under `tsx`: "۱٬۲۳۴" → `1.234`, "١٬٢٣٤" → `1.234`, "١٢٬٥٠" → `12.50`, "۱٬۲۳۴٬۵۶۷" → `1234567`. `Intl.NumberFormat("fa").format(1234)` gives "۱٬۲۳۴", so the standard Persian way of writing 1,234 is saved 1000× too small.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `62533363c`):** in New Transaction I pasted `۱٬۲۳۴` into the first posting's amount. The auto leg reads **−1.234**, and Done is enabled. Nothing was saved.

## Fix

Treat U+066C only as a thousands separator, never a decimal mark. Add tests: "۱٬۲۳۴" → 1234, "١٢٬٥٠" refused.

**Acceptance:** pasting `۱٬۲۳۴` gives an auto leg of −1,234.00.
