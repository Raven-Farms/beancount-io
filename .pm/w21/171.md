# After a subscription price rise, the Recurring row's typical amount keeps showing the old price, with no "~"

**Severity:** minor. A user who checks what Spotify now costs sees last year's price. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the typical amount is the median over the whole 37-month window, so it stays at the old price until more than half of that history is at the new one. A rise under 10% also skips the "~" uncertainty marker.

Code-level at `main` `698f8aba5`. The reviewer ran `detectRecurrence` on the fixture: "Spotify", `Expenses:Entertainment:Streaming` on the 5th, 10.99 USD for Jan–Jun and 11.99 USD for Jul–Oct 2026. The row reads "Monthly · **$10.99** · Next Nov 5" with no tilde, after four consecutive $11.99 charges. With 15.49 → 17.99 it reads "~$15.49".

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:315-319` (median) and `:198` (the 10% spread rule).

## Fix

Use the most recent charge, or the median of the last 3, as the typical amount. Show "~" whenever the recent amount differs from the long-run one.

**Acceptance:** the fixture shows $11.99. A steady price is unchanged. Add a unit test.
