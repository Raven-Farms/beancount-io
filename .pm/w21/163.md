# Account detail shows "−$0.00" (or "+$0.00") on a day whose entries net to zero

**Severity:** minor. A day that nets to zero reads as a signed non-zero total, which contradicts the rows beneath it. This is the account-detail sibling of w21/055, which fixed the same float residue in Reports. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile.
