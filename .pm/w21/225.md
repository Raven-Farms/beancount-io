# Returning to the app refreshes Home's charts and Recent Transactions but not its budget panel, or the drawer's ledger list

**Severity:** moderate. After spending recorded elsewhere (web, CLI, an agent), Home shows the new entry and the new net worth, while the budget row beside them keeps the old amount. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/024's foreground refresh runs `client.refetchQueries({ include: "active" })`, which only reaches *watched* queries. The budget panel's actuals are one-off `client.query` reads, re-run only on pull to refresh, `ledgerRevisionVar` (bumped by in-app writes) or a change to the budget list. The drawer's ledger directory revalidates only on mount.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `7e42f850e`, on the disposable QA ledger (restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`). The fixture `; qa-20261020-budq` was a quarterly 900 USD budget on `Expenses:QaBudQtr` with 250 spent.
1. Home: the panel row reads "$250.00 of $205.43, 122% used, Above target"; Net Worth reads $16,858.51.
2. Background the app (open Settings). Through the API, add `2026-10-12 * "QA FG SPEND"` for 100.00 USD to the same account.
3. More than 30 s after the last refresh, return to the app. Net Worth reads **$16,758.51**, and Recent Transactions lists **QA FG SPEND −$100.00**. The budget panel still reads **"$250.00 of $205.43"**.

A reviewer reproduced the same split in Node with a real `ApolloClient`, the app's default options and `createForegroundRefresher`. After foregrounding, the watched journal moved from 50 to 250, while the panel read stayed at 50 and ran only once. The drawer half (a ledger created on the web doesn't appear until pull to refresh or relaunch) is code-read only.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/common/hooks/use-foreground-refresh.ts:18` refetches active watched queries only.
- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/hooks/use-budget-panel.ts:64` onward: the actuals are `client.query` reads keyed on `refreshSignal`, `ledgerRevisionVar` and the budget list.
- `mobile/src/common/ledger-directory/ledger-directory-provider.tsx:22`: `revalidate()` runs on mount only.

## Fix

In the foreground refresher, also bump `ledgerRevisionVar` (or a dedicated foreground signal the panel listens to) and call the directory's `revalidate()`. The hook is mounted in `app/(app)/_layout.tsx` outside `LedgerDirectoryProvider`, so move it inside, or let the provider subscribe to `AppState` itself. Keep the 30 s throttle.

**Acceptance:**
- In the steps above, the panel reads "$350.00 of $205.43" after the return.
- A ledger created elsewhere appears in the drawer after a return.
- A unit test drives the refresher and checks the panel's signal changes.
