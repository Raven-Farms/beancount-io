# A guest who opens an example entry or account link (one the app's own Share or Copy link made) is sent back to Welcome, and the link is silently replayed after sign-in

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (proven under `tsx`): `common/app-links/guest-link-visit.ts:15-21, 36` maps only plain tab targets, so `…/entry/<hash>` and `…/account/<name>` give `guestLinkVisit = null`. The link rewrites to the signed-in `/transaction-detail?…` route, whose layout (`app/(app)/_layout.tsx:22-23`) redirects to `/auth/welcome`, and the example visit is lost. `pending-app-link.ts:69-71` and `oauth-sign-in-finalizer.ts:37-58` keep the link and open it after a later sign-in, in place of what the user was viewing. The guest has `/examples/transaction-detail` and `/examples/account-detail` routes, and the link came from the guest's own Copy link (`transaction-detail-screen.tsx:519-540`). Code-level at `main` `9d7337ab6`; not run natively (it needs signing out, or a deep link, which can't be accepted on this simulator).

## Fix

Map entry and account links on example ledgers to the guest routes (finishing what w21/345 and w21/490 started), or don't offer Share or Copy to guests.

**Acceptance:** signed out, a copied example entry link opens that entry in the example. A unit case maps entry and account links.
