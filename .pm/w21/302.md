# A negative amount on an Expenses budget is stored as typed, and the card treats it as an income target: always red, always "0% used"

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `buildBudgetEntry` normalises the sign only for accounts under `name_income`, and `isBudgetAmount` accepts a leading `-` for any account. `budgetDirection` then reads the negative value as an income target.

Code-level at `main` `10857997b`. Run under Node: "-500" on `Expenses:Food` builds `{ number: "-500" }`. With 100 spent and with 900 spent alike, the card gives `status: below`, unfavourable, progress 0.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/add-budget-screen/build-budget-entry.ts:36-38` (`isBudgetAmount`) and `:76-78` (income-only normalisation), and `budget-selectors.ts:444-446` (`budgetDirection`).

## Fix

Mirror `asIncomeTarget`: store the absolute value for non-income accounts, or show an inline error for a negative amount there.

**Acceptance:** "-500" on an Expenses account saves 500, or is refused with a message. Unit cases are added.
