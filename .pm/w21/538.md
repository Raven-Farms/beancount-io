# Scan Receipt's "Receipt read" screen shows the date as raw ISO ("2026-03-01") and the total with no grouping or currency ("1234.50"), in every language

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `680e4879f`, proven under `tsx`): `receipt-capture-screen/reveal-view.tsx:101` shows `phase.date` and `receiptAmountText(...)` as passed from `receipt-capture-screen.tsx:238-241`, and the spoken summary (`preview-view.tsx:128-129`) uses the same values. VoiceOver reads the ISO date as digits, which w21/366 fixed for budget history. In Deutsch, the list helpers would give "1. März 2026" and "$1,234.50".

Not run natively: it needs a receipt parse, and this QA run doesn't spend AI parse quota.

## Fix

Format the date with the list date helper and the total with the money formatter (with the parsed currency).

**Acceptance:** in Deutsch, the reveal reads "1. März 2026" and "1.234,50 $" (or the app's chosen money form), and VoiceOver reads them as words. A unit case is added.

Sibling of w21/366 (dates read as digits). Searched `.pm/**` for reveal-view, receiptAmountText and "Receipt read".
