# New Transaction writes every posting in the ledger's first operating currency, with no way to choose another

**Severity:** major for anyone with more than one currency. A EUR card, a JPY cash account or a brokerage account can't be used from the phone: the save is refused, or USD is silently written into the foreign-currency account. A currency exchange can't be entered at all. **Estimate:** 2–3 hours. Owner: mobile. A3: the form takes `currencies[0]` once and stamps it on every posting. It has no currency control and no price (`@` / `@@`) field.

Native observation on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `7e42f850e`: posting rows show only an amount ("0.00", "-12.50"), with no currency shown or editable anywhere in the form. The payload was proven in Node by running `buildEntryInput` on a USD-operating ledger with `2026-01-01 open Assets:Bank:EUR EUR`. Picking that account and typing −12.50 gives `[{"account":"Assets:Bank:EUR","amount":"-12.50 USD"},{"account":"Expenses:Food","amount":"12.50 USD"}]`. The engine refuses a currency outside the account's `open` constraint (code-read).

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:469`: `const currency = currencies.length > 0 ? currencies[0] : "USD"`.
- `:619-623` pass it to `buildEntryInput`.
- `postings-utils.ts:350-361` append `opts.currency` to every posting.

## Fix

Give each posting a currency. Default it to the account's `open` constraint when it has exactly one, else the operating currency, and show it on the row with a picker. Balance each currency separately in the remainder. When exactly two currencies are present, offer a price on one leg (`@` per unit or `@@` total) so an exchange balances. Keep the single-currency flow unchanged.

**Acceptance:**
- Picking `Assets:Bank:EUR` shows EUR on that row, and the payload carries EUR.
- A EUR→USD exchange with `@` saves and balances.
- A unit test covers `buildEntryInput` with mixed currencies.
