# A transfer payee loses its whole recurring series when any one entry has an Expenses leg, such as an ATM fee (w21/173 follow-up)

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. ATM withdrawals, which 173 names, often carry a fee leg. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mapPayeeSeries` decides once per payee whether to read money legs or debited transfer legs. A single fee leg anywhere in the 37-month window makes it read only that leg.

Code-level at `main` `61ad1916c`. Under Node, "QA SWEEP" with 12 weekly 100 USD transfers plus one 2.50 `Expenses:Bank:Fees` leg gives the series `[{2026-07-03, 2.5}]` only. Detection is null, and a manual mark reads "Not enough history", which is 173's exact symptom. Combined with 169's spelling merge, a fee on a differently-cased spelling turns the amount into "~$100".

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/payee-series.ts:152, 164`.

## Fix

Choose per transaction or date: use money legs when that transaction has them, otherwise its debited legs. Or use whichever series has more distinct dates.

**Acceptance:** the fixture is detected as Weekly at $100. A unit case is added.
