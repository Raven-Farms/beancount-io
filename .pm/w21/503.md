# w21/454's "announced" sign-in failure is still silent on iOS: it relies on accessibilityRole="alert", which React Native maps to no trait

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/screens/welcome/index.tsx:193` now gives the failure text `accessibilityRole="alert"`, like the session-ended notice at `:198`. React Native maps `"alert"` to `UIAccessibilityTraitNone` on iOS (`node_modules/react-native/React/Views/RCTViewManager.m:31`), and the file has neither `InlineError` nor `announceForAccessibility`, so VoiceOver on iOS is still told nothing when Sign In fails. w21/354 introduced `InlineError` for exactly this, and w21/484 lists the same pattern elsewhere.

Code-level at `main` `0cca7cf29`; not exercised with VoiceOver. The server-change half of w21/454 isn't affected.

## Fix

Render the failure text (and the session-ended notice) with `InlineError`, and add Welcome to w21/354's announce guard.

**Acceptance:** a failed or timed-out sign-in is announced on iOS, and the guard covers Welcome.

Follow-up to w21/454 (done); sibling of w21/484 and w21/479. Searched `.pm/**` for accessibilityRole alert together with iOS, Welcome together with VoiceOver, and InlineError.
