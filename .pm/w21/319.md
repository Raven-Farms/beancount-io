# Narration suggestions get the keyboard inset twice on iOS after w21/145 (a blank band below the last suggestion)

**Severity:** minor (layout). Confidence is medium: it's proved from React Native's source, not on a device. It's the same class as w21/247, a manual adjustment on top of one iOS already makes. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/145 added `automaticallyAdjustKeyboardInsets` to the shared suggestions list. That list also renders inside the multiline branch's `KeyboardAvoidingView behavior="padding"` (Narration). The ScrollView insets its content by the keyboard height while the avoiding view also shrinks it, leaving about 300 pt of blank scroll space.

Code-level at `main` `3e830700b`: `mobile/src/components/text-input-screen/index.tsx:203` (the prop) and `:280-304` (the multiline `KeyboardAvoidingView`), with `RCTScrollViewComponentView.mm:205-214` and `KeyboardAvoidingView.js:113,161`. Not seen natively, because the simulator's hardware keyboard suppresses the software one.

## Fix

`automaticallyAdjustKeyboardInsets={Platform.OS === "ios" && !multiline}`, and extend `keyboard-inset-lists.test.ts`.

**Acceptance:** with the software keyboard up in Narration, the last suggestion sits just above the keyboard with no extra blank band.
