# One weekend-shifted payment anywhere in 37 months turns off the day-of-month anchor, so Next drifts by a day

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/007 anchored monthly bills to their day of the month, but `monthDayAnchor` requires every event in the window on that day, and takes the latest day seen. A single shifted posting falls back to `last + round(median gap)`.

Code-level at `main` `de881e646`. Run under Node:
- A bill due on the 1st (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026) predicts 11-01. With March paid on the 2nd, it predicts **10-31**.
- A bill due on the 25th predicts 10-25. With December paid on the 26th, it predicts **10-26**.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:147-161`, with the fallback at `:337-339`.

## Fix

Pick the anchor as the most common day of the month, allow ±3 days of tolerance, and accept month steps of at least 1.

**Acceptance:** both shifted cases predict 11-01 and 11-25. Unit cases are added.

w21/007 (done) is the parent; its notes don't cover a single shifted payment. Searched `.pm/**` for monthDayAnchor, weekend and "business day".
