# Regression from w21/290: budgets in zero-decimal currencies now show two decimals ("¥1,016.13", "¥1,200.00")

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate (every JPY, KRW or VND budget on the Budget card, Home's panel and VoiceOver). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budgetScale` floors at 2 (`budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:339-348`). It is passed as `minScale`, and `formatMoneyWithCurrency` takes `Math.max(currencyMinorUnits(currency), minScale)` (`common/number-utils.ts:254-262`), so 2 overrides JPY's 0. The callers are `budget-group-card.tsx:220-222, 318-322`, `home-screen/components/budget-card.tsx:145` and `select-budget-panel.ts:215-222`.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `669120634`):** a disposable monthly 1500 JPY budget on `Expenses:QaRamen` with 1200 JPY spent shows **"Budget ¥1,016.13 · Actual ¥1,200.00 · Variance +¥183.87"** on the Budget card, and Home's row reads "¥1,200.00 of ¥1,016.13". The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`.

## Fix

Keep the 2-decimal floor only for proration and the variance tolerance. For display, pass the recorded scale without a floor, so it only raises precision above the currency's minor units.

**Acceptance:** the JPY budget reads "¥1,016 · ¥1,200 · +¥184".
