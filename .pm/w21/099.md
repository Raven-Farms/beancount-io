# The Accounts tab lists closed, emptied accounts as live "$0.00" rows

**Severity:** moderate. Closed accounts clutter the tree indefinitely, change how parents fold, and open like live accounts. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the tree keeps every account named in `getLedger.attributes.accounts`, even at zero, so a newly opened account appears (w21/056). That list comes from every `open` directive, including closed accounts, and nothing filters them out. Mobile already has close dates (`use-account-close-dates.ts`, w21/045), and the dashboard hides closed zero-balance accounts by default (`show_closed_accounts`, w4/m2/t004).

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `3504fd3cf`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-cl` opened `Assets:Bank:Qa20261020Old`, seeded it with 100.00 USD, moved the 100.00 back out, and added `2026-08-04 close Assets:Bank:Qa20261020Old`.

Accounts → Assets → Bank lists **"Qa20261020Old $0.00"** under Savings and Checking, styled like the live rows and tappable into account detail.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/accounts-screen/accounts-screen.tsx:105` → `components/account-list/select-trial-balance.ts:108-111` → `components/account-list/select-account-list.ts:385-413` grafts and keeps every listed account. The keep check at `:133` and `:276` overrides the zero-balance filter.
- `common/hooks/use-ledger-read-context.ts:34` passes `attributes.accounts`, which the ledger builds from all opens (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/directives-report.ts:102-104`).

## Fix

Exclude from the keep set any account whose close date is on or before today and whose balance is zero, using `useAccountCloseDates`. Keep closed accounts that still have a non-zero balance; that is a real ledger problem worth showing. Optionally add a "Show closed accounts" toggle, matching the dashboard.

**Acceptance:**
- With the fixture, Bank shows only Savings and Checking.
- A closed account with a leftover balance still shows.
- A newly opened zero-balance account still shows (w21/056).
- Add a selector unit test.
