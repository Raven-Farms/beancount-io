# An income target's stored negative sign shows in budget history rows, the Delete alert and the Update placeholder ("-4250.50 USD"), unformatted

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the card shows the absolute, formatted value, but `budget-history-rows.tsx:125, 177, 187` print the raw `row.amount`, and `add-budget-screen/amount-placeholder.ts:14-15` uses it as the placeholder. Under `tsx`: typed 4250.50 is stored as −4250.50, the card stat reads 4,250.50, and the history row, Delete alert and Update placeholder all show `-4250.50 USD` / `-4250.50`, ungrouped in every locale. The placeholder suggests typing a negative number where the form asks for a positive one.

Code-level at `main` `f628d4727`.

## Fix

Use `formatMoneyWithCurrency(Math.abs(value), currency, locale)` in the rows and the alert, and the absolute value in `budgetAmountPlaceholder`.

**Acceptance:** an income budget of −4250.50 reads "$4,250.50" in its history row, the alert and the placeholder.
