# The Reports and Budget chart descriptions read by VoiceOver ignore plural forms ("over 1 months", "за 2 периодов")

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: this is the same kind of bug as w21/077, but in different keys. i18n-js applies plural rules only when a key's value is a plural object. These two keys are plain strings with `{{count}}`, so w4/m28's plural support never applies.

Code-level at `main` `89ddcfe9e`.

## Where

- The callers are `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:160-161` and `budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:166-168`.
- `translations/en.ts:369-370` reads "Income and expenses over {{count}} months…", so 1M gives "over 1 months".
- `en.ts:662-663` reads "…across {{count}} periods…".
- `ru.ts:640` gives "за 2 периодов"; `uk.ts:640` is similar.
- `sk.ts:626` gives "za 1 mesiacov" and `sk.ts:646` is similar; `bg.ts:611, 631` are similar.

## Fix

Convert both keys in all 13 locales to plural objects (`one`/`few`/`many`/`other`, as each language needs), following the w21/077 pattern.

**Acceptance:** unit tests render count 1, 2 and 5 for en, ru and sk with grammatical output. VoiceOver on Reports 1M reads "over 1 month".

## Dedupe

w21/077 covers commit stats; w4/m28 is the plural infrastructure. Searched `.pm/**` for incomeExpenseChartSummary, budgetChartSummary, "1 months" and periods.
