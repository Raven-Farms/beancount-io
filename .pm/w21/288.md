# Changing username and name together can say "Failed to save" after the username (and every ledger id) already changed

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: Profile writes the username first, then the names. If `updateProfile` fails, it shows only "Failed to save", though the username, and with it the ledger owner, has already been renamed on the server.

Code-level at `main` `567bd1801`: `mobile/src/screens/profile/profile-screen.tsx:267-288`. Scenario: edit `alice` → `alice2` plus the first name, and lose the network after the first request. The footer says "Failed to save", yet the account is `alice2` and its ledgers are renamed.

## Fix

When the username succeeded and the name failed, say so ("Username saved; name couldn't be saved"), and keep the name field dirty for retry.

**Acceptance:** a harness test with `updateProfile` failing after a successful `updateUsername` shows the partial message.
