# Regression from w21/271: a short every-four-weeks series that spans February gets a day-of-month anchor, so Next is about 5 days late

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since `41253a6e3`, `monthDayAnchor` (`merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:199-207`) needs only two events and half the series on the most common day, with ±3-day tolerance including neighbouring months (`:223-230`), and the calendar step wins whenever an anchor exists (`:515-519`). February's 28 days put two four-weekly payments on the same day: 02-02 and 03-02, while 03-30 counts as April's payment due on the 2nd. Under `tsx`, the series 02-02, 03-02, 03-30 (and 01-05, 02-02, 03-02, 03-30) now gives Next `2026-05-02`; the parent commit gave `2026-04-27`, the correct 28-day step. This breaks w21/007's rule that four-weekly bills keep the day-count step, and Overdue fires about 5 days late.

Code-level at `main` `c9c36d8e7`.

## Fix

Reject the anchor when the gaps look four-weekly (`round(medianGap) === 28`, tight spread), or require no two consecutive events 28 days apart that share a nominal month step. Add the February-spanning case to the tests.

**Acceptance:** that series predicts 2026-04-27.
