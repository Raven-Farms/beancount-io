# Profile says "Photo from Gravatar" under the initials placeholder for users who have no Gravatar

**Severity:** minor (wrong copy). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the caption is chosen from the URL alone (`isGravatarUrl`). The backend returns a Gravatar URL for everyone without an uploaded picture, and the avatar requests it with `d=404` and falls back to initials, so users without a Gravatar see their initials captioned "Photo from Gravatar".

Code-level at `main` `567bd1801`: `mobile/src/screens/profile/profile-screen.tsx:355`, `user-display.ts:44`, `components/user-avatar/index.tsx:46`, and `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/auth/data/user-model/postgres-impl.ts:18`.

## Fix

Have `UserAvatar` report a failed image load, and in that case hide the caption or change it to "Add a photo on Gravatar".

**Acceptance:** with a 404 Gravatar, the caption isn't shown (or reads the add-photo copy). A component test covers it.
