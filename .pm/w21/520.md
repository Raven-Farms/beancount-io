# New Transaction writes USD into a ledger without `operating_currency` (w21/415 and w21/395 missed this form)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `dc8e781e1`, proven under `tsx`): `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:478` has `const currency = currencies.length > 0 ? currencies[0] : "USD"`, where `currencies` is `options.operating_currency` only (`common/ledger-meta-utils.ts:64`). w21/415 added `ledgerPrimaryCurrency` (`common/currency-util.ts:29`) for Home, Accounts and Reports, and w21/395 fixed the same fallback in Add Budget. With meta `operating_currency: []`, a typed −12.50 gives `Assets:Girokonto -12.50 USD` and `Expenses:Lebensmittel 12.50 USD`.

Scenario: in an EUR ledger with no operating-currency option, New Transaction's remainder reads "$", and Done writes USD. Home then puts the entry under "Not in total", and on accounts opened with an `EUR` constraint the save is refused. Native check: removing the option from the disposable ledger (restored) still showed "$", but that ledger's data is all USD, so it doesn't tell the fallback apart from the right answer.

## Fix

Use `ledgerPrimaryCurrency` for the form's default currency.

**Acceptance:** with no operating currency and EUR-only entries, the remainder shows € and saved postings are in EUR. A unit case is added.

Missed by w21/415 and w21/395 (done). Distinct from blocked/228, which is about choosing a second currency, not the fallback. Searched `.pm/**` for "operating_currency" and `: "USD"`.
