# Money flow prints per-unit price and cost to 28 digits for `@@` total prices and `{{…}}` total costs ("@ $3.3333333333333333333333333333")

**Severity:** moderate. The basis line is unreadable and squeezes the account name to an ellipsis ("Ex…Fx"). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile, plus an optional ledger fix. A3: the ledger divides a total into a per-unit figure at full Decimal precision (`price: "3.3333333333333333333333333333"`, `per_unit: "33.333333333333333333333333333"`), and 13eed3d58's basis line keeps the scale it is given, with no digit cap.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `562a1b8ef`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-td` was `2026-08-30 * "QA ATPRICE"`, with `Expenses:Qa20261020Fx 3.00 EUR @@ 10.00 USD` / `Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa -10.00 USD`. Transaction detail → Money flow: TO **"Ex…Fx" · +3.00 EUR · "@ $3.3333333333333333333333333333"**.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-transaction-detail.ts:113-118` (`formatPerUnit`) and `:134-139`
- `components/posting-row.tsx:79-82, 121` (a non-shrinking amount column with no `numberOfLines`)
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/journal-serialize.ts:139-157`

## Fix

Round a derived per-unit figure for display to the currency's display precision plus a few digits (for example at most 4–6 significant decimals), and mark it as approximate ("≈ $3.3333"). Better still, show the total as written ("@@ $10.00", "{{$100.00}}"). Let the basis line wrap or truncate, not squeeze the account.

**Acceptance:**
- The fixture shows "@@ $10.00" or "@ ≈$3.3333", with the account name readable.
- Add a selector unit test for `@@` and `{{}}`.
