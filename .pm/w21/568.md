# A transaction whose payee is only spaces shows as a blank row in Transactions, Home and Reports, and its detail title is blank

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transactions-screen/entry-row/index.tsx:146` uses `entry.payee || entry.narration || untitledLabel`, so a payee of `" "` wins. Then `entry-row-secondary.ts:11-13` trims it to empty and drops the narration from the second line too. The detail title has the same check (`select-transaction-detail.ts:268`). Account detail trims first (`select-account-journal.ts:282`), so the same entry reads correctly there. rustledger keeps a `" "` payee as written, which importers produce from blank, padded columns.

Native (iPhone 17e at `d7c0a5ea9`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): `2026-10-15 * " " "qa-20261020 Coffee"`. The Transactions row shows only the category icon and "−$4.50", with no text at all.

## Fix

Trim the payee before choosing the title, everywhere it's chosen (the row, the detail, Home Recent, Reports), as account detail does.

**Acceptance:** that row reads "qa-20261020 Coffee  −$4.50", and the detail title matches. Unit cases are added.
