# The first bar of a 1Y (or weekly YTD/LY) budget chart is held to a whole period's budget though its actuals cover only part of the period

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. The first bar's color and budget line are wrong, and the VoiceOver chart summary's total budget is too high; the header is unaffected. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the server starts its interval series exactly at the window's first day, so 1Y's first monthly period is 2025-10-21..10-31. The mobile budget for that bar is computed from the period's calendar start (1 October) and prorates a full month.

Code-level at `main` `5edfef868`. Not exercised natively: the QA ledger's data starts in 2026, so a 1Y window has no spending in its partial first month.

## Scenario

On 2026-10-21, a monthly budget of 310 USD on Expenses:Food with 150 USD spent on 2025-10-25. Budget → **1Y** shows the first "OCT" bar with actual 150 against a budget of 310, in green. It should be 110 (310 × 11/31), in red.

The same thing happens to weekly budgets on YTD and LY: 2026-01-01 is a Thursday, so the first week is 1–4 January but is held to a full Monday-to-Sunday week. Quarterly and yearly budgets on 1Y are affected too.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:352` `budgetForInterval` uses `intervalStartISO(intervalEnd, chartInterval)` as the start. The end is already clamped through `asOf`; the start is not clamped to the window.
- It is called from `hooks/use-budget-actuals.ts:74-79`.
- The server side is `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts` (`seriesRanges` → `intervalRanges(..., complete=false)`).

## Fix

Pass the window start into `budgetForInterval` / `calculateBudgetForInterval` and clamp the period start to `max(intervalStart, windowStart)`, mirroring the `asOf` end clamp. The existing proration then gives 11/31 of the month.

**Acceptance:**
- Unit cases: 1Y monthly gives 110 for the scenario.
- A YTD weekly budget of 70 with a Thursday 1 January gives a first-bar budget of 40.
- Full periods are unchanged.
