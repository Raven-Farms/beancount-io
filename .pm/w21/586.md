# The Android build declares the advertising-ID permission (`AD_ID`) though nothing in the app uses it

**Severity:** low (privacy posture and Play declaration). **Estimate:** 10 minutes, plus a check with the store owner. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/app.json:69` lists `"permissions": ["com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID"]` (added in `a3bfda1b9`, "add meta data for release"). The permission is granted automatically, but no dependency reads the advertising ID: there's no ads or analytics SDK, and `expo-application` isn't imported in `src/`. Google Play's Data safety form and advertising-ID declaration must match the manifest, so the shipped posture is inconsistent. It may have been added to silence a Console warning, so confirm with whoever ran the store release. Code-level at `main` `bb5156667`; Android isn't exercised here.

## Fix

Remove the permission (add it to `android.blockedPermissions` if a library merges it in), and set Play's advertising-ID declaration to "no".

**Acceptance:** the built manifest has no `AD_ID`, and the Play declaration matches.
