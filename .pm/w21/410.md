# Merchants splits an NFC and an NFD spelling of the same payee into two identical-looking merchants (sibling of w21/256 and 257)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/merchant-key.ts:8-9` keys on `payee.trim().toLowerCase()` with no NFC, and `merchants-screen/selectors/aggregate-payees.ts:133-135` groups by `toLowerCase()`. Under `tsx`, `merchantKey("Café Central") === merchantKey(NFD form)` is `false`. Each row gets half the history, so recurrence can be missed or flagged Overdue wrongly: w21/169's symptom, through Unicode rather than case.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `c9c36d8e7`):** with one NFC and one NFD "Café Central" entry on the disposable QA ledger, Merchants lists **two** "Café Central" rows with 1 transaction each. The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`.

## Fix

Normalise to NFC in `merchantKey` and in the aggregate-payees grouping key.

**Acceptance:** the two spellings form one merchant with 2 transactions.

## Dedupe

w21/169 covers case variants only, and 256 and 257 cover the picker and suggestions. Searched `.pm/**` for merchantKey and NFD with merchant.
