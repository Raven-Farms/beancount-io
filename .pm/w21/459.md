# One skipped month in a monthly bill's history drops its day-of-month anchor, so Next can be up to 3 days late

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `detect-recurrence.ts:236` (`if (previous !== null && nominal - previous !== months) return null;`) requires consecutive months, so a missing month falls back to `lastDay + gapDays` (`:535`). w21/271's proposed fix said to accept month steps of at least one; the shipped check needs exact steps.

Code-level at `main` `6854ec3a0`; reviewer's tsx runs:
- On the 1st, Sep 2025 to Feb 2026 with no gap, Next is `2026-03-01`; with December dropped, it is `2026-03-04`.
- On the 15th, Aug 2025 to Feb 2026, Next is `2026-03-15`; with October dropped, it is `2026-03-18`.

Not exercised on the device. After a paused or combined month, Next drifts off the bill's day for as long as that gap is in the window.

## Fix

Accept a `nominal - previous` that is a positive multiple of `months`; keep rejecting 0 (two payments in one month).

**Acceptance:** both gapped series keep their anchor (`2026-03-01`, `2026-03-15`). Unit cases are added.
