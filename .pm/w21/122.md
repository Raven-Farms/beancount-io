# The account picker offers "Create" for a closed account, then Open Account refuses with "already open in this ledger"

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. A user who searches the exact name of a closed account is led into a dead end whose message contradicts the picker. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/045 hides accounts closed by the entry date from the picker, so an exact-name search finds nothing and shows the Create row. Open Account's duplicate check uses `ledgerMeta.accounts`, which includes closed accounts, and reports "already open". Beancount can't reopen a closed account, so refusing is right; the wording and the Create offer are wrong.

Code-level at `main` `9c7878b18`.

## Scenario

Fixture: `2026-01-01 open Liabilities:QaOldCard USD` and `2026-09-01 close Liabilities:QaOldCard`. New Transaction (today) → a posting's account → search `Liabilities:QaOldCard` → "Create …" → Open Account shows **"Liabilities:QaOldCard is already open in this ledger."** with Done disabled. The Accounts tab + path shows the same.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/account-picker-screen/account-picker-screen.tsx:300-307` applies the `openOn` filter.
- `picker-sections.ts:134-143` adds the Create row; `onCreate` is at `:487-504`.
- `open-account-screen.tsx:264-270` and `common/account-name.ts:177-197` check for duplicates.
- `open-account-screen.tsx:428` shows `openAccountAlreadyOpen`.

## Fix

- In the picker, when the search exactly matches a closed account, show a non-actionable "Closed on {date}" row instead of Create.
- In Open Account, distinguish closed from open duplicates with a new translated message, "{account} was closed on {date} and can't be reopened", using `useAccountCloseDates`.

**Acceptance:**
- With the fixture, the picker shows "Closed on Sep 1, 2026" and no Create row for the exact name.
- Open Account, reached directly, shows the closed message.
- An open duplicate still shows "already open".

## Dedupe

w0/037 lists "a previously closed account" as not checked; w21/045 doesn't cover the Create row; w21/099 and w21/103 are other closed-account surfaces. Searched `.pm/**` for "already open", "closed account" together with create, and openAccountAlreadyOpen.
