# Zeroing an income budget flips its whole card to expense rules: income shows as negative bars, and a missed month turns green

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile, plus a dashboard port. A3: `budgetDirection` (`budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:459-461`) reads only the sign of the amount in effect today, and `buildBudgetEntry` stores a zeroed income target as `"0"`. It is used by `budget-group-card.tsx:185`, `select-budget-card-stats.ts:92` and `home-screen/selectors/select-budget-panel.ts:67`; the dashboard's `budget-chart-card.tsx:107` does the same. Zeroing is the natural way to stop tracking a target, since Delete rewrites history.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `f628d4727`):** I set up a disposable fixture: `Income:QaSal` with a monthly target of −3000 from 2026-01-01, then 0 from 2026-10-01, and 3000 earned in August and 1000 in September. Budget ▸ YTD drew August and September as **negative green bars** (−$3K, −$1K) on a $500 to −$3K axis. September, which missed its target by $2,000, is green, and Home's row read "$0.00 of $0.00, On target". The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`. Under `tsx`, the control (without the zero entry) marks September unfavourable.

## Fix

Take the direction from the account's root (`name_income`), or from the newest non-zero entry in the history, never from the current amount's sign. Apply it on all three surfaces and on the dashboard.

**Acceptance:** with the fixture, the bars are positive and September is unfavourable.

## Dedupe

w21/076 (zero expense budget on Home), 302 (negative expense) and 304 (Liabilities/Assets direction) differ. Searched `.pm/**` for budgetDirection, "zero budget", "income target" and asIncomeTarget.
