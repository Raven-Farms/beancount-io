# Create Ledger's stored request fingerprint gives away the opening amount the draft must not keep (w21/434 vs w21/053)

**Severity:** low (privacy at rest). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `create-ledger-screen/ledger-name.ts:112` fingerprints the whole request, amount included, with unsalted 32-bit FNV-1a, and `create-ledger-draft.ts:38` stores it as `sentFingerprint` beside every other field. w21/053 decided that the draft never holds an amount.

Code-level at `main` `18b56a778`. In the reviewer's tsx run, `serializeCreateLedgerDraft` stored amount `""` and fingerprint `1in7xp8`, and brute-forcing 0.00–100000.00 recovered `48213.57` in 11.7 s. Anyone who can read AsyncStorage during the 24-hour draft window (a backup or a device dump) can recover the opening balance.

## Fix

Fingerprint the request without the amount, salt it with a random per-draft key kept in SecureStore, or keep the fingerprint only in memory.

**Acceptance:** the stored draft can't be used to recover the amount. A test pins the fingerprint input or the salt.
