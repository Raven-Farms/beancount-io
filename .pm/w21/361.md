# Merchant detail's exact-payee filter can exceed the ledger's 256-character pattern limit, so a merchant with long merged spellings fails to load its journal

**Severity:** moderate for affected merchants. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since w21/181 (`bab8acf5f`), `filter-exact-payee.ts:57-69` joins every merged spelling into one escaped alternation and adds 12 characters. The ledger service refuses patterns over `MAX_FILTER_PATTERN_LENGTH = 256` (`directive-filter.ts:64, 193-197`) with `BadUserInputError`.

Under `tsx`, a 59-character bank payee with 3 case variants and 1 trailing-space spelling produced a 259-character pattern. Two of its alternatives were identical, because the filter trims spellings that w21/093 deliberately keeps distinct. Before the change, one plain needle was sent.

Code-level at `main` `24df51bf0`. Not run natively.

## Fix

Lowercase and de-duplicate the alternatives, since the server match is case-insensitive. If the pattern still exceeds 256 characters, fall back to the plain free-text filter for the primary spelling.

**Acceptance:** that merchant's detail loads its journal.
