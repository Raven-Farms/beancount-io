# Several controls are read with icon-font private-use characters ("Currency, USD, U+F23B"), some lack a button role, and the label guardrail can't catch them

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate (accessibility). **Estimate:** 1 hour. Owner: mobile. A3: with no explicit label, iOS joins child Texts into the control's name, including the Ionicons glyph. w2/069 confirmed this on a device ("Checking, U+F23B"). The guardrail test lets any pressable through if a `<Text` appears within 400 characters, and never checks for a role.

Code-level at `main` `aa4a9793a`.

## Sites (`mobile/src/screens/`)

- `transactions-screen/entry-context/index.tsx:268`: the Entry Context header reads "U+F…, Entry context, main.bean:3047, U+F…".
- `budget-screen/components/budget-history-rows.tsx:146`: the history toggle.
- `open-account-screen/open-account-screen.tsx:436, 454`: the Currency and Open date rows.
- `ledger-file-editor-screen/index.tsx:250`: the error rows that jump to a line have no role and glyphs at both ends.
- The same file, `:657` (Save) and `:741`, `:751` (Reload, Back to Files): no button trait.
- `account-detail-screen/components/account-entry-row.tsx:172, 255`: on non-transaction rows (Open, Balance, Note), the icon glyph is a focus stop of its own.

The guardrail is `mobile/src/__tests__/accessibility-labels.test.ts:172-175`.

## Fix

- Give each site an explicit translated `accessibilityLabel`, plus `accessibilityValue` where a value is shown, and `accessibilityRole="button"` where tappable.
- Mark icon glyphs `accessible={false}` or `importantForAccessibility="no"`.
- Tighten the guardrail: require a role on pressables, and flag a pressable whose children include an `Ionicons` without an explicit label.

**Acceptance:**
- Accessibility Inspector shows none of these controls with a U+F… character in its label.
- Save, Reload and Back to Files have the Button trait.
- The guardrail fails on a reintroduced unlabeled icon-and-text pressable.
