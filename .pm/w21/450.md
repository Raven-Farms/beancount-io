# A parent's "(own)" row shows only its operating-currency value: its own euros or shares appear in no row, and its own commodity reads as plain dollars

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/components/account-list/select-account-list.ts:140-150` builds the own row only when the operating-currency value is non-zero, and its key (`X\0own`) has no entry in `displays` (`account-table.tsx:524`), so it never gets units or "Not in total" notes.

Code-level at `main` `17da1541a`; reviewer's tsx runs:
- With `Assets:Bank` holding €500 of its own above a $100 child, the Bank row says "Not in total: 500.00 EUR", but no row beneath it carries the euros.
- With `Assets:Broker` holding 10 VTI of its own, the own row reads "$2,500.00 (own)", where a leaf would read "10 VTI · at market $2,500".

Not exercised on the device.

## Fix

Build the own row whenever `raw.balance` holds anything, and compute its display (units and notes) from `raw.balance`, like a leaf's.

**Acceptance:** both fixtures show the parent's own EUR or VTI on its own row. Unit cases are added.
