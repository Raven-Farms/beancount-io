# The account-detail balance chart skips months with no postings and spaces points by index, so it draws balances that never existed

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. A dormant month has no point, label or scrub stop, and the monotone curve between neighbours invents an intermediate balance (a September "~$1,750" where the true close was $2,000). The same money draws differently on Home's Assets chart, which uses a dense month-end series. The header and change line stay correct. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: account detail reads `linechartData`, which the ledger emits only on dates the balance changed. `pointsToMonthlySeries` keeps one point per month that has data and fills no gaps, and the chart lays points out by index rather than by date.

Code-level at `main` `17565bec1`. A native probe on this ledger was inconclusive, because its accounts have few active months. Decide this one consciously: w4/036 called the series "monthly and sparse" and judged "the plot itself is fine" while fixing only the baseline.

## Scenario

Assets:Bank:Savings: +2,000 on 2026-08-05 and −500 on 2026-10-12. Account detail → 3M shows two points, "08" and "10", sloping from 2,000 to 1,500, and the scrubber can't reach September. Home's Assets chart has Aug, Sep and Oct points.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-balance-series.ts:83-107`
- `mobile/src/common/series-util.ts:45-68` (`pointsToMonthlySeries`)
- `mobile/src/common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:181-184` (index spacing)
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts:407-444` (change-date points)

## Fix

Use the dense month-end `accountBalanceData` series, which is still in the schema and was dropped from the mobile query in w4/035, or forward-fill missing months on the client before charting.

**Acceptance:** for the scenario, 3M shows Aug, Sep and Oct points with September at 2,000, matching Home's Assets chart for the same account.
