# Budget and Home show a refund month's Actual without its minus sign: "Actual $50.00 · Variance −$404.84" (budget $354.84)

**Severity:** minor (the figures on the card don't add up). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budget-screen/components/budget-group-card.tsx:319`, `home-screen/components/budget-card.tsx:159` and the VoiceOver text in `home-screen/selectors/select-budget-panel.ts:239, 245` use `formatMoneyWithCurrency`, which drops the sign. `stats.actual` is negative when the period nets to a refund (`use-budget-actuals.ts` multiplies by `direction`).

Native (iPhone 17e at `5743f62d0`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-22): `custom "budget" Expenses:QaShop "monthly" 500.00 USD` dated 2026-01-01, a 120.00 purchase on 09-12 and a −50.00 return on 10-03. Home ▸ the QaShop budget row ▸ Budget (YTD) reads "Budget $354.84 · Actual $50.00 · Variance −$404.84", and the chart's October bar is below zero. $50.00 − $354.84 would be −$304.84, so the card contradicts itself.

## Fix

Format Actual with its sign (`formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency`, or "−$50.00 (refund)"), on both cards and in the VoiceOver text.

**Acceptance:** that fixture reads "Actual −$50.00", and Home's panel shows the same. Unit cases are added.
