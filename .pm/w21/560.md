# The time-range pills are named only by their abbreviations ("1M", "YTD", "1Y", "ALL"; Russian "1Г", "С нач. года")

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `components/time-range-pills/index.tsx:289-291` sets `accessibilityLabel={option.label}` from `RANGE_LABEL_KEYS` (`common/series-util.ts:20`). This affects Home charts, account detail, Reports, Budget and the account picker, and the Transactions filter chips use the same keys (`filters/types.ts:31`). VoiceOver is likely to say "1 M" and spell out "Y T D". Code-level at `main` `18664fa5e`; the spoken output is derived from the props (and proven under `tsx` where a builder is involved), not from a VoiceOver run.

## Fix

Add spoken labels per range ("1 month", "3 months", "Year to date", "1 year", "All time", "Last year") in every locale, and use them as `accessibilityLabel`.

**Acceptance:** the pills read their full names in English and the other locales. A parity test checks that every locale has the new keys.
