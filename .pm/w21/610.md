# Cash flow merges a whole category into one node when all its accounts sit under one sub-account (e.g. `Income:US:*`), while the cards below split it, and the Refunds ribbon disappears

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts:15` (`SANKEY_DEPTH = 2`) and `:251-280` (`extractAtDepth`) key every node at depth 2. The cards' `skipPassThroughLevels` (`components/account-list/select-account-list.ts`, about `:285`) lifts a single top-level account's children, so they show "US:Hoogle", "US:ETrade" and so on. Inside the merged node, a refunded category nets against spending, so w21/112's Refunds ribbon never appears. bean-example's `Income:US:*` layout is common.

Native (iPhone 17e at `9c394e685`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): October income only under `Income:QaUS:Hoogle` (9000) and `Income:QaUS:Etrade` (400), and expenses only under `Expenses:QaP:Rent` (2000) and `:Food` (300). Reports ▸ 1M ▸ Cash flow draws only "QaUS" → hub → "QaP" and "Savings". Under `tsx`, the cards list the individual sub-accounts, and with a −100 refund there's no Refunds ribbon.

## Fix

Apply the same pass-through skip to the Sankey's depth (extract at depth 2 *after* skipping single-child levels), or offer the dashboard's depth choice.

**Acceptance:** that fixture's Cash flow shows Hoogle and Etrade, and Rent and Food, matching the cards, and a refund shows its ribbon. Unit cases are added.
