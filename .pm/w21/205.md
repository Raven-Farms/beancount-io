# Receipt upload can't be cancelled on iOS, and its 60 s whole-transfer limit fails slow but working uploads of full-resolution photos

**Severity:** moderate. On a weak connection the user is stuck behind "Uploading photo…" with no Cancel, and the fullScreenModal has no swipe-dismiss. A 3–4 MB full-resolution photo can't finish in 60 s at about 400 kbps, so every attempt on slow cellular fails while bytes are still flowing, and w21/204 then discards the photo. There is no progress indicator. **Estimate:** 1 hour. Owner: mobile. A3: no step of the workflow is abortable. The limits are 30 s for the upload URL and 60 s for the whole transfer (not idle time), the parse has no limit, and photos are never downscaled (quality 0.85 at full resolution).

Code-level at `main` `f7bcaa7de`.

## Where (`mobile/`)

- `app/(app)/_layout.tsx:78-81` (`fullScreenModal`)
- `src/screens/receipt-capture-screen/preview-view.tsx:130-135` (a busy overlay with no controls)
- `use-receipt-workflow.ts:43-122` (limits at `:54-59` and `:66-75`; no limit at `:86-89`)
- `camera-view.tsx:114-116` and `receipt-capture-screen.tsx:58-61` (full resolution)

## Fix

- Add a Cancel button to the busy overlay, wired to an `AbortController` across all steps.
- Downscale to about 1600 px on the long edge before upload (expo-image-manipulator, if already present; ask before adding a dependency).
- Make the upload limit an idle or progress limit, and show progress.

**Acceptance:** with 100% uplink loss, Cancel returns to the preview within 1 s. On an Edge-profile link, a typical receipt photo uploads successfully.
