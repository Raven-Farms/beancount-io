# A user with no ledger still can't reach Settings to log out or delete the account: every tab's drawer button sits inside the ledger guard

**Severity:** moderate. App Store review requires account deletion to be reachable. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/217 removed the guard from Settings, but Settings is opened only from the drawer (`ledger-drawer.tsx:715`) and from Profile (`profile-screen.tsx:307`), and Profile is itself reached from Settings. The drawer opens only from `LedgerDrawerHeader`, and every tab renders that header *inside* `<LedgerGuard>`:
- `home-screen.tsx:206` and `:287`
- `accounts-screen.tsx:187` and `:235`
- `transactions-screen.tsx:299` and `:383`
- `reports-screen.tsx:177` and `:288`
- Files, through `ledger-screen.tsx:6`

The drawer has no swipe gesture. With no ledger, the guard (`ledger-guard.tsx:143-189`) offers only Create and Discover, and neither links to settings or logout. So the 217 fix is unreachable without the `beancount:///(app)/settings` deep link. 217's own resolution lists the entry point as "optional second part … not done".

Code-level at `main` `ebb642e45`, from the regression audit. Not driven natively, because the QA account owns ledgers.

## Fix

Add a Settings/account button, or the drawer's identity row, to the guard's no-ledger and ledger-gone states. Alternatively, render `LedgerDrawerHeader` outside the guard on every tab.

**Acceptance:** a signed-in user with zero ledgers reaches Log out and Delete Account in at most two taps from Home.
