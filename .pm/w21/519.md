# New Transaction's Payee misses its suggestion, usual accounts and merchant when iOS Smart Punctuation types a curly apostrophe ("Trader Joe’s") (sibling of w21/401)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `dc8e781e1`, proven under `tsx`): the payee field sets only `autoCorrect={false}`, and w21/401 showed that this still lets Smart Punctuation (on by default) turn ' into ’. w21/401 fixed only Transactions search. These three places still compare apostrophes literally:
- `components/text-input-screen/match-suggestions.ts:7` (`fold`): `matchSuggestions(["Trader Joe's"], "Trader Joe’")` returns `[]`.
- `common/suggestion-utils.ts:345` (exact `trim()` match): `deriveTwoPostingSuggestions(…, "Trader Joe’s").to.autoFill` is `null`, while the straight quote gives `Expenses:Food:Groceries`.
- `common/merchant-key.ts:12`: the two spellings become two merchants.

Scenario: with an existing `"Trader Joe's"` entry, go to New Transaction ▸ Payee and type "Trader Joe's" on the on-screen keyboard. No suggestion appears after the apostrophe, the accounts stay at the defaults, and after saving, Merchants lists "Trader Joe’s" separately. Not run natively: the driver can't type on the soft keyboard, and a pasted string skips Smart Punctuation.

## Fix

Fold ’ ‘ ʼ to ' (and “ ” to ") in the shared fold that `matchSuggestions`, the suggestion lookup and `merchantKey` use, as w21/401 did for search. Consider `smartQuotesType="no"` on the payee field.

**Acceptance:** the three calls above treat ’ and ' alike. Unit cases are added.
