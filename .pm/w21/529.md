# Month-end and quarter-end recurring bills show "Next" on the 30th instead of the 31st (w21/271 regression of w21/007)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:223-229` (`monthDayAnchor`) has used the most common day of the month since w21/271; w21/007 used the latest day seen. A last-day-of-month bill clamps to the 30th in 30-day months, so when those outnumber the 31-day ones, the anchor is 30. `semiMonthlyDays` (`:312, :319`) has the same flaw.

Native (iPhone 17e at `52a06aa36`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte; today 2026-10-22): "QA Acme Bank" 25.00 USD on every quarter-end from 2023-09-30 to 2026-09-30 (13 entries; seven 30ths and six 31sts). Merchants ▸ Recurring reads "QA Acme Bank · Quarterly · Next Dec 30, 2026", where it should be Dec 31. Under `tsx`, monthly `04-30, 05-31, 06-30` gives Next 07-30, and a 15th-and-last-day payroll gives Next 07-30.

## Fix

Count an event on its month's last day as matching any anchor at or above that day, and prefer the largest such anchor (treat it as "last day of month").

**Acceptance:** that fixture reads Next Dec 31, 2026, and the monthly and twice-a-month cases land on the 31st or the month's last day. Unit cases are added.
