# Home's Recent Transactions rows show no date, so a pre-entered future payment looks like it already happened

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the card renders bare `EntryRow`s, which never draw a date (Transactions and merchant detail get dates from `DateSectionHeader`). The journal comes newest first, future dates included, and the VoiceOver label has no date either. Reports' Recent transactions card has the same rows.

Observed natively throughout this session on the iPhone 17e simulator at `main` `26fb7ee69`: Home's Recent Transactions lists QA fixtures dated October and real rows dated August with no date anywhere on the card. For example, "QA BUDGET QTR −$250.00" (5 October) sat directly above "FARMERS MARKET −$23.50" (28 August). With rent pre-entered for 1 November, the top row would read "Rent −$1,500.00" with nothing saying it's next month.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/components/recent-transactions-card.tsx:113-130`.
- `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/entry-row/index.tsx:155-268`.

- `reports-screen/components/account-transactions-card.tsx:130`.

## Fix

Show a short date on each card row (`formatLedgerDateShort`), marked as upcoming when it's after today, and include the date in the accessibility label.

**Acceptance:** each Home and Reports card row shows its date, a future row is marked upcoming, and the label includes the date.
