# Account detail's chart ignores price moves between postings, then jumps to today's value ("+$900" over 1M for a +$100 month)

**Severity:** moderate for investment accounts. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `linechartData` has a point only where the balance changed, each valued at that date's price. w21/164's `forwardFillMonths` (`common/series-util.ts:78-106`) copies that value through quiet months, and w21/179 ends the line at today's market value (`select-account-balance-series.ts:120-149`). Price movement in between is never drawn. Under `tsx`, 10 VTI plus $100 last posted 2026-01-15 (VTI 200), worth $3,000 today, plots `2100 … 2100, 3000`, so 1M and 6M both read **+$900 (+42.86%)** while the real one-month move is about +$100. Home's dense series draws the same money correctly.

Code-level at `main` `3e4352ec8`.

## Fix

Chart from the dense month-end `accountBalanceData` (valued at each period end; w4/035 removed it from the query), or value the forward-filled months at their month-end prices.

**Acceptance:** with monthly VTI prices from 210 to 290, the 1M change matches the last month's price move.

## Dedupe

w21/164 introduced the forward fill; 179 and 240 differ. Searched `.pm/**` for forwardFill, linechartData, accountBalanceData, "flat line" and "price moves".
