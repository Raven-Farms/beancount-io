# In New Transaction, changing the payee keeps the first payee's accounts

**Severity:** moderate. Someone who taps the wrong suggestion and corrects it saves the new payee against the old payee's card and category. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `multi-postings-transaction/payee-seed.ts:41` fills accounts only while the postings are `untouched(current, initial)`. After the first payee fills them, they no longer equal the opening postings, so the next payee is treated as if the user had edited the accounts. Under `tsx`, after TRADER JOES fills `[Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa, Expenses:Food:Groceries]`, `seedPostingsFromPayee(first, initial, history("CITY BUS"))` returns `null`.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `c06a57c9a`):**
1. Home → + → Enter new transaction → Payee → TRADER JOES filled **Liabilities:CreditCard:Visa / Expenses:Food:Groceries**.
2. Payee → clear → CITY BUS left the postings as **Visa / Groceries**, instead of CITY BUS's usual Cash/Transport.

The draft was discarded with nothing saved.

## Fix

Remember the postings the last payee fill produced, and treat them as untouched too (compare against `lastSeed ?? initial`, as `shouldReseed` does).

**Acceptance:** re-picking the payee replaces the previous payee's filled accounts, unless the user edited them.
