# New Transaction writes zero-decimal currencies with ".00": a typed 1500 JPY is saved as "1500.00 JPY"

**Severity:** minor. It writes non-idiomatic amounts into the ledger file, which then show as "1,500.00" in Fava, bean-query and the CLI. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/070 taught the *display* helpers each currency's minor unit, but the entry serializer still pads every amount to two decimals.

Code-level at `main` `7e42f850e`. Run under Node, on a JPY ledger:
- Typing −1500 gives an auto leg of `1500.00`, and a payload of `-1500.00 JPY` / `1500.00 JPY`.
- A 1000 / −1500 draft shows the remainder as `-¥500.00`.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/postings-utils.ts:118-125` (`formatExactAmount` pads with `padEnd(2, "0")`). It is used by `buildEntryInput` (`:360`), the auto leg (`:144`) and `formatRemainderAmount` (`:387-394`).

## Fix

Pad to `currencyMinorUnits(currency)` (from w21/070) instead of 2, never truncating a longer typed value, so 1500 JPY stays `1500` and 12.5 KWD becomes `12.500`.

**Acceptance:** on a JPY ledger, the payload is `-1500 JPY` / `1500 JPY`, and the remainder reads `-¥500`. USD amounts are unchanged. Unit cases cover JPY, KWD and USD.

## Dedupe

w21/070 (done) fixed the display helpers only, and w21/069 (done) the receipt prefill. Searched `.pm/**` for formatExactAmount, "1500.00 JPY" and padEnd.
