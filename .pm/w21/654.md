# A `"` in an org-mode heading or `#` comment line silently drops every entry after it in the hosted engine: no error, missing balances and assertions

**Severity:** major (silent data loss in every report). **Estimate:** 1 hour (upstream, plus a ledger-service guard). Owner: ledger (rustledger upstream). A3: rustledger 0.24 doesn't treat a whole `* …` heading or `# …` line as a comment when it holds a `"`. The quote opens a string that swallows the following entries. Beancount 3.2.3 skips those lines. Compared at `main` `1ec9cb250`: bundled `@rustledger/wasm` 0.24.0 against Python Beancount 3.2.3 with beanquery 0.2.0, on the same text. With `* Notes: 12" pizza budget` before 19 transactions and a balance assertion, rustledger reports no errors and `count(*) = 0`, while Beancount gives 38 postings and a passing assertion. `# It's 5" wide` does the same. w5/844 saw this only for managed price precedence, patched just the overlay, and didn't report it upstream.

Production (2026-10-22, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): appending `* Notes: 12" pizza budget` followed by a new transaction leaves `getLedgerJournal.total` at 29 (the new entry vanished), with `getLedgerErrors` empty.

## Fix

Report upstream. Meanwhile, pre-process heading and comment lines (neutralise or blank them) in the ledger service before parsing, or surface a warning for lines the engine treats differently.

**Acceptance:** that fixture keeps the following entry, and a test runs the real engine.
