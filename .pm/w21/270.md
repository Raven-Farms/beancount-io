# Merchant logos show the payment processor, not the merchant ("PAYPAL *NETFLIX" gets PayPal), and "#" bypasses the common-word guard

**Severity:** minor (cosmetic, and a wrong-company logo reads as wrong data). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: only `sq|tst|sp|pp` count as processor prefixes. `split(/[*#]/)[0]` keeps the processor and throws away the merchant after `*`, and the processor word then matches exactly before the common-word check runs.

Code-level at `main` `de881e646`. `matchBrand` under Node:
- "PAYPAL *NETFLIX" gives paypal.com, while "PP*NETFLIX" gives netflix.com.
- "PAYPAL *SPOTIFY" gives paypal.com.
- "GOOGLE *YouTube Premium" gives google.com.
- "UBER *EATS" and "UBER* EATS PENDING" give uber.com, while "Uber Eats" gives ubereats.com.
- "United #1 Plumbing" gives united.com: the text after `#` is cut, so w4/031's common-word rule never applies.
- Also: "Lowes Foods" gives lowes.com, and "Citi Trends" gives citi.com.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/common/brand-matcher/index.ts:202-203` (prefixes and split) and `:228` (exact match before the common-word check).

## Fix

When the part before `*` is a processor or aggregator (paypal, google, uber, amazon, doordash, square…), match the part after `*` first. Cut at `#` only when digits follow.

**Acceptance:** the cases above give netflix.com, spotify.com, youtube.com, ubereats.com and no logo for "United #1 Plumbing". Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w4/031 (done; Delta Dental) and w21/172 (accented letters) are different. Searched `.pm/**` for paypal, processor, stripTransactionNoise and "PP*".
