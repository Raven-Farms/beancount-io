# Transaction and posting metadata (`invoice:`, `note:`, …) never appear on the transaction detail screen

**Severity:** moderate. Metadata is how Beancount users attach invoice numbers, receipts and notes, and on mobile it's only reachable by expanding Entry Context's raw source. An entry with metadata but no tags gets no Details section at all. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `entry.meta` and `posting.meta` are in the payload, but `hasMetadata` only considers tags and links, and `PostingRow` takes no meta.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `562a1b8ef`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture was `2026-08-30 * "QA META"`, with `invoice: "INV-0914"`, `Expenses:Transport 1.00 USD` (posting meta `note: "posting meta"`) and `Assets:Cash -1.00 USD`. The detail shows the hero, Money flow and Entry Context only, with no `invoice` and no `note` anywhere.

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/transaction-detail-screen.tsx:518` (`hasMetadata`) and `:671-722`, plus `components/posting-row.tsx`.

## Fix

Render user metadata key/value pairs in the Details section, and posting metadata under each posting row. Filter out the backend-added `filename`, `lineno` and internal `__…` keys. Localize the section heading.

**Acceptance:** the fixture shows "invoice: INV-0914" under Details and "note: posting meta" under the Transport posting. Entries without metadata are unchanged.

w4/046 (the dead toggleMetadata key) and w4/115 (dashboard) are related. Searched `.pm/**` for entry.meta, hasMetadata, "posting meta" and invoice.
