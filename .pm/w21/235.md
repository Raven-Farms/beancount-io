# On a ledger with renamed roots, Reports' Cash flow says "No cash flow in this range" under a chart full of income and spending

**Severity:** moderate. Non-English ledgers (`option "name_income" "Ingresos"` …) are the group w21/073 fixed elsewhere. The Sankey is the one Reports card that fix missed. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transformToSankeyData` classifies accounts by hard-coded `"Income"` / `"Expenses"` roots and excludes everything else. With both roots renamed, every node is excluded and the card shows its empty state. With only one renamed, the links are wrong: a reviewer's Node run with only `name_expenses "Gastos"` produced `Income:Salary → cash-flow 3000` and `cash-flow → savings 3000`, which claims all income was saved.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `68c1c39cc`, on the disposable QA ledger rewritten with all five roots renamed (`Activos`, `Pasivos`, `Patrimonio`, `Ingresos`, `Gastos`), then restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. On Reports (6M), the income/expense chart shows about K income and K expenses for July and August. Directly below it, **Cash flow** reads **"No cash flow in this range"**. With English roots, the same data draws Salary → Housing / … / Savings.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts:66-83` (`categorizeAccount`) switches on the literal root names.
- `:234` (`extractAtDepth`) skips the nodes marked `"exclude"`.
- `:157-179` (`sankeyRoleForId`, node colours) repeats the hard-coding.
- w21/073's `renamed-roots.test.ts` has no Sankey case, and its write-up said the Sankey "shows activity".

## Fix

Pass the ledger's root names (`useLedgerRootNames()`, from w21/073) into `transformToSankeyData` and use them in `categorizeAccount` and `sankeyRoleForId`. Add a Sankey case to `renamed-roots.test.ts` for all-renamed and one-renamed ledgers.

**Acceptance:**
- On the renamed fixture, Cash flow draws Salario → … and Savings, as the English ledger does.
- The VoiceOver summary's income, expenses and savings match the chart.

w21/073 (done) covered Open Account, Reports' Recent transactions, Merchants and rows, but not the Sankey. w21/112 is the refund and Savings mismatch. Searched `.pm/**` for categorizeAccount, cashFlowEmpty, "No cash flow" and renamed together with Sankey.
