# Profile says "Add a photo on Gravatar" to users who have one whenever the image fails to load (offline or slow network)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since w21/289, `UserAvatar`'s `onError` reports a fallback for any load failure (`components/user-avatar/index.tsx:34-38, 54-57`), not just Gravatar's `d=404`, and Profile maps it straight to `profileAddGravatar` (`profile/profile-screen.tsx:368-370`). An offline or flaky load shows a call to action to someone who already has a Gravatar.

Code-level at `main` `669120634`. The QA account's Gravatar loads normally (w21/289 recheck).

## Fix

Treat only a confirmed 404 as "no Gravatar" (a HEAD or fetch status check), and hide the caption on other failures.

**Acceptance:** with a Gravatar and the network off, Profile does not say "Add a photo on Gravatar".
