# The file editor's error banner cuts long messages at two lines, with no way to read the rest there

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile.

Code-level at `main` `ba784a628`: `mobile/src/screens/ledger-file-editor-screen/index.tsx:268` sets `numberOfLines={2}` on 12 pt text (roughly 105 characters), and tapping a row only jumps to its line. The skipped-plugin message (127 characters) loses "its effects were not applied", and a URL-include refusal (197 characters) loses its whole guidance. The full text is only in Notifications or the VoiceOver label.

## Fix

Let a long row expand in place, or drop the line limit inside the banner's list, which already scrolls and is capped at 140 pt.

**Acceptance:** a 200-character message is fully readable in the banner.
