# Ledger setup validation messages are garbled ("Validation failed for Unsupported operating currency \"XX\": setup"): ValidationError's arguments are swapped

**Severity:** minor (on REST, GraphQL and MCP, and shown raw on mobile's Review). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: backend-v2. A3: `backend-v2/src/features/ledger/setup/build-ledger.ts:37-39` calls `new ValidationError(message, "setup")`, but the constructor is `(field, reason)`. The same swap is at `ledger-workflow.ts:550, 561, 610`. Under `tsx`, the real `previewLedgerSetup` with currency "XX" returned the message `Validation failed for Unsupported operating currency "XX": setup`, with `{field: "Unsupported operating currency \"XX\"", reason: "setup"}`. Mobile shows `previewError.message` on Review (`create-ledger-screen.tsx:783`), and REST and MCP `field` is the whole sentence.

Code-level at `main` `9bd067834`.

## Fix

Use `new ValidationError("setup", message)`, and flip the workflow calls the same way (`"requestId"`, `"setup"`), with a parity test on the error shape.

**Acceptance:** that preview returns `field: "setup"` with a readable reason on all three surfaces.
