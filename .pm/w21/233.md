# Two screens still freeze "today" at mount after w21/078: Home's budget panel stays on last month past midnight, and Merchants' Overdue never advances

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/078 added `useToday()` and moved Transactions, Accounts, Home's other cards, account detail and `useBudgetGroups` onto it. Two readers still capture the date once.

Code-level at `main` `93588654e`. A reviewer ran the real helpers under Node with the clock pinned; output was identical in America/Los_Angeles, Asia/Tokyo and Pacific/Auckland.

## 1. Home's budget panel

`mobile/src/screens/home-screen/hooks/use-budget-panel.ts:157`: the effect depends on `[client, ledgerId, groupsKey, refreshSignal, ledgerRevision, attempt]`, with no day. Inside it, `timeSpanToFilter(DEFAULT_BUDGET_SPAN)` (`:101`), `currentPeriodRange(group.interval)` (`:113`) and `settleBudgetPanel(…, getFormatDate(new Date()))` (`:143-146`) read the clock. `groupsKey` (`:76-82`) leaves out the interval, so the daily change in `useBudgetGroups` doesn't re-run it either. For a monthly 500 USD budget:

```
frozen (ran 2026-10-31 23:50): periodEnd 2026-10-31, budget 500,   actual 480, 96%
fresh  (2026-11-01 08:00)    : periodEnd 2026-11-30, budget 16.67, actual 0,   0%
frozen (2026-10-21, 300 spent): budget 338.71, 89%   vs fresh 2026-10-22: budget 354.84, 85%
```

With the app left open overnight, the row reads October's "$480 of $500, 96%" on the morning of 1 November, while the Budget screen shows November. Mid-month, the prorated budget lags a day. w21/225's fix covers a return from the background, but not midnight passing while the app stays open.

## 2. Merchants' recurrence

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/use-payee-recurrence.ts:21`: `const today = useMemo(() => getFormatDate(new Date()), [])`. It feeds `detectRecurrence(series, today)` (`:47`) and the 12-month cutoff. For a 15 USD bill paid on the 1st, June to October: 2026-11-09 gives `overdue false`, and 2026-11-10 gives `overdue true`. With Merchants open on 9 November, returning the next morning refetches the data, but the bill isn't marked Overdue until the screen remounts. The comment's reason ("so overdue doesn't flicker") doesn't apply to `useToday()`, which changes only at a day boundary.

## Fix

Use `useToday()` in both places. For the panel, add it to the effect's dependencies and pass it to `timeSpanToFilter`, `currentPeriodRange` and `settleBudgetPanel`. For Merchants, replace the mount-time `useMemo`.

**Acceptance:** with the mocked clock crossing midnight (as `today-watcher.test.ts` does), the panel's period end moves to the new month and the 10 November overdue flag turns on. Wiring checks are added to that test file.
