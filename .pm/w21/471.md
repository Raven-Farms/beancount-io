# Upcoming entries can fill all five Recent Transactions rows on Home, hiding everything that has actually happened

**Severity:** moderate for anyone who pre-enters bills or uses the forecast or amortize plugins. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `recent-transactions-card.tsx:57-68` asks for `limit: 5` with no `time` bound, and the journal sorts by date descending with no cap at today (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/service/ledger-legacy-journal.ts:255-256, 356-373`). w21/261's native check already showed a future row on top ("QA RENT · Nov 1, 2026 · Upcoming"), and this session's 337 recheck showed "QA Landlord · Oct 30 · Upcoming" first.

This follows deterministically from those lines at `main` `c039662e6`; not exercised with five future rows. With five or more transactions dated after today, the card titled "Recent Transactions" shows only "Upcoming" rows and hides today's spending.

## Fix

Pass `time: "- <today>"` (from `useToday`) for the recent list, and show upcoming entries separately or cap them at one or two above the past rows.

**Acceptance:** with five monthly rent entries pre-entered from Nov 1 and a coffee yesterday, the card shows the coffee. A unit or wiring test is added.
