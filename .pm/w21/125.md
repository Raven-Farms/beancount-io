# Holdings smaller than 0.000001 units read "0 BTC" because balance strings arrive in exponential notation

**Severity:** moderate for crypto users. A non-empty holding reads as zero on the Accounts row, the account header and the root's "Not in total" note, breaking m11's rule that nothing non-empty reads as zero. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: ledger (serializer), plus a mobile guard. A3: the ledger stringifies tree and series balances with `BigNumber#toString()`, which bignumber.js switches to exponential notation below 1e-6 (`"5e-7"`). Mobile's `amountScale` only counts digits after a dot, so `"5e-7"` gets scale 0 and renders `0 BTC`, and `"1.5e-7"` renders `0.0000 BTC`.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `c26bdfe94`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-dust`: `commodity BTC`, open `Assets:Qa20261020Wallet BTC`, and `2026-08-05` `0.00000050 BTC` from `Equity:Qa20261020Seed`.

Accounts → Assets lists **"Qa20261020Wallet · 0 BTC"**, and Assets shows "1 not in total".

## Root cause

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/account-tree.ts:141` and `interval-series.ts:231` use `toString()`. `accountOwnUnitBalances` (`account-tree.ts:236`) already uses `toFixed(scale)`.
- `mobile/src/common/balance-display.ts:79` (`amountScale(String(raw))`) and `common/number-utils.ts:56-60` are the mobile side.

## Fix

Serialize every balance map with plain decimal notation: `toFixed()`, or set `BigNumber.config({ EXPONENTIAL_AT: 1e9 })` in the ledger service. This covers REST, GraphQL and MCP alike. In mobile, make `amountScale` and `formatNumber` tolerate exponent strings as a guard.

**Acceptance:**
- The fixture shows `0.0000005 BTC` on the Accounts row and in the account header.
- The API returns `"0.00000050"` (or `"0.0000005"`), not `"5e-7"`.
- Add ledger and mobile unit tests.
