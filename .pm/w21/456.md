# The Budget chart's y-axis never goes below 1, so a BTC budget's bars are invisible slivers on a 0–1 axis

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/d3/budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx:92` sets `yMax = Math.max(1, ...actuals, ...budgets)`. w21/236 fixed the shared `barChartValueDomain` for Spending and Reports, but this chart still has its own floor of 1.

Code-level at `main` `3a3c7e4fb`. In the reviewer's tsx run, actuals `[0.0021, 0.0042, 0.0035]` against a 0.003 budget get the domain `[0, 1]` (ticks 0, 0.2 … 1), so the bars are about 0.3–0.6 px tall. The shared helper returns `[0, 0.0042]`. Not exercised on the device. The sub-unit budgets that w21/290 enabled chart as a flat line, and bars over the target can't be told apart.

## Fix

Use `barChartValueDomain([...actuals, ...budgets])` here too.

**Acceptance:** the fixture's bars fill the chart height, and a USD budget's axis is unchanged. A unit case is added.

Follow-up to w21/236 (shared domain) and w21/290 (fractional budgets); w21/360 is the axis width. Searched `.pm/**` for budget-bar-chart, BudgetBarChart, yMax and "Math.max(1".
