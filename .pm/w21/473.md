# Commit detail's "Show N more lines" ignores plural forms: "Show 1 more lines", and wrong word forms in Russian and Ukrainian (w21/379 follow-up)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `commit-detail-screen.tsx:401` passes `count` to `commitDetailShowMore`, which is a single fixed string in all 13 locales (`en.ts:540` "Show {{count}} more lines", `ru.ts:541` "Показать ещё {{count}} строк"). The neighbouring `commitDetailAdditions` already uses `{one, few, many, other}`.

Code-level at `main` `fe720cf39`. The reviewer's tsx run with the real i18n-js pluralizers gives: a 501-line diff shows en "Show 1 more lines", ru "Показать ещё 1 строк" and uk "Показати ще 1 рядків"; 502 lines show ru "ещё 2 строк" (should be "строки"); 521 lines show ru "ещё 21 строк" (should be "строку"). This session's native check showed "Show 209 more lines" (correct for en). Not exercised natively for the singular.

## Fix

Make `commitDetailShowMore` a plural object in all 13 locales (`one`/`other`, plus `few`/`many` for ru, uk, sk and bg as their other plural keys do).

**Acceptance:** 1, 2, 5 and 21 remaining lines read grammatically in en and ru. The locale parity test covers the plural shape.
