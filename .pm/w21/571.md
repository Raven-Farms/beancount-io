# A stock split or merger reads as shares gained or lost in money colours ("+90.00 NVDA", green), though no money moved

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transaction-display-utils.ts:215-228` (one commodity: its net units, signed) and `:200-209` (two commodities at cost: the bigger unit count wins). The dashboard (`transaction-summary.ts:194-199`) shows it as a neutral "mixed".

Native (iPhone 17e at `0c0609c4e`, disposable ledger, restored): after a buy of 10 NVDA {1200.00 USD}, a 10:1 split `Assets:QaNvda -10 NVDA {1200.00 USD}` / `Assets:QaNvda 100 NVDA {120.00 USD}` reads "+90.00 NVDA" in green on the Transactions row. Under `tsx`, a merger (`-10 OLDCO {100}`, `+5 NEWCO {200}`) reads "−10.00 OLDCO" in red.

## Fix

When the cost side nets to zero (no money moved), show the entry as a neutral, unsigned corporate action (for example "10 → 100 NVDA").

**Acceptance:** the split and the merger read as neutral, with no money colour or sign. Unit cases are added.
