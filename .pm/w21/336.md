# Recurrence fixes leave two small regressions: an exact float compare adds "~" to a steady price, and the budget panel's batches keep firing after cancel

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

Code-level at `main` `61ad1916c`:
1. **w21/171:** `detect-recurrence.ts:398-401` compares `latest !== overall` as raw floats. With 7 months at 11.87 as one leg, then 3 as 10.99 + 0.88 legs (summed to 11.870000000000001 in `mapPayeeSeries`), the row reads "~$11.87" for an unchanged price. Fix: compare at money scale (`Math.abs(latest - overall) >= 0.005`) and round the summed amounts.
2. **w21/165:** `mapInBatches` (`common/map-in-batches.ts:12`, used at `use-budget-panel.ts:104, 139, 157`) has no abort. Cleanup only sets `cancelled`, so after a ledger switch or any write, every remaining `network-only` query still fires, and overlapping runs exceed the 12-in-flight ceiling. With 40 budgets that's 40 reads per write. Fix: pass a cancel check or `AbortSignal` and stop between batches.

**Acceptance:** a unit case for each fails before its fix.
