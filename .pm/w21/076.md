# Home's budget panel measures against the full month's budget, not the budget up to today, so it disagrees with the Budget screen; a 0 budget that has been overspent reads "0% used"

**Severity:** major (the two surfaces contradict each other). **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: this is a follow-up to w4/038 (done). That fix said Home's panel shared both halves of the YTD defect. But the "budgeted no further than today" (`asOf`) half went only into the Budget screen.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0), dev build 1.20261002.50, `main` at `4db11d8b8`. "Today" is 2026-10-21. The fixtures were on the disposable QA ledger, which was restored byte-for-byte to blob `f1a0b83c`.

1. **The two surfaces disagree.** The ledger has `2026-10-01 custom "budget" Expenses:QaBudFood "monthly" 300 USD` and 250 USD spent on 2026-10-05.
   - Home's budget row has the accessibility value **"$250.00 of $300.00, 83% used, Below target"**.
   - Tapping it opens the Budget screen, which shows **Budget $203.23 · Actual $250.00 · Variance +$46.77 · Above target** (300 × 21/31).
   - Home ranks its three rows by this percentage, so a quarterly or yearly budget is misranked for most of its period.
2. **A zero budget that has been overspent.** With `… "monthly" 0 USD` and 80 USD spent, Home reads **"$80.00 of $0.00, 0% used, Above target"**. The meter is empty in the over colour. Because rows sort by percent, highest first, the one budget that is actually blown sorts last and can fall outside the three rows shown.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/selectors/select-budget-panel.ts:51` calls `calculateBudgetForInterval(periodEnd, group.interval, group.budgetHistory)` without `asOf`. `budget-screen/hooks/use-budget-actuals.ts:73-78` passes `getFormatDate(new Date())`.
- `select-budget-card-stats.ts:87` returns `progressPercent: null` for a budget of 0 or less. `select-budget-panel.ts:65` coerces that to `0`, and `:75` sorts by it.

## Fix

- Pass today as `asOf` in the Home selector, through the same helper the screen uses.
- For a budget of 0 or less with spending, rank the row as over (e.g. `Infinity` for sorting), and render a full or over meter with the actual amount rather than "0% used".

**Acceptance:**
- Selector tests: on 2026-10-21, a 300 monthly budget yields 203.23 on Home, matching the screen.
- A 0 budget with spending sorts first and isn't labelled 0%.
- The Home row and the Budget screen agree on target and status for the same budget.
