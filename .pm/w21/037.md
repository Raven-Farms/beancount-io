# In Persian, the "Invite Friends" title still sits on the physical left (residual of w21/019)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 5–10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2/A3: w21/019 moved Persian UI copy to the leading edge, and Settings' section titles (حساب, تنظیمات برنامه, پشتیبانی) are now right-aligned. The first title on the same screen, **دعوت دوستان** (Invite Friends), is still on the left, directly above the right-aligned rows.

Environment: `main` at `cc2d8a34c`+ (includes `d8c585e4f`), dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0), Persian after the RTL restart. Restored to English afterwards.

## Repro

Settings → Language → فارسی → restart → Settings. The **دعوت دوستان** heading starts at the left edge, while the section titles below it start at the right. The same component heads the Referral screen.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/referral-screen/components/invite-section.tsx:16-21`: the `title` style has no `textAlign`. `d8c585e4f` added `LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to the listed surfaces and `leading-text-align.test.ts`, but not this component.

## Fix

Add `textAlign: LEADING_TEXT_ALIGN` to `styles.title` (and `styles.summary` if it is full-width), and add `invite-section.tsx` to the surfaces `src/common/__tests__/leading-text-align.test.ts` pins.

**Acceptance:** in Persian, دعوت دوستان starts at the right edge on Settings and Referral. English is unchanged. The guard test lists the file.

## Dedupe

Residual of w21/019 (done). No other item.
