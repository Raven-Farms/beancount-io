# A holding priced only in an unrelated currency the account also holds is labelled priced, though the ledger values it at cost (VTI priced in USD in a EUR ledger)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `e47e7641c`, proven under `tsx`): `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:309-319` takes `costCurrencies` from every currency in the cost map (or in the market map when there's no cost map). That includes cost-less holdings such as USD cash, which aren't a cost currency of anything.

Scenario (EUR operating currency): `Assets:Broker` holds 500.00 USD cash and 10 VTI {180.00 EUR}, with price VTI 210.00 USD. Home ▸ Assets' caption reads "At market value · 1 not in total", and the sheet shows "10 VTI · Price Oct 21". The ledger doesn't hop through USD, so it should read "1 at cost (no price) · 1 not in total". Without the USD cash line, it reads correctly.

Not run natively (it needs the operating currency swapped to EUR).

## Fix

Exclude currencies that appear in `units` as cost-less holdings from `costCurrencies`.

**Acceptance:** that fixture reads "1 at cost (no price) · 1 not in total". Unit cases are added.

Named as a known limit in w21/124's resolution, but never filed. Searched `.pm/**` for costCurrencies and "priced in" together with "at cost".
