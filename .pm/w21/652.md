# Capital-letter flags other than P, S, T, C, U, R and M (e.g. "A", "X") are refused, though both engines and the `bea` CLI accept any capital

**Severity:** low (API and agents). **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3 (code-level at `main` `c1c3be649`; validator and engine compared): `entry-tokens.ts:22-23` lists only `PSTCURM` among letters for transaction and posting flags. rustledger 0.24 and Beancount 3.2.3 (`grammar.y`, `CAPITAL` at 305, 357 and 418) accept any capital letter, and the CLI accepts "a single capital letter" (w5/382, w5/526), so the hosted API and `bea` disagree.

## Fix

Accept any `[A-Z]` flag.

**Acceptance:** "A" and "Z" flags are accepted on all three surfaces. Tests are added.

## Dedupe

See w5/382 and w5/526. Searched all of `.pm/` for PSTCURM and "capital letter" together with flag.
