# Create's auto-capitalisation skips Cyrillic and Greek, so ru, uk and bg users land on a refused account name

**Severity:** minor. It is the incomplete half of w4/010. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `capitalizeAccountPrefill` capitalises only Latin initials, and a component after ": " (with a space) isn't capitalised either.

Code-level at `main` `26fb7ee69`. Run under Node: `продукты`, `їжа`, `храна` and `φαγητό` prefill as `Expenses:продукты` and similar, and fail `componentMustStartUppercase`; rustledger 0.24 accepts the capitalised forms. `expenses: food` prefills `Expenses:food`, which is also refused.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/account-picker-screen/create-account-prefill.ts:2, 20` (`LATIN_LETTER`).

## Fix

Capitalise any cased letter whose uppercase is a single character, still excluding `ß` and the Turkish dotted and dotless i. Trim each component before checking its first character.

**Acceptance:** `продукты` prefills `Expenses:Продукты`, `expenses: food` prefills `Expenses:Food`, and `ß` and `i` cases are unchanged. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w4/010 chose Latin-led on purpose without considering cased non-Latin scripts. w21/192 covers other scripts' validity. Searched `.pm/**` for capitalizeAccountPrefill, Cyrillic and Greek.
