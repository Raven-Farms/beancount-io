# Create Ledger's "Books begin" refuses Persian, Arabic-Indic and full-width digits, and the years 0001–0099

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate for `fa` users. This is the app's only typed date field. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `isHistoryStart` (`create-ledger-screen/setup-presets.ts:211`) matches with an ASCII-only `\d`. It then validates with `Date.UTC(year, …)`, which maps years 0–99 to 1900–1999, the trap w21/043 fixed elsewhere with `utcTime`. Under `tsx` with `TZ=Asia/Tehran`: `"2026-10-21"` passes, but `"۲۰۲۶-۱۰-۲۱"`, `"٢٠٢٦-١٠-٢١"`, `"２０２６-１０-２１"`, `"0050-03-01"` and `"0099-12-31"` are all refused. Meanwhile the app shows a `fa` user today's date as `۲۱ اکتبر ۲۰۲۶`.

Code-level at `main` `c8be51ed8`. Not run natively, because ledger creation is outside the QA write contract.

## Fix

Normalize `\p{Nd}` digits to ASCII before matching, as w21/230 proposes for amounts. Validate the calendar day with `utcTime` from `common/calendar-date`, and submit the ASCII value.

**Acceptance:** Persian, Arabic-Indic and full-width dates are accepted and submitted as ASCII, and `0050-03-01` validates as year 50.

## Dedupe

This is the mobile counterpart of w4/325 (dashboard) and w21/230 (amounts). w21/051, 107, 238 and 330 differ. Searched `.pm/**` for "Books begin", historyStart, isHistoryStart, "Persian digit", Arabic-Indic and Date.UTC.
