# Weekly and daily budget charts label periods as US month/day (`3/2`) in every language

**Severity:** minor. In fr, es, pt, ca, nl, uk and bg, `3/2` reads as 3 February, and any day of 12 or less is ambiguous. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

Code-level at `main` `89ddcfe9e`.

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:40-41` returns `${month}/${day}` for weekly and daily intervals. That feeds both the chart axis (`budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx:167-176`) and the spoken span (`budget-labels.ts:160-163`). Monthly and yearly labels are fine.

## Fix

Format with `Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { month: "numeric", day: "numeric", timeZone: "UTC" })`, or the app's date helper with the current locale, so de gives `2.3.`, fr `02/03` and en `3/2`.

**Acceptance:** unit cases for en, fr and de on `2026-03-02`. The Budget chart's x-axis in Español reads day-first.

Searched `.pm/**` for periodAxisLabel, "weekly budget", M/D and month/day. w3/189 (English digit grouping) is a separate, deliberate choice.
