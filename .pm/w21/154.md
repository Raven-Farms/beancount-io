# On iOS, deleting and reinstalling the app silently signs the previous account back in from the Keychain

**Severity:** moderate (privacy). Users expect deleting the app to remove their sign-in, but a reinstall, or another person setting up the same phone, opens straight onto the old account for up to a year (the refresh-token lifetime). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: iOS deletes the app's sandbox (AsyncStorage) on uninstall but keeps its Keychain items. The session, refresh token included, lives in SecureStore (Keychain) and is restored at launch, and nothing detects a first launch after install.

Code-level at `main` `bca6a678c`. Not exercised natively, to avoid uninstalling the dev build mid-run.

## Where

- `mobile/src/common/vars/session.ts:12-17`
- `mobile/src/common/apollo/secure-session-storage.ts`
- `mobile/src/common/providers/splash-provider/splash-provider.tsx:56-84`

## Fix

Write an install marker to AsyncStorage on first launch. At startup, if the marker is missing, delete the Keychain session (and attempt a best-effort revoke) before restoring anything, then write the marker.

**Acceptance:** sign in, `xcrun simctl uninstall` the app, reinstall and launch: it opens on Welcome, signed out. A normal relaunch keeps the session.
