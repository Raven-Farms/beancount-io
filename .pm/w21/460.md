# Payees that differ only by internal whitespace ("Whole  Foods") become two near-identical merchants, and search finds only one

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchantKey` (`mobile/src/common/merchant-key.ts:8-9`) and the grouping key in `aggregate-payees.ts:133` are `trim().toLowerCase()`, and `filterMerchants` (`:190-195`) matches plain substrings, so double spaces from bank imports split one merchant in two.

Code-level at `main` `6854ec3a0`. In the reviewer's tsx run, rows `Whole Foods` ×5, `Whole  Foods` ×3 and `WHOLE FOODS` ×1 give two merchants (6 and 3 transactions), and `filterMerchants(m, "whole foods")` returns only "Whole Foods". Not exercised on the device. Recurrence sees part of the history (the w21/169 failure class), and search hides one row.

## Fix

Collapse `\s+` to a single space in `merchantKey`, in the aggregate key, and in both the search needle and haystack; land it with w21/410's NFC change. Detail already queries the raw spellings, so the merge carries through.

**Acceptance:** the fixture yields one "Whole Foods" with 9 transactions, which a search for "whole foods" finds. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w21/093 is trailing whitespace, w21/410 is NFC/NFD, and w21/046 and w21/169 are case. Searched `.pm/**` for whitespace, "double space", collapse and merchantKey.
