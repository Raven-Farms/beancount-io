# After w21/172, a brand written next to Chinese or Japanese text no longer matches ("Amazon购物", "Netflix会员", "Uberタクシー")

**Severity:** minor (regression). zh is a shipped locale. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: 172 made any `\p{L}` a word character, but Han, kana and Thai don't put spaces between words, so an ASCII brand touching them no longer sits on a word boundary.

Code-level at `main` `61ad1916c`. `matchBrand` under Node, before and after 172:
- "Amazon购物": amazon.com → null
- "京东Amazon旗舰店": amazon.com → null
- "Netflix会员": netflix.com → null
- "支付宝Starbucks": starbucks.com → null
- "Uberタクシー": uber.com → null

## Root cause

`mobile/src/common/brand-matcher/index.ts:222` (`WORD_CHAR = /[\p{L}\p{N}\p{M}]/u`), used at `:243-245`.

## Fix

Count as word characters only letters from space-separated scripts (for example `\p{Script=Latin}`, Greek, Cyrillic), plus digits and combining marks, or exempt Han, Hiragana, Katakana and Thai. Keep 172's "Café Amazonía" case passing.

**Acceptance:** the five cases match again, and "Café Amazonía" still doesn't. Unit cases are added.
