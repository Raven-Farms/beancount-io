# Create Ledger: a liability's "Amount owed" can't express a card in credit (500 and −50 both become debt), and Review shows the opposite sign to the file

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes, needs a product call. Owner: mobile and backend-v2. A3: `create-ledger-screen.tsx:427-429, 898-903` and `backend-v2/src/features/ledger/setup/build-ledger.ts:100-106` (`ledgerOpeningAmount`). w21/104 negates a plain liability amount and keeps an explicit negative as written.

Production (2026-10-22, read-only `previewLedgerSetup`, PERSONAL with `Liabilities:CreditCard` KNOWN): amount "500" writes `balance Liabilities:CreditCard -500 USD` (owed 500, correct), and amount "-50" writes `balance Liabilities:CreditCard -50 USD`, also owed. Under the field's "Amount owed (optional)" label, −50 should mean the card is 50 in credit (+50). Review shows "Opening balance: Liabilities:CreditCard 500", while the file has −500.

## Fix

Under an "Amount owed" label, negate every typed amount (so −50 means +50 in credit). Have Review show the amount in the label's terms ("Owed $500.00", or "In credit $50.00").

**Acceptance:** 500 previews as −500 and −50 as +50, and Review reads them as owed and in credit. Unit and parity cases are added.

**Blocked 2026-10-22** — Still wanted, but it is a contract decision, as the note itself says. `ledgerOpeningAmount` is shared by REST, GraphQL and MCP, and today an explicit negative is taken as the ledger's own sign (an API client or agent that sends `-30.50` for a card gets `-30.50`; w21/104 chose that). Negating every typed amount under an "Amount owed" label flips the meaning for every existing client of those surfaces and of the saved app builds that send a typed `-` today, and Review's wording ("Owed" / "In credit") needs new strings in every language.

**Unblock:** the owner decides whether the setup API keeps "explicit negative = ledger sign" (and only the app's form negates, sending the ledger sign) or changes the API contract for all three surfaces; then it is a mobile change plus, for the second choice, a backend-v2 change with parity cases and a deploy. Who can clear it: the repository owner (API contract and product decision).
