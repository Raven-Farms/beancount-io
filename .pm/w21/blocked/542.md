**Blocked 2026-10-22** — Still wanted, but the acceptance needs a chart-design choice and a visual check this environment cannot give. The dashed line can sit at the opening balance only if the y-domain includes it: `computeDomain` sees the plotted points alone, so in the note's fixture (flat at 15,000 after a 10,000 opening) the opening is off the plot. Widening the domain changes every balance chart's scale (an ALL window opens at zero) and has to be carried through the range-to-range morph (`buildMorphFrames` blends two domains and pins its end frames to the resting render, and the dashed `Line` is drawn outside the morph), which needs each previous series' baseline. The other way out — keep the dashed line on the first point and make VoiceOver's "From" figure the opening balance the "since start" change uses — leaves the sighted mismatch the note describes.

**Unblock:** the owner picks the design (widen the domain to the opening, with the baseline threaded through the morph; or measure the change from the first plotted month everywhere; or keep the dashed line and fix only the spoken "From"), and a simulator pass can compare ranges; then it is a mobile-only change. Who can clear it: the repository owner (design decision).
