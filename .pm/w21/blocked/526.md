# Transactions, Account detail and Merchant detail drop back to the newest 20 rows after a return to the app or any write, losing the reader's place (and a stale "Couldn't load more" footer)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `0b838cf02`; proven with the real Apollo 3.14.1 client, the app's default options and its own `refreshOnForeground` and `invalidateLedgerData`):
- These lists page with `fetchMore` + `updateQuery` from offset 0, 20 rows at a time: `transactions-screen.tsx:156-170, 212`, `account-detail-screen.tsx:343` and `merchant-detail-screen.tsx:384`.
- The foreground refresh (`common/apollo/refresh-on-foreground.ts:19`) and every write's `invalidateLedgerData` (`invalidate-ledger.ts:181`) re-run them with their original variables, which replaces the merged list with page 1.

Proof: 60 rows after two pages, 20 after a foreground refresh, 60 again after scrolling, and 20 after an `entries` invalidation.

Scenario A: scroll Transactions to about 60 rows, leave the app for more than 30 s, and come back (or delete a row from its detail). The list shrinks to the newest 20, and the view jumps and pages again.

Scenario B, a sibling of w21/465: the paging-failure state (`loadMoreFailedFor`, `transactions-screen.tsx:196-204, 266`; `account-detail-screen.tsx:329-340, 416`) is cleared only by pull-to-refresh. After an offline "Couldn't load more entries", a foreground refresh or any save resets the list but keeps the footer error, and scrolling never pages again until Retry.

Not run natively: the driver can't scroll to page.

## Fix

Refetch the loaded depth (or keep the merged rows and refresh them in place) when the first page is re-run, and clear the paging-failure state whenever the first page reloads. This is the same pattern as w21/431 and w21/512 on Notifications.

**Acceptance:** after a foreground refresh or a write, the loaded rows stay (with fresh data), and a stale failure footer is gone. Tests cover both.

## Dedupe

Same pattern as w21/431 and w21/512 (Notifications), and w21/465 (pull-to-refresh only). Distinct from w21/419. Searched `.pm/**` for fetchMore, loadMoreFailedFor, "first page" and "loses its place".

---

**Blocked 2026-10-22** — Still wanted, but the fix is a design choice that reaches three lists and cannot be verified here. The lists page with `fetchMore` + `updateQuery` over one cached query, so any re-run of the first page (foreground refresh, every write's invalidation) replaces the merged rows. The ways out differ in cost and risk: a Apollo field policy that splices pages by offset (and still falls back to replacing when the total changed, which every add or delete does); refetching the loaded depth after the first page lands (a visible shrink-then-grow, and a restore loop to guard against); or accepting the drop to page 1 as Notifications does (w21/431, w21/512). Scenario B (the stale "Couldn't load more" footer surviving a foreground refresh or save) is a smaller change that can ship on its own once the owner picks the direction for A, since it is the same reset point.

The driver cannot scroll to page, so none of these can be checked on the simulator.

**Unblock:** the owner picks the approach for A (field policy, refetch to depth, or accept page 1), or confirms Scenario B alone is enough for now; then it is a mobile-only change. Who can clear it: the repository owner (product and architecture decision).
