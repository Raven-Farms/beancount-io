# The dashboard's Budget chart still takes an income target's direction from the amount in effect, so a zeroed income target runs on expense rules (w21/394 mobile half)

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: dashboard. A3: split from w21/394, whose mobile half shipped. Zeroing an income budget stores `"0"`, whose sign says nothing, and the dashboard's `budget-chart-card.tsx` (`dashboard/src/features/ledger-data/budget/`) still reads its direction from the sign of the amount in effect today, as mobile's `budgetDirection` did. Income bars then draw negative and a missed month reads as on the favourable side.

Mobile now takes the direction from the newest history entry that has a sign (`groupDirection`, `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts`) and falls back to the amount in effect only when every entry is zero.

## Fix

Apply the same rule in the dashboard's budget chart and its stats: the newest non-zero entry in the account's budget history decides the direction; a budget that was only ever zero is an expense cap. Add a unit case with `-3000` followed by `0`.

**Acceptance:** an income target of −3000 from January, zeroed in October, draws positive bars and marks a month that earned less than 3,000 as missed.
