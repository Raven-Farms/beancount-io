# Create Ledger: an invalid amount hidden by "Decide later" silently blocks Next

**Severity:** moderate. The user is stuck at step 2 with no message. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/106 hides the amount field under "Decide later" but doesn't clear the amount. w21/105's validate-all Next still runs the `openingAmount` refine on the hidden value, and its error renders only when an account is chosen.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `3e830700b`, with nothing created. Create Ledger → step 2 → Liabilities:CreditCard → amount **"12."** → **Decide later** (the field disappears) → Next. The form stays on **"Step 2 of 3"** with no visible message. Re-selecting CreditCard shows "12." still in the field. The draft value was then cleared.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/create-ledger-screen/create-ledger-screen.tsx:701` ("Decide later" changes only `openingAccount`), `:711` and `:744` (the field and error render only with an account), and `:218-222` (the refine).

## Fix

Clear `openingAmount` when "Decide later" is picked (as `resetOpening()` does), or skip the refine when no account is chosen.

**Acceptance:** the steps above advance to Review. A unit or harness test covers it.
