# An account holding USD plus a commodity shows dollars to 9–10 decimals in its journal ("+$7,422.647539491")

**Severity:** moderate. Every row change, "Balance:" line and day header of the account reads with 9–10 decimals from the buy onward, while the Accounts row shows $8,427.65. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile (plus a ledger rounding option). A3: the ledger multiplies units × cost with Decimal-style scale addition (8 + 2 = 10 places). w3/189's fix then displays money at whatever precision the string carries, up to 20 digits, with no cap to the currency's display scale for at-cost money views.

## Fix

**Acceptance:** the fixture's journal reads "+$7,422.65", "Balance: $8,422.65" and "$8,427.65", matching the Accounts row. Add a selector unit test.
