# The Budget chart's y-axis is still a fixed 50 px, so in German, Russian, Spanish and other languages its labels lose their leading digits

**Severity:** moderate: the clipped labels read as wrong values, not just cut text. This is a sibling chart that w21/177 left out. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `scrollable-axis-chart.tsx:54` defaults `axisWidth` to `LEFT_PADDING` (50), and `budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx:115` passes no `axisWidth`. The income/expense chart measures its labels (`income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:95`). Running `axisLabelWidth` on ticks 0–2500 gives:

| Language | Label | Width needed |
|---|---|---|
| de | `2,5 Tsd. $` | 92 px |
| ru, bg, uk | `2,5 тыс. $` and the like | 92 px |
| es | `2,5 mil $` | 84 px |
| fr | `2,5 k $` | 67 px |
| en | `$2.5K` | 50 px |

The labels are end-anchored at x=46 inside a 50 px `Svg`, so the leading digits are clipped.

**Native confirmation (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, German):** I set up a disposable fixture: `Expenses:QaBudAxis` with a monthly budget of 3000 USD and 2,500 USD spent in October. On Budget ▸ YTD, the y-axis reads, top to bottom, **`3 Tsd. $`, `5 Tsd. $`, `2 Tsd. $`, `5 Tsd. $`, `1 Tsd. $`, `500 $`, `0 $`**. The leading `2,` and `1,` are clipped, so 2,500 and 1,500 both read as "5 Tsd. $", above "2 Tsd. $". The first x-axis label is clipped to `!`, the w4/038 class. The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c` and the app returned to English. The auditor's `axisLabelWidth` run under `tsx` at `main` `24df51bf0` gives the same widths.

## Fix

Have `BudgetBarChart` compute `axisLabelWidth(...)`, pass it as `axisWidth`, and shrink `availableWidth` by the same amount.

**Acceptance:** in German, the Budget card axis reads `2,5 Tsd. $` in full.

This is a follow-up to w21/177 (done). w4/038 is the x-axis period label, w4/060 the unverified `MUSD` gutter, and w21/220 the plot width. Searched `.pm/**` for budget-bar-chart, LEFT_PADDING, axisLabelWidth and "clipped axis".
