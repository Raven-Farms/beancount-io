# The Budget chart's y-axis is still a fixed 50 px, so in German, Russian, Spanish and other languages its labels lose their leading digits

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. This is a sibling chart that w21/177 left out. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `scrollable-axis-chart.tsx:54` defaults `axisWidth` to `LEFT_PADDING` (50), and `budget-bar-chart-d3.tsx:115` passes no `axisWidth`. The income/expense chart measures its labels (`income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:95`). Running `axisLabelWidth` on ticks 0–2500 gives:

| Language | Label | Width needed |
|---|---|---|
| de | `2,5 Tsd. $` | 92 px |
| ru, bg, uk | `2,5 тыс. $` and the like | 92 px |
| es | `2,5 mil $` | 84 px |
| fr | `2,5 k $` | 67 px |
| en | `$2.5K` | 50 px |

The labels are end-anchored at x=46 inside a 50 px `Svg`, so the leading digits are clipped.

Code-level at `main` `24df51bf0` (the auditor ran the real `axisLabelWidth` under `tsx`). Not run natively.

## Fix

Have `BudgetBarChart` compute `axisLabelWidth(...)`, pass it as `axisWidth`, and shrink `availableWidth` by the same amount.

**Acceptance:** in German, the Budget card axis reads `2,5 Tsd. $` in full.
