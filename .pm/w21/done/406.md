# Reports and account detail still render in the "USD" and English-root fallbacks until the read context loads; on a failed load Reports says "No transactions yet" (w21/262 and 266 sibling)

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/262 gated only Home and Accounts on `selectReadContextState`. `reports-screen.tsx:72` still uses `getPrimaryCurrency(currencies)` ("USD" while loading), `:75-76` falls back to the "Income" and "Expenses" root names, and `:163-168` takes `isLoading` from the statement only. `:279` passes only `statementLoading` to the Recent transactions card (w21/266). `account-detail-screen.tsx:131-136` has the same currency fallback. Under `tsx`, `getPrimaryCurrency([])` gives `"USD"`, and on a ledger with renamed roots (Gastos/Ingresos), `hasAnyAccountTransaction(journal, ["Income","Expenses"])` is `false`, so 6M shows 0 rows instead of 2. So on a EUR ledger the Reports charts render in USD (mostly $0) until the context arrives, and indefinitely, with no error, if it fails. A renamed-root ledger's Recent transactions reads "No transactions yet", which 266's Acceptance rules out.

Code-level at `main` `c9c36d8e7`, from the regression audit.

## Fix

Gate Reports and account detail on `selectReadContextState` as Home does. Pass `statementLoading || contextState === "loading"` to the card, and show `CardLoadFailure` for a failed context read.

**Acceptance:** a EUR or renamed-root ledger never shows USD or "No transactions yet" on Reports while its context is loading or after it fails.

## Dedupe

This is a sibling of w21/262 and 266 (done); w21/073 and 395 differ. Searched `.pm/**` for getPrimaryCurrency, moneyRoots and "read context".

Fixed. Reports and account detail are now held on the ledger's read context, as Home and Accounts are since w21/262. Reports treats `selectReadContextState` like the statement: its cards stay skeletons while the context loads (so a EUR ledger never draws USD charts of 0 and a renamed-root ledger never reads "No transactions yet" off the English names), a failed context shows the failure card, and its one retry refetches the statement and the context together; Recent transactions is left out while that failure stands and gets `statementLoading` for the context too. Account detail holds its chart and list on the context (skeleton while it loads), turns a failed context into the chart's failure card and the list's "journal failed to load" message instead of rows in a blind USD, and its Retry also refetches the context. Tests (`reports-screen/__tests__/read-context-gate.test.ts`: the three context states, and both screens' gating, failure and retry wiring; the pinned first-load-failure source checks were updated; the wiring cases fail with the two screens reverted). Not run natively (needs a slow or failing read context).

Native recheck: not exercised. The fix changes what shows while the read context loads (a sub-second skeleton on this network) and when it fails (which can't be forced here). Reports and account detail render normally on the disposable ledger at `e057a4b1a`. It rests on its tests.
