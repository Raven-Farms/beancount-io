# Merchant detail shows a range for a single day: "Feb 10, 2026 – Feb 10, 2026"

**Severity:** low (cosmetic, on most merchants). **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `merchant-detail-screen/merchant-detail-screen.tsx:531-545` with `merchantDetailDateRange: "{{first}} – {{last}}"` (`en.ts:109`) has no case for first == last.

Native (iPhone 17e at `d820babc8`): Merchants ▸ NETFLIX (1 transaction) reads "Feb 10, 2026 – Feb 10, 2026" under "$15.49". Under `tsx`, a single pre-entered entry reads "Nov 1, 2026 – Scheduled Nov 1, 2026".

## Fix

Show one date when first equals last.

**Acceptance:** single-day merchants show one date. A unit case is added.
