# Home's budget panel now ranks an unpaid monthly salary at the top for most of the month, pushing out a nearly used-up expense cap

**Severity:** minor (a ranking judgment). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since w21/218, `select-budget-panel.ts:128-135` scores any row off target the wrong way as `[3, |progress − 100|]`. The panel prorates targets to today (`use-budget-panel.ts:142-145`), so a salary paid once a month reads 0% "Below target" until payday and scores 100. That beats every overspend under 200% of its prorated cap. Under `tsx`, `asOf 2025-01-15` with monthly Salary −5000 (0 received), Shopping 300 (200), Food 600 (288) and Transport 150 (70) yields Salary first, then Shopping and Food. Transport, at 96% of its cap, drops out of the three slots. Before `ac2d78e6e`, the income row was last.

Code-level at `main` `ebb642e45`. w21/218 explicitly wanted income that is behind to rank, so this is about *when* it counts.

## Fix

Don't treat an income shortfall as a miss until its period has ended, or past a threshold, or score income misses below expense overspends within the period.

**Acceptance:** mid-month with no salary yet, the panel's three slots go to the expense rows closest to or over their caps.

## Dedupe

This is a follow-up to w21/218 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for payday, "unpaid salary", "income target behind" and "income prorat".

Fixed. Home's budget panel no longer counts an income shortfall as a miss until the period is over. A target prorated to today is "behind" for most of a month for an income that arrives once, so an unpaid salary scored as the biggest miss (tier 3) and pushed an expense at 96% of its cap out of the three slots. An income row that is behind with its period still running (`periodEnd` after the day the panel measures against) now ranks in a new tier between spending near its cap and a beaten income target; once `periodEnd <= asOf` it is a miss again and ranks first by size, as w21/218 wanted, and with no day given the old behaviour is kept. In the note's scenario (mid-January: Salary 0 of 5,000, Shopping 200 of 300, Food 288 of 600, Transport 70 of 150) the three slots go to the expense rows. Tests (`select-budget-panel.test.ts`: the note's scenario, the period-over cases on the last day and after it, and a behind income still ranking above a beaten one; the first fails with the selector reverted; the w21/218 ranking test is unchanged because it measures on the period's last day). Deviation: a behind income still sits above a beaten one rather than being hidden, so it fills a free slot when there are fewer than three expense rows. Not run natively.
