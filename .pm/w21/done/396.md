# A parent category such as Expenses:Food can't be budgeted on mobile without first writing an `open` for it

**Severity:** moderate. "Food: 600 a month" across Groceries, Restaurants and Coffee is the most common budget shape. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `account-picker-screen.tsx:309-314` adds `withParentAccounts` only for `type === "filter"`; budget pickers get `openOn(opened)`, so `Expenses:Food` (never opened itself) is not offered. The only route is Create → Open Account, which writes a spurious `open`. Actuals already roll children up by prefix on the server (`interval-series.ts` `matchesPrefix`). Beancount does not require a custom directive's account to be open, and the dashboard's `AccountCombobox` takes free text. Under `tsx`, the budget picker lists the Groceries, Restaurants and Coffee children but not `Expenses:Food`.

Code-level at `main` `f628d4727`. w21/036's resolution noted that "budgets … still offer only opened accounts", but no item tracked it.

## Fix

Pass budget pickers through `withParentAccounts` too (`type === "filter" || type === "budget"`).

**Acceptance:** Budget → + → Account offers `Expenses:Food`, and the budget saves without an `open`.

## Dedupe

This is a follow-up to w21/036. Searched `.pm/**` for withParentAccounts, "parent account" and "Expenses:Food".

Fixed. The budget account picker now offers parent categories. `pickerAccounts` (`picker-sections.ts`) is the one place that decides what a picker lists: a filter keeps every opened account plus their parents, as before; a budget gets the accounts open today plus their parents, so `Expenses:Food` appears just before `Expenses:Food:Groceries`; a posting still gets only accounts open on its date and no parent. A budget on a parent needs no `open` (the server rolls children up by prefix), and since the parent is now in the list, a search for it no longer offers Create or writes a spurious `open`. Closed accounts are still left out of the budget list. Tests (`parent-accounts.test.ts`: the parent leads its children, a closed account stays out, a posting never gets a parent, the filter list is unchanged; the screen's call is pinned in it and in `account-close-dates.test.ts`; the new cases fail with the picker reverted). Not run natively.
