# Searching Transactions or Merchants for "iş" or "izmir" misses payees with a Turkish capital "İ" (sibling of w21/060 and w21/257)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile and ledger. A3:
- Transactions: the search text goes to the ledger service, which compiles it with `new RegExp(value, "i")` without the `u` flag (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/directive-filter.ts:174`). That never matches `i` against `İ`.
- Merchants: `mobile/src/common/merchant-key.ts:13` lowercases "İ" to `i` + U+0307, so a typed "iş" is never a substring (`filterMerchants`, `aggregate-payees.ts:198`).

Payee suggestions and account search already fold this (`match-suggestions.ts` `fold()`).

Native (iPhone 17e at `d7c0a5ea9`, disposable ledger, restored): with a payee "İşBank ATM", Transactions ▸ search "iş" shows "No transactions match your search or filters". Under `tsx`, `filterMerchants(..., "iş" / "izmir")` returns `[]`.

## Fix

Fold case with the same rule as `fold()` (lowercase, NFD, drop combining marks) on both sides: in `merchantKey` and in the ledger's search matcher (`u` flag plus folding, or a folded comparison).

**Acceptance:** "iş" and "izmir" find "İşBank ATM" and "İZMİR Kahve" in Transactions and Merchants. Unit cases are added on both sides.

## Dedupe

Siblings of w21/060 (account search) and w21/257 (payee suggestions). See w21/401. Searched `.pm/**` for "Turkish", "U+0307" and directive-filter.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — both sides fold the Turkish dotted capital. Ledger service: the filter matcher turns İ (U+0130) and i + U+0307 into plain i in both the pattern and the text it searches (`foldDottedI`), since neither the `i` nor the `iu` regex flag matches `i` against `İ`; the new case fails without the change. Mobile: `merchantKey` folds the same, so "iş" and "izmir" find "İşBank ATM" and "İZMİR Kahve" in Merchants (`merchant-key.test.ts`) and, once the ledger service is deployed, in Transactions (`directive-filter.test.ts`). One side effect: a manual recurring mark stored under the old key of a payee with İ no longer matches it. REST, GraphQL and MCP all filter in the ledger service.
