# A payee's usual source and target accounts are ranked independently, so the filled pair can be one never used together, even the same account twice

**Severity:** moderate. A no-op self-transfer, or a mismatched card and category, saves without warning. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/suggestion-utils.ts:346-352` (`deriveTwoPostingSuggestions`) ranks FROM and TO separately, and the result flows into `payee-seed.ts:45-47`. Under `tsx`, a PAYPAL history with 3 top-ups (`Assets:Bank:Checking → Assets:PayPal`) and 4 purchases from PayPal fills FROM = TO = **`Assets:PayPal`**; `validatePostings` accepts it, and the payload is `[-25.00 USD Assets:PayPal, 25.00 USD Assets:PayPal]`. An AMAZON history fills `Liabilities:Card → Expenses:Subscriptions`, a pair that never occurs in it.

Code-level at `main` `c06a57c9a`. Not run natively; it needs that payee history in the shared ledger.

## Fix

Rank (from, to) pairs and fill the most common pair. Refuse a seed, and ideally a save, where the source equals the target.

**Acceptance:** the PAYPAL history fills `Assets:PayPal → Expenses:…`, never the same account twice.

Fixed. `deriveTwoPostingSuggestions` now fills the most common (from, to) pair seen for a payee, with ties to the pair seen first, instead of ranking the two sides independently; the other accounts seen on each side stay as chips, in frequency order. A self-transfer pair in history (the same account twice) is never chosen as the fill, and `seedPostingsFromPayee` refuses to seed the same account on both sides.
