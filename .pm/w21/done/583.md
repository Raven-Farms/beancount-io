# The "Saved, with a new ledger error" alert's dismiss button reuses the `close` key, which is the Close *directive*: zh shows "关户" (close the account), es "Cierre", fr "Clôture"

**Severity:** minor (a scary label right after a save that broke the ledger). **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile.
