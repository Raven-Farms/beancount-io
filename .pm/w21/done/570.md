# A currency exchange with a fee (or a rounding line) reads as "−$110.00" spent, though only the $1.00 fee left (the dashboard shows the fee)

**Severity:** minor (wrong amount on the row, the detail and the merchant). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/utils/transaction-display-utils.ts:172-197` treats an exchange between your own accounts as neutral only when *every* posting is Assets or Liabilities, so one fee or rounding posting falls through to the whole USD side. The dashboard (`dashboard/src/features/reports/overview/lib/transaction-summary.ts:143-153`) reports only the fee.

Native (iPhone 17e at `0c0609c4e`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): `Assets:Cash -110.00 USD`, `Assets:QaEurBank 100.00 EUR @ 1.09 USD`, `Expenses:QaFees 1.00 USD`. The Transactions row reads "−$110.00". Under `tsx`, the same exchange without the fee reads a neutral "$109.00", and an `Income:Rounding -0.01` line in place of the fee also gives "−$110.00". Merchants' header for that payee sums only Expenses and Income, so it reads "$1.00" above a "−$110.00" row.

## Fix

Exclude Expenses and Income postings from the own-accounts test, and show the transfer as neutral with the fee as the signed amount (or "Exchange · fee $1.00"), as the dashboard does.

**Acceptance:** that entry reads as an exchange with a $1.00 fee, not −$110.00, on the row, the detail and the merchant. Unit cases are added.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — `selectTransactionAmount` now recognises a priced exchange between your own accounts that also carries Expenses or Income lines (a fee, a rounding line): the row, the detail hero and the merchant read only what those lines left (a $1.00 fee is "-$1.00"; a rounding income line is the small amount that came in), and lines that cancel out, or sit in two currencies, leave it the neutral exchange amount. The price (`@ 1.09 USD`) is the test, since that is how such an entry balances: a card purchase that also earns miles balances without one and still reads as the money spent (the existing case fails if the test is dropped). Cases in `transaction-display-utils.test.ts` (`mobile`).
