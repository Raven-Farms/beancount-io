# A cancelled every-six-months bill stays red "Overdue" at the top of Recurring for about 17 months (a quarterly one for about 8)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/267's lapse rule (`detect-recurrence.ts:77` `LAPSE_PERIODS = 3`, `:524-527`) scales with the cadence, so the new long cadences from w21/269 take years to lapse. Under `tsx` (today 2026-10-21), semi-annual payments from 2023-01-10 to 2025-01-10 give `nextExpected 2025-07-10, isOverdue: true, isLapsed: false`, lapsing only around 2027-01-04. w21/267's original symptom, stopped bills pinned in red above live ones, comes back.

Code-level at `main` `c9c36d8e7`.

## Fix

Cap the lapse threshold in days, for example `min(3 × gap, ~120 days)` or `max(one period, 90 days)`, and add semi-annual and bimonthly cases to `merchant-lapsed.test.ts`.

**Acceptance:** a semi-annual series last paid 2025-01-10 reads "Last paid" by 2025-11, not Overdue.
