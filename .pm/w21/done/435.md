# A Create Ledger draft resumed on Review silently drops the opening balance, and Create builds the ledger without it

**Severity:** moderate. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the draft restores `step` 2, Review (`create-ledger-screen.tsx:280`), keeps `openingAccount` but blanks `openingAmount` (`create-ledger-draft.ts:45, 86`), so `openingSelection(...)` returns `null` (`:389-395`). Under `tsx`, a Review line `{Liabilities:CreditCard, 500}` restores to Review with no opening line and no `setup.accounts`. The user returns to Review, taps Create, and gets a ledger with no opening balance and no balance assertion, without any notice.

Code-level at `main` `9bd067834`. Not driven natively.

## Fix

When a restored draft has an `openingAccount`, resume on the form step and mark the amount for re-entry, or show "Opening balance: re-enter" on Review and block Create until it's entered or set to "Decide later".

**Acceptance:** a draft left on Review with a card balance never creates a ledger without that balance silently.

## Dedupe

w21/053 and 106 are the designs this falls between. Searched `.pm/**` for resume, Review, openingAmount and "draft amount".

Resolved. `restoreCreateLedgerDraft` now reopens a draft on the form step when it has an opening account, because the amount is never stored and Review would have built the ledger with no balance and no assertion, silently. The account choice is kept and the amount field is empty, so the person re-enters it (or clears the account for "decide later") before Create can be reached. A draft with no opening account still resumes on Review. Tests cover both and the first fails against the old restore. Deviation: no native run; the form's own validation of an account with an empty amount is what stops Create from there, and was not changed.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `bc0682f28`): in Create Ledger I chose Assets:Cash as the opening account with an amount of 2, went to Review (which showed "Opening balance: Assets:Cash 2"), and left the screen with the back arrow without tapping Create. Reopening + New resumed on Step 2 of 3 (the form), with Assets:Cash still selected and the opening balance empty, not on Review. Fixed. The draft was reset to "Decide later"; no ledger was created.
