# Mobile shows dates in years 0001–0099 as 1901–1999 (mobile counterpart of w4/277, w4/283 and w4/m38)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20–30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: w4/m38 made the backend accept directive dates in years 0001–0099, and w4/277 and w4/283 fixed the dashboard's displays and periods, which were shifting them by 1900. The mobile date helpers build dates with `Date.UTC(year, …)`, which applies the same legacy two-digit-year mapping. An entry dated `0050-03-01` reads as **"March 1, 1950"** in the app.

Environment: `main` at `994d5d671`. Verified by running the formatter logic of `mobile/src/common/date-format.ts` under Node 24:

| input | `formatLedgerDate` | expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0050-03-01` | March 1, **1950** | March 1, 50 |
| `0099-12-31` | December 31, **1999** | December 31, 99 |
| `0100-01-01` | January 1, 100 | (control) |
| `2026-08-28` | August 28, 2026 | (control) |

## Root cause

`Date.UTC(year, …)` and `new Date(year, …)` treat years 0–99 as 1900–1999. Call sites:

- `mobile/src/common/date-format.ts:50,74`: `formatLedgerDate`, `formatLedgerDateShort` (every list, detail and merchant date).
- `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:171`: price-age arithmetic.
- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:91`: budget period math.
- `mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:77,121,131` and `payee-series.ts:82`: recurrence and series.
- `mobile/src/screens/open-account-screen/open-date.ts:16`: local `new Date(y, m, d)`.

## Fix

Add one helper, `utcDate(year, monthIndex, day)`, that builds `new Date(Date.UTC(2000, monthIndex, day))` and then calls `setUTCFullYear(year)` (or `setUTCFullYear(year, monthIndex, day)` directly), and use it at every call site above. Port the dashboard's regression tests (w4/277, w4/283) to mobile with `0050-03-01`, `0099-12-31`, `0001-01-01` and a 4-digit control.

**Acceptance:** `formatLedgerDate("0050-03-01", "en")` returns "March 1, 50" (locale-appropriate year with no 1900 offset). Recurrence and budget math give the same results for a series shifted into year 0050 as for 2050. The unit tests fail on the old construction.

## Dedupe

Dashboard: w4/277 (CSV import display), w4/283 (statement periods), w4/m38 (backend acceptance), all done. Searched `.pm/**` for early-year, 1900 offset and `Date.UTC` with mobile. No mobile item. Not exercised natively: entering such a date through the app's date picker. The display path is shared by every screen.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-21** — A new `mobile/src/common/calendar-date.ts` builds dates with `setUTCFullYear`/`setFullYear`, which take the year as given: `utcTime`, `utcDate` and `localDate` keep `Date.UTC`'s month/day overflow. It replaces the 1900-offset constructions at every listed call site: `formatLedgerDate`/`formatLedgerDateShort`, valuation's day arithmetic, budget period epochs, recurrence day parsing and month math, payee-series month shifting, and Open Account's local open date. **Also fixed, found by the new tests:** four formatters wrote years before 1000 unpadded (`50-03-01`, not a ledger date): the budget ISO formatter, recurrence's `formatLedgerDay`, `shiftLedgerMonths`, and `getFormatDate`, which writes picker dates into entries. They now pad to four digits; an invalid date still reads `NaN-NaN-NaN`. `calendar-date.test.ts` covers construction for 0050/0001/0099 and overflow, "March 1, 50" / "December 31, 99" / "January 1, 1" / the 2026 control, the short form, an early-year open date, day counts and month-end clamping matching 2050, four-digit writing, and an 0050 budget prorating to exactly the 2050 value (322.58). Reverting the call sites fails 6 cases. Not exercised natively: picking such a date in the app's date picker. Native gates pass: format, lint, typecheck, `yarn test:unit` (2507 passed, 1 skipped). Shipped in the commit that moved this note (`mobile`).
