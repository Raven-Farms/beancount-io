# German, Dutch and Spanish mix formal and informal "you" across the mobile app

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. Owner: mobile (translations). A2/A3: in German the same Settings screen says "Teilen **Sie** … helfen **Sie**" and, two rows below, "Gefällt es **dir**? Hinterlasse …". Switching register mid-screen reads as unedited machine output to native speakers, in locales the app markets in the store.

Environment: `main` at `ab6f2adf7`+, dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0). Settings → Sprache → Deutsch (restored to English afterwards).

## Evidence

- **On device (German Settings):** `inviteFriends` copy, "Teilen Sie dieses professionelle Finanzmanagement-Tool und helfen Sie anderen …", sits above `reviewApp`, "Gefällt es dir? Hinterlasse eine Bewertung :)".
- **Counts in `mobile/src/translations/`:**
  - `de.ts`: **39** lines in *Sie/Ihr/Ihnen* (delete account, filters, receipts, Create Ledger, Open Account …) and **13** lines in *du/dein/dir/dich*: `discoveryNoMatches` (39), the read-only notice (50), `reviewApp` (105), server settings (150, 170), the file editor's new-error warning (481), Ask AI (546–566), and Profile's username hint (636).
  - `nl.ts`: informal *je/jouw* throughout (33 lines), except **u/uw** at 115 (the RTL restart prompt) and 653 (a Create Ledger template).
  - `es.ts`: informal *tú* throughout, except the formal "Registre una compra y entienda su efecto…" at 660 (Create Ledger template).
  - `fr.ts`: consistently *vous*, no mix (control).

## Fix

Choose one register per locale and rewrite the outliers, ideally the majority register so the change is smallest: German *Sie* (rewrite the 13 *du* strings), Dutch *je* (2 strings), Spanish *tú* (1 string). If the product prefers informal German (the newer Ask AI and Profile strings use *du*), flip the 39 instead. Record the choice in `mobile/AGENTS.md`'s translation section so new strings follow it. Optionally add a unit test under `src/translations/__tests__/` that fails when `de.ts` contains both `\bSie\b` and `\b(du|dein\w*|dir|dich)\b` outside an allowlist.

**Acceptance:** each of de, nl and es uses one form of address throughout. German Settings shows a consistent register between "Freunde einladen" and the review row. The guard test (if added) passes, and fails when one outlier is reintroduced.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` (including `done/`) for formal, register, *Sie*/*du* and Siezen/duzen. w0/m1 and w0/m3 (English copies in non-English catalogs), w4/155 (untranslated OAuth strings) and w4/063 (iOS locale declaration) are about missing translations, not register. No open item.

## Not verified

Native-speaker review of which register to standardize on. Other locales (pt, ru, uk, sk, ca, bg) were not scanned for T–V mixing.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-20** — Each locale now uses its majority register: German *Sie*, Dutch *je*, Spanish *tú*.

- **German:** the note's 13 *du* lines plus about 17 more strings in the same register with no pronoun: informal imperatives such as *Prüfe … versuche*, *Gib … ein*, *Wähle*, *Speichere*, *Lege … fest*, *Verwende*. They are in examples, discovery, server settings, sign-in errors, budgets, Ask AI, Profile and the file editor's new-error warning.
- **Dutch:** the RTL restart prompt and the Personal preset.
- **Spanish:** the Create Ledger review text, the amount prompt, all eight preset outcomes (the note named one), and three older *ingrese/agregue* prompts.

`src/translations/__tests__/form-of-address.test.ts` scans de, nl and es for the other register's pronouns and the imperatives the catalogs had used, and fails when one is reintroduced (checked by restoring German `reviewApp`). `mobile/AGENTS.md`'s translation section records the per-locale choice. Mobile gates pass: format, tsc, eslint, knip, and 2399 unit tests. Not reviewed by a native speaker, and pt, ru, uk, sk, ca and bg were not scanned. Shipped in `4067c2c0b` (`mobile`).
