# Typing an account's exact name doesn't rank it first once a longer match has been picked before ("rent" puts Expenses:Rental-Car above Expenses:Rent)

**Severity:** low (the top row is easy to mis-pick). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `e9f772e72`, proven under `tsx`): `common/account-search.ts:136-141` scores a whole-segment or whole-name match the same as a prefix match, and `:204-209` then sorts by usage before length. One past pick of `Expenses:Rental-Car` puts it above `Expenses:Rent` for "rent", and `Expenses:Food:Groceries` above `Expenses:Food` for "Expenses:Food". Without that usage, the exact match ranks first. It affects the posting, filter and budget pickers.

## Fix

Give an exact last-segment or full-name match a higher score than a prefix match, ahead of usage.

**Acceptance:** "rent" and "Expenses:Food" put the exact account first regardless of usage. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w21/060, w21/255, w21/452 and w21/569 cover matching, not ranking. Searched `.pm/**` for account-search together with rank.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — mobile's `filterAccounts` (`common/account-search.ts`) now ranks an account the query names exactly ahead of score and usage: first its whole name, then its last segment, under the same smartcase and NFC rules as the match. "rent" puts `Expenses:Rent` above a much-used `Expenses:Rental-Car`, and "Expenses:Food" and "food" put `Expenses:Food` above a much-used `Expenses:Food:Groceries`, in the posting, filter and budget pickers. Multi-word queries keep the score order. Unit case in `common/__tests__/account-search.test.ts` (fails without the change) (`mobile`). Not run natively.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e): not exercised. It needs stored pick history for a longer match (a past pick of `Expenses:Rental-Car`), which comes from saved entries, and this pass didn't save transactions to build it. The fix's unit cases cover the exact-match ranking.
