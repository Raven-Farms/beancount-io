# Tapping a parent's "(own)" row opens the parent's full page: header, chart and journal include all its children ($28,100 for a $10,000 own row)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `5a71c68fb`, proven under `tsx`): `components/account-table/flatten-rows.ts:124-125` gives the own row the parent's account, `account-table.tsx:408` navigates with it, and account detail always asks for `with_children: true` (`account-detail-screen.tsx:350`, `use-account-journal.ts:26`). With `Assets:Bank` holding $10,000 of its own and `Assets:Bank:Checking` $18,100, tapping "Bank (own) $10,000.00" opens "Bank · $28,100.00" with Checking's entries mixed in, the same page as the "Bank" row above it.

Not run natively: the disposable ledger has no own postings on a parent.

## Fix

Pass an `own` flag to account detail (`with_children: false` for the journal and series, and the own balance for the header), or don't navigate from the own row.

**Acceptance:** the own row's page reads $10,000.00 and lists only `Assets:Bank`'s own entries. A test covers the flag.

## Dedupe

See w21/174 and w21/451 (the own row's existence). Searched `.pm/**` for "(own)" together with tap and with_children.
