# A Create Ledger retry across local midnight with a blank "Books begin" mints a new requestId and reports "already exists" (w21/436 with w21/434)

**Severity:** low (narrow). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `setup-presets.ts:229` fills a blank field with `defaultHistoryStart(now)` on every call, so the date is part of the fingerprinted request. Code-level at `main` `18b56a778`, from the reviewer's tsx run (TZ America/Los_Angeles) of the real `setupFields` and `isNewCreateRequest`: a send at 23:59:50 carries `2026-10-21`, and the retry at 00:00:10 carries `2026-10-22`, giving a new id (`true`); before 436 the id was kept. A retry after a lost response is then reported as "A ledger with this name already exists" instead of returning the ledger that was made.

## Fix

On entering Review, write `defaultHistoryStart()` into a blank field once (`setValue`), so the preview, the create and the draft carry the same date.

**Acceptance:** a retry across midnight reuses the id. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Interaction of w21/436 and w21/434 (both done). Searched `.pm/**` for defaultHistoryStart and requestId together with midnight.

Fixed. Leaving the form step for Review now writes the settled Books begin back into the field (`setValue("historyStart", setupFields(getValues()).historyStart)`), so a blank field becomes today once and the preview, the create and a retry past local midnight carry the same date and the same fingerprint, keeping the requestId. Unit cases show a blank field moving with the clock and a written-back one holding across midnight; a source check pins the write-back; reverting the screen fails the suite.
