# Once anything is Recurring, every merchant below appears to sit under the "RECURRING" heading

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15–25 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: Merchants pins recurring payees in a titled section, then lists every merchant again in a section with no header. With one recurring payee, the screen reads "RECURRING" followed by ACME CORP twice, CITY BUS, LANDLORD LLC and the rest, as if all ten were recurring.

Environment: `main` at `ef7004ade`, dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0), English. QA account, synthetic ledger `mcp-agent-eval`. The Recurring toggle is device-local, and it was switched back off afterwards.

## Repro

1. Drawer → **Merchants** → **ACME CORP** → switch **Recurring** on → Back.
2. The list shows the **RECURRING** heading, then `ACME CORP — Not enough history` (the pinned row), then `ACME CORP [RECURRING] 2 transactions`, then CITY BUS, LANDLORD LLC, TRADER JOES … with no heading between the pinned section and the full list.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/merchant-sections.ts:87-101` emits the general section with `titleKey: null`, and `merchants-screen.tsx:257-268` renders no header for it. That's fine when it is the only section, but ambiguous once the Recurring section is present.

## Fix

When a Recurring section exists, give the general section a title such as "All merchants" (new key in all locales), and keep it untitled when it is the only section. Keep pinned rows in the general list (they carry the RECURRING badge there), since the duplication is intentional.

**Acceptance:** with ACME CORP marked, the list reads RECURRING → ACME CORP, then ALL MERCHANTS → every payee. With nothing recurring there is no header. The `merchant-sections` unit tests assert the title only when both sections exist.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for Merchants section header, "All merchants" and Recurring section. The merchant items (w0/039, w2/042, w2/056, w3/112, w3/177, w21/007) cover other aspects. No item covers the untitled general section.
