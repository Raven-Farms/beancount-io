# Update Budget is offered on a closed account's card and writes a directive that never applies

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `6f6046fab`, proven under `tsx`): `budget-screen/budget-screen.tsx:150-153` passes `onUpdate` to every card. The account is locked on Update (`add-budget-screen.tsx:368`), which bypasses the picker's closed-account filter. For `Expenses:OldCar` closed 2026-06-15, Update ▸ 400 ▸ Done writes a directive. The card's current amount becomes 400, but its budget stays $0 after the close, so the edit does nothing.

## Fix

Hide Update on closed accounts' cards (or refuse with "This account was closed on Jun 15"), consistent with the picker and w21/045.

**Acceptance:** a closed account's card offers no Update. A test covers it.

## Dedupe

w21/398 kept the card visible but didn't cover Update; see w21/045. Searched `.pm/**` for "closed" together with "Update Budget".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — A budget card whose account has closed by today (`budgetHasEnded(endsOn, today)`, from `budgetEndsOn`) no longer offers Update; a close date still ahead, or a parent with open children, keeps it. Unit cases plus a source check on the card; the w21/398 source check follows the card's renamed `endsOn` line. Shipped in the commit that moves this note (`mobile`).
