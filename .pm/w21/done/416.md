# A change of exactly zero from a zero baseline reads "+$0.00" in the gain colour (empty ledger, liabilities-only Assets)

**Severity:** cosmetic. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:442-446, 473` draws neutral only when `shownPct?.flat`, and `changePercent(0, x)` is `null`. `:656-662` treat 0 ≥ 0 as favourable. Under `tsx`, an empty ledger's Net Worth and a liabilities-only ledger's Assets both read `$0.00 · +$0.00` in green, against w21/237's rule that a change that rounds to nothing is neutral.

Code-level at `main` `6d8c869b2`.

## Fix

Treat a change that rounds to zero as flat whatever the percentage: neutral colour, no "+".

**Acceptance:** an empty ledger's Home reads "$0.00 · $0.00" in neutral grey.
