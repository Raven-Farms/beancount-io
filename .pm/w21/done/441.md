# Home's balance charts are announced without saying which curve (Net Worth, Assets or Liabilities), and the stepping hint uses VoiceOver wording on Android

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `home-screen/components/account-charts-card.tsx:266-276` passes no `label`, so `chart-accessibility.ts:52` returns the bare summary ("From May 2026, $X, to Oct 2026, …") for all three curves; only account detail passes one. The hint `chartAdjustHint` (`translations/en.ts:264`) says "Swipe up or down…" on both platforms, which is VoiceOver's gesture, not TalkBack's. The Android part is inferred from the code.

Code-level at `main` `0150cff1d`.

## Fix

Pass `label={t(key)}` from `account-charts-card`, and use a platform-specific hint key in all 13 locales.

**Acceptance:** the Assets chart is announced as "Assets, From …", and Android uses TalkBack wording.

## Dedupe

This is a follow-up to w21/316. Searched `.pm/**` for chartSummary, chartAdjustHint and TalkBack.

Resolved. Home's three charts now pass their own title (`label={t(key)}`: Net worth, Assets, Liabilities) to the interactive chart, so the summary is announced as "Assets, From …" instead of three identical summaries, and the stepping hint is chosen by platform: iOS keeps "Swipe up or down…", Android gets the new `chartAdjustHintAndroid` ("Open the actions menu to step through the months"), translated into all 13 locales. Pins fail against the old chart and card. Deviation: the Android wording relies on TalkBack listing the chart's increment/decrement actions in its actions menu, which is how React Native exposes them; it was not tried on a device or emulator, nor was VoiceOver rerun.

Native recheck: not exercised. The chart's accessibility label is only reachable through VoiceOver or a testID lookup, and the chart has no testID; the Android wording needs an Android device. It rests on its pins.
