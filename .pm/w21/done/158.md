# Delete Account's confirmation phrase must match exactly, and a trailing space leaves Delete greyed out with no reason

**Severity:** minor. Tapping an iOS suggestion appends a space, and the button then stays disabled with nothing explaining why. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: both checks compare `text === t("deleteAccountConfirmPhrase")` with no trim. The validator returns a plain `false`, so the explanation slot that `TextInputModal` gained in w21/014 stays empty. The phrase is the same English literal in all 13 locales, so typing it is otherwise possible.

Code-level at `main` `f18be1458`.

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/setting/main-content.tsx:105` and `:308`, with `mobile/src/components/text-input-modal/index.tsx:133-138, 190-192`.

## Fix

Compare `text.trim()`, and return a translated reason from `validateInput` ("Type the phrase exactly as shown") when the text is non-empty but doesn't match.

**Acceptance:** the phrase followed by a trailing space enables Delete. A wrong phrase shows the reason. Nothing is submitted while testing.

## Dedupe

w4/043 and w3/184 touch this modal, but not this. Searched `.pm/**` for "trailing space", deleteAccountConfirmPhrase and "sudo delete".

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** Delete Account's modal and its confirm handler share `deleteConfirmationCheck` (`setting/delete-confirmation.ts`). It compares the trimmed text, so a trailing or leading space enables Delete. A non-empty mismatch returns the translated reason (`deleteAccountConfirmMismatch`, 13 locales), which `TextInputModal` shows under the field; an empty field disables without a message. Test: `setting/__tests__/delete-confirmation.test.ts` (the wiring case fails with the screen reverted). Nothing was submitted.
