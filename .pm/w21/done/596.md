# JPY, KRW and VND budgets: "Budget ¥968 · Actual ¥968 · Variance +¥0 · Above target", in red

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `f7f0c1de4`, proven under `tsx`): `budget-screen/selectors/select-budget-card-stats.ts:131-134` judges status at `budgetScale` (at least 2 decimals) against a budget prorated to cents (967.74). The card shows amounts at `budgetDisplayScale`, which is 0 for JPY, the split w21/425 introduced. A monthly 1500 JPY budget with ¥968 spent reads "Budget ¥968 · Actual ¥968 · Variance +¥0 · Above target" in the unfavourable colour, and Home's panel agrees.

Not run natively (it needs a JPY operating currency).

## Fix

Judge status and variance at the display scale, or round the prorated budget to the currency's minor units first.

**Acceptance:** that card reads "On target" with ¥0 variance. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Follows w21/425; see w21/070. Searched `.pm/**` for budgetDisplayScale and "Above target" together with "+¥0".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — a budget's status is judged at the scale the card shows: `budgetScale(history, currency)` floors at the currency's own minor unit (0 for JPY, KRW and VND) instead of cents, and `varianceStatus` no longer raises its tolerance to cents, so ¥968 spent against a prorated ¥967.74 is "on target" with ¥0 variance, on the card, in the charted periods (`selectPointFavorability` takes the currency) and in Home's panel (which goes through `selectBudgetCardStats`). Dollar budgets are still judged to the cent, and a real yen overspend is still above target. Cases in `select-budget-card-stats.test.ts` (`mobile`).

Native recheck (iPhone 17e): not exercised. It needs a JPY-operating ledger with a budget, and the disposable ledger's USD operating currency wasn't swapped. The fix's unit case covers judging status at the display scale.
