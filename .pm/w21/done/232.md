# A pasted amount with a leading space (" -12.50") is saved as negative but shown as positive, and the sign toggle then breaks it

**Severity:** minor. Amounts copied from bank statements and messages often carry a leading space. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the row decides its sign from `amountInput.startsWith("-")` on the raw text, while `parseExactAmount` trims first.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `7e42f850e`, with nothing saved:
1. New Transaction → first posting's amount → Select All → paste ` -12.50`.
2. The field reads **-12.50** in **green** with a **"+"** sign badge, and the auto leg reads **+12.50**, also green. Both legs look like inflows, and Remainder says ✓ $0.00.
3. Tap the "+" badge to flip the sign. The field becomes invalid: "Use digits and one decimal point, like 1234.56". The auto leg drops to 0.00, and Done disables. The toggle prepended a second "-" to the untrimmed text.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:284-287` (`isNegative = posting.amountInput.startsWith("-")`, `absValue = slice(1)`). `postings-utils.ts:42` trims before parsing.

## Fix

Trim the input in `updatePostingAmount` when it is stored, or test the sign on the trimmed text in both the row and the toggle.

**Acceptance:** pasting ` -12.50` shows a red −12.50 with a "−" badge, and the toggle turns it into 12.50. A unit test covers the leading-space case.
