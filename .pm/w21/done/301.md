# A new budget reads "Above target" the moment it's saved: its first period is budgeted from today but measured against the whole period's spending

**Severity:** moderate. This is the default flow (Date defaults to today), and Home's panel shares it. Every new monthly budget on an account with any spending this month starts deep in the red. **Estimate:** 30 minutes, plus an owner decision. Owner: mobile. A3: the budget for a period counts only days on or after the directive's date, capped at today. The actuals come from the whole period, including spending before the budget existed. `beforeFirstBudget` skips judging only when the budget is exactly 0, so the partial first period is judged.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `10857997b`, on the disposable QA ledger (restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`). The fixture `; qa-20261020-budnew` added 300 USD spent on 2026-10-05, plus `2026-10-21 custom "budget" Expenses:QaBudNew "monthly" 500 USD` (today).
- **Budget card:** "Monthly · USD · **Above target** · Budget **$16.13** · Actual $300.00 · Variance **+$283.87**", with a full red meter.
- **Home panel:** "$300.00 of $16.13, **1860% used**, Above target", ranked first.

A reviewer's Node run on the same case gives weekly 71.43 (420%), quarterly 5.43 (5525%) and yearly 1.37 (21898%); daily is correct. The dashboard has the same pre-budget actuals, but budgets the full month (about 177), so mobile is far worse.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-selectors.ts:373-382` (`asOf` cap) and `:411-429` (days from the directive's date).
- `use-budget-actuals.ts:84-85` (actuals from the period start).
- `select-budget-card-stats.ts:52-60` (`beforeFirstBudget` only when 0).
- `home-screen/selectors/select-budget-panel.ts:60-65`.

## Fix

Pick one and record the choice (w21/033 chose to judge partial first periods without considering this):
- (a) Budget the period from its start when it contains the first directive.
- (b) Show the period as "Starts {date}" and don't judge it, as w21/101 does.
- (c) Fetch the first period's actuals from the directive's date.

**Acceptance:** in the fixture, the card reads either about $500 budget / $300 actual / Below target, or a neutral "Starts" state, never 1860%. Unit cases cover monthly, weekly and quarterly.

## Dedupe

w21/033, 076, 101 and 102 (all done) are related. 102 clamps the *window* start, not the directive's date. Searched `.pm/**` for "partial first period", beforeFirstBudget, "effective date" and "16.13".

**Also (2026-10-21, from the fix audit):** `beforeFirstBudget` in `select-budget-card-stats.ts` compares the *period's end date*, so a budget whose first directive falls later in the current period (for example, dated today) is judged against $0.00, or a sliver, and reads "Above target". Whichever option this item chooses should cover that case too.

Fixed, choosing option (a), recorded here as the decision: the first directive back-fills the period it falls in. `budgetForInterval` treats the first directive as effective from the start of the chart period that contains it (when it is already in effect, i.e. not after the period's budgeted end), so a budget set on the 21st is the month's budget and is judged against the month's spending. Later directives still start on their own date (a mid-period raise is prorated as before), periods before the first directive stay at 0 and unjudged (w21/101), and a period in progress is still budgeted only up to today (w21/076). The fixture (monthly 500 USD set 2026-10-21, 300 spent): budget 338.71 (21 of 31 days of 500), 89% used, "Below target", on the card and Home, instead of $16.13 and 1860%; weekly and quarterly agree. The "Also" case: a first directive dated after today now reads neutral with "Starts {date}" (`beforeFirstBudget` takes `today`; the card, per-bar favourability and Home pass it) rather than being judged against $0.00. Deviation: the budgeted amount for a period in progress is the prorated-to-today figure (the existing rule), not the full 500 the note mentions as one acceptable outcome; the status is what matters and it is Below. One test that pinned the old mid-period proration of a first directive (`does not apply a directive before its effective date`, 160) was replaced by the new expectation (310) plus a case that a later raise still starts on its date. Tests in `budget-screen/selectors/__tests__/first-period.test.ts` (six fail with the two selectors reverted). Mobile gates and deadcode green.
