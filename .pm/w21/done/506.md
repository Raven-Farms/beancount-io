# Money flow pads share counts in tickers that are also currency codes (BND, AMD, NOK) as money: "+10.00 AMD" for 10 shares (w21/445 regression)

**Severity:** low (display). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `select-transaction-detail.ts:112` decides "is this a currency" with `getCurrencySymbol`, and `currency-icons` has symbols for codes that are also common tickers (AMD `֏`, BND `$`, NOK `kr`, ALL `L`, CVE `$`). Code-level at `main` `138c47333`: in the reviewer's tsx run of `selectPostingRows`, 10 AMD shares give `+10.00 AMD` and 3 NOK give `+3.00 NOK` (before `cdcec224a`, `+10 AMD` / `+3 NOK`). VTI and BTC are unchanged. A Boglehead VTI/BND row reads like cash, against the fix's own rule that a share count keeps its recorded digits.

## Fix

Pad only codes on an ISO-4217 allowlist, or skip padding for a commodity held at cost or declared as non-currency.

**Acceptance:** `10 BND {80 USD}` reads "+10 BND", and `4.5 EUR` still reads "+4.50 EUR". Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Regression of w21/445 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for formatPostingUnits and BND together with ticker.

Fixed, with a deviation from the note's first option. An ISO-4217 allowlist cannot separate these cases (BND, AMD and NOK are ISO codes), so `formatPostingUnits` takes the note's second signal: units held at a cost are a commodity whatever their name, and keep their recorded digits. `10 BND {80 USD}` reads "+10 BND" (AMD, NOK likewise); `4.5 EUR` still reads "+4.50 EUR" and a cost-free `10 NOK` is still money. A share count of a ticker that is also a currency code but is held without a cost (no `{}`) still pads; that case cannot be told apart from cash. Unit cases added; reverting the selector fails the suite. Mobile gates and knip green.
