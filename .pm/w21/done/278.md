# A commodity held both with and without a cost basis hides its market value on Accounts, and the root loses "At market value"

**Severity:** moderate. Common with crypto staking rewards or RSUs recorded with `@ price` beside lots bought `{cost}`. Home's net-worth caption uses the same function. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: a commodity counts as "converted" only when none of it is left in the market map. One cost-less lot, which the ledger leaves in units, switches off the valuation for the whole commodity, and the row drops its value whenever any not-in-total holding exists.

Code-level at `main` `4bbc36785`. A reviewer ran the selectors under Node: Exchange holds 2 ETH {2000 USD} (valued $5,000) plus 0.05 ETH of `@`-priced rewards, and Bank holds $100.
- **Exchange row:** `{ kind: "units", units: "2.05 ETH", value: null }`, with no notes. The $5,000 it adds to Assets appears nowhere on the row.
- **Assets root:** "1 not in total", `valuesHoldings: false`.
- **Control without the reward lot:** "At market value · 1 price not updated since Jul 15, 2026".

## Root cause

`mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:281-285` (the "converted" test), `mobile/src/common/balance-display.ts:117` (value dropped when any holding is not in total), and `lot-inventory.ts:135-143` (cost-less lots stay in units).

## Fix

Treat a commodity as converted when its units exceed what remains in the market map. For a one-commodity row, keep the operating-currency value and its basis, and add the not-in-total note for the cost-less remainder.

**Acceptance:** the Exchange row shows $5,000 with "At market value" and "Not in total: 0.05 ETH", and the root keeps "At market value". Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w21/124 (done) is the price basis of a converted holding, and w21/208 and w3/241 are other valuation captions. Searched `.pm/**` for cost-less, costless, "with no cost", valuesHoldings and "mixed lots".

Fixed. Two changes. (1) `selectValuation` counts a commodity as converted when more of it is held than the market map still carries (`|units| > |remainder|`) instead of only when none is left, and values the converted part (2.05 ETH held with 0.05 ETH cost-less values 2 ETH, rounded at the recorded scale since 2.05 − 0.05 is not 2 in floating point), so `valuesHoldings` stays true and the root keeps its basis line with the remainder counted as not in total. (2) `selectBalanceDisplay`'s one-commodity `units` row keeps its value when the only thing left in the market map is the same commodity, and carries that remainder as a new `notInTotal` field; `balanceNotes` appends "Not in total: 0.05 ETH" after the "$5,000 at market" note. When nothing converted, the row stays value-less and note-less as before. Existing unit-row expectations gained `notInTotal: []`. Tests: new cases in `balance-display.test.ts` and `valuation.test.ts` (nine fail with the two source files reverted, five of them the shape change). Mobile gates and deadcode green. Not checked: the note's Exchange fixture end to end on a device.
