# Home's Spending card never shows its two monthly totals to sighted users, and an empty ledger draws a $0–$1 axis

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `spending-card.tsx:162-172` and `bar-chart-d3.tsx` draw only bars and short ticks. The exact amounts exist only in the VoiceOver label (w21/018). Under `tsx`, `[1234.56, 987.65]` gives ticks `$0 $200 … $1.2K` with neither total printed, and `[0, 0]` gives `$0 $0.2 … $1` over two 2 pt bars, which suggests cents of spending.

Code-level at `main` `6d8c869b2`.

## Fix

Print each month's amount on or under its bar, and show an empty state when both months are $0.

**Acceptance:** the card reads "This month $1,234.56 · Last month $987.65", and an empty ledger shows "No spending yet".
