# An open, zero-balance account disappears from Accounts when its only sub-account is closed and emptied

**Severity:** minor. It is an edge left by w21/099's fix. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: grafting skips any listed account that is an ancestor of an existing node. The raw tree includes closed accounts, so the open parent isn't grafted. Then the closed child is filtered out, and the zero-balance filter removes the parent too.

Code-level at `main` `4bbc36785`. Run under Node with `Assets:Savings` open at $0, `Assets:Savings:CD` closed and emptied, and `Assets:Checking` at $100: the rows are `assets=100, Checking=100`, with no Savings. Without the closed child, Savings=0 lists.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/components/account-list/select-account-list.ts:385` and `:400-406` (`graftZeroAccounts`).

## Fix

Keep listed ancestors as well, or skip grafting only when a descendant will actually survive the filters.

**Acceptance:** the fixture lists Savings $0 and hides CD. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

w21/099 (done) and w21/056 are the parent fixes. Searched `.pm/**` for graftZeroAccounts, "closed child" and "open parent".

Fixed. `graftZeroAccounts` no longer treats a listed account as represented just because the raw tree has some descendant: it skips grafting only when a descendant will survive the filters (listed, holding a balance in the operating currency, or, in the Accounts view, holding a commodity the total cannot express). A closed and emptied child is none of those, so the open zero parent is grafted and kept, and the closed child is still hidden. The note's fixture (Savings open at $0, Savings:CD closed and emptied, Checking $100) lists Savings $0 and Checking and hides CD; a listed parent whose child renders is still replaced by the child (control test). Tests in `select-account-list.test.ts` (the fixture fails with the source reverted). Mobile gates and deadcode green.
