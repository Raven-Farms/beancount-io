# After w21/184, Home counts this month's pre-entered entries: the budget panel says "Above target" while the Budget page shows $0 spent, and Spending's "This month" includes bills not yet paid

**Severity:** moderate (regression). Pre-entered rent and subscriptions are common (see w21/166 and w21/167), and Home now disagrees with the Budget page on the same budget. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/184 widened Home's Spending and budget-panel reads to the end of the current month, so their cache key stays stable offline. The panel still prorates the *budget* up to today (w21/076), so its actuals now run to month end while its budget runs to today. A payment pre-entered for later this month counts as spent today.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `57826d105`, on the disposable QA ledger (restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`). The fixture `; qa-20261020-futmo` was a monthly 500 USD budget on `Expenses:QaBudFut`, with 400.00 USD pre-entered for **2026-10-30**; today is 2026-10-21.
- **Home budget panel:** "**$400.00 of $338.71, 118% used, Above target**".
- **Budget page (YTD), same budget:** "Budget $338.71 · **Actual $0.00** · **Below target**".

184's resolution notes the Spending side as a deviation ("this month now includes entries dated later in the month"); the panel side wasn't considered.

## Root cause

`spendingWindow` and `budgetPanelWindow` (from w21/184) end at the month's end, while `calculateBudgetForInterval(…, asOf=today)` prorates to today.

## Fix

Keep the month-long `time` window as the cache key, but filter the returned actuals to entries on or before today on the client. Or query with the month window and subtract future-dated entries using the journal the screen already has. Apply the same rule to Spending's "This month".

**Acceptance:** with the fixture, Home's panel reads "$0.00 of $338.71" (Below target) like the Budget page, and the Oct 30 bill isn't in Spending's "This month" until that day. Offline launches still hit the cache. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

This is a follow-up to w21/184 (done), with w21/166 and w21/168 as the Reports and Budget siblings. Searched `.pm/**` for spendingWindow, budgetPanelWindow and "future" together with "budget panel".

Fixed with a design that keeps w21/184's stable cache key in the common case. The month-long window stays the default read; a new check (`useScheduledThisMonth` and, in the panel's effect, `scheduledThisMonthBql`) asks the ledger with one cheap BQL statement (`SELECT date WHERE date > <today> AND date <= <month end> LIMIT 1`) whether anything is dated after today this month. Only when it is, the in-progress period is read to today instead: Spending's card also reads `<month start> - <today>` and `withMonthToDate` swaps that month in for the window's, and the budget panel reads each group's current period to today (never before the window's start, so a weekly period spanning New Year's stays clipped), falling back to the stable window and then to the cache if that read fails. The fixture (monthly 500 USD with 400 pre-entered for Oct 30, today Oct 21) now reads $0.00 of $338.71, Below target, like the Budget page, and the Oct 30 bill is out of "This month" until that day. A failed or loading check reads as "none", so offline launches behave exactly as after w21/184. Guests skip the check (their examples cannot run it, and have no budgets). Deviations: while the to-today read has not landed the figure briefly shows the window's value, and offline with scheduled entries the fallback is the cached window (which includes them). Tests in `home-screen/selectors/__tests__/scheduled-this-month.test.ts` cover the statement, the month swap on Spending's numbers, and the wiring (they fail with the card and hook reverted); the panel's `mapInBatches` wiring test was updated for the new chain. Mobile gates and deadcode green; not run against a live ledger.
