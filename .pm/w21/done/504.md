# The first copy of an amortized or forecast series still offers Delete when its narration has an escaped quote, so Delete removes the whole series (w21/443 gap)

**Severity:** major (the w21/443 harm, for every series whose narration contains `\"`). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `isPluginCopy` (`mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-plugin-copy.ts:49-50`) skips the narration comparison when the written string contains a backslash (`!written.includes("\\")`), and the first copy keeps its template's day, so nothing marks it as a copy. Until `generated_from` is deployed (w21/443's server half isn't yet), this check is the only guard.

Reproduced natively (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro at `9e5987d07`), with `plugin "fava.plugins.amortize_over"` and `2026-10-01 * "QA Insurer" "qa car \"premium\" insurance"` amortized over 12 months (disposable ledger, restored). Its "(1/12)" copy shows the Edit pencil, and ⋯ offers Delete. The reviewer's run of the real rustledger plugins shows the same for a forecast's first month (`# "Landlord" "Rent \"flat\" [MONTHLY]"`).

## Fix

Unescape Beancount's `\"` and `\\` before comparing instead of skipping the comparison; or, better, deploy `generated_from` and prefer it.

**Acceptance:** the fixture's "(1/12)" shows no Edit and no Delete. Unit cases cover escaped narrations for amortize and forecast.

## Dedupe

Unit cases cover amortize, forecast, the template, and the other-escape case; reverting the selector fails two tests. Mobile gates and knip green. Not re-run natively; `generated_from` (blocked 508) remains the authoritative fix once deployed.
