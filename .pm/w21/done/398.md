# A budget on a closed account keeps running forever: a former job's income target shows red every month and outranks real overspends on Home

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budgetForInterval` and `groupBudgetEntries` (`budget-selectors.ts:378-452`) and `select-budget-panel.ts` never consult close dates, although `useAccountCloseDates` exists. Under `tsx`, `Income:Salary:OldCo` (−3000 monthly, closed 2026-06-30) shows red bars from July onward and the card reads "Below target". On Home it ranks above `Expenses:Food` at 470 of 500. The only ways out are deleting its history or zeroing it, and zeroing hits w21/394.

Code-level at `main` `f628d4727`.

## Fix

End each budget history at its account's close date, so no budget applies after it, and hide the card once the window starts after the close, on the Budget page and on Home.

**Acceptance:** a budget whose account closed in June shows no budget after June and leaves Home's panel.

## Dedupe

Only w21/045 (New Transaction's picker) matched. Searched `.pm/**` for "closed account", "close date" and useAccountCloseDates with budget.

Fixed. A budget on a closed account now ends with the account. `budgetForInterval` and `calculateBudgetForInterval` take an `endsOn` day: a period is budgeted only up to it (periods wholly after it get 0, the one containing it is prorated, e.g. 15 of June's 30 days for a 15 June close), through the same end-of-span rule as `asOf`. The Budget card passes its account's close date (`useAccountCloseDates`) to `useBudgetActuals`, so a former job's -3000 income target stops after June instead of reading red every month, and its history stays visible. Home's panel leaves out a group whose account closed before today (`closedBefore`: the close day itself still counts, since Beancount books the day's postings first), so a closed account no longer outranks real overspends. Tests (`closed-account-budget.test.ts`: full budget until the close and none after, the partial period, `closedBefore` on the close day, and the card and Home wiring; three fail with the selectors and Home reverted, and the pinned `budgetForInterval` call in `budget-actuals-as-of.test.ts` was updated for the new argument). Deviation: the Budget page still lists the closed account's card, with no budget after the close; it is not hidden. Not run natively.
