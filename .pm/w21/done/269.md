# Every-two-months and every-six-months bills are never detected, and marking one Recurring labels it "Irregular"

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. Semi-annual car insurance and bimonthly water bills are common. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `CADENCE_BANDS` jumps from monthly (27–32 days) to quarterly (84–98) to yearly (350–380). Gaps of about 60 or about 182 days fall in no band, so `classifyCadence` returns null, and a manual Recurring mark shows "Irregular", the label w0/039 established is wrong for regular payments.

Code-level at `main` `de881e646`. Run under Node:
- 612 USD on 04-01 and 10-01 each year, 2024–2026, is not detected, and marking it shows `merchantsCadenceIrregular`.
- 84.20 USD on the 12th every other month, 2025-10 to 2026-10, gives the same.
- The same amounts quarterly come out `quarterly`, next 2027-01-12.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:54-64` and `resolve-recurring.ts:37`.

## Fix

Add bimonthly (about 56–66 days, month step 2) and semi-annual (about 175–190 days, month step 6) bands, with labels in 13 locales.

**Acceptance:** both fixtures are detected with the right cadence and next date. Unit cases are added.
