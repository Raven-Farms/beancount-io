# One future-dated transaction moves every Reports range onto a month that hasn't happened, so "1M" on 21 October shows only November

**Severity:** moderate. Pre-entered rent, subscriptions and scheduled transfers are common. With one in the ledger, Reports' 1M, chart, Cash flow, breakdowns and Recent transactions all describe next month, today's month drops out, and 3M shifts forward. Home's Spending card ("this month", through today) and its See all destination then disagree. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile, plus a ledger check. A3: Reports anchors every window to the series' *latest data point*, so stale ledgers still show history, and the ledger ends the monthly series at the last transaction or price date, even a future one. Nothing caps the anchor at today.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `903cdec8d`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-fut` was `2026-10-14` "QA FUT bus" (Transport 40.00 from Cash) and **`2026-11-01` "QA FUT rent"** (Housing:Rent 1500.00 from Cash). Today is 2026-10-21. Reports → **1M**: a single **NOV** column (Expenses about $1,500, Net Profit about −$1,500), and Cash flow shows Savings → Housing. The October bus fare isn't in 1M at all.

## Where

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts:201-214` (`firstAndLastDate` includes future dates)
- `mobile/src/common/series-util.ts:111-131` (`filterSeriesByRange`)
- `mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/selectors/select-ranged-account-tree.ts:143-150`
- `select-income-expense-chart.ts:22-38` (`selectStatementAnchorMonth`)
- `select-account-transactions.ts:71-74` (no upper bound)

## Fix

Use `min(latest data month, current month)` as the reference month, keeping the latest-data fallback only when the ledger's data ends before today. Do it in one helper shared by the chart, the breakdowns and Recent transactions, and exclude rows dated after today from 1M/3M/6M unless the user picks a range that includes them.

**Acceptance:**
- With the fixture on 2026-10-21, 1M shows October with the $40 bus fare and no November column.
- 3M is Aug–Oct.
- A stale ledger still shows its last active months.
- Add selector unit tests.

## Dedupe

w21/101 and w4/done/038 are future-dated budget items. The documented latest-data anchor (stale ledgers) is intended; this is the future-data side it doesn't handle. Searched `.pm/**` for future-dated, postdated, "scheduled transaction", anchor, "latest data point" and rangeStartMonth.

## Scope caution for the fix (added 2026-10-21)

Home's net worth, Assets and Liabilities headline, the Accounts tab and account detail all *include* future entries, consistently with each other and with Fava. If the today cap goes into the shared `filterSeriesByRange` (`common/series-util.ts:111-131`), Home's and account detail's charts would end at October while their headlines and the Accounts tab still include the pre-entered rent. Cap Reports' flow series, and keep balance series consistent with their headlines. The budget ALL range has the same root cause and is filed as w21/168.

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** Reports' flow ranges now end on `flowAnchorMonth(latest data month, current month)` (`common/series-util.ts`), so a future month falls outside every range but ALL. The current month comes from `useToday`.
- **Chart:** `filterFlowSeriesByRange`.
- **Recent transactions:** the statement anchor (`selectStatementAnchorMonth`), plus an upper bound in `selectAccountTransactions`.
- **Breakdowns:** `selectRangedAccountTree`, which also feeds Cash flow.

A stale ledger keeps its latest month. Per the scope caution, `filterSeriesByRange`/`filterBalanceSeriesByRange` and the balance series (Home, account detail) are unchanged, so they stay consistent with headlines that include future entries.

Tests: `reports-screen/selectors/__tests__/future-dated-ranges.test.ts`. With the fixture's shape, 1M is October only, 3M is Aug–Oct, ALL keeps November, and the stale ledger keeps June; Recent transactions and the breakdowns drop the November rent. These fail with the selectors reverted. The ledger still ends its series at the future date, so no ledger check or change was made. Not re-run natively. w21/168 (budget ALL) is separate.
