# A Reports period with nothing in the operating currency says "No accounts", even under "Not in total: 420.00 EUR (Travel)"

**Severity:** minor (wrong empty-state copy). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `category-breakdown.tsx:399-400` shows `t("noAccounts")` whenever `items` is empty. The tree is empty for any range with no operating-currency activity (`select-ranged-account-tree.ts:208, 231`). Under `tsx`: tree `[]`, headline `$0.00`, note "Not in total: 420.00 EUR (Travel)", rows **"No accounts"**. A quiet month or a stale ledger on YTD shows the same text.

Code-level at `main` `d5c763aa2`. Not run natively.

## Fix

Use a range-specific empty string, such as "No expenses in this period", and reword or skip it when `notInTotal` is non-empty.

**Acceptance:** a USD ledger whose only October expense is `Expenses:Travel:Hotel 420 EUR` never says "No accounts" on Reports ▸ 1M.
