# Android: hardware Back during Uploading or Reading leaves the upload and AI parse running, so receipt quota is spent on an abandoned scan

**Severity:** minor on Android. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `5867356e4`; Android not exercised): `workflow.cancel` is wired only to the busy overlay's Cancel (`receipt-capture-screen.tsx:252`). `use-receipt-workflow.ts:185-193` doesn't cancel when the screen goes away, and there's no `BackHandler` or `beforeRemove`. On Android, the `fullScreenModal` route (`app/(app)/_layout.tsx:78-81`) pops on Back, while the PUT and `parseReceipt` finish in the background. The quota is charged, no form opens, and a user near the limit hits `quota_exhausted` on the next scan.

## Fix

Cancel the workflow on unmount and on Back (the same path as the overlay's Cancel, w21/205).

**Acceptance:** Back during Uploading or Reading aborts both requests and charges no quota. A test covers unmount cleanup.

## Dedupe

See w21/205. Searched `.pm/**` for receipt together with BackHandler, and unmount together with cancel.

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — `useReceiptWorkflow` now abandons the attempt in flight when the screen unmounts, which is what Android's hardware Back does to the `fullScreenModal`. It goes through the same `abandonAttempt` as the busy overlay's Cancel (moved to `upload-guard.ts`): late results are ignored, the upload-URL and `parseReceipt` requests are aborted, and the S3 transfer is cancelled. Back during Uploading now never sends the parse, so no quota is charged. Back during Reading aborts the parse request on the client, but a parse the server had already started may still count, as with Cancel (w21/205). Tests: `abandonAttempt` behaviour and source checks for the unmount cleanup in `receipt-capture-screen/__tests__/receipt-cancel.test.ts` (fail without the change) (`mobile`). Not exercised on Android.
