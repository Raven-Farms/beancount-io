# Profile says "Photo from Gravatar" under the initials placeholder for users who have no Gravatar

**Severity:** minor (wrong copy). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the caption is chosen from the URL alone (`isGravatarUrl`). The backend returns a Gravatar URL for everyone without an uploaded picture, and the avatar requests it with `d=404` and falls back to initials, so users without a Gravatar see their initials captioned "Photo from Gravatar".

Code-level at `main` `567bd1801`: `mobile/src/screens/profile/profile-screen.tsx:355`, `user-display.ts:44`, `components/user-avatar/index.tsx:46`, and `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/auth/data/user-model/postgres-impl.ts:18`.

## Fix

Have `UserAvatar` report a failed image load, and in that case hide the caption or change it to "Add a photo on Gravatar".

**Acceptance:** with a 404 Gravatar, the caption isn't shown (or reads the add-photo copy). A component test covers it.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for profilePhotoFromGravatar and isGravatarUrl; there are no matches.

Fixed. `UserAvatar` takes an optional `onFallbackChange` and reports whether its picture is not what shows (no picture, or a load that failed). Profile keeps that in state: a Gravatar URL still reads "Photo from Gravatar" while the image loads or has loaded, and once the image fails (the avatar requests Gravatar with `d=404`) the caption reads "Add a photo on Gravatar" (`profileAddGravatar`, 13 locales) and keeps its link to gravatar.com, with the same text as its accessibility label. Tests: the real-component harness in `user-avatar.test.ts` gained the effect stub and a case for the report (false then true after `onError`, true with no picture), and `profile/__tests__/gravatar-caption.test.ts` pins the Profile wiring (both fail with the two source files reverted). Mobile gates and deadcode green. Deviation: the caption reads the add-photo copy instead of being hidden, one of the note's two options.
