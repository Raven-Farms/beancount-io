# If the profile fails to load, Settings silently drops its whole Account section, which is the only way into Profile

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `setting/main-content.tsx:136-147` renders the Account header and the Profile row only when `user` exists. `useCurrentUser` returns `user: null` both while loading and after an error, and Settings ignores the `loading` and `error` it also returns. With no cached profile (a first launch or fresh sign-in) and a failed request, Settings shows no Account section and no explanation, so the name and username can't be edited. The section also pops in after loading and shifts the rows below. The drawer's identity row already handles the failure (`ledger-drawer.tsx:349-360`), and Profile shows a Retry card (`profile-screen.tsx:439`).

Code-level at `main` `41253a6e3`. It needs an empty cache plus a failed `userProfile` request.

## Fix

Show a skeleton row while loading, and on error a "Couldn't load your profile" row that refetches.

**Acceptance:** with `userProfile` failing, Settings shows an Account row with a retry instead of nothing.
