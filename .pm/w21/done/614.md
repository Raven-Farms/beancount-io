# An account that opens in the future is listed on Accounts as a live $0.00 row (mirror of w21/099)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/account-close-dates.ts:143-150` (`listedAccountsAsOf`, used at `accounts-screen.tsx:114`) drops accounts closed by today but ignores `openedOn`. New Transaction's picker already hides such accounts (`isOpenOn`).

Native (iPhone 17e at `5a71c68fb`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): `2026-12-01 open Liabilities:QaNewCard USD` (today is 2026-10-22). The Accounts tab lists "QaNewCard $0.00" under Liabilities, looking exactly like an open account. Under `tsx`, on a ledger with no other liabilities, it brings back a whole "Liabilities $0.00" section.

## Fix

Hide accounts that aren't open yet (or tag them "Opens Dec 1"), consistent with `isOpenOn`.

**Acceptance:** QaNewCard isn't listed (or is tagged) until Dec 1. A unit case is added.
