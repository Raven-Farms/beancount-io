# An income target's stored negative sign shows in budget history rows, the Delete alert and the Update placeholder ("-4250.50 USD"), unformatted

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the card shows the absolute, formatted value, but `budget-history-rows.tsx:125, 177, 187` print the raw `row.amount`, and `add-budget-screen/amount-placeholder.ts:14-15` uses it as the placeholder. Under `tsx`: typed 4250.50 is stored as −4250.50, the card stat reads 4,250.50, and the history row, Delete alert and Update placeholder all show `-4250.50 USD` / `-4250.50`, ungrouped in every locale. The placeholder suggests typing a negative number where the form asks for a positive one.

Code-level at `main` `f628d4727`.

## Fix

Use `formatMoneyWithCurrency(Math.abs(value), currency, locale)` in the rows and the alert, and the absolute value in `budgetAmountPlaceholder`.

**Acceptance:** an income budget of −4250.50 reads "$4,250.50" in its history row, the alert and the placeholder.

## Dedupe

w21/366 covers only the ISO dates, and w21/034 the placeholder's existence. Searched `.pm/**` for budgetAmountPlaceholder, "history row" and budgetDeleteMessage.

Fixed. `formatBudgetEntryAmount` shows a history entry's amount the way the card shows its budget: the magnitude, grouped and annotated with its currency, at the entry's own precision (so a stored -4250.50 USD reads $4,250.50, -0.003 BTC reads 0.003 BTC, and an amount that names no currency takes the budget's). The history row, the Delete alert (`budgetDeleteMessage`) and the Delete button's accessibility label all use it. The Update placeholder is now the magnitude too ("4250.50", never "-4250.50"), so it no longer suggests typing a negative number into a form that asks for a positive one. Tests (`closed-account-budget.test.ts`: the formatter for signed, unsigned, fractional and currency-less amounts and the three call sites; `amount-placeholder.test.ts` updated for the positive placeholder; both fail with the component and placeholder reverted). Deviation: the placeholder is the plain number, not the grouped "$4,250.50" of the note's acceptance, because the amount field refuses grouping commas and a grouped placeholder would invite text it then rejects. Not run natively.
