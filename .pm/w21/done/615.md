# A Recurring row shows a one-off foreign charge as the bill's usual amount: "Spotify · Monthly · ¥980" for a $10.99 bill

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `d820babc8`, proven under `tsx`): `merchants-screen/merchant-row-text.ts:11-20` (`primaryTypical`, used at `:62-63`) picks the currency with the largest raw number. `detectRecurrence` keeps a typical amount for every currency in the window, even one seen once. A monthly $10.99 Spotify bill with one 980 JPY charge while abroad is detected as monthly `{USD: 10.99, JPY: 980}` (not approximate), and the row reads "¥980". Someone who moved countries keeps seeing their old ¥120,000 rent after it became $2,000.

## Fix

Pick the currency with the most events, or the latest charge's currency, not the largest number.

**Acceptance:** that row reads "$10.99". A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

See w21/007, w21/271 and w21/535. Searched `.pm/**` for primaryTypical and typicalAmountByCurrency.
