# Every dollar currency shows as "$" (and ¥ for CNY/JPY, kr for SEK/NOK/DKK), so a merchant paid in USD and CAD shows two identical "$42.50" totals

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/currency-util.ts:14-20` (`getCurrencySymbol`) takes `currency-icons`' bare symbol. That reaches `merchant-detail-screen/selectors/format-merchant-total.ts`, `merchants-screen/merchant-row-text.ts:68`, and every `format(Signed)MoneyWithCurrency` caller. Under `tsx`, `formatMerchantTotal` gives "-$42.50" for USD, CAD, AUD and MXN alike, "-¥" for CNY and JPY, and "-kr" for SEK and NOK.

Native (iPhone 17e at `680e4879f`, disposable USD ledger, restored byte-for-byte): "QA Maple" paid 42.50 USD on 10-10 and 42.50 CAD on 10-11. Merchants ▸ QA Maple: the header shows two total lines, "$42.50" and "$42.50", with no currency. The rows below read "−42.50 CAD" and "−$42.50".

## Fix

Use the bare symbol only for the ledger's primary currency, and a disambiguated form (US$, CA$, or the code) for any other currency that shares the symbol, as Intl's `currencyDisplay: "symbol"` does relative to a locale.

**Acceptance:** that header reads "$42.50" and "CA$42.50" (or "42.50 CAD"), and CNY and JPY are told apart. Unit cases are added.

Related: w21/070 (JPY formatting), w21/445 and w21/506 (padding). Searched `.pm/**` for getCurrencySymbol, "CA$" and "same symbol".

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — scoped to where the symbols collide: the merchant header formats its currency totals together (`formatMerchantTotals`), and a currency whose symbol another total shares keeps it only when it is the ledger's primary currency, the others reading by code ("-$42.50", "-42.50 CAD"; CNY/JPY and the krone currencies likewise). Cases in `merchant-shared-symbol-totals.test.ts` (`mobile`). `getCurrencySymbol` itself is unchanged: a ledger whose primary currency is CAD or AUD still reads its own figures with `$` everywhere, and a Merchants row shows one typical currency, so nothing there collides.
