# A stock split or merger reads as shares gained or lost in money colours ("+90.00 NVDA", green), though no money moved

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `transaction-display-utils.ts:215-228` (one commodity: its net units, signed) and `:200-209` (two commodities at cost: the bigger unit count wins). The dashboard (`transaction-summary.ts:194-199`) shows it as a neutral "mixed".

Native (iPhone 17e at `0c0609c4e`, disposable ledger, restored): after a buy of 10 NVDA {1200.00 USD}, a 10:1 split `Assets:QaNvda -10 NVDA {1200.00 USD}` / `Assets:QaNvda 100 NVDA {120.00 USD}` reads "+90.00 NVDA" in green on the Transactions row. Under `tsx`, a merger (`-10 OLDCO {100}`, `+5 NEWCO {200}`) reads "−10.00 OLDCO" in red.

## Fix

When the cost side nets to zero (no money moved), show the entry as a neutral, unsigned corporate action (for example "10 → 100 NVDA").

**Acceptance:** the split and the merger read as neutral, with no money colour or sign. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

See w21/506 (ticker padding) and w21/057. Searched `.pm/**` for split, merger and "corporate action".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — `selectTransactionAmount` recognises a corporate action first: every posting is a balance-sheet lot with a cost and the cost of what left equals the cost of what arrived in each cost currency. The split reads a neutral "100.00 NVDA" and the merger "10.00 OLDCO", both unsigned with value 0, in no money colour; buys and sales (which carry a cash posting) are untouched. Cases in `transaction-display-utils.test.ts` (`mobile`). The "10 → 100 NVDA" wording the note floats was left out: it needs new strings in every language.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, same bundle with `7647b265d`; disposable ledger restored): a 10:1 split `-10 NVDA {1200.00 USD}` / `100 NVDA {120.00 USD}` now reads "100.00 NVDA" in neutral text, with no sign or money colour (it used to read "+90.00 NVDA" in green). The money colour and sign are fixed. One observation: the share count is still padded to ".00", which is w21/601's open issue.
