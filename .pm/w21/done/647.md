# Agents' and API clients' balanced entries with an `@` price are refused as UNBALANCED: the precheck never converts a posting's price into its weight

**Severity:** minor (REST `POST …/entries`, GraphQL `bulkEntries`, MCP `addLedgerEntries`). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: ledger. A3: `backend-cluster/ledger/src/features/ledger/utils/transaction-balance.ts:121-148` (the sum at `:133`) adds each posting in its own currency and ignores `price` (and cost), though the wire posting carries it. The check runs from `src/api/entries.ts:216-224` unless `allowInvalid` is set. bean-check and the engine both accept such entries. Mobile's `addEntries` sends only units, so the app can't reach this.

Production (2026-10-22, disposable ledger; nothing written, ledger identical to its snapshot): `bulkEntries` with `Assets:Cash -110.00 USD` and `Assets:Bank:Checking 100.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD` returns `UNBALANCED` "entry 0: Transaction does not balance: residual 110.00 USD, 100.00 EUR" (the residual is also shown with the USD sign flipped).

## Fix

Weigh each posting the Beancount way: units × price, or units × cost for postings at cost, in the price's or cost's currency, before summing.

**Acceptance:** that entry is accepted on all three surfaces, and a truly unbalanced priced entry is still refused. Tests cover price, total price (`@@`) and cost.

## Dedupe

None found (searched all of `.pm/` for checkTransactionBalance, UNBALANCED together with price, and weight).

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — the ledger service's entries precheck now weighs a priced posting the Beancount way: units × price, summed in the price's currency (tolerance is still inferred from units, as Beancount does by default). So `-110.00 USD` with `100.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD` is accepted on REST, GraphQL and MCP; they share one posting schema, and backend-v2 passes `price` through. A truly unbalanced priced entry is still refused as UNBALANCED. Residuals keep their sign, as both engines print them ("residual -5.00 USD"). Deviation from the note: there are no total-price (`@@`) or cost tests, because no surface can send either (the REST/MCP posting schema is strict, units/price/flag, and GraphQL's `LedgerPostingInput` has the same fields), and the renderer writes neither. Tests: `transaction-balance.test.ts`, a dry-run REST case in `entries-amount-syntax.test.ts`, and a live check in `scripts/verify-rustledger.ts` that the precheck agrees with the real engine on five priced cases (all fail without the change) (`backend-cluster/ledger`). A ledger service deploy carries it to production.
