# Marking an older merchant Recurring says "Not enough history" under a header of "54 transactions" (history counted only in the 37-month window)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `d820babc8`, proven under `tsx`): `merchants-screen/selectors/resolve-recurring.ts:37` with `payee-series.ts:25` (`PAYEE_SERIES_WINDOW_MONTHS = 37`). `enoughHistory` counts only dates inside the window, while the header counts the whole ledger. A gym paid monthly from Jan 2019 to Jun 2023 (54 entries, none inside the window) resolves to `"unknown"`, so the chip reads "Not enough history" beside "54 transactions · Jan 1, 2019 – Jun 1, 2023".

## Fix

Say "No recent payments" (or "Stopped") when the history exists but falls outside the window, or judge the cadence from the full series for a manual mark.

**Acceptance:** that merchant doesn't read "Not enough history". A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

See w21/170 and w21/408. Searched `.pm/**` for "Not enough history" and PAYEE_SERIES_WINDOW_MONTHS.
