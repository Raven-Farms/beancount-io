# One shifted, early or missing twice-a-month payment turns "Twice a month" into "Every 2 weeks", with Next 2–3 days early

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `semiMonthlyDays` (`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:255-270`) requires every event to land exactly on the min or max day of the month (`slot` returns null otherwise), and the halves to alternate strictly. One payment moved off a weekend, or one missing, sends the series to the median-gap biweekly band (`:469`, `:535`). w21/271 gave monthly bills ±3-day tolerance; twice-a-month bills got none.

Code-level at `main` `6854ec3a0`; reviewer's tsx runs, with payments on the 1st and 15th from 2026-01 to 2026-10 and today 2026-10-20:
- the clean series gives `semimonthly`, next `2026-11-01`;
- 02-15 paid on 02-13 gives `biweekly`, next `2026-10-29`;
- 08-01 paid on 07-31 gives the same;
- 05-15 missing gives the same.

Not exercised on the device. Payroll and parking bills paid on the 15th and on the last business day rarely keep "Twice a month", and Overdue is judged too early.

## Fix

Apply `ANCHOR_TOLERANCE_DAYS` to the two slots, pick the two most common days rather than min and max, and allow one missing occurrence.

**Acceptance:** each variant above stays semimonthly, with next `2026-11-01`. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Follow-up to w21/170 (semimonthly) and w21/271 (monthly tolerance). Searched `.pm/**` for semi-monthly, "twice a month", biweekly, monthDayAnchor and weekend.

Resolved. Semi-monthly detection now tolerates the irregularities a real twice-a-month bill has. The two due days are the most common day and the most common one at least ten days from it (not the min and max), each payment may sit within the existing three-day anchor tolerance of its due day in its own month or the next or previous one, and one occurrence may be missing in the series; "Next" is the occurrence after the last one paid, not derived from the last payment's own day (a payment made on the 31st for the 1st no longer skews it). To keep a short 14-day run from fitting two anchors by chance, the modal day must repeat and be at least a third of the payments. The note's variants (the 15th paid on the 13th, the 1st paid on 07-31, 05-15 missing, and the clean series) all stay semimonthly with next 2026-11-01; two missing, a payment a week off its day, and a five-payment 14-day run do not. Existing semi-monthly cases (1st and 15th; 15th and last day, February included) are unchanged. Tests fail against the old detection. Deviation: no native run.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `58ee6596f`): "QA Parking" paid on the 1st and 15th from June to Oct 15, with the Aug 1 payment made on Jul 31 (disposable ledger, restored), reads "Twice a month · $120.00 · Next Nov 1, 2026" in Merchants' Recurring section, not "Every 2 weeks" with an early Next.
