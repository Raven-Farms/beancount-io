# Books begin in years 0001–0099 passes the Create Ledger form, but the server refuses it ("Invalid history start") because `Date.UTC` reads those years as 1900–1999

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: backend-v2. A3: `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/features/ledger/setup/build-ledger.ts:80-89` (`isCalendarDate`) still uses `Date.UTC`. Mobile's `isHistoryStart` moved to `utcDate` in w21/365, and the ledger itself accepts year-50 dates (w4/m38).

Production (2026-10-22, read-only): `previewLedgerSetup(setup: {preset: PERSONAL, operatingCurrency: "USD", historyStart: "0050-03-01"})` returns "Validation failed for setup: Invalid history start \"0050-03-01\"" (`VALIDATION_FAILED`). 0099-12-31 and 0001-01-01 behave the same, per `tsx`. On mobile, Review shows that raw text with Create disabled after the form accepted the date.

## Fix

Validate with `setUTCFullYear` (as w21/365 did on mobile), with a parity test on REST, GraphQL and MCP.

**Acceptance:** those dates preview successfully on all three surfaces, and impossible dates (0050-02-30) are still refused.

## Dedupe

Server half of w21/365 (done); see w4/m38. Searched `.pm/**` for isCalendarDate, "Invalid history start" and "year 50".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — `isCalendarDate` builds its date with `setUTCFullYear` (as `parseDirectiveDate` does), so 0001-01-01, 0050-03-01 and 0099-12-31 preview successfully while 0050-02-30, 0100-02-29 and year 0000 are still refused. The check sits in `previewLedgerSetup`, which REST, GraphQL and MCP all call, so the three surfaces change together; the case is in `build-ledger.test.ts` (`backend-cluster/backend-v2`, full suite green). A backend-v2 deploy carries it to production. Left alone: with a known opening balance, a Books begin of 0001-01-01 would put the opening one day earlier, in year 0.
