# Budget's "Last year" chip is resolved on the server's calendar while "This year" uses the device's, so they disagree for hours around New Year

**Severity:** minor. On 1 January, users east of the server's zone see "Last year" chart the year before last. West of it, on 31 December, "Last year" charts the current year, the same as "This year". **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: "This year" is an explicit range built from the device's date, but "Last year" sends the relative filter `"year-1"`, which the ledger service resolves in its own time zone with no `today` passed in.

Code-level at `main` `f5e4d1cb2`.

## Scenario (server on UTC)

- Tokyo, 2027-01-01 08:00: "This year" is 2027, while "Last year" charts **2025** until 09:00. In Auckland the window lasts until 13:00.
- Los Angeles, 2026-12-31 17:00: "Last year" already charts **2026**.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:102-110` (`"last-year"` → `"year-1"`)
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/directive-filter.ts:1131, 1211-1214`

## Fix

Send an explicit range built from the device's date, `${y-1}-01-01 - ${y-1}-12-31`, the same way "This year" is built.

**Acceptance:** a unit test with a fixed device date of 2027-01-01 gives "Last year" = 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, whatever the server's zone.

## Dedupe

w4/done/114 and w4/done/184 are dashboard export and labelling issues. Searched `.pm/**` for "year-1", "Last year" and rangeLastYear.

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** `timeSpanToFilter("last-year", today)` now returns the explicit `${y-1}-01-01 - ${y-1}-12-31` range from the device's date, as "This year" already does, instead of fava's server-resolved `year-1`. The pinned test that encoded `year-1` was replaced: `budget-labels.test.ts` checks 2027-01-01 → 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 and 2026-12-31 → 2025 (fails with the fix reverted).

**Native recheck 2026-10-21:** not exercised. The bug shows only in the hours around New Year, and the simulator follows the host clock (2026-10-21). On today's date, Budget's LY chip renders 2025 as before. Covered by `budget-labels.test.ts` only.
