# New Transaction pre-fills a closed account as FROM when it was the most recently used asset

**Severity:** moderate. A user who types an amount and taps Done is refused with "Posting to inactive account … (closed on …)". This is an incomplete part of w21/045. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/045 filtered closed accounts out of the account picker and the payee autofill. The form's own default posting still takes `assets[0]` and `expenses[0]` from `ledgerMeta.accounts`. That list is ranked by recent use (Fava's `ExponentialDecayRanker`) and still includes closed accounts, so a heavily used account that was just closed ranks first.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `5edfef868`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. I didn't submit the form.
- The fixtures `; qa-20261020-clo` and `-clo2` opened `Assets:Bank:Qa20261020Old`. They used it in 32 October entries, 28 of them zero-amount ticks against Expenses:Transport, moved its balance to Cash, and added `2026-10-19 close Assets:Bank:Qa20261020Old`.
- `ledgerMeta.accounts` (API) starts `["Assets:Bank:Qa20261020Old", "Expenses:Transport", "Assets:Bank:Checking", …]`.
- After a relaunch, Home → + → Enter new transaction shows **FROM `Assets:Ban…261020Old`** and TO `Expense…ransport (auto)`.

A side observation, not filed: the one-shot seeding uses whatever `ledgerMeta` is cached at mount. The launch right after the ledger change still seeded the previous ranking (Checking/Groceries), and the order caught up only on the next launch.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/multi-postings-transaction/multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:537-556`:
- `createInitialPostings(assets[0], secondAccount)` and `prefillSourceAccount || assets[0]` (the receipt path) read the ranked lists with no close-date check.
- `closeDates` (`:513`) is already in scope, and `isOpenOn` is imported.

## Fix

Pick the first account in `assets` and `expenses` that passes `isOpenOn(account, date, closeDates)` for both seeded postings and the receipt fallback. Wait for `closeDates` to load before seeding, so a cold start doesn't seed a closed account.

**Acceptance:**
- With the fixture, New Transaction pre-fills the first open asset (Checking).
- A receipt prefill with a closed parsed account falls back to an open one.
- Add a unit test for the selection helper.

**Resolved 2026-10-21** — Shipped in the commit that moved this note (`mobile`). New Transaction now seeds its postings through the new `defaultPostingAccounts(assets, expenses, isOpen)` (`postings-utils.ts`). It picks the first open asset as FROM and the first open expense (else another open asset) as TO, by the ledger's ranking and `isOpenOn` for the entry's date. The receipt path keeps its parsed accounts only while they are open, and otherwise falls back to those defaults. Seeding waits until the close dates have settled: the new `useAccountCloseDatesState` returns them with a `settled` flag, and a failed read counts as settled. A cold start therefore can't seed a closed account. Tests in `postings-utils.test.ts` cover the note's ranking (`Qa20261020Old` first gives Checking → Transport), the no-open-expense fallback, and the seeding and receipt wiring, which fails with the screen change reverted. Not re-checked on the simulator, and the note's side observation about a stale cached ranking was not addressed.
