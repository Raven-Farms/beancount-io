# Transactions search is literal about Unicode form and spacing: "Café" misses "Café", and "whole foods" misses "Whole  Foods" and a no-break space

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile (or ledger). A3: `toPlainSearchFilter` (`mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/utils/plain-search-filter.ts:37-45`) escapes the text literally, and the ledger service compiles it as `new RegExp(value, "i")` (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/directive-filter.ts:172-201, 598-607`).

Code-level at `main` `bed2252b0`, from the reviewer's tsx run of the real helper, matched the way the server does:
- `"Café"` (composed, as iOS types it) doesn't match `"Café Rouge"` (decomposed), and the reverse also fails;
- `"whole foods"` doesn't match `"Whole  Foods Market"` (double space) or `"Whole Foods"` (a no-break space from a bank export);
- control: `"whole foods"` matches `"Whole Foods"`.

Not exercised on the device.

## Fix

Emit a tolerant pattern: turn runs of whitespace into `\s+` (which also matches NBSP in JS), and each composable letter into `(?:é|e\u0301)`. Land it with w21/401's apostrophe handling, or normalise on the server instead.

**Acceptance:** all three searches above find their rows. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Transactions-tab sibling of w21/410 and w21/460 (Merchants) and w21/401 (curly quotes). Searched `.pm/**` for NFC, NFD, normaliz, whitespace, "double space" and toPlainSearchFilter.

Resolved. The Transactions search pattern is now tolerant of how the same text is written: a run of whitespace in the typed text matches any run of whitespace (a double space, a no-break space from a bank export, via `\s+`), and each character that can be composed or decomposed matches either form (`(?:é|é)` with the combining accent), so "Café" finds a decomposed "Café Rouge" and the reverse, and "whole foods" finds "Whole  Foods Market" and "Whole Foods". The apostrophe and double-quote tolerance (w21/401) and the 256-character cut (w21/402, now by whole units) are kept, and the filter stays one lexable literal. Because merchant detail's fallback and the two-posting suggestions go through the same helper, their pinned expectations now show `\s+` for spaces; they and the helper's own tests were updated, and new tests cover the three searches in the note plus the length and lexing guarantees (the new ones fail against the old helper). Deviation: handled on the client rather than by normalising on the ledger service, so other clients' searches are unchanged; no native run.
