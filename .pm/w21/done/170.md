# Bills paid on the 1st and 15th are labelled "Every 2 weeks", and Next comes 2–3 days early

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. Semi-monthly bills (parking, payroll-linked transfers, some rents) read as biweekly, and the predicted date drifts early every cycle. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: semi-monthly gaps alternate 14/16/17 days, so the median lands in the 13–16-day biweekly band, and Next is last date + median gap. There is no semi-monthly cadence, and the month-day anchor applies only to monthly, quarterly and yearly cadences. w21/007 (done) left the weekly and biweekly day-count step as it was.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `698f8aba5`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture was 20 "QA PARKING" entries (`Expenses:Transport 120.00 USD` from Checking) on the 1st and 15th of each month, 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-15. Merchants → Recurring shows **"QA PARKING · Every 2 weeks · $120.00 · Next Oct 29, 2026"**; the real next payment is Nov 1.

## Where

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:60` (biweekly band) and `:339` (next = last + median).

## Fix

Detect a semi-monthly cadence when events cluster on two stable days of the month (for example 1/15, or 15/last day), label it "Twice a month", and predict the next stable day.

**Acceptance:** the fixture reads "Twice a month · Next Nov 1"; the 15th/last-day variant gives Oct 31. True 14-day cadences stay "Every 2 weeks". Add unit tests.

## Dedupe

w21/007 (done) covers monthly drift. Searched `.pm/**` for semi-monthly, "twice a month", biweekly and "Every 2 weeks".

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** `detectRecurrence` recognises a semi-monthly series before the gap bands. That means at least 4 events alternating between two days of the month at least 10 days apart: the first day, then the second within the same month (clamped to a short month's last day), then the first again next month. It reports cadence `semimonthly`, labelled "Twice a month" (`merchantsCadenceSemimonthly`, 13 locales), and predicts the next of those two days. The fixture reads "Twice a month · Next Nov 1", the 15th/last-day variant gives Oct 31 across February's 28th, and a true 14-day series stays "Every 2 weeks". Tests: `src/__tests__/detect-recurrence.test.ts` (fails with the detector reverted). Not re-run natively.

**Native recheck 2026-10-21** (same session; parking entries on the 1st and 15th, January to October 15): **QA PARKING · "Twice a month" · Next Nov 1, 2026**. **Fixed.** The row read $240.00 and 40 transactions only because this session's fixtures held each date twice; that was a test artefact, not an app bug.
