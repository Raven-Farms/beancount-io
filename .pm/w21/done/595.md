# In a BTC ledger, Accounts, Spending, Reports categories and merchant totals round BTC to 2 decimals ("Wallet (own) 0.00 BTC" for 0.004)

**Severity:** minor for crypto ledgers. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `f7f0c1de4`, proven under `tsx`): `components/account-table/account-table.tsx:319` calls `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency(row.value, currency)` with no scale, and `currencyMinorUnits` (`common/currency-util.ts:73`) returns 2 for any non-ISO commodity. The same unscaled call is in `select-spending-compare.ts:127-146`, `category-breakdown.tsx:249, 313, 401` and `format-merchant-total.ts:22-23`. With w21/451's fixture (operating currency BTC; Wallet holds 0.004 of its own, Cold 1.000, Exchange 0.500, Dust 0.003), the rows read "Assets 1.51 BTC", "Wallet (own) 0.00 BTC" and "Dust 0.00 BTC", while the account journal shows "0.004 BTC".

Not run natively (it needs an operating-currency swap to BTC).

## Fix

Pass the ledger's inferred or display precision for the currency (the scale the journal already uses) to these formatters.

**Acceptance:** that tree reads 1.507, 0.004 and 0.003 BTC. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w21/451 kept the own row but not its display; w21/290, w21/541 and w21/236 cover budgets, Cash flow and chart axes. Searched `.pm/**` for currencyMinorUnits and "0.00 BTC".

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — rather than plumbing a scale to each call site, the money formatters (`formatMoneyWithCurrency`, `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency`, which Accounts, Spending, Reports' categories and merchant totals all use) give a commodity (not a national currency: `isFiatCurrency`) the digits its value has, from two up to eight, so the note's tree reads 1.507, 0.004 and 0.003 BTC; national currencies keep their minor unit, a custom commodity that needs no more stays at two (551,620.00 MUSD), and a recorded scale still wins. Cases in `commodity-money-digits.test.ts` (`mobile`). A commodity value of 12.3456 now reads that way instead of 12.35.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e): not exercised. It needs a ledger whose operating currency is BTC, and the disposable ledger's USD operating currency wasn't swapped. The fix's unit cases cover the precision passed to these formatters.
