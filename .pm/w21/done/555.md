# A commodity bought in two currencies shows bare units ("1.0 BTC") on its Accounts row and account header, with no value and no "not in total" note

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/balance-display.ts:129-132`: the `partial` check accepts only a remainder in the same commodity (w21/278). When the remainder is a second cost currency, the operating-currency value is dropped and nothing is said.

Native (iPhone 17e at `e47e7641c`, disposable USD ledger, restored): `Assets:QaWallet` holds 0.5 BTC {30000.00 USD} and 0.5 BTC {28000.00 EUR}, with price BTC 60000.00 USD. The Accounts row reads just "1.0 BTC". The Assets total ($47,251.76) still includes $30,000 from it, and its caption says "At market value · 1 not in total". A single-currency lot reads "$30,000.00 at market".

## Fix

Show the operating-currency market value of the convertible part, plus a "Not in total: 14,000.00 EUR" note for the rest, as the root rows do.

**Acceptance:** that row reads "1 BTC · $30,000.00 at market · Not in total: 14,000.00 EUR". Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Gap in w21/278 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for balance-display together with partial, and "two currencies" together with cost.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — `selectBalanceDisplay` keeps the operating-currency value of a one-commodity balance whenever the valued map has one, whichever currencies the unconverted remainder is in (the old check allowed only the commodity itself), so the row reads the units, the value at market and the remainder as not in the total ("Not in total: 14,000.00 EUR" in the fixture); with nothing converted it stays plain units. Cases in `balance-display.test.ts` (`mobile`; they fail without the change).

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` with `c91da2301`, second launch; disposable ledger restored byte-for-byte): `Assets:QaWallet` holding 0.5 BTC {30000.00 USD} and 0.5 BTC {28000.00 EUR}, with BTC priced at 60000.00 USD. The Accounts row now reads "1.0 BTC · $30,000.00 at market · Not in total: 14,000 EUR" (it used to read just "1.0 BTC"). Fixed. One observation: "14,000 EUR" drops the cost's two decimals.
