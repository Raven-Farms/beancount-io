# Recurrence fixes leave two small regressions: an exact float compare adds "~" to a steady price, and the budget panel's batches keep firing after cancel

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile.

Code-level at `main` `61ad1916c`:
1. **w21/171:** `detect-recurrence.ts:398-401` compares `latest !== overall` as raw floats. With 7 months at 11.87 as one leg, then 3 as 10.99 + 0.88 legs (summed to 11.870000000000001 in `mapPayeeSeries`), the row reads "~$11.87" for an unchanged price. Fix: compare at money scale (`Math.abs(latest - overall) >= 0.005`) and round the summed amounts.
2. **w21/165:** `mapInBatches` (`common/map-in-batches.ts:12`, used at `use-budget-panel.ts:104, 139, 157`) has no abort. Cleanup only sets `cancelled`, so after a ledger switch or any write, every remaining `network-only` query still fires, and overlapping runs exceed the 12-in-flight ceiling. With 40 budgets that's 40 reads per write. Fix: pass a cancel check or `AbortSignal` and stop between batches.

**Acceptance:** a unit case for each fails before its fix.

## Dedupe

These are follow-ups to w21/165 and w21/171 (done). w21/268 is refunds. Searched `.pm/**` for isApproximate, RECENT_CHARGES, mapInBatches and BUDGET_PANEL_BATCH.

Both fixed, one failing-before test each. (1) w21/171: `mapPayeeSeries` rounds each summed point to ten decimals, removing the float noise of adding a charge's legs (10.99 + 0.88 is 11.870000000000001), and `detectRecurrence` compares the latest and long-run amounts with a round-off tolerance (`sameAmount`, 1e-9 relative) instead of `!==`, so the note's series (seven months at 11.87, then three charges split 10.99 + 0.88) reads Monthly at an exact $11.87 with no "~". A relative tolerance was used rather than the note's half-cent one, since a half-cent would hide a real change in a fractional commodity. (2) w21/165: `mapInBatches` takes an optional `cancelled` check and starts no further batch once it says so, returning what it has; the budget panel's effect passes its own `cancelled`, so after a ledger switch or a write the remaining `network-only` reads of a superseded run are not fired (40 budgets were 40 reads per write) and overlapping runs no longer exceed the 12-in-flight ceiling. Reads already in a batch still finish. Tests in `payee-series.test.ts` and `map-in-batches.test.ts` (the two behaviours fail with the sources reverted; the wiring is checked on the hook's source).
