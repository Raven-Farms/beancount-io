# Account detail's units view sums the cost of every costed commodity in the entry, so a fund exchange reads "+5 VOO · at cost +$0.00"

**Severity:** low-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `postingsCost` (`select-account-journal.ts:370-392`) never checks `posting.units.currency === units.currency`. Under `tsx`, an exchange of `-10 VTI {200 USD}` for `+5 VOO {400 USD}` gives `{"change":5,"units":{"currency":"VOO","cost":0}}`, although those units cost $2,000. Exchanges, mergers and splits booked as two lots all show a wrong basis.

Code-level at `main` `3e4352ec8`.

## Fix

Only add postings whose units currency is the shown commodity.

**Acceptance:** the exchange row reads "+5 VOO · at cost +$2,000.00".

## Dedupe

w21/124, 249 and 291 are about the valuation basis. Searched `.pm/**` for postingsCost, "at cost" and "units cost".

Resolved. `postingsCost` now takes the shown commodity and counts only postings whose units are in it, so exchanging 10 VTI {200 USD} for 5 VOO {400 USD} reads "+5 VOO · at cost +$2,000.00" (and −$2,000.00 on the VTI side) instead of summing both legs to $0.00. Regression test covers both sides of the exchange and fails without the change. Deviation: no native run.
