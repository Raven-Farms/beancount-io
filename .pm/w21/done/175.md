# An account opened under a collapsed parent is invisible after Done, which re-opens w21/056 for nested accounts

**Severity:** minor. After Open Account → Done, the user can't see the account they just created, so it looks like nothing happened. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: Open Account only navigates back. The Accounts table collapses levels below the top, and Equity, Income and Expenses by default, and nothing expands the new account's ancestors. w21/056's recheck covered only a top-level account.

Code-level at `main` `dbfdff600`.

## Scenario

Fresh launch → Accounts → + → Assets: `Bank:QaBrokerage` → Done. Assets lists Bank and Cash with no QaBrokerage, and Bank's balance is unchanged. It only shows after expanding Bank. `Expenses:QaGym` behaves the same, since Expenses starts collapsed.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/open-account-screen/open-account-screen.tsx:317-322`
- `mobile/src/components/account-table/flatten-rows.ts:41,47` (default collapse)
- `account-table.tsx:474` (expand state)

## Fix

Return the new account name from the open flow (route param or reactive var), have the Accounts table expand its ancestors and scroll to and briefly highlight it.

**Acceptance:** after opening `Assets:Bank:QaBrokerage`, Accounts shows it expanded and visible. The same holds for `Expenses:QaGym`.

## Dedupe

w21/056 (done) is the top-level case; collapse state across a ledger switch was dropped. Searched `.pm/**` for "open account" together with collapsed or expanded, and w21/056.

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** a successful Open Account sets `revealAccountVar` (`common/vars/reveal-account.ts`) to the new account. The Accounts table then opens its category and every ancestor row (`revealAccountOverrides` in `flatten-rows.ts`). Once the refreshed tree holds the row, the table scrolls it to mid-screen (with a fallback when the row isn't yet measured) and clears the request. `Assets:Bank:QaBrokerage` opens Assets and Bank, and `Expenses:QaGym` opens the collapsed-by-default Expenses.

Tests: `account-table/__tests__/flatten-rows.test.ts` (the wiring case fails with the table and screen reverted). Deviations: no brief highlight. Opened from a picker, the reveal waits until Accounts is next shown. Not re-run natively.

**Native recheck 2026-10-21** (iPhone 17e, iOS 27.0, `main` `19021a1dd`, Metro restarted with `--clear`; disposable QA ledger, restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`): with Expenses collapsed, Accounts → + → root **Expenses**, name "QaGym" ("Will be created as Expenses:QaGym") → Done returns to Accounts with **Expenses expanded** and **QaGym $0.00** listed. **Fixed** for a root-level reveal; a nested parent wasn't tried. Side note: Open Account's placeholder still reads "Bank:Checking" after switching the root to Expenses.
