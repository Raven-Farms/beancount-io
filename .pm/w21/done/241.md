# A transaction with no payee or narration is titled with the English word "Transaction" in account detail, in every language

**Severity:** minor. Localization, and the VoiceOver row label. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `entryTitle` falls back to the raw `directive_type`, which is never empty, so the row's translated fallback `row.title || t("transactions")` never runs.

Code-level at `main` `4ba42392a`. Run under Node: an entry with empty payee and narration gives `{ title: "Transaction", directiveType: "Transaction" }`.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-journal.ts:237-244` (`entryTitle`, the `directive_type` fallback). `account-entry-row.tsx:160` holds the unreachable translated fallback.

## Fix

For transactions, skip the `directive_type` fallback: use the counter-posting's account leaf (as the Transactions tab does), or let the translated fallback apply.

**Acceptance:** in Deutsch, a bare `2026-10-01 *` entry's row is titled with the counter account or "Transaktion", never "Transaction".

## Dedupe

w3/178 and w21/050 cover labels for non-transaction directives, and w21/212 the missing narration. Searched `.pm/**` for entryTitle and "empty narration".

Fixed in mobile: a transaction with no payee or narration no longer takes the English directive type as its title. In account detail, `entryTitle` returns an empty title for it and the row's own fallback now runs, as the singular `t("transaction")` ("Transaktion" in German; the fallback was the plural "Transactions", which would have read wrong for one entry). The same hole existed on the Transactions tab (`name = payee || narration || directive_type`), so `EntryRow` now titles a bare transaction with the translated word too, including the amount slot, which showed "Transaction" for an entry with nothing to total. Other directives (Open, Close, Balance, ...) keep their account or type, as w3/178 and w21/050 left them. Tests: four cases in `select-account-journal.test.ts` (empty title for a bare transaction with missing or empty fields, payee then narration preserved, other directives unchanged, and the wiring of both rows); two fail with the three files reverted. Deviation: the note's alternative of titling with the counter-posting's account leaf was not used, since the Transactions tab has no such rule; no device run in German.

**Native recheck (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, Metro restarted with `--clear`):** fixed. I added a bare `2026-08-29 *` transaction (no payee or narration) on Savings. In German, Savings detail titles the row **"Transaktion"**, singular and translated, and English shows "Transaction". The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`, and the app returned to English. The Transactions-tab row was not checked natively.
