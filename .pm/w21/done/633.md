# The example holdings sheet offers guests "Sign In" where owners see "Update prices", but signing in doesn't let them update anything

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `components/valuation-sheet/index.tsx:254-268` shows a "Sign In" button to guests in the slot where an owner sees "Update prices on the web", so it reads as "sign in to update these prices". After sign-in the example is still read-only and the button is gone. On the post-sign-in fallback (signed in but still in examples), it still says "Sign In" but goes to Home. Code-level at `main` `9d7337ab6`; not run natively (it needs signing out, or a deep link, which can't be accepted on this simulator).

## Fix

Show no button to guests, matching a signed-in reader of a public ledger (the m29 scope).

**Acceptance:** the guest's holdings sheet has no Sign In button. A render test covers it.
