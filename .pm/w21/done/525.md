# A guest journal link's filters follow the guest to every other example, and come back after Reset (w21/489 gap)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `ae229c963`, proven under `tsx`):
- `screens/examples/examples-layout.tsx:77-81`: `select` calls `updateGuestVisit({ ledgerId, resumeFailure: undefined })`.
- `common/guest/guest-state.ts:45`: the update merges (`{ ...visit, ...update }`), so `filters` survive.
- `example-ledger-provider.tsx:83-95`: the new example seeds its filter var with `{ ledgerId: <new>, filters: visit.filters }`. That passes Transactions' ledger-scope check.

After `startGuestVisit`, the link's `updateGuestVisit`, then `select` to nvidia, `guestVisitVar()` is `{"ledgerId":"open_ledger/nvidia","view":"transactions","filters":{…"account":"Expenses:Food:Restaurant"}}`.

Scenario: signed out, open `https://beancount.io/ledger/open_ledger/example/journal?account=Expenses:Food:Restaurant`, then switch to "Company finances" ▸ Transactions. It shows "1 filter" and an empty or narrowed journal. Reset on the first example, then switching away and back, re-applies the filter. Not run natively: `simctl openurl` deep links can't be accepted here (see w21/489).

## Fix

Clear `filters` (and `view`) when `select` switches examples, and on Reset.

**Acceptance:** a link's filters apply only to the example it named. Switching or Reset leaves them gone. A unit case is added.

Gap left by w21/489 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for updateGuestVisit, "guest" together with filter, and "examples" together with filters.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — a guest journal link's filters are spent once the example seeds its Transactions filter (the provider clears them from the visit), and switching examples clears them too, so they apply only to the example the link named and do not return after Reset. Cases in `link-filters-spent.test.ts` (`mobile`); not run natively (deep links cannot be accepted on the simulator).
