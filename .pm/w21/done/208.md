# A commodity with as many direct as inverse price quotes is labelled "at cost (no price)" although it's valued at market

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. The Accounts row says "$35,714.29 at cost" for a lot that cost $30,000, Home says "1 at cost (no price)" next to "Unrealized +$5,714.29", and the holdings sheet says "No price · At cost". **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile (and the ledger's pair listing). A3: `commodityPairsWithPrices`, behind `getLedgerCommodities` and matching Fava, keeps a pair only when one direction has strictly more quotes, so a tie drops it. The ledger's price map merges both directions and values the holding at market. Mobile then finds no pair and labels it as cost, against m26's "either direction" rule.

Code-level at `main` `6263a73fd`.

## Scenario

`0.5 QACOIN {60000.00 USD}`; `2026-09-01 price QACOIN 62000.00 USD`; `2026-10-01 price USD 0.0000140 QACOIN`. The row reads "0.5 QACOIN · $35,714.29 at cost". Control: adding a second direct quote makes it "at market".

## Where

- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/directives-report.ts:284`
- `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:163, 291`

## Fix

Classify priced vs at-cost from the same merged price map the ledger values with (expose priced pairs from the valuation path), or have mobile treat a pair as priced when either direction has any quote.

**Acceptance:** with the tie fixture, all three surfaces read "at market", matching the valuation.

## Dedupe

w21/124 is the cost-currency basis; w5/556 and w5/740 are CLI. Searched `.pm/**` for inverse, reciprocal, "forward pair" and getLedgerCommodities.

Fixed in the ledger service: `commodityPairsWithPrices` (behind `getLedgerCommodities`) dropped BOTH directions of a commodity quoted equally often each way, because it kept a direction only when its inverse had strictly fewer quotes; the commodity then had no pair while the ledger still valued it at market from the merged price map. A tie now keeps one direction (the one whose base sorts first), whose history already holds both directions' quotes. Mobile needed no change: `priceDates`/`dateIndex` already read either direction, so with the pair present the Accounts row, Home and the holdings sheet classify the lot as priced. Test: a tie fixture (the note's QACOIN case) lists exactly one pair with both dates, and both directions once when both are operating currencies; it fails with the fix reverted. Deviation: this diverges from the vendored Fava, which drops both directions of a tie (its docstring says "most commonly found"); the divergence is deliberate and noted in the code. Requires a ledger deploy for the app to see it; no simulator verification.
