# Regression from w21/418: the new visible Spending caption drops the sign of a net-refund month ("This month $380.00" for −$380)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 5 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `select-spending-compare.ts:145` formats the caption with the unsigned `formatMoneyWithCurrency`. Code-level at `main` `51ffdab6f`: in the reviewer's tsx run, rows `{09-30: 1200}` and `{10-31: −380}` ($120 spent, a $500 refund) give "This month $380.00 · Last month $1,200.00", so a refund reads as spending. The VoiceOver summary was unsigned before; the sighted caption is new in w21/418.

## Fix

Use `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency` for both amounts, in the caption and the summary.

**Acceptance:** the case above reads "This month −$380.00". A unit case is added.
