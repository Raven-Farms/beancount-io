# Cash flow drops flows under 0.005 units whatever the currency, so a BTC ledger loses its 0.004 BTC Food node and Savings is wrong (sibling of w21/451 and w21/290)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `5743f62d0`, proven under `tsx`): `reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts:13` (`HALF_CENT = 0.005`, used at 273, 287, 377, 385, 437, 460 and 467) treats anything under 0.005 as float residue regardless of currency. In a BTC ledger (Salary 0.05, Rent 0.02, Food 0.004 in one month), the nodes are only Cash flow, Salary, Rent and Savings. Savings is 0.030000000000000002, where it should be 0.026. VoiceOver says "Income 0.05 BTC, expenses 0.024 BTC, savings 0.03 BTC". A month with income 0.004 and expenses 0.003 gives no nodes ("No cash flow in this range") while the bar chart above still draws bars.

Not run natively (it needs an operating-currency swap to BTC).

## Fix

Scale the threshold to the currency's precision (or compare in integer minor units), as w21/451 did for Accounts.

**Acceptance:** that fixture keeps Food and gives Savings 0.026 BTC. Small-flow months still draw. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Sibling of w21/451 (Accounts) and w21/290 (Budget). Searched `.pm/**` for HALF_CENT and sankey together with BTC.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — `transformToSankeyData` takes the currency and scales what counts as residue to it: half a cent for a currency the app knows, 1e-9 for a commodity it has no symbol for (BTC), so the note's fixture keeps its 0.004 Food node and Savings reads 0.026, and a month of 0.004 in and 0.003 out still draws; float residue is still dropped. Cases in `sankey-data.test.ts` (`mobile`).
