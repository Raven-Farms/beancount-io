# On a ledger with renamed roots (`option "name_assets" "Activos"`), Open Account can only write `Assets:…`, and Reports' Recent transactions is always empty

**Severity:** major for non-English ledgers. **Estimate:** 45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: Beancount lets a ledger rename its five roots, and New Transaction, Add Budget and the Home spending card already honour `name_*`. Two screens still hardcode the English roots:

1. **Open Account.** On a ledger with `option "name_assets" "Activos"`, the root picker offers only Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income and Expenses.
   - Typing `Banco` composes `Assets:Banco` with Done enabled, and saving writes an `open` for a root that doesn't exist in that ledger.
   - Typing `Activos:Banco` isn't recognised as a root, so it becomes `Assets:Activos:Banco`.
   - There is no way to open an account under the ledger's real root.
2. **Reports.** The Recent transactions card filters postings by `startsWith("Income" | "Expenses")`. On a ledger with `name_income "Ingresos"` and `name_expenses "Gastos"`, it shows its empty state for every period, while the chart, Sankey and breakdowns above it, which come from the server's income statement, show activity.

Environment: `main` at `3bc8ae39c`. Code-level, after a review of the call sites. Not reproduced natively, because renaming the roots of the shared disposable QA ledger would invalidate every account other QA workers depend on. A dedicated synthetic ledger is needed for a native check.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/open-account-screen/open-account-screen.tsx`:
  - `:224-229` seeds the root from `ACCOUNT_ROOT_PREFIXES`.
  - `:258` calls `validateAccountName(account)` without the `rootPrefixes` parameter that `account-name.ts:106-109` already accepts.
  - `:276` builds the picker from the fixed list.
  - The screen's `useLedgerMeta` already carries `options.name_*`.
- `mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/reports-screen.tsx:232` passes `accountPrefix={["Income", "Expenses"]}` to `AccountTransactionsCard`, which filters in `selectors/select-account-transactions.ts:17-29`.

## Fix

Resolve the ledger's root names once from `useLedgerMeta().options`, as `common/ledger-meta-utils.ts:19-21` does. Use them for:
- the Open Account picker, seed, `peelTypedRoot` and `validateAccountName(account, roots)`
- the Reports `accountPrefix`

Keep the icon and colour keyed by the canonical root type.

**Acceptance:**
- On a synthetic ledger that renames all five roots, Open Account offers the renamed roots and writes `open Activos:Banco`.
- Reports' Recent transactions lists the Gastos and Ingresos postings of the selected period.
- English ledgers are unchanged.

## Also: Merchants (added 2026-10-21)

A third hard-coded root pair, which a code review of the Merchants screens found at `main` `d7a950d7f`:
- `mobile/src/screens/merchant-detail-screen/selectors/merchant-stats.ts:67` (`buildMerchantCurrencyTotalsBql`) filters with `account ~ '^Expenses' OR account ~ '^Income'`.
- `mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/payee-series.ts:48` (`buildPayeeSeriesBql`) uses the same filter.

On a ledger with `name_expenses "Gastos"` / `name_income "Ingresos"`, a merchant's detail shows its count and date range but no total line. Recurrence detection (`use-payee-recurrence.ts`) gets no rows, so there is no Recurring section and no cadence chip, and the toggle reads "not enough history". The fix is the same: build the regex from `getLedger.options.nameExpenses`/`nameIncome`, as `spending-card.tsx:90` already does.

## Also: transaction rows (added 2026-10-21)

`mobile/src/common/account-root/index.ts:26,35-36` (`getAccountRoot`) and `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/utils/transaction-display-utils.ts:73, 178` (`isBalanceSheet`, `"Expenses:"`) recognize only the English root names. On a renamed-roots ledger every row shows the grey ⇄ transfer glyph with an unsigned grey amount: a salary isn't green, and a purchase has no sign or category icon. Account detail uses the renamed roots for its colours, so the two surfaces disagree. The fix's assumption that rows "keep icon and colour keyed by the canonical root type" doesn't hold today; resolve roots from `ledgerMeta.options.name_*` here too.

**Resolved 2026-10-21** — Shipped in the commit that moved this note (`mobile`). The new `useLedgerRootNames()` (`common/hooks/use-ledger-root-names.ts`) reads the guarded ledger's `name_*` options from the cached read context, and every surface the note names now uses it:
- **Open Account:** the screen keeps the canonical root as state, which drives the icon and colour. `ledgerRootName` supplies the ledger's own name for the picker label, the prefix, `composeAccountName`, `peelTypedRoot`, `splitPrefillAccountName` and `validateAccountName`, so a renamed ledger writes `open Activos:Banco` and refuses `Assets:Banco`. The validator's ASCII-only root rule is gone, because the root must already be one of the ledger's own names.
- **Reports:** Recent transactions filters by the ledger's Income and Expenses names.
- **Merchants:** the payee-series and currency-totals BQL take the ledger's roots through `moneyRootsBqlFilter`. English ledgers send byte-identical statements.
- **Transaction rows:** `getAccountRoot`, `pickAccountRoot`, `orderAccountsByRootPriority`, `pickPrimaryAccount`, `matchCategory` and `resolveBrandDomain` take optional root names. `AccountTypeIcon`, the entry-row amount and the detail hero pass them, and `selectTransactionAmount`'s balance-sheet and Expenses checks use them. A `Gastos`/`Activos` purchase is now signed and gets its category glyph.

Tests in `src/__tests__/renamed-roots.test.ts` cover each surface on a ledger with all five roots renamed, plus English parity. That suite fails to load with the fix reverted. A synthetic renamed-roots ledger was not checked natively.
