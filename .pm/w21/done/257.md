# Payee and narration suggestions miss Turkish "İ" and NFD text, and show NFC and NFD spellings as two identical rows

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the text-input screen filters with a plain `toLowerCase().includes()` and dedupes on the raw string. `"İ".toLowerCase()` becomes `i` plus U+0307, and rustledger keeps payees as written (NFD stays NFD). This is the payee-screen counterpart of w21/060's account-search fix.

Code-level at `main` `26fb7ee69`. Run under Node:
- `iş` and `işbank` give `[]` against `İşBank ATM`.
- `café` gives `[]` against an NFD `Café Central`.
- The NFC and NFD spellings appear as two identical "Café Central" rows.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/components/text-input-screen/index.tsx:160, 169-173`.

## Fix

Compare in NFC with index-aligned lowercasing (reuse `lowerAligned` or a shared fold), and dedupe on the normalized form while keeping the ledger's spelling for display.

**Acceptance:** the three cases above match, and there is one row. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

w4/055 covered autocorrect only, and w21/082 (done) the multiline suggestions list. Searched `.pm/**` for text-input-screen, TextInputScreen, diacritic, and NFD together with payee.

Fixed in mobile: the payee and narration suggestions use a new `matchSuggestions` (`components/text-input-screen/match-suggestions.ts`) instead of inline `toLowerCase().includes()`. Text and query are folded to NFC, lower-cased, with the dot a lower-cased Turkish "İ" leaves (`i` + U+0307) removed, so `iş` and `işbank` match `İşBank ATM` and a composed `café` matches an NFD `Café Central` (and the reverse). Suggestions are de-duplicated on the NFC form, so the NFC and NFD spellings of one payee are one row, shown as the ledger first wrote it. Trimming, dropping empties, the substring rule, the empty-query-shows-all rule and the 30-row limit are unchanged. Tests: `match-suggestions.test.ts` covers the three cases in the note plus the unchanged behaviour; the existing vm harness for the screen stubs the new module. They fail with the screen reverted. Deviation: the fold is a targeted one (NFC, lowercase, İ dot) rather than a full diacritic-insensitive match, so "cafe" does not find "café".

**Native recheck (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, Metro restarted with `--clear`):** fixed for the NFD case. With an NFD `"Café Central"` payee on the disposable QA ledger, New Transaction → Payee → typing the composed `café` suggests **one** "Café Central" row. The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`, and nothing was saved. I did not check the Turkish `İ` case natively.
