# Amounts with 17 or more significant digits show wrong digits: "1.234567890123456690 ETH" for 1.234567890123456789 ETH

**Severity:** low (wrong figures for crypto). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `mobile/src/common/number-utils.ts:176` (`groupThousands`) calls `toFixed(scale)` on a JS number. `amountScale` keeps the written scale (18), but the value is already a float, so the float's binary noise is printed. This reaches `formatUnits` and `formatHolding`: the Accounts row, the account header and chart, and the journal's change and balance. The ledger sends exact strings (`ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/account-tree.ts:147`), so the loss happens on the client.

Native (iPhone 17e at `f7f0c1de4`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): `Assets:QaEth 1.234567890123456789 ETH`. The Accounts row reads "1.234567890123456690 ETH". Under `tsx`, "0.100000000000000006 ETH" for 0.1 at 18 decimals, and a chart change of "+0.100000000000000089 ETH".

## Fix

Format from the exact decimal string (or a decimal library) when the scale is more than about 15 significant digits. Do arithmetic on scaled integers (BigInt), or show at most 15 significant digits.

**Acceptance:** that row reads "1.234567890123456789 ETH", and 0.1 ETH at 18 decimals reads exactly. Unit cases are added.

None found. Searched `.pm/**` for "18 decimals", toFixed and groupThousands.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — two layers. A held amount that needs more than 15 significant digits keeps the ledger's exact string (`Holding.exact`, set by `amountIn` only then) and `formatHolding` prints it as written, so the Accounts row and header read "1.234567890123456789 ETH". Any other figure computed from doubles (a chart change, a balance difference) now shows digits a double does not hold as zeros instead of its binary noise (`fixedDigits`: 0.1 at 18 places reads "0.100000000000000000", not "…006"); that is honest to 15 significant digits, not exact, because the arithmetic stays in floats, which a decimal-through-the-pipeline rewrite would be needed to change. Cases in `exact-digits.test.ts` (`mobile`).
