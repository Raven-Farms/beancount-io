# A pre-entered future payment counts as already paid in Merchants: "Next" skips it, and a missed bill no longer shows Overdue

**Severity:** moderate. The recurring-merchant view exists to catch missed bills, and pre-entering next month's rent hides a missed one. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the payee series query has no upper date bound, and recurrence detection takes the latest event, future ones included, as "last paid". It computes Next and Overdue from that.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `3e99967ba`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-rec` was "QA LANDLORD" rent of 1500.00 from Checking on **2026-07-01, 08-01 and 09-01** (October missed) and **2026-11-01** (pre-entered). Today is 2026-10-21. Merchants → Recurring: **"QA LANDLORD · Monthly · $1,500.00 · Next Dec 2, 2026"**, and the All Merchants row's last date reads "Nov 1, 2026". Per the reviewer's selector run, without the 11-01 entry the same row reads **Overdue** (10-01 is past the grace period).

## Where (`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/`)

- `selectors/payee-series.ts:49` (`date >= cutoff`, no `<= today`)
- `selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:330-341` (`lastDay` includes future events)
- `merchant-row.tsx:202-208`

## Fix

Detect cadence from events on or before today. Compute Overdue from the last *past* event, and show a pre-entered future event as the upcoming occurrence ("Scheduled Nov 1") rather than as paid. Keep the last-date column at the last past occurrence, or label it as scheduled.

**Acceptance:**
- The fixture reads Overdue for October, plus "Scheduled Nov 1".
- Without the missed month, Next is Nov 1.
- Add a unit test in detect-recurrence for a future event.

## Dedupe

w21/done/007 (Next drifting late), w21/139 (VoiceOver) and w2/done/051 (text size) are different issues. Searched `.pm/**` for Next, Overdue, nextExpected, recurr together with future or pre-entered, and payee-series.

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** `detectRecurrence` now reads cadence, typical amount, Next and Overdue from events on or before today only, and reports the earliest future event as `scheduled`. With the fixture (Jul, Aug, Sep paid, Nov pre-entered) the row reads "Overdue · Scheduled Nov 1". Without the missed month, Next is Nov 1. In `merchantRowText`, a last date after today reads "Scheduled {date}" rather than passing as a payment made (`merchantsScheduled`, 13 locales).

The payee series query stays unbounded, since the scheduled date comes from it. Tests: `src/__tests__/detect-recurrence.test.ts` and `merchants-screen/__tests__/merchant-row-text.test.ts`, both failing with the change reverted. Not re-run natively.
