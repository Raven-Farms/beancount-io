# Transaction detail's Money flow drops lot cost and price: a sale reads "−4 QAX" with no cost basis or sale price (mobile counterpart of w4/220)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30–40 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: for a trade or a currency conversion, the cost basis (`{1.50 USD}`) and the price (`@ 2.00 USD`) are the facts a reader opens the detail to check. They are different amounts, and together they explain the realized gain. Mobile's Money flow prints only the units. w4/220 fixed the same omission in the dashboard's narrow Journal.

Environment: `main` at `072232314`, dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0). QA account, synthetic disposable ledger `mcp-agent-eval` with `commodity QAX`, a buy `Assets:…Brokerage 10 QAX {1.50 USD}` and a sale `Assets:…Brokerage -4 QAX {1.50 USD} @ 2.00 USD`, `Assets:Bank:Checking 8.00 USD`, `Income:Salary -2.00 USD` (restored byte-for-byte afterwards).

## Repro (observed)

Home → Recent Transactions → **QA BROKER · sell qax**. The hero reads +$8.00 (correct cash side), and Money flow reads:

- FROM Assets:Qa20261020Brokerage **−4 QAX**: no `{1.50 USD}` cost, no `@ 2.00 USD` price.
- TO Assets:Bank:Checking +$8.00.
- FROM Income:Salary −$2.00.

The cost and price appear only inside **Entry Context → Source**. A cross-currency purchase (`50.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD`) likewise shows just "+50.00 EUR". The account journal *does* add "−$6.00 at cost" under the row, so the data is available to the app.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-transaction-detail.ts:108-130` (`selectPostingRows`) maps each posting to `formatPostingUnits(p.units.number, p.units.currency)` only. `p.cost` and `p.price` (present in the journal payload; the selector's own tests construct `cost`) are never surfaced to the row component.

## Fix

Carry `cost` (per-unit number and currency, plus date or label when present) and `price` into `PostingDisplayRow`. Render a secondary line under the units, for example "at cost 1.50 USD · price 2.00 USD", or "@ 1.10 USD" for a priced conversion, formatted at each amount's recorded scale. Include it in the row's accessibility label. Keep plain single-currency postings unchanged.

**Acceptance:** the sale above shows "−4 QAX" with "cost 1.50 USD" and "price 2.00 USD". The EUR purchase shows "@ 1.10 USD". Ordinary postings are unchanged. Selector tests cover cost only, price only, both, and neither.
