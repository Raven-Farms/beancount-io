# In Persian, Money flow's cost/price line and the budget Delete alert still put the currency on the wrong side (amounts missed by w21/095 and w21/249)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `680e4879f`; tsx output laid out with CoreText, the same check w21/095 recorded):
- `transaction-detail-screen/selectors/select-transaction-detail.ts:261-263` (`postingBasisText`) passes `row.cost` and `row.price` into `postingCost` and `postingPrice` without `isolateLtr`.
- `budget-screen/components/budget-history-rows.tsx:133-138` does the same for the `budgetDeleteMessage` amount.

In فارسی, `-10 VTI {185.00 USD} @ 190.00 USD` lays out as "190.00$ تمیق · 185.00$ هدشم‌امت یاهب", and a code currency is swapped ("EUR 10.00"). The budget Delete alert shows "300.00$ …". Wrapping each amount in isolates fixes the order.

Native (iPhone 17e, فارسی after its restart; disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte; the app was switched back to English afterwards): the sale `-10 VTI {185.00 USD} @ 190.00 USD` shows, under "-10 VTI" in Money flow, "بهای تمام‌شده 185.00$ · قیمت 190.00$", with the dollar sign after each number, while the headline "+$1,900.00" and the other rows keep it before. ## Fix

Wrap the cost, price and budget amounts in `isolateLtr` like the other amounts.

**Acceptance:** in فارسی, the basis line reads "بهای تمام‌شده $185.00 · قیمت $190.00" with each amount in normal order, and the Delete alert likewise. Unit cases are added.
