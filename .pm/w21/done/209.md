# Home hides stale prices once the ledger has had no activity this month, while the Accounts root flags them

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. A user who stops updating in September never sees a stale-price warning on Home, while Accounts → Assets says "1 price not updated since Sep 25". **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: Home measures price age against the date of the balance series' last point, the month-end of the last transaction or price (Sep 30). The Accounts tree measures against today.

Code-level at `main` `6263a73fd`. Same family as w21/179, the account-detail header, but on Home.

## Scenario (today 2026-10-21; nothing dated October or later)

QAETH bought 08-04, prices on 08-04 and 09-25.
- Home: only "At market value", and the sheet shows "Price Sep 25" with no "Not updated" (age 5 days from Sep 30).
- Accounts: "At market value · 1 price not updated since Sep 25" (26 days).

## Where

- `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:285`
- `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/selectors/select-balance-sheet-series.ts:37-43`
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts:201-220, 395`

## Fix

Measure staleness against `min(today, valuation date)` for the headline (the Accounts rule), or always against today, so both surfaces agree.

**Acceptance:** the scenario shows the same stale warning on Home and on Accounts. Add a selector test.

## Dedupe

w21/179 is the account-detail header valuation date. Searched `.pm/**` for "figure date", "month end", stale together with Home, and endInclusive.

Fixed in mobile: `selectSeriesValuation` (Home's headline) no longer passes the series point's date to `selectValuation`, so price age is measured against today, as the Accounts tree does, instead of against the month-end of the last activity (Sep 30 in the scenario). The headline is the latest point, valued at the ledger's latest prices, so today is the right reference; with no date the price lookup also takes every price up to the latest, the same set the point already holds. The note's scenario (price Sep 25, today Oct 21, nothing dated October) now reads stale on Home as on Accounts. Test: added to `select-balance-sheet-series.test.ts`; it fails with the fix reverted. Deviation: no simulator verification; the ledger-side `interval-series.ts` point dating was left alone (the client measure is enough).
