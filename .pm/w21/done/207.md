# An account holding USD plus a commodity shows dollars to 9–10 decimals in its journal ("+$7,422.647539491")

**Severity:** moderate. Every row change, "Balance:" line and day header of the account reads with 9–10 decimals from the buy onward, while the Accounts row shows $8,427.65. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile (plus a ledger rounding option). A3: the ledger multiplies units × cost with Decimal-style scale addition (8 + 2 = 10 places). w3/189's fix then displays money at whatever precision the string carries, up to 20 digits, with no cap to the currency's display scale for at-cost money views.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `6263a73fd`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-exch`:
- `commodity QABTC`, open `Assets:Qa20261020Exch`
- 08-03: +1000.00 USD from Checking
- 08-04: `0.12345678 QABTC {60123.45 USD}` against −7422.65 USD from Checking
- 09-01: +5.00 USD

Accounts row: "Qa20261020Exch · $8,427.65". Detail:
- 4 August header "+$7,422.647539491"
- row "QA EXCH BUY · +$7,422.647539491 · Balance: $8,422.647539491"
- 1 September "Balance: **$8,427.647539491**"

## Where

## Fix

In money (converted) displays, round to the currency's display scale (2 for USD), and keep full precision only for unit amounts as recorded. Optionally quantize cost products in the ledger's `at_cost` conversion to the cost currency's precision.

**Acceptance:** the fixture's journal reads "+$7,422.65", "Balance: $8,422.65" and "$8,427.65", matching the Accounts row. Add a selector unit test.

## Dedupe

w3/189 (recorded scale), w21/100 (missing other-currency rows) and w21/160 (Money flow per-unit digits) are related. Searched `.pm/**` for moneyScale, format-account-journal and "decimal places".

Fixed in mobile: `selectAccountJournalRows` now caps a money row's display scale (`moneyScale`) at the most fraction digits the ledger recorded in that currency (`recordedScale`: the units, cost and price of the journal's postings in the currency, never below its minor units), instead of the scale of the converted string. A unit-times-cost product such as 0.12345678 x 60123.45 (7422.647539491) no longer sets the precision, so the fixture's journal reads "+$7,422.65", "Balance: $8,422.65" and "$8,427.65", and the day header's net rounds to the same scale. A sub-cent amount the ledger really recorded ($0.005) still shows. Units views are unaffected. Tests: two cases in `select-account-journal.test.ts`; they fail with the fix reverted. Deviations: the optional ledger-side quantization of `at_cost` products was not done (the client cap covers the display); journal pages whose entries carry no postings keep the old uncapped behavior; no simulator verification.
