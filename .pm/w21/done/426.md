# Account detail of a single-commodity account still shows no price note ("10 VEUR" with no value or stale warning), w21/291's account-detail acceptance

**Severity:** moderate (the same "add a price that exists" impact as w21/291). **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `selectAccountBalanceDisplay` returns a `units` display with no valuation (`account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-balance-series.ts:59`), and `balanceNotes` returns `[]` whenever `value` is null (`common/valuation.ts:466-467`). Under `tsx` with 291's own fixture, an account holding only 10 VEUR (priced in EUR) shows `{kind:"units", value:null}` and no notes. A HOOL-only account with a stale USD price shows only "$5,000.00 at market", with no "not updated since" warning. The Accounts root is fixed (w21/279 and 291 attach the valuation there); account detail isn't, and 291's note says detail wasn't opened natively.

Code-level at `main` `669120634`, from the regression audit.

## Fix

Attach `selectValuation(...)` to `units` displays in `selectAccountBalanceDisplay`, as 279 did for the root, and have `balanceNotes` emit the price attention and not-in-total notes when the units value is null but `valuesHoldings` is true.

**Acceptance:** the VEUR-only account's detail shows the stale-price note, and the HOOL-only account's detail shows "1 price not updated since …".

## Dedupe

This is a follow-up to w21/279 and 291; w21/179 and 420–422 differ. Searched `.pm/**` for selectAccountBalanceDisplay, VEUR and "account detail" with stale or price.

Resolved. `selectAccountBalanceDisplay` now attaches `selectValuation(...)` to a single-commodity (units) display, as the Accounts root does (w21/279), and `balanceNotes` emits the price-attention and not-in-total notes for a units display whose value is null (a holding priced only in its cost currency, like 10 VEUR priced in EUR), where it returned nothing. A HOOL-style account with a stale price now carries "prices not updated since …". Two display tests that compared the whole object were narrowed to the figure; new tests cover the stale price and the VEUR case and fail without the change.
