# Chart axes and VoiceOver summaries abbreviate amounts with English K/M/B/T ("$305.0B", "$1.5K") in every language

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. In German "B" isn't a recognized abbreviation ("Mrd." is), and German *Billion* means 10¹². Russian users see "$1.5K" where "1,5 тыс. $" is expected, and VoiceOver reads "Доходы 305.0B $". **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: a hand-rolled English-only compact formatter (`toFixed` plus a Latin suffix). w3/189 kept full amounts in English digit grouping so they match the `.bean` file, but compact values never appear in a `.bean` file, so that reasoning doesn't apply.

Code-level at `main` `55e5919a6`.

## Where (`mobile/src/`)

- `common/number-utils.ts:3-10` (the suffix table), `:12-44` (`shortNumber`) and `:174-184` (`formatShortMoneyWithCurrency`)
- Axes: `common/d3/scrollable-axis-chart.tsx:81` (Reports and Budget), `common/d3/bar-chart-d3.tsx:59` (Home Spending) and `common/d3/income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:96`
- Spoken summaries: `income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:160-174`, `budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:156-160` and `reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts:450-451`

## Fix

Use `Intl.NumberFormat(locale, { notation: "compact", maximumFractionDigits: 1 })` with the app locale (verify Hermes' ICU compact support on a device), or add translated per-locale suffix keys.

**Acceptance:** in Deutsch the axis reads "1,5 Tsd. $" or "305 Mrd. $", and in Русский "1,5 тыс. $". English is unchanged. Add a unit test per locale.

## Dedupe

w4/024, w4/060 and w4/054 (sign, zero and currency fixes, done) and w3/189 (full-amount grouping) are related. Searched `.pm/**` for shortNumber, formatShortMoneyWithCurrency, "compact notation", Mrd and млн.

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** `shortNumber` and `formatShortMoneyWithCurrency` (`common/number-utils.ts`) take the app locale. A per-language table, after CLDR's short compact forms, gives each of the 13 languages its decimal separator, abbreviations and currency position: "1,5 Tsd. $" and "305 Mrd. $" in German, "1,5 тыс. $" in Russian. English is unchanged ("$1.5K"), and an unknown language falls back to it.

All chart axes pass the locale (scrollable axis, Home Spending bars, income/expense chart), and so do the spoken summaries (income/expense chart, budget chart summary, Cash flow).

Deviation: hand-kept abbreviations rather than `Intl.NumberFormat`'s compact notation, which Hermes on iOS cannot be relied on for. Chinese uses 千/百万/十亿 multiples of 10³ rather than CLDR's 万/亿 grouping. Tests: `common/__tests__/number-utils.test.ts` (fails with the formatter reverted). Not checked on a device.

**Native recheck 2026-10-21** (iPhone 17e, iOS 27.0, `main` `61ad1916c`, Metro restarted with `--clear`): with the app in Deutsch, the Reports (Berichte) 6M axis reads **"4 Tsd. $ · 3 Tsd. $ · 2 Tsd. $ · 1 Tsd. $ · 0 $"**. **Fixed.** Separately, the Cashflow Sankey's German "Ersparnisse" label is truncated to "Ersparnis…" at this width. The app was switched back to English.
