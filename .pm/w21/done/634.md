# Signing in from the example drawer says "Could not complete sign-in" for every failure, including offline, instead of Welcome's specific reasons

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `screens/examples/examples-layout.tsx:56-61` and `components/ledger-drawer/ledger-drawer.tsx:729-731` use `t("signInFailed")` for every `failure.reason`, while Welcome (`screens/welcome/index.tsx:89-106`) maps unreachable, timeout, incompatible-server and mismatch cases to their own messages. Offline, the drawer's toast and its persistent line blame the sign-in itself. Code-level at `main` `9d7337ab6`; not run natively (it needs signing out, or a deep link, which can't be accepted on this simulator).

## Fix

Reuse Welcome's reason-to-message mapping in the drawer and examples layout.

**Acceptance:** offline sign-in from the example drawer reads "Couldn't reach the server". A unit case covers the mapping.

None found. Searched `.pm/**` for signInFailed.

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — The reason-to-message mapping moved out of Welcome into `welcome/failure-message.ts` (`signInFailureMessageKey`), and the example layout now builds its toast and the drawer's persistent line from it, so an offline sign-in from the drawer reads the server-unreachable message instead of "Could not complete sign-in". The drawer's `signInFailed` flag became a `signInFailure` message. Unit cases cover every reason and the wiring; two older harnesses learned the new module. Not run natively (needs signing out).
