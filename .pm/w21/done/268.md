# A refund counts as a recurring payment: Next moves, the amount turns approximate, and a short history stops being detected

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: recurrence detection turns every dated point into an event whatever its sign, mixes refunds into the median and spread, and requires every event on the bill's day for the day-of-month anchor.

Code-level at `main` `de881e646`. Run under Node, with Spotify at 11.99 USD on the 5th, January to October 2026:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Charges only | next 2026-11-05, exact amount |
| Plus a −11.99 refund on 10-09 | next 2026-11-09, `isApproximate` ("~$11.99") |
| Plus a refund on 03-09 instead | next 2026-11-04 |
| Jul–Oct charges plus a refund on 08-20 | not detected |
| Missed November charge, checked 2026-11-14 | Overdue without the refund; not Overdue with it |

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/merchants-screen/selectors/detect-recurrence.ts:253-275` (events), `:292-306` (amount median and spread) and `:147-161` (`monthDayAnchor`).

## Fix

Run cadence and anchor logic only on events with the payee's main sign, and keep refunds out of the amount statistics.

**Acceptance:** every refund case above matches the charges-only control. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

No merchant or recurrence item covers refunds. Searched `.pm/**` for refund, credit, "negative amount" and reversal together with merchant or recurrence.

Fixed. `detectRecurrence` and `hasEnoughHistory` now run on the payee's main sign only (`withoutCounterSign`: the sign most points carry, the larger total on a tie, zeros kept), so a refund or reversal no longer counts as an event. Against the note's Spotify cases: a late refund, a mid-history refund and a Jul–Oct series with a refund now match the charges-only control (same Next, exact amount, still detected), and a missed November charge stays Overdue with the refund on file.
