# Budget history rows, their VoiceOver Delete labels and the Delete alert show raw ISO dates in every language

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budget-history-rows.tsx:173` (row text), `:185` (the accessibility label of Delete) and `:123` (the `budgetDeleteMessage` alert) all use the raw `row.date`, while the same card's "Starts …" line uses `formatLedgerDate` (`budget-group-card.tsx:273`). Under `tsx`:
- `de` gives the row `2026-03-01` and the label "Budgeteintrag vom 2026-03-01 löschen, 500 USD", next to "Beginnt am 1. März 2026".
- `fa` and `zh` behave the same way.

VoiceOver reads the ISO date as a run of numbers.

Code-level at `main` `c8be51ed8`. Not run natively.

## Fix

Pass `formatLedgerDate(row.date, locale)` to all three, or `formatLedgerDateShort` for the dense row.

**Acceptance:** in German, the history row, the Delete label and the alert show `1. März 2026` or the short form.
