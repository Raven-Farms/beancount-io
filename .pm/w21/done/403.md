# On Android, the status bar icons turn white on the light theme after leaving Scan Receipt, and stay that way

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `receipt-capture-screen.tsx:241` mounts `<StatusBar style="light" />`, the only status-bar setting in `src/` and `app/`. On unmount, RN pops it (`StatusBar.js:412-416`) back to its built-in default `'default'` (`:241`), which is synced to Android every time (`:474`). Android's `WindowUtil.kt:100-113` turns dark icons on only for `"dark-content"`, and `StatusBarModule.setStyle` (`:159`) runs even in edge-to-edge mode. So after one Scan Receipt visit, the clock, battery and notification icons are white on the light header until the app restarts. iOS's default follows the interface style, so iOS is unaffected.

Code-level at `main` `41253a6e3`. It's Android-only, so it can't be driven on this iOS simulator.

## Fix

Mount one `<StatusBar style="auto" />` from `expo-status-bar` in the root layout, so leaving the camera restores the theme-following style.

**Acceptance:** on Android with the light theme, the status bar icons are dark again after closing Scan Receipt.
