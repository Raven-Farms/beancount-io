# Profile says "Add a photo on Gravatar" to users who have one whenever the image fails to load (offline or slow network)

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since w21/289, `UserAvatar`'s `onError` reports a fallback for any load failure (`components/user-avatar/index.tsx:34-38, 54-57`), not just Gravatar's `d=404`, and Profile maps it straight to `profileAddGravatar` (`profile/profile-screen.tsx:368-370`). An offline or flaky load shows a call to action to someone who already has a Gravatar.

Code-level at `main` `669120634`. The QA account's Gravatar loads normally (w21/289 recheck).

## Fix

Treat only a confirmed 404 as "no Gravatar" (a HEAD or fetch status check), and hide the caption on other failures.

**Acceptance:** with a Gravatar and the network off, Profile does not say "Add a photo on Gravatar".

## Dedupe

This is a follow-up to w21/289. Searched `.pm/**` for gravatar, profileAddGravatar and onFallbackChange.

Resolved. `UserAvatar` still shows the initials as soon as an image fails, but it now reports "no picture" to its caller only when the server confirms it: a new `imageIsMissing` (user-display) sends a HEAD request for the failed URL and counts a 404 only; no connection, a timeout or a 5xx leaves it unknown, so Profile keeps "Photo from Gravatar" instead of "Add a photo on Gravatar" for someone who has one. No picture given still reports at once. Tests drive the real component against a stubbed fetch (404 reports; offline and 503 do not) and the 503/offline case fails against the old component; the caption wiring pin was updated. Deviation: while unconfirmed the caption keeps its "from Gravatar" wording rather than being hidden, as the note suggested; no native run.
