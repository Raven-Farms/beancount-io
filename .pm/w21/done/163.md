# Account detail shows "−$0.00" (or "+$0.00") on a day whose entries net to zero

**Severity:** minor. A day that nets to zero reads as a signed non-zero total, which contradicts the rows beneath it. This is the account-detail sibling of w21/055, which fixed the same float residue in Reports. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the day-section total sums `r.change` as JS floats, then takes its sign from `> 0` / `< 0` with no rounding. 4.10 + 8.20 − 12.30 gives −1.776e-15, which formats as "−$0.00".

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `17565bec1`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-ad` added three `2026-08-30` "QA GROCER" entries: Groceries +4.10, +8.20 and −12.30, each against Cash. Accounts → Expenses: the **30 August section header reads "-$0.00"**, over rows −$12.30, +$8.20 and +$4.10. The reviewer computed "+$0.00" for Assets:Cash.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-journal.ts:247, 257-261`
- The units branch at `:250-256` has the same issue.
- `utils/format-account-journal-balance.ts:24` takes the sign.

## Fix

Sum with the exact-decimal helpers used elsewhere, or round `net` to the section's money scale before taking the sign. Show an exact zero unsigned, in the muted style.

**Acceptance:** the fixture's header reads "$0.00" on Expenses and on Cash. Add a selector unit test for [−12.3, 8.2, 4.1].

## Dedupe

w21/055 is the Reports twin (done); w21/075 is the New Transaction remainder. Searched `.pm/**` for "-$0.00", "+$0.00", float and "day total".

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** `groupAccountJournalRowsToSections` rounds each day's float sum to the section's display scale (units or money) before taking its sign, normalising −0 to 0. A day of [−12.30, +8.20, +4.10] now reads "$0.00" on both Expenses and Cash, and the "only other currencies moved" check (w21/100) sees that true zero too. A real day total keeps its sign. Test: `select-account-journal.test.ts` (fails with the fix reverted). Deviation: no muted style for an exact zero. Not re-run natively.
