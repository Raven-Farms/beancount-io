# No headings in journals, lists or cards, so VoiceOver's Headings rotor can't jump through a long journal

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: only 8 `accessibilityRole="header"` elements exist in the app (drawer, sheets, commit detail, account detail). Date sections, Merchants sections, account-picker sections and card titles aren't headers. w4/m7 t002 planned header roles that weren't applied here.

Code-level at `main` `aa4a9793a`.

## Where (`mobile/src/`)

- `screens/transactions-screen/date-section-header.tsx:73`, used by Transactions, account detail and merchant detail. On account detail, the day total is also read as a bare amount after the date.
- `screens/merchants-screen/merchants-screen.tsx:271`
- `screens/account-picker-screen/account-picker-screen.tsx:575`
- `components/dashboard-card/index.tsx:123` (Home and Reports card titles)

## Fix

Add `accessibilityRole="header"` to these, and give date headers a combined label ("August 22, 2026, total minus 60 dollars").

**Acceptance:** Accessibility Inspector shows the Header trait on Transactions date headers, and the Headings rotor steps day by day.
