# Receipt capture's preview, uploading, error and reveal states are silent to VoiceOver

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3:
- Retake, Upload and the error's Cancel/Retake buttons have no `accessibilityRole`.
- The busy overlay, the error text and the reveal appear with no announcement.
- The reveal moves on to New Transaction by itself after 1.2 s.

So a VoiceOver user taps Upload, the focused button disappears, and nothing is said, either on failure or on success.

Code-level at `main` `c612cae39`: `mobile/src/screens/receipt-capture-screen/preview-view.tsx:107-126, 130-141, 143-162`, `reveal-view.tsx`, and `receipt-capture-screen.tsx:143`. `grep -c accessibilityRole` finds 0 in `preview-view.tsx` and `reveal-view.tsx`, against 7 in `camera-view.tsx`.

## Fix

Add button roles. Announce the uploading/parsing label, the error and the reveal summary (`AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility`, `accessibilityLiveRegion` on Android), and move focus to the error text. Extend `accessibility-labels.test.ts`.

**Acceptance:** each state is announced, and every button reads as a button.
