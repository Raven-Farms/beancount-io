# A new every-two-weeks payment whose history spans February reads "Twice a month", with Next up to 6 days late

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `52a06aa36`, proven under `TZ=America/Los_Angeles tsx`): `detect-recurrence.ts:312-319` (`semiMonthlyDays`). Because February has 28 days, two of 4–6 biweekly payments share a day of the month (Feb 13 and Mar 13). That meets the `keptCount * 3 >= length` bar, and the ±3-day tolerance with one allowed miss then fits the 14-day series onto two monthly anchors. This is the same class as w21/408, in the twice-a-month path.

Fixture: "Sunrise Daycare" 1850 USD on 2026-01-02, 01-16, 01-30, 02-13, 02-27 and 03-13. With today pinned to 2026-03-20, Merchants ▸ Recurring reads "Twice a month · Next 2026-03-30", where it should be "Every 2 weeks · Next Mar 27". A sweep of every start day in 2024–25 misfires for 42, 56 and 50 of 730 start days with 4, 5 and 6 payments; every misfire spans a non-leap February (worst: Next 03-31 against 03-25). Series of 7 or more are correct.

Not run natively: it needs the device clock inside the series.

## Fix

When every gap is exactly 14 days (or 14 ± tolerance), classify it as every two weeks before trying twice a month.

**Acceptance:** that fixture reads Every 2 weeks with Next Mar 27, and the sweep has no misfires. Unit cases are added.
