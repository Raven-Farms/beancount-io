# A pending account reveal that never resolves keeps re-expanding Accounts rows the user collapses, even on another ledger

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: since w21/175 (`817c05ae8`), `revealAccountVar` is global and is cleared only once its row appears (`account-table.tsx:490-503`); a ledger switch doesn't clear it. While a reveal is pending, every `overrides` change re-runs the effect and re-opens any revealed ancestor the user collapsed. Example: open an account from a picker, switch ledger, then open Accounts. The other ledger's `Assets` / `Assets:Bank` open on their own and can't stay collapsed for the rest of the session.

Code-level at `main` `24df51bf0` (read, not run).

## Fix

Store `{ledgerId, account}` and drop the reveal on a mismatch. Clear it once the overrides have been applied and a refreshed tree still lacks the row.

**Acceptance:** after a ledger switch, collapsing `Assets` stays collapsed.

## Dedupe

This is a follow-up to w21/175 (done). Searched `.pm/**` for revealAccountVar and "open account" with collapsed or expanded.

Fixed. `revealAccountVar` now holds `{ledgerId, account}` (Open Account sets it with the ledger it opened in), and the Accounts table's effect: drops a reveal whose ledger is not the one shown, so it can never act on another ledger's tree; opens the revealed account's ancestors once per reveal (`openedReveal` ref) instead of on every `overrides` change, so a row the user collapses stays collapsed; and drops the reveal if, after opening, the row is not there (an ancestor collapsed in between) instead of waiting forever. The scroll-to-row and the clear on success are unchanged. Tests (`account-table/__tests__/reveal-account.test.ts`: the stored shape and the call site, the mismatch drop, the one-shot open and the hidden-row drop; the pinned wiring test in `flatten-rows.test.ts` updated for the new shape; three fail with the table reverted). Deviation: source checks of an effect in a component that needs the native runtime; not run on a device through a ledger switch.
