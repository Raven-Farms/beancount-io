# The pending "P" badge is white on amber, about 1.9:1 in dark mode and 2.7:1 in light mode

**Severity:** minor (accessibility). The flag that marks a transaction as pending fails WCAG contrast everywhere rows render (Transactions, Home recent, the Reports account card, merchant detail and account detail). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile.

Checked natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `c26bdfe94`, with `appearance dark` and app theme System, on the disposable QA ledger (fixture `2026-08-30 ! "QA PENDING"`, removed afterwards, ledger back at `f1a0b83c`). The Transactions row shows a small white "P" on the amber `#f0b24e` chip. The reviewer computed 1.88:1 in dark (`#f0b24e`) and 2.68:1 in light (`#e08a1e`).

## Root cause

The badge text color is hard-coded `"#fff"`:
- `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/entry-row/index.tsx:82-92` (`:91`)
- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/components/account-entry-row.tsx:74-84` (`:83`)

`theme.white` (charcoal in dark) would give about 9.4:1. `src/__tests__/text-style-contrast.test.ts` has no badge-on-warning case.

## Fix

Use a theme token with at least 4.5:1 on `theme.warning` in both themes, for example `theme.white` or a dedicated `onWarning` token. Add the pair to the contrast test.

**Acceptance:** the "P" badge text is at least 4.5:1 in light and dark, and the contrast test covers it.
