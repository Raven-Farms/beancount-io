# The pending ("!") badge is a literal English "P" in all 13 languages

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `680e4879f`): the letter is hard-coded in `transactions-screen/entry-row/index.tsx:226`, `account-detail-screen/components/account-entry-row.tsx:208` and `transaction-detail-screen/components/posting-row.tsx:189`. `t("pending")` exists in every locale (待确认, В ожидании, در انتظار), so a reader in 中文, Русский or فارسی sees a Latin "P" with no meaning on Transactions, Home Recent, account detail and Money flow for any `!` entry.

## Fix

Use a language-neutral marker (an icon, with `t("pending")` as its accessibility label), or a translated short form per locale.

**Acceptance:** in a non-Latin locale, the badge is not a Latin "P", and VoiceOver reads the translated "pending". Render tests are updated.
