# VoiceOver number readouts: the Reports chart's net reads "−$0.0000000000000036", and Home's budget rows round to "100% used" when under or over target, and to "0%" when something was spent

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `f7f0c1de4`, proven under `tsx`):
1. `common/d3/income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:183` sums monthly nets as floats with no clean-up, and `shortNumber`'s `fractionDigits` (`number-utils.ts:104`) prints up to 20 decimals. With nets +10.10, +20.20 and −30.30 over 3M, VoiceOver reads "net -$0.0000000000000036".
2. `home-screen/selectors/select-budget-panel.ts:249` uses `Math.round(row.progressPercent)`. With a $500 budget, spending $497.60 reads "100% used, Below target", $502.00 reads "100% used, Above target", and $2.00 reads "0% used".

Not run natively (VoiceOver).

## Fix

Round the net to the currency's minor units before formatting. For the percentage, floor below 100 and ceil above it, and use "less than 1%" for a tiny non-zero spend.

**Acceptance:** those cases read "net $0", "99% used", "101% used" and "less than 1% used". Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

See w21/373 (share rounding) and w21/543. Searched `.pm/**` for "100% used" and fractionDigits.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — the chart's spoken income, expense and net are summed then settled at money precision (`roundMoney`: the currency's minor unit, eight places for a commodity, never a negative zero), so a net of +10.10, +20.20 and -30.30 reads "$0". A budget row's spoken percent (`spokenPercent`) rounds down under 100 and up over it, is 100 only at the budget (float noise aside), and is "<1" for a small non-zero spend, which a screen reader says as "less than 1 percent" — the existing translated sentence takes it, so no new strings. $497.60 of $500 reads "990sed", $502.00 "1010sed", $2.00 "<10sed". Cases in `spoken-readouts.test.ts` (`mobile`); not run under VoiceOver.
