# Home's Spending counts expenses in raw units, while Reports (its See all) and the dashboard count at cost, so an expense posted at cost goes "Not in total"

**Severity:** low (needs an Expenses posting with a cost). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `0c0609c4e`, proven with the ledger's `intervalTotals` under `tsx`): `home-screen/components/spending-card.tsx:129, 150` uses `conversion: "units"`, while mobile Reports (`use-income-statement.ts:15`) and the dashboard (`overview/constants.ts:13`) use `at_cost`. With `Expenses:Charity 5 VOO {400.00 USD}` (a donation of shares) and `Expenses:Food 30.00` in October, Spending reads This month $30 with "Not in total: 5 VOO", while Reports reads $2,030.

Not run natively.

## Fix

Use `at_cost` for Spending, as Reports does.

**Acceptance:** that fixture reads $2,030 on Home and on Reports. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

See w21/011, w21/418 and w21/523 (Home vs Reports). Searched `.pm/**` for conversion together with units and spending-card.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — both of Home Spending's interval-totals reads (the month-long window and the to-today read) ask for `BALANCE_CONVERSION` (`at_cost`), as Reports and the dashboard do, so an expense posted at cost (5 VOO {400 USD}) counts as $2,000 instead of going "Not in total"; foreign-currency expenses with no cost still do. Cases in `spending-conversion.test.ts` (`mobile`).
