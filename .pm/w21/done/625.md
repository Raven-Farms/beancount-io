# At AX2, the bell badge's number outgrows its fixed 16pt red pill: the "1" is cut off and its lower half vanishes onto the white header

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `components/ledger-drawer/ledger-drawer-header.tsx:59-76` (styles) and `:122-126`. The pill is a fixed `height: 16`, while its text (`fontSize: 10, lineHeight: 14`) has no `maxFontSizeMultiplier`, so it scales with Dynamic Type (about 21pt with a 30pt line box at AX2). The 24pt bell doesn't scale.

Native (iPhone 17e at `9b513aede`, AX2 `accessibility-large`, restored to `large` afterwards; disposable ledger with one failing balance, restored byte-for-byte): Home's bell shows a red pill with a white "1" that sits low and is clipped. Its lower half falls outside the pill onto the white header and can't be seen. Under `tsx` with RN's multipliers, "99+" widens the pill toward the menu button.

## Fix

Cap the badge text (`maxFontSizeMultiplier` of about 1.3, as iOS badges do), or let the pill grow with the text and centre it.

**Acceptance:** at AX2, the count sits fully inside the pill. A render test pins the cap.
