# Settings sits behind the ledger guard, so a user with no usable ledger can't log out or delete their account

**Severity:** moderate. Log out and Delete account exist only in Settings, and App Store review requires account deletion to be reachable. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `SettingScreen` wraps its whole body in `<LedgerGuard>`, although nothing under it reads a ledger id. When the guard has no ledger it renders "Create ledger / Discover ledgers" instead, and when the selected ledger is gone it renders "ledger gone". Either way the signed-in user can't sign out, delete the account, or change language or theme until they create or pick a ledger.

Code-level at `main` `9e6f6c9ba`. Not driven natively, because the QA account owns ledgers and deleting one is out of bounds.

## Scenario

- **No ledgers:** sign up without a default ledger (`withDefaultLedger` defaults to false in backend-v2's auth resolver), or delete your last ledger on the web. Home shows the guard's Create / Discover screen, and its drawer header lives inside the guard too. Reaching Settings anyway (drawer swipe, or `beancount:///(app)/settings`) shows the same guard screen.
- **Gone ledger:** if the selected ledger was deleted elsewhere (w21/054), Settings shows "ledger gone".

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/setting/setting-screen.tsx:12` wraps the screen in `<LedgerGuard>`. `about.tsx` and `main-content.tsx` never call `useLedgerGuard`.
- Log out is only in `main-content.tsx` (`logout-action.ts`), and Delete account only in `main-content.tsx` / `delete-account.ts`. No other screen offers either (searched `src/` for logout/signOut).

## Fix

Drop `LedgerGuard` from `SettingScreen`. Also consider showing the drawer's identity row (its way into Settings) on the guard's empty screens, so the route is discoverable.

**Acceptance:**
- With no selected ledger, or a deleted one, Settings renders Profile, Log out and Delete account.
- A test asserts that `setting-screen.tsx` doesn't use `LedgerGuard`.
