# Budget "ALL": one future-dated entry makes every card's header describe next month at a $0 budget, unlike YTD and Home's budget panel

**Severity:** moderate. With a single pre-entered transaction, every budget card on ALL reads a future month: "Budget $0.00 · Actual $1,500.00 · Above target" on Rent, and "$0.00 · $0.00 · On target" with no meter on the others. YTD and Home show October correctly. This breaks w21/010's acceptance ("ALL shows the same header as YTD"). **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: ALL is the only budget range sent without a time filter. The ledger's interval series runs to the month of the last transaction, even a future one. The app pads forward to today but never trims periods after today, and a period that hasn't started gets a budget of 0.

Code-level at `main` `3e99967ba`. The reviewer ran the mobile selectors on the fixture. Same root as w21/166 (Reports), on the budget surface.

## Scenario (today 2026-10-21)

`2026-01-01 custom "budget" Expenses:Housing:Rent "monthly" 1500.00 USD`; rent of 1500 on 2026-10-01 and 2026-11-01; Groceries budget 300 with $120 spent in October.
- **ALL:** the last bar is NOV, in red. Rent reads Budget $0.00 · Actual $1,500.00 · Variance +$1,500.00 · Above target. Groceries reads $0.00 / $0.00 / On target.
- **YTD and Home:** October, Groceries $120 of its prorated budget.

## Where

- `mobile/src/screens/budget-screen/selectors/budget-labels.ts:125` (ALL has no filter)
- `hooks/use-budget-actuals.ts:80-93` (pads forward, never trims)
- `selectors/budget-selectors.ts:372-374`
- `select-budget-card-stats.ts:73-78`
- `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts:201-214`

## Fix

Trim ALL's series to the period containing today, or send ALL a window that ends today. Share the cap helper with w21/166.

**Acceptance:** with the fixture, ALL's headers match YTD's (October), and there's no NOV bar. Deleting the 11-01 entry changes nothing.

## Dedupe

w21/010 (ALL stopping at the last posting), w21/101 (pre-budget periods), w4/done/038 (YTD's calendar span) and w21/076 (Home proration) are related. Searched `.pm/**` for future-dated together with budget, ALL, timeSpanToFilter and periodsThrough.

**Resolution (2026-10-21):** `useBudgetActuals` now cuts ALL's unwindowed totals at the period containing today (`throughCurrentPeriod` in `budget-selectors.ts`, built on `currentPeriodRange`) before padding through today as w21/010 does. A pre-entered November entry no longer adds a NOV period, so ALL's headers describe October like YTD's, and deleting the 11-01 entry changes nothing. Windowed ranges already end today on the server. The cap is the budget counterpart of w21/166's `flowAnchorMonth`; it is a separate helper because budget periods are not always months. Tests: `budget-all-range.test.ts` (the wiring case fails with the hook reverted). Not re-run natively.

**Native recheck 2026-10-21** (same session; a monthly 1500 rent budget with rent on 2026-10-01 and a pre-entered 2026-11-01, restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`): Budget → **ALL** ends at **OCT**. The header reads Budget $1,016.13 · Actual $1,500.00 · Above target, the October period up to today, as YTD does, with no $0.00 November period. **Fixed.**
