# Accounts folds away real balances under half a cent in any currency: a BTC ledger's 0.004 BTC wallet row disappears and the rows stop adding up

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `sameAmount` (`mobile/src/components/account-list/select-account-list.ts:166-168`) treats anything under 0.005 as equal, regardless of currency. It decides the own row (`:143`), chain folding (`:187`) and pass-through (`:215`).

Code-level at `main` `17da1541a`. In the reviewer's tsx run, the operating currency is BTC, `Assets:Wallet` holds 0.004 BTC of its own, and `Assets:Wallet:Cold` holds 1.000 BTC. The rows come out as `Wallet:Cold 1`, `Exchange 0.5` and `Dust 0.003`, which add up to 1.503 under an Assets total of 1.504; the 0.004 own row is gone. w21/236 shows that BTC ledgers are supported. Not exercised on the device.

## Fix

Derive the tolerance from the currency's recorded scale (half a unit of the last recorded digit, via `currencyMinorUnits` or the data's decimals), or compare rounded decimal strings.

**Acceptance:** the fixture keeps the 0.004 BTC own row, and the rows add up to the category. USD behaviour is unchanged. Unit cases are added.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for sameAmount, 0.005, compressChain, fold together with chain, and BTC together with Accounts. w21/236 is BTC display and w21/055 is float residue in Reports.

Resolved. The Accounts tree no longer treats anything under half a cent as zero for every currency. The tolerance is now part of the tree rules (`TreeRules.same`) and comes from the currency: half a cent for a currency the app knows (USD and the rest, unchanged), and for a commodity it has no symbol for, such as BTC as an operating currency, half a unit of the finest digit recorded in the tree (never coarser than a cent, and bounded so float residue near 1e-15 still folds). A wallet's own 0.004 BTC now keeps its row, and the rows add up to the category. Tests cover the note's fixture, residue still folding, and USD's half-cent rule; the BTC case fails against the old tolerance. Deviation: no native run; the choice to key on "has a symbol" mirrors how the app already tells a stock ticker from a currency.
