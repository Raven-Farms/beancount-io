# The posting sign toggle reads only "Toggle amount sign" (plus "Selected" when negative): no plus or minus, and no posting named

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:383-394` uses `accessibilityLabel={t("toggleAmountSign")}` and `accessibilityState={{ selected: isNegative }}`, while the visible glyph is "+" or "−". Every posting's toggle sounds the same, and "Selected" is the only clue that the sign is negative. The amount field next to it does carry the account. This matters more given w21/518. Code-level at `main` `18664fa5e`; the spoken output is derived from the props (and proven under `tsx` where a builder is involved), not from a VoiceOver run.

## Fix

Label it with the account and the current sign ("Sign for Assets:Bank:Checking: minus"), using `accessibilityValue` for plus or minus rather than `selected`.

**Acceptance:** VoiceOver reads the account and the current sign, and the reading changes after a double-tap. A render test covers it.

## Dedupe

w21/232 and w21/518 cover the sign logic, not the label. Searched `.pm/**` for toggleAmountSign.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — the posting sign toggle is labelled with its account ("Sign for Assets:Cash"; the old generic label when no account is chosen yet) and carries the current sign as its value ("Plus" or "Minus"), replacing the `selected` state, so every toggle is told apart and the reading changes after a double-tap. New keys `postingSignToggle`, `signPlus`, `signMinus` in all 13 languages (German, French, Dutch and Slovak keep "Plus", and German "Minus", recorded in `known-gaps.ts`). Source-level cases in `sign-toggle-label.test.ts` (`mobile`); not run under VoiceOver, and the translations are mine.
