# Reports silently drops spending in other currencies; Home and Accounts say "not in total", Reports says nothing

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: w4/m11 made Home and Accounts disclose amounts left out of a converted total ("1 not in total", "Not in total: 150 EUR"). Reports is the screen people use to see what they spent, and it omits a 50 EUR travel expense from both the Expenses total and the breakdown, with no hint that anything is missing.

Environment: `main` at `c3d496791`+, dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0). QA account, synthetic disposable ledger `mcp-agent-eval` (operating currency USD) with `Assets:Bank:EuroAcct` and `Expenses:Travel` (EUR) and a 2026-09-05 `-50.00 EUR` travel expense. Restored byte-for-byte afterwards.

## Repro

1. Home: Net Worth shows **"1 not in total"**. Accounts: Bank shows **"Not in total: 150 EUR"**, and Expenses shows **"1 not in total"**. Correct disclosures.
2. Reports → **6M**. The Expenses card reads **$4,076.00**, with Housing:Rent, Food and Transport rows. There is no Travel row and no "not in total" note. The income/expense chart and the Cash flow chart likewise show only USD.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/selectors/select-ranged-account-tree.ts:97-155` reads each node's balance with `resolveCurrencyBalance(balances, currency)`, the active currency only. Other currencies are discarded before the breakdown, the chart and the Sankey are built. The disclosure helper (`mobile/src/common/balance-display.ts`, w4/m11) is not used on Reports.

## Fix

While building the ranged tree, collect per-node amounts in currencies other than the active one. Show a "Not in total: 50 EUR" (`notInTotal`) caption under the Expenses and Income card totals, and under affected category rows, reusing `balance-display.ts`. Mention it in the cards' accessibility summaries. Charts can stay single-currency, but the card under them must disclose the omission.

**Acceptance:** with the repro fixture, Reports 6M shows Expenses $4,076.00 with "Not in total: 50 EUR", and the Travel category shows as a not-in-total row or note. A USD-only ledger shows no caption. A unit test feeds a two-currency tree and asserts the disclosed amounts.

Same root as w21/058 (`resolveCurrencyBalance` / `operatingKey`'s USD fallback relabels USD as the operating currency in non-USD ledgers). Fix both together.

w4/m11 (done) added not-in-total disclosure to Home and Accounts; Reports was not in its scope. w3/500 is the dashboard's market-value period issue. Searched `.pm/**` for "not in total" with Reports and for multi-currency Reports. No item.
