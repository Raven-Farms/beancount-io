# Home's lower cards keep their old text measurements when text size changes with the app open

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 30–45 minutes. Owner: mobile. A2: users of larger text often adjust it from Control Center while the app is running. The upper Home cards (charts, header, journal date headers) already rebuild on a size change (`mobile/AGENTS.md`, "Live text-size changes — key the Text on `fontScale`"). The cards below the fold were never covered, so they clip or leave blank gaps until the app is relaunched.

Environment: `main` at `890072e30` (clean worktree served by Metro), dev build `io.beancount.ios` 1.20261002.50 on iPhone 17e (iOS 27.0), light, English. QA account, synthetic disposable ledger `mcp-agent-eval`. AX2 (`accessibility-large`) is within the supported ceiling (`.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`).

## Repro

1. Open Home at the default `large` size. With the app open, run `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-large`, then scroll to Spending.
2. **Spending:** the subtitle "This month vs. last month" renders at the larger size but stays in its old frame, so the bottom of the glyphs is cut off.
3. Set `content_size large` again with the app open. **Budget**'s empty-state line and every **Latest Updates** row keep their enlarged heights, leaving tall blank bands under small text.
4. **Control:** terminate and relaunch at each size. All three cards lay out correctly. The Net Worth chart card and Recent Transactions header remeasure live (`account-charts-card.tsx:154-202` keys on `fontScale`).

## Root cause

The texts in `mobile/src/screens/home-screen/components/spending-card.tsx` (subtitle style, lines 29-34), `budget-card.tsx` (empty-state copy and rows) and `feed-card.tsx` (row title and meta) have props that don't change with font scale. iOS reuses their native measurement, which is exactly the case `mobile/AGENTS.md` describes.

## Fix

Follow the documented pattern: key only the affected `Text` nodes (or their smallest containers) on `useWindowDimensions().fontScale`, as `DateSectionHeader` and `TimeRangePills` do. Don't key a whole card or list, so scroll position and query state survive. Extend `src/__tests__/` live font-scale coverage (the m37 test suite) with these three owners.

**Acceptance:** repeating steps 1–3 with the app open shows no clipped subtitle and no blank bands, matching a cold launch at each size, in light and dark. The font-scale coverage test lists Spending, Budget and Latest Updates.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for fontScale, live text-size and remeasure, together with feed / spending / budget card. The m37 work (journal date headers, filter labels, range pills, picker rows) and "Reflow enlarged Home card titles beside See all" (`d0c2ab216`) covered other owners. No item names these three cards.
