# Closed accounts that still hold balances disappear from Accounts when they net to zero under their parent

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: a closed account isn't in the listed set (`select-account-list.ts:137`). When its siblings net to exactly zero, the parent's value is 0, nothing keeps it (`graftZeroAccounts`, `:413-437`), and it is dropped with its non-zero children. w21/099 explicitly wants a closed account that still holds a balance to stay visible. Under `tsx`, Receivable's closed children Alice +20 and Bob −20 vanish entirely; with both open they list.

Code-level at `main` `3e4352ec8`.

## Fix

Keep a node when any descendant survives the filters, and never drop a node with kept or non-zero children.

**Acceptance:** closed Alice +20 and Bob −20 still show under Receivable.

## Dedupe

w21/099 set the intent, and w21/280 is the ancestor case. Searched `.pm/**` for graftZeroAccounts, "closed with balance" and "net zero parent".

Resolved. The zero-value filter in `select-account-list.ts` is now one `spared` check (holds a value, is listed, or has a row beneath it) used for sub-accounts and top-level rows alike, so Receivable's closed Alice +20 and Bob −20 stay visible under a parent that nets to zero. A parent holding nothing anywhere is still dropped (pinned). The new test fails without the change. Deviation: no native run.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `cbcb20c32`): with Assets:QaNet:Plus +100.00 and Assets:QaNet:Minus −100.00 USD (disposable ledger, restored), the Accounts tab keeps "QaNet $0.00" with an expand chevron under Assets instead of dropping it. Fixed.
