# w21/435 is incomplete: a resumed Create Ledger draft with an opening account still reaches Review and Create with no balance, silently

**Severity:** low-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `create-ledger-draft.ts:98-101` reopens the draft on the form, but `create-ledger-screen.tsx:226-229` accepts an empty `openingAmount` (the field is "(optional)", and `openingAmountValue("")` is `""`, not null). Next passes, and `openingSelection` returns null, so no `setup.accounts` is sent.

Reproduced natively (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro at `5b9620947`). I chose Assets:Cash with an amount, went to Review ("Opening balance: Assets:Cash 2") and left. Reopening resumed on the form with Assets:Cash selected and the amount empty. Tapping Next opened Review with no "Opening balance" line and Create enabled. Create wasn't tapped; the draft was reset to "Decide later".

## Fix

When restoring a draft with an `openingAccount`, set an `openingAmount` error ("re-enter the balance") and require a value or "Decide later" while that flag is set.

**Acceptance:** after the steps, Next is refused until the amount is re-entered or the account cleared. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

Follow-up to w21/435 (done; its note assumed form validation would stop Create). Searched `.pm/**` for restoreCreateLedgerDraft and openingAmount.

Fixed. A resumed draft that had an opening account now sets `amountLost`; the form schema refuses Next with `createLedgerAmountReenter` ("Enter the opening balance again, or choose Decide later…", 13 locales) while `openingAmountOwed(amountLost, account, amount)` holds, i.e. until the amount is entered again or the account is cleared with Decide later. Unit cases cover the rule; a source check pins the schema and restore wiring; reverting the screen fails the suite. Mobile gates and knip green. Not re-run natively.
