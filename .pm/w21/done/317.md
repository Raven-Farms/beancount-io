# On a range change, the balance chart's end dot and cursor jump to the new curve while the line is still animating

**Severity:** cosmetic (about 450 ms per change). **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the resting end dot and the scrub cursor read the new series' final coordinates immediately, while the line and area morph through transition frames.

Code-level at `main` `2e850e737`. A reviewer drove the real `resampleSeries` / `lerpSeries` with copied domain mapping, going from 6M `[10000, 11000, 12000, 11800, 11400, 11000]` to ALL (13 points ending at 11000, peak 12000). At progress 0, 0.25 and 0.5, the line's end is at y = 85.0, 53.1 and 43.9, while the dot sits at 37.1, floating up to 48 px off the line. It happens whenever the latest value isn't the window's extreme.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/common/d3/interactive-line-chart.tsx:836-845` (the dot), `:726-734` (the cursor), and `:587-635` (the morph frames).

## Fix

While transition frames are active, take the dot's and cursor's y from the same blended series and domain at the current progress (in the worklet), or hide them until the transition ends.

**Acceptance:** during a 6M → ALL change, the dot stays on the line's end throughout.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for "end dot", endDot, "resting dot" and morph.

Fixed with the note's second option (hide until the transition ends). The resting end dot, the scrub cursor and its guide line now show only once the morph has landed (`morph.value >= 1`, read in their animated-props worklets): on a range change they no longer jump to the new curve's final coordinates while the line and area are still animating, and the dot re-appears on the new line's end. The shared `morph` value is 1 at rest and while no transition runs, so nothing changes outside a range change. Deviation: the dot is absent for the ~450 ms of the morph rather than tracking the blended line; tracking would need the blended series and its y-domain in the worklet, which the existing frames do not carry. Source checks added to `common/d3/__tests__/chart-accessibility.test.ts` (fail with the chart reverted). Mobile gates and deadcode green; not watched on a device.
