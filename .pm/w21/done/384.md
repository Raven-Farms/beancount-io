# Accounts and Reports carry their expand/collapse state over to another ledger

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `account-table.tsx:481` and `category-breakdown.tsx:214` keep `overrides`, keyed by `"assets"`, `"expenses"` and account paths, in component state, and `accounts-screen.tsx:166-169` remounts the table only when the new ledger has no cached data. Under `tsx` with the real `flattenRows`, ledger B renders fresh as `v assets / > expenses`. With A's overrides (`{assets:false, expenses:true, "Expenses:Food":true}`) it renders `> assets / v expenses / v Expenses:Food …`, a layout the user never chose on B. The scroll position carries over too. The file browser path, Transactions search and the chart measurements already scope this kind of state by ledger.

Code-level at `main` `c8b7b6ea2`. Not run natively.

## Fix

Key `AccountTable` and `CategoryBreakdown` by `ledgerId`, or store `{ledgerId, overrides}` and reset on a mismatch.

**Acceptance:** after a switch, the new ledger's Accounts and Reports trees open in their default state.
