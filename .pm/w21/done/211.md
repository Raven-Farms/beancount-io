# A row's category icon compares legs across currencies, so a $500 flight that earns 1,500 miles shows the green Income tile beside "−$500.00"

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. The icon contradicts the amount it sits next to, on Transactions, Home Recent, Reports and merchant detail (all one `EntryRow`) and on the detail header. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `pickPrimaryAccount` gives the icon to the category leg (Expenses, Income or Equity) with the largest *raw* absolute amount, comparing across currencies, so 1500 MILES beats 500 USD. w21/057 fixed the same mistake for the amount only. Likewise, `Groceries 3000 JPY` plus `Transport 150 USD` shows fork-and-knife.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `709af935d`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-rows` was "QA AIRLINE": Visa −500.00 USD, Transport +500.00 USD, `Assets:Qa20261020Miles` +1500 QAMILES and `Income:Qa20261020Miles` −1500 QAMILES. Transactions shows **"QA AIRLINE · flight · −$500.00" with the green cash (income) tile**; it should be the car tile.

## Where

`mobile/src/common/tx-category/index.ts:43-58` (`pickPrimaryAccount`) and `mobile/src/screens/transactions-screen/utils/entry-utils.ts:40`.

## Fix

Pick the icon leg from the same leg and currency the headline amount uses, or prefer category legs in the operating currency before comparing magnitudes.

**Acceptance:** the fixture shows the car tile. A JPY plus USD mix picks the operating-currency leg. Add unit tests.

## Dedupe

w21/done/057 (amount only; its repro listed "income icon"). Searched `.pm/**` for pickPrimaryAccount, "income icon", "category leg" and "row icon".

Fixed in mobile: `pickPrimaryAccount` no longer compares category-leg magnitudes across currencies. `PostingLite` carries an optional `currency` (set by `getEntryPostings`, the account journal's postings and the transaction detail hero), and legs are compared within one currency chosen by `comparableCurrency`: the ledger's operating currency when a leg is in it (`AccountTypeIcon` reads it with `useOperatingCurrency`), else the currency an Expenses leg is in, else the alphabetically first, so posting order never matters. The note's flight (-500 USD Transport, 1500 MILES earned) now takes the Transport tile, and a JPY/USD grocery-and-transport mix takes the operating-currency leg. Postings without a currency behave as before. Tests: two cases in `tx-category.test.ts` (flight in both orders, JPY/USD mix both ways) fail with the fix reverted; three pinned `getEntryPostings` / wiring expectations were updated for the new field. Deviation: no simulator verification.

**Native recheck (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, Metro restarted with `--clear`):** fixed. I re-ran the note's `; qa-20261020-rows` fixture, "QA AIRLINE" (Visa −500 USD, Transport +500 USD, +1500 QAMILES to Assets and −1500 QAMILES from Income).
