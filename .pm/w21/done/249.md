# In Persian, the at-market / at-cost note and the "Not in total" list still put the minus sign on the wrong side

**Severity:** minor to moderate for Persian users. A negative amount reads as positive with a stray trailing minus. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/095 wrapped the journal, cost-basis and off-by amounts in `isolateLtr`. Two other notes interpolate signed amounts into Persian sentences unwrapped.

Code-level at `main` `6a7949ccc`. A reviewer laid the strings out with macOS CoreText (as w21/095 did):
```
-$540.00 به ارزش بازار              → رازاب شزرا هب $540.00-
⁦-$540.00⁩ به ارزش بازار (isolated)  → رازاب شزرا هب -$540.00
در جمع لحاظ نشده: -500 EUR, 20 GBP   → EUR, 20 GBP 500- :هدشن ظاحل عمج رد
```
Not driven natively, because switching to فارسی forces an RTL restart of the shared simulator.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/common/valuation.ts:428-430`: the `atMarket` / `atCost` note uses `formatSignedMoneyWithCurrency` without `isolateLtr`. Lines 403-404 wrap the cost line correctly.
- `mobile/src/common/balance-display.ts:136-137` and `mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/selectors/select-ranged-account-tree.ts:242-247`: the `notInTotal` holdings list.
- The strings are at `src/translations/fa.ts:210, 213, 214`.
- Shown under account-table rows, the account detail header and the Reports card. For example, a EUR credit card in a USD ledger shows "≈ −$540.00 at market".

## Fix

Wrap each amount in `isolateLtr`. For `notInTotal`, wrap each holding separately so the list order survives. Extend `bidi.test.ts`'s wiring check to these sites.

**Acceptance:** the CoreText layout of both notes in fa keeps "-$540.00" and "-500 EUR" intact, and the wiring check fails with the change reverted.
