# In a multi-currency account, journal rows and day totals show "$0.00" for movements in other currencies

**Severity:** moderate. A 500 EUR deposit reads as a no-op, and only the header's "1 not in total" hints that anything is missing. **Estimate:** 1 hour. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/058 made the account journal read only the operating currency for an account holding two or more currencies. The header and the Accounts row disclose the excluded holdings, but individual rows and day totals drop them silently.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `3504fd3cf`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-cl` posted 1000.00 USD to `Assets:Qa20261020Wise` on 5 Aug, then 500.00 EUR on 6 Aug.
- The Accounts row reads "$1,000.00 · Not in total: 500 EUR". That is correct.
- Detail header: "Balance · 1 not in total · $1,000.00".
- **6 Aug section: header "$0.00"; row "QA WISE EUR · $0.00" in the muted zero style, with "Balance: $1,000.00".**
- 5 Aug: "+$1,000.00". That is correct.

## Root cause

- `mobile/src/screens/account-detail-screen/selectors/select-account-journal.ts:324-332` uses `amountIn(item.change, currency)` for rows, and `:247-261` does the same for day totals. A `{EUR: 500}` change therefore reads as 0. The row is drawn by `components/account-entry-row.tsx:137-142`.
- The test "reads each map in the active currency only" pins the 0.
- The units branch at `:334-345` has the mirror problem: once an account holds only ITOT, its earlier cash deposits read "+0 ITOT".

## Fix

When a row's or a day's change has no amount in the display currency but has other currencies, show those amounts in their own currency, for example "+€500.00" with a "not in total" style. Don't render a zero. Keep the running balance in the display currency, as it is now.

**Acceptance:**
- The fixture's 6 Aug row and day header show "+€500.00", or "+500.00 EUR", marked as not in total.
- USD rows are unchanged.
- Update the pinned unit test.

**Resolved 2026-10-21** — Shipped in the commit that moved this note (`mobile`). An account-journal row that moved nothing in the currency shown, but moved other currencies, now carries them as `otherChanges`. This applies to the operating currency, or the account's commodity in units mode, which covers the "+0 ITOT" mirror case. The row shows them signed and code-suffixed ("+500.00 EUR") in the neutral colour, with a "Not in total" note that VoiceOver also reads, instead of "$0.00". A day whose shown-currency net is zero sums its rows' other currencies the same way, so its header reads "+500.00 EUR". The running balance stays in the shown currency, and USD rows are unchanged.
