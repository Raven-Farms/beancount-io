# Bar charts never scale below 1 unit, so a BTC (or other high-value) ledger draws its spending as slivers on a 0–1 axis

**Severity:** minor. It affects ledgers whose operating currency is BTC, ETH or another unit with sub-1 amounts, on both Home's Spending card and Reports' income/expense chart. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `barChartValueDomain` returns `[min(…, 0), max(…, 1)]`. The `1` was meant to stop an all-zero series collapsing, but it floors every series at 1.

Code-level at `main` `68c1c39cc`. Run under Node: a series of `[0.042, 0.031]` gets domain `[0, 1]`, ticks `0 BTC | 0.2 BTC | … | 1 BTC`, and bars 7.1 px and 5.3 px tall out of 170. A USD `[420, 310]` series correctly gets `[0, 450]`.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/common/d3/bar-chart-domain.ts:9`: `Math.max(...numbers, 1)`.

## Fix

Use `[0, 1]` only when every value is 0. Otherwise take the real max and let `.nice()` round it. `shortNumber` keeps one decimal, so ticks such as 0.01 / 0.02 would all read "0.0"; give small steps enough precision in the same change.

**Acceptance:** `[0.042, 0.031]` gets a domain near `[0, 0.05]` with distinct tick labels. All-zero and USD series are unchanged. Add unit cases next to the existing domain tests.
