# Merchants splits an NFC and an NFD spelling of the same payee into two identical-looking merchants (sibling of w21/256 and 257)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/merchant-key.ts:8-9` keys on `payee.trim().toLowerCase()` with no NFC, and `merchants-screen/selectors/aggregate-payees.ts:133-135` groups by `toLowerCase()`. Under `tsx`, `merchantKey("Café Central") === merchantKey(NFD form)` is `false`. Each row gets half the history, so recurrence can be missed or flagged Overdue wrongly: w21/169's symptom, through Unicode rather than case.

**Natively confirmed (2026-10-21, iPhone 17e, `main` `c9c36d8e7`):** with one NFC and one NFD "Café Central" entry on the disposable QA ledger, Merchants lists **two** "Café Central" rows with 1 transaction each. The ledger was restored to `f1a0b83c`.

## Fix

Normalise to NFC in `merchantKey` and in the aggregate-payees grouping key.

**Acceptance:** the two spellings form one merchant with 2 transactions.

## Dedupe

w21/169 covers case variants only, and 256 and 257 cover the picker and suggestions. Searched `.pm/**` for merchantKey and NFD with merchant.

Fixed. `merchantKey` now composes the payee (NFC) as well as trimming and lowercasing it, and the Merchants directory groups its rows by that key (it used `payee.toLowerCase()`), so a composed "Café Central" and a decomposed one ("e" plus a combining accent) are one merchant with 2 transactions, the other spelling kept in `otherSpellings` so the detail's exact payee queries (which compare codepoints on the server) still read both. The directory search folds the same way, so a query typed in either form finds it, and recurrence and manual marks, keyed by `merchantKey`, see the whole history. Tests (`merchant-spellings.test.ts`: the two spellings give one merchant of 2 with both kept and the later last date, a search in either form finds it, and the keys match; two fail with the key and aggregation reverted). The exact-payee filter still dedupes spellings by case only on purpose (NFC and NFD are different strings to the server). Not run natively.

Native recheck (iPhone 17e, fresh Metro `--clear` at `026513434`): payees "QA Café" (composed é) and "QA Café" (decomposed; disposable ledger, restored) merge into one Merchants row, "QA Café · 2 transactions". Its detail lists both entries ($3.00 and $4.00, total $7.00). Fixed.
