# A refunded category is left out of the Cash flow chart's Savings, so Savings disagrees with Net Profit and the VoiceOver summary contradicts itself

**Severity:** moderate. With one refund in the period, "Savings" overstates or understates what the bar chart directly above reports as Net Profit. **Estimate:** 1 hour, plus a product call. Owner: mobile; the dashboard transformer has the same skip. A3: the Sankey deliberately drops categories whose period net runs against their normal direction (refund, rebate, clawback), so the central node never draws taller than its inflow. Savings/Shortfall is then computed from the drawn ribbons only. The summary combines the full Income/Expenses totals with that ribbon-only Savings figure.

Reproduced natively on the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.0) at `main` `cc64d8bf1`, on the disposable QA ledger, which I restored byte-for-byte to `f1a0b83c`. The fixture `; qa-20261020-sk`:
- opened `Expenses:Qa20261020Food`
- `2026-10-01` Income:Salary −900 → Assets:Cash
- `2026-10-05` Food 300 from Cash
- `2026-10-10` Expenses:Transport **−100** (a refund) to Cash

On Reports → **1M (Oct)**:
- The bar chart shows Income $900, Expenses $200, and a Net Profit point at about $700.
- **Cash flow** shows Salary → Cash flow → Qa20261020Food plus **Savings**, with the Savings ribbon about 197 px tall against Food's 98, a 2:1 ratio, so **600 vs 300**. A net of 700 would be 7:3.
- Per the code, the chart's VoiceOver label reads "Income $900, expenses $200, savings $600". I didn't read it natively, because the chart has no testID.

The same happens on the income side: a clawback (`Income:Salary +50`) with Food 300 shows Shortfall $300 where the deficit is $350.

## Root cause

`mobile/src/screens/reports-screen/selectors/sankey-data.ts`:
- `:259` `toRankedNodes` drops values below half a cent, which includes every negative.
- `:350-353` and `:371-374` draw no ribbon for rows at or below zero.
- `:398-419` computes Savings/Shortfall from the drawn ribbons only.
- `:452-461` `cashFlowChartSummary` mixes the full totals with that Savings.

`selectors/__tests__/sankey-data.test.ts:200-219` pins totals of 950/380 with Savings 600, although 950 − 380 = 570. The dashboard has the same skip at `dashboard/src/…/sankey-data-transformer.ts:261-287`.

## Fix (choose one)

1. Draw refunds and clawbacks as their own inflow or outflow node ("Refunds" into Cash flow, "Clawbacks" out of it). The ribbons stay conserved and Savings equals Net Profit.
2. Keep the ribbons as they are, but compute Savings/Shortfall from the true totals, and say in the chart and summary that refunded categories are netted. For example: "Savings $700 (incl. $100 refunds)".

Update the pinned test either way, and decide about the dashboard counterpart.

**Acceptance:**
- With the fixture, Savings reads $700 (ribbon or label), matching the bar chart's Net Profit.
- The VoiceOver summary's income − expenses = savings.
- The clawback variant reports Shortfall $350.
