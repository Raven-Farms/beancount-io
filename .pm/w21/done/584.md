# Translation meaning errors: zh's amount hint says the sign toggles "income/expense", sk's write-unconfirmed reads as a refusal, uk's Rental preset says "returned" deposit, and four locales quote a "Decide later" button that doesn't exist

**Severity:** low to medium. **Estimate:** 20 minutes. Owner: mobile. Code-level at `main` `ef83e5894`:
1. **zh `postingAmountHint`** (`zh.ts:687`): "使用正负号按钮切换收支方向" ("switches between income and expense"). A posting's sign is debit/credit, not income/expense (a negative Assets posting isn't income). It's shown under every amount in New Transaction (`multi-postings-transaction-screen.tsx:411`). Use "输入金额。使用正负号按钮在正数和负数之间切换。"
2. **sk `ledgerWriteUnconfirmed`** (`sk.ts:373`): "Server neprijal potvrdenie o uložení…" reads as a refusal, but the real state is "may have been saved; check before retrying". Reading it as a refusal invites a duplicate retry. Use "Server nepotvrdil uloženie. Skontrolujte to skôr, než to skúsite znova."
3. **uk `createLedgerPresetRentalOutcome`** (`uk.ts:844`): "поверненої застави" (a deposit already *returned*), where English means a *refundable* deposit still held. Use "зворотної застави".
4. **`createLedgerAmountReenter`** quotes a "Decide later" label that differs from the real `createLedgerOpeningNone` button: bg "Реша по-късно" vs "Реши по-късно" (`bg.ts:807`), ca "Decideix més tard" vs "Decidir més tard" (`ca.ts:829`), fa "بعداً تصمیم می‌گیرم" vs "تصمیم‌گیری در آینده" (`fa.ts:796`), and pt "Decidir mais tarde" vs "Decidir depois" (`pt.ts:825`; also European rather than Brazilian Portuguese). Shown in the w21/500 and w21/510 message.

## Fix

Apply the corrections above. For item 4, interpolate the button's own label into the message, so the two can't drift.

**Acceptance:** the strings above read as stated, and a locale test checks that `createLedgerAmountReenter` contains `createLedgerOpeningNone`.
