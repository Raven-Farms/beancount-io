# On a back-dated New Transaction, the account picker opens blank with "No matching accounts" before anything is typed, with no hint that the accounts open later

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3 (code-level at `main` `e9f772e72`, proven under `tsx`): `account-picker-screen/picker-sections.ts:109` returns no sections when every account opens after the entry's date, so `account-picker-screen.tsx:672-703` shows the search-miss copy. There's no Create row (it needs a query, `:143`), and w21/374's "opens on {date}" note appears only for an exact typed name. With accounts opened 2024-01-01 and an entry dated 2023-12-31, `pickerAccounts("posting", …)` returns `[]`. A brand-new ledger with no accounts gets the same misleading empty state.

Not run natively (the date picker can't be driven here).

## Fix

When nothing is open on the entry's date, say so ("Your accounts open on Jan 1, 2024 — change the date") rather than "No matching accounts". For a ledger with no accounts, offer Create.

**Acceptance:** that case shows the date explanation. A unit case is added.

## Dedupe

w21/229, w21/374 and w21/531 cover hiding back-dated accounts and the typed-name note, not this blank state. Searched `.pm/**` for "No matching accounts".

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — mobile's account picker now explains a blank list. When every account a posting or budget picker could offer opens after the entry's date (the new `firstOpeningAfter` in `common/account-close-dates.ts`), the empty state reads "Your accounts open on Jan 1, 2024. Change the date to use them." instead of "No matching accounts". A ledger with no accounts for the picker says "This ledger has no accounts yet." and offers the Create row, labelled "Open Account", to a writer before anything is typed (`showCreateAccountRow` takes whether the picker has accounts).
