# Reports' income/expense chart draws an all-zero range as 2 pt bars over a "$0–$1" axis, while Cash flow below says "No cash flow" (w21/418's sibling)

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `common/d3/income-expense-bar-chart.tsx:82` shows its placeholder only when `months.length === 0`. The axis comes from `barChartValueDomain` (`:90`), which makes an all-zero series 0 to 1, and `restingBarRect` (`bar-geometry.ts:50, 65-68`) gives every bar a 2 pt minimum, even at exactly 0. The server returns a row for every month in the ledger's span, so a month with only a transfer or a price yields zero rows.

Code-level at `main` `b870e2a09`, from the reviewer's tsx run of `selectIncomeExpenseChartSeries` on period-end rows where October has only a transfer:
- 1M gives `{"months":["2026-10"],"income":[0],"expense":[0],"net":[0]}`, ticks `$0 $0.2 … $1`, and a 2 pt green and a 2 pt red bar;
- on 3M, October's zero still draws 2 pt bars beside $5K and $1.5K.

Not exercised natively (the disposable ledger's October is empty, so Reports anchors on August).

## Fix

Show `ChartPlaceholder` (`cashFlowEmpty`) when every income, expense and net value is 0, and give a value of exactly 0 no resting height (keep the 2 pt minimum for non-zero values).

**Acceptance:** a transfer-only month shows the empty state on 1M and no bar on 3M. Unit cases are added.

Sibling of w21/418 (Home Spending, done) and w21/236 (axis domain). Searched `.pm/**` for restingBarRect, MIN_BAR_HEIGHT, and Reports together with all-zero.
