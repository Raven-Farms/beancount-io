# A JPY receipt scan still saves "1500.00 JPY", because receiptAmountText always pads to two places

**Severity:** moderate for zero-decimal currencies. **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/231 padded typed amounts to the currency's minor unit, but `receipt-capture-screen/receipt-utils.ts:23` (`receiptAmountText`) always does `padEnd(2, "0")`, and `formatExactAmount` (`postings-utils.ts:178`) never trims extra zeros. Under `tsx`, `receiptAmountText(1500)` gives "1500.00", and through `createPrefilledPostings` and `buildEntryInput({currency:"JPY"})` the payload is `-1500.00 JPY` / `1500.00 JPY`, exactly the symptom 231 meant to remove. 231's resolution lists this as a known deviation.

Code-level at `main` `62533363c`. Not run natively; it would spend AI receipt quota.

## Fix

Don't pad in `receiptAmountText`; pad to the currency's minor unit downstream. Add a JPY receipt-to-payload test.

**Acceptance:** a scanned 1500 JPY receipt saves `1500 JPY`.

## Dedupe

Fixed. `receiptAmountText` no longer pads a whole total: a fractional total keeps at least two places (42.5 reads "42.50") and a whole one has none ("1500"), at the precision the parser returned. The reveal on the capture screen shows a whole total as "1500" rather than "1500.00".
