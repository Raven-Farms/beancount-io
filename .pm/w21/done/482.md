# Budget card status badges fail contrast, worst in dark mode ("Above target" 2.22:1)

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate (accessibility). **Estimate:** 10 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: `budget-screen/components/budget-group-card.tsx:278-297` draws the badges on a `theme.black20` fill, with `statusColor` (error or success) or `black80` text at 12 pt medium (`:124-127`). Computed ratios at `main` `f8222c71e`:

| Badge | Dark | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Above target" (error) | 2.22 | 3.97 |
| "Below target" (success) | 3.02 | 3.88 |
| "Starts {date}" (black80) | 2.95 | 4.41 |

The badges were visible throughout this session's native budget checks (light mode); dark mode wasn't screenshotted.

## Fix

Keep the fill and colour the text `text01`, with the status colour on the border, or use `controlFill` as the fill. Add a contrast test.

**Acceptance:** all three badges are at least 4.5:1 in both themes.

## Dedupe

Searched `.pm/**` for statusBadge, statusText and budget badge together with contrast. No hits.

Resolved. The Budget card's three badges ("Above target", "Below target", "Starts {date}") keep their `black20` fill and take the primary text colour `text01` for their text, with the status shown in the border instead of in text that failed contrast; the status badge gained the 1 px border it was missing (its `borderColor` had no border width, so the colour cue was in the text alone). The fill and text colour now live in the styles, not inline. `text-style-contrast.test.ts` checks `statusText` on the badge fill in both themes (≥ 4.5:1) and fails against the old styles in both. Deviation: no screenshots in dark mode.
