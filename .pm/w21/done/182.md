# A ledger-service outage at launch permanently drops the selected ledger

**Severity:** moderate. A user with several ledgers who opens the app while the ledger service is briefly down or slow (more than 30 s) loses their selection for good. They land on their first ledger, or on "Create ledger / Discover ledgers" as if they had none, and the selection doesn't return when the service recovers. **Estimate:** 30 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: the splash's ledger check clears `ledgerVar` on *any* GraphQL error, not just on "gone". backend-v2 maps a 502, 503, 504 or timeout from the ledger service to HTTP 200 with `extensions.code: SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, and RATE_LIMITED or INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR also count as errors. The cleared selection is persisted, and LedgerGuard then picks `listLedgers[0]` or the empty state.

Code-level at `main` `b8c58c186`. It needs a proxy rewrite of `GetLedger` to `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, or a local stack with the ledger service stopped.

## Where

- `mobile/src/common/providers/splash-provider/splash-provider.tsx:117-134`: "Clear only when the server rejects the ledger", but every non-transport error is treated as a rejection.
- `mobile/src/components/ledger-guard/ledger-guard.tsx:113-120, 135-191`
- `mobile/src/common/ledger-directory/ledger-directory.ts:126-135` (cache misses for argument-less `listLedgers`)
- `backend-cluster/backend-v2/src/foundation/fava/error-to-domain.ts`

## Fix

Clear the selection only for "gone" outcomes (`isLedgerGone`, NOT_FOUND, FORBIDDEN). For SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE, RATE_LIMITED or INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR, keep it and render cached data, as for an offline launch.

**Acceptance:**
- With `GetLedger` rewritten to SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE, a cold launch keeps the second ledger selected and shows its cached Home.
- With NOT_FOUND it still clears.
- Add a splash unit test per code.
