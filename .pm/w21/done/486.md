# Regression from w21/398: a budget on a parent account that was closed when it was split into children reads $0 and red "Above target" every month, and leaves Home

**Severity:** minor-to-moderate. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile. A3: w21/398 ends a budget on the close date of its exact account (`budget-group-card.tsx:194-201` passes `endsOn: closeDates.get(group.account)`, applied at `budget-selectors.ts:427`), and Home drops it with `closedBefore` (`home-screen/components/budget-card.tsx:98-103`). The actuals still roll up every descendant (`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/interval-series.ts:247-250`), and Beancount refuses postings only to the closed account itself, so children keep taking spending. w21/396 made parent-category budgets a supported feature.

Code-level at `main` `ec58435d2`. The reviewer ran the real `closeDatesOf`, `budgetForInterval`, `selectBudgetCardStats` and `closedBefore` with today = 2026-10-21. The fixture: `Expenses:Food` opened 2024-01-01 and closed 2026-06-30; `Expenses:Food:Groceries` and `:Restaurants` opened 2026-06-01 and still open; a monthly budget of 500 USD on `Expenses:Food`; period-end rows June 480, July 470, August 455, September 490. The result: June's budget is 500, July to September's budget is **0**, September's card reads `{budget: 0, actual: 490, status: 'above', favorable: false}`, and `closedBefore` is true, so Home drops the group. Not exercised natively; w21/398's own native check (a closed leaf account) still holds.

## Fix

End the budget only once the account and all its descendants are closed: take the latest close date across that subtree, or no end while any descendant is open. Use it for both `endsOn` and Home's `closedBefore`.

**Acceptance:** with the fixture, the card keeps its 500 target from July on and Home still lists the budget, while a closed leaf with no open children still ends as in w21/398. Unit cases are added.
