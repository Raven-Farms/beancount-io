# A `#tag` or `^link` search in Transactions matches only the exact, case-sensitive name: "#japan2026" misses an entry tagged #Japan2026

**Severity:** low. **Estimate:** 15 minutes. Owner: mobile and ledger. A3: `transactions-screen/utils/plain-search-filter.ts:35` sends a lone tag or link unquoted, and the ledger compares exactly (`directive.tags.includes(tag)`, `backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/rustledger/directive-filter.ts:566-588`). w21/400's quoted fallback searches only payee and narration. The search field has autocapitalize off, so lowercase is what people type. Payee search is case-insensitive and partial.

Native (iPhone 17e at `6a12162e0`, disposable ledger, restored byte-for-byte): with `2026-10-12 * "QA Ichiran" "qa-20261020 ramen" #Japan2026`, Transactions ▸ search "#japan2026" shows "No transactions match your search or filters." Under `tsx` with the ledger's filter, "#Japan", "^receipt-abc" and "^receipt" also return nothing.

## Fix

Match tags and links case-insensitively (and by prefix) for the search box's tag form, either on the client (an OR of the case variants found in the ledger's tag list) or in the ledger's matcher.

**Acceptance:** "#japan2026" and "#japan" find that entry, and payee search is unchanged. Tests cover both sides.

## Dedupe

w21/025 and w21/400 cover how tags are sent, not case. Searched `.pm/**` for "case-sensitive" together with tag.
