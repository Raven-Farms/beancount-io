## Verification

`/simplify` ran four reviewers (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude) over the diff.

**Applied:**
- `landed()` is a plain try/await. The default error policy always rejects a failed mutation.
- `usePendingSubmit` no longer re-checks `=== true`, which `run` already does.
- `handleUpdateName` builds its variables before the call.
- Three test lines that could not fail on their own are dropped.
- The AGENTS.md wording now covers both lock styles: a disabled fieldset, or `readOnly` on the input that starts the save.

**Confirmed as is:**
- The guard sits at each dialog's submit handler.
- The extraction leaves one focus-handback implementation behind seven callers.
- `readOnly` instead of a fieldset keeps focus in the field.
- The dialogs need no session token.
- There are no efficiency costs.

**Skipped as out of scope:** moving the repeated "ignore close while pending" guard into the shared Dialog primitive. It is filed separately as follow-up work.
