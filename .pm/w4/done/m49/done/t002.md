## Verification

**Fix (bounded pending lock):**
- The panel reports a pending write through `onPendingChange`; the cleanup also releases an owner when the panel unmounts mid-write.
- `EntryContextDialog`'s `requestLeave` refuses every exit while a write is pending: Escape, Close, an outside click and the source link. No Discard choice is offered then, because discarding cannot stop a sent write.
- The dialog stays with the draft that sent the write, so its completion can only close that draft. No later entry can be opened behind the modal meanwhile.
- **The standalone EntryPage, found by the simplify altitude review:**
  - It now passes `onPendingChange` and adds a silent route blocker while a write is pending. The save's own exits (the returned hash, or the Journal after a delete) still leave through `leavingRef`.
  - So an old save can no longer skip the draft guard of an entry page opened after it.

**Native check, 1440/390:**
- During a held save, Escape and Close leave one Entry Context dialog with no Discard prompt.
- The modeled acknowledgement closes that dialog.
- With nothing pending, Escape → Discard your edit? → Discard still closes.

**Regressions:**
- `entry-context-pending-save.test.tsx` holds against all four exits.
- `entry-page-draft-guard.test.tsx` holds Back to journal during a save, then leaves by the save's new-hash exit.
