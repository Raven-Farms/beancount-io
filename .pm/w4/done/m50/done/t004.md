## Verification

`/simplify` ran four reviewers.

**Applied:**
- Following the altitude review, `QueryView` owns the retry hold through `useQueryRetry` instead of taking a `retrying` prop. Commodities therefore passes only `onRetry={refetch}`, and a caller can no longer forward the raw `error` mid-retry.
- The read-side rule moved from `reports/AGENTS.md` to `dashboard/AGENTS.md`, beside the write-lock rule.
- The regression link was given the smallest real Cash Flow and Account Journal payloads, so no test leaves an unhandled render error.

**Confirmed:**
- No existing retry or held-error helper exists in `common/`.
- The hook adds no renders when a page loads normally, and nothing receiving `onRetry` is memoized.
- Keeping the five statement pages on their own `ReportErrorState` (not `QueryView`) preserves their settled-data, reconciliation and pagination behaviour.

**Not reviewed:** the simplification reviewer was still running when this shipped. My own pass over the final diff found nothing further. Any later finding will land as a follow-up commit.

**Follow-up filed:** five `QueryView` consumers still pass no `onRetry` (Settings, Accounts, Errors, Documents, Events). Three hand-rolled retries could adopt `useQueryRetry`: General Settings w4/257, Billing usage w4/312 and reset-password.
