# Keep switch states visible in forced colors

**Severity:** minor (visual state accessibility). **Estimate:** 40–55 minutes, dashboard. **A2:** readers customizing an Overview should be able to see which modules are enabled after focus leaves the control.

Production https://beancount.io, 2026-10-20, local main `c5e4cd11a`; QA-owned isolated headless Chromium155.0.8059.85, English. Chromium forced-colors media emulation, fresh owner contexts at 1440×1000/light, 390×844/light and 1440×1000/dark. OS settings are untouched; physical Windows high-contrast palettes are unverified. Only context-local Overview preferences changed, with zero mutation requests in the independent narrow/dark repeats.

## Fresh reproduction and controls

1. Open the designated synthetic ledger Overview, wait for the populated dashboard, then Customize.
2. Read Show module: Financial position checked=true. Move focus to Reset layout and pointer away so focus/hover cannot stand in for selection. Capture the switch.
3. Focus the switch, press Space to uncheck it, then move focus away again. aria-checked=false and the real module hides, but the switch looks like the same empty outlined track. Fresh narrow and dark repeats reproduce.
4. In light forced colors, both states have a white track/white thumb, black two-pixel track border and no shadow. In dark forced colors, both track and thumb are black, with a white track border and no shadow. The 16×16 thumb has no border; its translate changes0→16px but offers no contrasting visible position.
5. Working same-context control: turn forced-color emulation off. Checked is green with a clearly visible displaced thumb; unchecked is gray with the thumb at its other end. Native Space and aria-checked remain correct throughout.

The captured44×28 crops are not byte-identical: each forced-state pair differs in36/1232 pixels. They nevertheless show the same empty outlined track with no contrasting thumb; do not claim exact pixel equality. Both state crops and the narrow whole panel were visually inspected. The panel shows the first module off and six others on, yet all seven tracks have the same appearance.

## Cause and bounded repair

`dashboard/src/common/components/ui/switch.tsx:14` distinguishes checked/unchecked through authored background colors. Its thumb at :22 uses bg-background and shadow with no border. Forced colors maps both surfaces to the same canvas color and removes shadows, leaving only the invariant track border. ARIA state and the individual CSS translate property still change; the issue is visual state, not failed toggling or missing semantics.

Give the shared Switch a persistent contrasting state/position indicator in forced colors, using system colors and/or explicit geometry. Preserve a separate keyboard-focus cue, disabled appearance, native Space/pointer behavior, ARIA state, sizes, thumb movement and ordinary light/dark presentation. Do not disable forced-color adjustment for the whole page or replace state with a focus-only effect. No API, dependency or cross-package change is needed.

Source-derived blast radius: the primitive's other production consumers are the new-ledger form's visibility choice and ledger Settings' visibility section. Those were not toggled live; no ledger creation or privacy mutation is authorized by this proof. Verify their unchanged semantics when implementing the shared repair.

Acceptance: after blur, checked and unchecked switches are visually distinguishable at desktop/narrow widths in forced light/dark emulation, and the visible state agrees with aria-checked. Native Space/pointer changes and focus remain usable; ordinary modes and disabled controls retain their behavior. Use browser pixels/geometry as evidence; emitted-CSS or class-name assertions alone cannot prove the thumb is visible. Run dashboard checks.
