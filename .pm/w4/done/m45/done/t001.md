## Verification — 2026-10-22

**Source and fix:**
- `formatNumber(value, renderCommas, locale, currency, fractionDigits = 0)` in `dashboard/src/common/lib/format/format-number.ts` sends non-ISO commodities to the existing `formatQuantity`, at `max(fractionDigits, 3 grouped / 2 ungrouped)`.
- With no digits passed, the output is identical to the old fallback, so other callers are unaffected. ISO currencies keep their minor units.
- `useFormatNumber` takes the optional third argument.
- `dashboard/src/features/reports/balance-sheet/hierarchy-list.tsx` passes `fractionDigitsOf(String(raw))` for the primary cell and for every OtherBalancesColumn commodity. These are the exact decimal strings the ledger service writes at their recorded scale; client summary rows are BigInt decimal sums.
