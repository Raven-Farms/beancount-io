# Retire useFormatQuantity in favor of useFormatNumber with source digits

Follow-up from the [w4/m45](./m45/README.md) simplify review (reuse and altitude reviewers), 2026-10-22. Minor, dashboard only, about 20–30 minutes.

**Problem.** Since m45, `useFormatNumber()(value, currency, fractionDigitsOf(source))` keeps a non-ISO commodity's source digits and gives an ISO currency its minor units. `dashboard/src/common/hooks/use-format-quantity.ts` is now a narrower parallel API that applies source digits to every unit, cash included. Its three callers therefore show cash without minor units, e.g. `4,559.8 USD` in a tooltip beside `4,559.80 USD` on a card, which runs against the [w0/010](../../w0/done/010.md) cash rule:
- `features/reports/balance-sheet/line-chart.tsx` (~140)
- `features/reports/income-statement/date-balance-chart/single-date-balance-chart.tsx` (~113)
- `features/reports/overview/components/net-worth-card.tsx` (~180)

**Change.**
- Move the three callers to `useFormatNumber()(value, commodity, fractionDigitsOf(raw))`.
- Delete `use-format-quantity.ts`. Keep `formatQuantity` as the formatter's internal primitive.
- Update those features' tests for the ISO minor-unit change, and leave commodity output unchanged.
- Confirm the rendered change before shipping: USD tooltips and net-worth holdings gain trailing cents, and non-ISO quantities stay identical.

**Out of scope.** `transaction-auto-balance.ts` has its own `decimalPlaces`, which duplicates `fractionDigitsOf`. Treat it separately if it is unified.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — the Balance Sheet line chart and Income Statement single-date chart tooltips and Net Worth's valuation holdings now use `useFormatNumber()(value, unit, fractionDigitsOf(raw))`. `common/hooks/use-format-quantity.ts` is deleted, and `formatQuantity` remains the formatter's primitive. Cash is therefore written at its minor units, and every other commodity keeps its source digits. The three tests drop their mocks of the deleted hook. `axis-small-values.test.tsx` now runs the real formatter and adds a tooltip case asserting `USD: 4,559.80` and `ETH: 4.00995`; with the old code that case reads `USD: 4,559.8`. In native checks at 1440 and 390 px on public `open_ledger/example`, the Balance Sheet tooltip read `USD: 57,137.70` and `USD: 53,552.15`. The Net Worth valuation details still showed `17 GLD`, `104 ITOT`, `597.748 RGAGX`, `-13 VACHR` and `193.442 VBMPX`. No page errors. Shipped in `f85f64eb5` (`dashboard`).
