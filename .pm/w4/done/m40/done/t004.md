## Verification

`/simplify` ran four parallel reviews (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude) over `eeb14c2b2`.

- **Applied:** `PopoverContent` no longer hand-merges a caller ref to attach native listeners. It composes the caller's `onWheel`/`onTouchMove` and stops their propagation in React's own handlers.
  - Radix portals into `document.body`, React listens on that container, and react-remove-scroll's Dialog/Sheet lock listens later in the bubble path, on `document`. So the lock still never sees a wheel from inside the popup, and the page behind stays locked.
  - The caller's ref passes through unchanged.
  - Wheel handlers on the popup's own content now fire, where the native stop had silenced them.
  - The DatePicker cap comment is shorter.
- **Altitude review:** confirmed the shared `PopoverContent` is the right layer.
  - Radix hard-codes the Dialog lock's shards.
  - A modal Popover would break Combobox typing.
  - Select and DropdownMenu carry their own locks.
- **Skipped:** making the available-height cap a `PopoverContent` default. The review noted that language-selector, ledger-group flyout, Net Worth and go-to-account use fixed caps or none and may overflow very short windows, but that changes all eight consumers beyond the reproduced failure. It is unverified and left as a possible follow-up.
