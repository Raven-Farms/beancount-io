## Verification — 2026-10-22

`/simplify` ran four parallel reviewers (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude) over the card change (e38a2b614) and the two sibling consumers from c293ab9c1. All four found one mechanism copied three times: the UTC-until-hydrated exact time and the relative-or-exact label in `RepositoryListItem`, `ProfileActivity` and `CommitMetadata`, the last already drifting to its own `Intl.DateTimeFormat` copy.

**Applied:**
- One `useHydratedTime()` in `dashboard/src/common/hooks/use-date-locale.ts`, next to `useFormatRelativeTime`.
  - `exactTime(date, options)` formats in UTC until hydration and in the browser zone afterwards.
  - `relativeTime(date, options)` shows the exact time in `options` until hydration, then the localized age.
- The three components call it, and each keeps its own output. The cards and activity rows show a medium date before hydration, and `CommitMetadata` keeps its long label.
- It adds no effect, timer, ticker, clock override or new state. The `i18n`/`useHydrated` imports and the duplicated comments are removed from the components.

**Tests:**
- Removed the `use-date-locale` module mocks in `repository-list-item.test.tsx` and `commit-metadata.test.tsx`. Outside a provider, the real module falls back to English.
- Removed the `i18n.language` mock lines in four profile suites. They existed only for the removed copies.
- The card and activity hydration tests now assert per-node identity (`toBe`) for retained `<time>` nodes after a rerender, instead of comparing content with `toEqual`.
- Dropped a redundant anchor-connectivity check, since connected `<time>` nodes already imply their links.

**Skipped:**
- A module-level `Intl.DateTimeFormat` cache (efficiency). A cached browser-zone formatter keeps the zone it was built in, and the Kiritimati title regressions failed with it, so per-call formatting stays.
- A `<HydratedTime>` component (simplification). `CommitMetadata` wraps its time in a tooltip trigger with `aria-label`/`tabIndex`, so only a hook fits all three.
- Out of scope: the five audit-only relative-time consumers, and the separate `features/auth/lib/use-hydrated.ts` copy of TanStack's `useHydrated`.

Behaviour and gates are recorded in [t010](./t010.md).
