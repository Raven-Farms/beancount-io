## Verification

- `forced-selected` in `dashboard/src/style.css` is a Tailwind utility. Under forced colors only, it sets `forced-color-adjust: none`, keeps `Highlight` for fill and border and `HighlightText` for text with `!important`, drops `box-shadow`, and gives descendants a transparent fill and `HighlightText`. The important declarations matter because the app's `dark` variant (`&:is(.dark *))`) out-ranks a plain forced-colors rule once adjustment is off.
- The four targets add it behind their own state: CommandItem `data-[selected=true]:`, Combobox options `aria-selected:`, default pill Tabs `data-[state=active]:`, and pressed Journal filters through `filterButtonState(true)`. Inactive options, underline Tabs and unpressed filters keep the browser's forced rendering.
- Local production build of current main, served against the repo's replaying GraphQL fixture (`dashboard/scripts/perf-fixture-api.mjs --synthetic-owner`, anonymous public `open_ledger` reads). The browser supplied synthetic ListLedgers, a no-subscription status and zero AI usage so the switcher rows and Free Billing picker render; every mutation was blocked in the browser. Headless Chromium 155 with forced-colors emulation, light and dark, at 1440×1000 and 390×844. This is a local simulation, not the deployed site.
- Paint was measured on real screenshots of each label's text range: a backplate leaves 2–7 colours with a 92–99% dominant colour, while a readable label draws 39–226 colours. On every selected element the probe also switched `forced-color-adjust` back to `auto`, which reproduced QA's solid block, so it detects the failure the fix removes.
- Results:
  - Account picker: 203–226 colours.
  - Ledger switcher: 144–154 colours; its visibility icon paints HighlightText.
  - Income Statement Time option: 108–109 colours.
  - Journal Transaction filter: 139–152 colours.
  - Income Statement and New Entry active pills: 170–180 colours.
  - Every case was read in both palettes and at both widths, after ArrowDown moved the selection where applicable.
