## Cause and bounded repair

The BQL page is the sole production card caller; the card is the sole export-control caller.

Make this card's download obey its own execution state: hide/disable it while the query is running, then offer the newly completed table. If retaining a previous-result export is deliberate, it must be explicitly distinguished from the pending result; silently exporting it under the current execution is the defect. Preserve ordinary settled exports, all rows/source decimal strings and CSV formatting, history/result lifetime, current network-only reruns, error recovery and exports from other completed cards.
