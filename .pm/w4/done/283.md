## Cause and bounded repair

`dashboard/src/features/reports/export/reporting-period.ts:26` constructs `new Date(Date.UTC(year, month - 1, day))`. Date.UTC remaps numeric years 0–99 into 1900–1999, so utcDate's strict year round-trip rejects a real 0099 date. parseConcreteTimeFilter consequently returns null; resolveReportingPeriod treats this concrete selection as unresolved. validReportDates uses the same guard. addMonths/addYears also construct through Date.UTC and need the same year-preserving audit, particularly arithmetic crossing the 0099/0100 boundary.

Preserve supplied calendar years in the existing UTC constructors and arithmetic. Keep malformed/nonexistent-date refusal, inclusive bounds, fiscal/week/range rules and the intentional unresolved-relative-selection fallback. Do not infer authoritative bounds from truncated chart buckets, manufacture dates for relative tokens, change report values or add a dependency/backend parser.

The shared resolver feeds buildProfitAndLossDocument, buildBalanceSheetDocument and buildCashFlowDocument in export/model.ts, then CSV, Markdown, print and filenames. Income Statement CSV/Markdown are live proven here; Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, print and every concrete-token variant are source-traced regression scope, not live claims.
