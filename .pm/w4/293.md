# Keep the DatePicker calendar reachable in short viewports

**Minor · dashboard · 60 minutes.** A2: every date in the displayed month must be visible or reachable through the picker at ordinary short browser heights. Production beancount.io, 2026-10-20 America/Los_Angeles (2026-10-21 UTC), owned isolated headless Chromium 155, English/System theme, fresh 844×390 and 568×320 contexts plus a 390×844 control. Authenticated QA admin on `<qa-owner>/<synthetic-ledger>`. Local main `8321bd0a0`; deployed main-D9qWGMxD.js, full deployment revision unverified. These are web viewport sizes; no physical device rotation, OS keyboard or native-mobile app was exercised.

Open Accounts → Open Account, enter Open Date `08/31/2026`, commit by Tab, then focus the field and use ordinary ArrowDown to open the calendar. Wait for animation to settle.

The popup stays 378.19px high, computed max-height:none and overflow-y:hidden. At both short heights, most of the month is below the viewport. Native browser-local wheel scrolling leaves popup scrollTop=0 and the same bounds; the body also stays at 0. A normal Playwright click on the actual August 30 button times out and the field remains `08/31/2026`. An independent initial run reproduced both geometries; screenshots were visually inspected. At 390×844 the same real button is reachable, the click succeeds and the field becomes `08/30/2026`. Its immediate post-click DOM count was transitional and is not a separate dismissal finding.

Two browser-only controls isolate the repair requirements. A temporary height cap to the existing Radix available-height variable plus overflow-y:auto fits the popup to 173px at the 568×320 viewport. Keyboard focus/ArrowLeft scrolls its internal viewport to 205 and reveals August 30; a normal automated click also succeeds via its scroll-into-view behavior. **That CSS-only control still cannot wheel-scroll:** the actual wheel event on a day button is defaultPrevented=true and scrollTop remains 0. Do not call the CSS-only change a complete pointer repair.

In a separate fresh 568×320 context, the same temporary cap plus popup-local wheel propagation containment permits native wheel down→scrollTop=205 and up→0. After scrolling down again, August 30 spans 275.19–307.19, fully inside the viewport. A raw browser pointer click at that visible button's center, with no locator auto-scroll, sets `08/30/2026` and closes the popup while the parent form stays open and the body stays at 0. This is a memory-only diagnostic, not a product fix or a prescribed global wheel override.

All account/API mutations were blocked before transport and zero were attempted. Only unsaved date fields and temporary owned-page CSS/listeners changed; all extra contexts closed. No ledger data or main browser preferences were written.

## Cause and bounded repair

`dashboard/src/common/components/ui/date-picker.tsx:227–233` gives its PopoverContent w-auto/overflow-hidden/p-0 and offsets, without consuming the available height or providing an internal vertical scroll region. The shared Popover portals to body. Installed Radix Dialog's overlay wraps RemoveScroll with shards:[context.contentRef] (react-dialog/dist/index.mjs:100–108); a nested portaled picker needs to preserve the modal's lock while permitting its own scroll. The wheel-cancellation observation and local containment control corroborate that modal integration, in addition to the missing height constraint.

Bound this DatePicker's popup to actual available space, retain access to Today/month/year controls and every calendar row, and make pointer/keyboard scrolling work inside the surrounding modal. Use the existing Radix sizing contract; its [official Popover documentation](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/popover) describes --radix-popover-content-available-height. Preserve normal-height layout, field focus/selection, typed date semantics, calendar-day identity, RTL and other popovers' scrolling. Do not simply crop dates, shrink them into tiny targets, unlock the underlying page or globally change every Popover.

Seven production DatePicker callers were found: receipt-review-form, Journal transaction/balance/note/open-account forms, Accounts OpenAccountDialog and Budget AddBudgetDialog. Only Accounts was live exercised; other modal and standalone callers are shared-source regression scope. No API, dependency or calendar-engine change is needed.

Acceptance: real portal geometry and native scroll/selection controls at844×390,568×320 and390×844 keep every date and header control reachable, including six-week months, above/below placement and resizing while open. Verify actual wheel and keyboard scrolling inside a modal, background lock, ordinary date selection/dismissal/focus return and a standalone caller. Add meaningful regressions with the existing tools, then run dashboard format/lint/test/build. A max-height class assertion or forced click alone cannot prove this repair.
