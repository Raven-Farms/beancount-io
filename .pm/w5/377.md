# The managed price feed validator accepts commodity names Beancount reads differently: `price BTC- 100000 USD` is cached and loads as a negative price, `BTC.`/`BTC_`/`USD-` break every load, and the bad cached pair then blocks the corrected feed

**Minor · cli · 20–30m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-14, HEAD `bce2c992`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. **Simulation:** the feed bodies come from a localhost origin allowed through `MANAGED_PRICE_ORIGINS`. The defect is in how the CLI validates them.

The feed grammar is meant to be "a strict subset of what Beancount's
lexer accepts". Its commodity pattern `[A-Z][A-Z0-9'._-]*` allows a
trailing `-`, `.`, `_` or `'`. Beancount needs a commodity to end in a
letter or digit. As a result:

- `BTC-` lexes as `BTC` followed by a minus sign. The price loads as
  **−100000 USD** with no error, so valuations go negative.
- `BTC.`, `BTC_` and `BTC'`, or a quote currency such as `USD-`, give
  invalid tokens. Every `check` and read fails until the cache is
  removed.
- The bad revision is cached as valid, and its pair (`BTC-/USD`) is
  pinned. When the corrected feed `price BTC 100000 USD` arrives,
  `price refresh` refuses it with `feed pair BTC/USD does not match the
  cached BTC-/USD`, and the bad revision keeps serving.

No product fix implemented.

Create disposable `/absolute/L/main.bean` that opens `Assets:Crypto BTC`,
buys 1 BTC at a cost of 100 USD, and has `include
"http://127.0.0.1:<port>/prices/BTC-USD"`. Serve the body `; alias:
BTC-USD` followed by `2026-01-10 price BTC- 100000 USD`. Use isolated
BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1,
`MANAGED_PRICE_ORIGINS=http://127.0.0.1:<port>`, no inherited
BEA_FILE/BEA_TOKEN, and closed stdin.

```sh
bea -f /absolute/L/main.bean price refresh
bea -f /absolute/L/main.bean check
bea -f /absolute/L/main.bean list price
bea -f /absolute/L/main.bean balance -x USD -t 2026-03
# then serve the corrected `2026-01-10 price BTC 100000 USD`
bea -f /absolute/L/main.bean price refresh
```

Actual:

- The first refresh exits 0 with `BTC-USD: none → 9be5…`, and `check`
  exits 0.
- `list price` shows `2026-01-10 BTC -100000 USD generated`. The hunter
  saw `balance -x USD` show `Crypto -100,000 USD`.
- The corrected refresh exits 1 with `invalid feed: feed pair BTC/USD
  does not match the cached BTC-/USD; serving revision 9be5…`.
- For `BTC.`, `BTC_`, `BTC'` or `USD-`, the revision is cached, and every
  later `check` or read exits 1 with `Invalid token: '.'` or `syntax
  error, unexpected MINUS` in the effective include.

Reproduced by the hunter twice per main variant, with pin repros, and by
me for `BTC-` and its pin.

Control: a valid `BTC` feed loads, and a later valid revision replaces
it. Injected `plugin`, `option`, `include`, `pushtag` lines, mixed pairs
and unknown metadata keys are all refused with `invalid feed at line N`.

Expected:

- The `cli/src/bea_engine/managed_prices.py:57-60` comment calls the
  grammar a strict subset of Beancount's lexer: "Anything looser
  validates a body that then fails to parse in the ledger".
- `validate_managed_price_text` promises "finite positive decimals" and
  that "a wrong instrument can never silently replace a right one".

A commodity that Beancount would not read as exactly that token should
make the feed invalid. It should never be cached, and no pair should be
pinned.

Cause:

- `_PRICE_RE` (`cli/src/bea_engine/managed_prices.py:64-67`) uses
  `[A-Z][A-Z0-9'._-]*` for both commodities.
- `check_feed_identity` (`:579-598`) then compares new feeds against the
  bad cached pair.

Fix:

- Validate both commodities with Beancount's grammar. `is_commodity` in
  `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/text.py` already exists.
- As a backstop, parse the validated body with Beancount's parser before
  caching, and refuse it unless it yields exactly the validated prices.
- Do not let a cached pair that fails the grammar block a valid
  replacement.
- Consider deriving the expected pair from the alias when the `; alias:`
  header is missing.
- No dependency change.

The hosted engine has the same gap. The feed validator's `PRICE_RE` in
`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/managed-prices/managed-price-feed.ts:150`
uses the same `[A-Z][A-Z0-9'._-]*` pattern. Per the repo parity rule, fix
both together.

Blast radius: every ledger with a managed price include, whenever a feed
revision carries such a name, whether by publisher bug or tampering. The
outcome is silent negative valuations, or a ledger that will not load
and cannot be refreshed back to health.

Dedupe: searched `.pm/**`, including `done/`, for feed pair, `_PRICE_RE`,
"strict subset", negative price and trailing hyphen. The grammar came
from w2/m31 (ADR 015 §4). w3/413, w3/433, w3/436, w3/387 and 354 cover
other managed-price problems. None covers commodity token edges.

Acceptance: feeds with `BTC-`, `BTC.`, `BTC_`, `BTC'` or `USD-` are
refused as `invalid feed at line 2`, and nothing is cached. A cache
already holding such a revision is replaced by the next valid feed. A
test covers each trailing character and the recovery.
