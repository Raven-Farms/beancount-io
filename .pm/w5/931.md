# Recognize FX transfer counterparts across price placement

**Major · cli · 45–60m · A1.** An FX transfer already booked from one bank
is booked again from the other bank when the receiving cash posting carries
the exchange price. Its units and currency match the receiving statement,
but the statement has no posting price. Preview calls it new and even
`--duplicates skip` writes it. Pricing the same exchange on its other posting
correctly offers a possible duplicate. No product fix implemented.

Cause: `cli/src/bea_engine/importing.py:_amounts_key` (:1657–1668) groups
source quantities by currency **and posting price**. The counterpart index
(:448) and `_transfer_counterpart` (:1691) therefore index
`100 EUR @ 1.1 USD` separately from the CSV's `100 EUR`. Even after a key
match, `_transfer_counterpart` and `_counterpart_row` (:1693, :1716–1719)
call price-sensitive `_legs_agree`. `_transfer_side` (:1629–1650), the
shared-native-reference review path, also requires those legs to agree.
The incoming bank row's absent valuation annotation vetoes an otherwise
matching cash movement. No branch becomes a possible duplicate, so skip
has nothing to skip and normal balanced postings double the receiving side.

Fix: give transfer review a comparison/key that recognizes matching posted
units/currency when a statement does not state the existing posting's price,
while retaining compatible lot constraints, date windows and one-counterpart
allocation. Apply it consistently to counterpart indexing, candidate selection,
row assignment and shared-reference review. Keep ordinary/native-ID source
fact comparisons and generic twin keys price-sensitive: `_amounts_key` and
`_legs_agree` have those other callers too. No dependency or backend/API change
needed.
