recalled or inferred mappings, "never typed". No product fix implemented.

`Date,Description,Amount`.

   --account Assets:Checking --csv date=Date,amount=Amount,narration=Description`

a.csv for c.csv: …`.

Cause (`cli/src/cli/commands/import_.py`):
- `settings_notes` (`:843`) is not used by the date-format mismatch
  (`details=recall_notes` around `:916`), the delimiter or decode refusals

  (`details=config_notes`, around `:815`).

Fix: use `settings_notes` in those refusals, but only for a remembered or
inferred origin.
