# The dated missing-price wording ("HOOL → EUR has no price before …") still appears when the only lot at the route's cost currency was sold before the period or bought after it (incomplete fix of w5/557)

**Trivial · cli · 10m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `a513b1113`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Follow-up to w5/557 (done, `83e22e186`).
No product fix implemented.

`p2.bean` (operating currency EUR):
- 2025-02-01: buys `5 HOOL {100.00 USD}`.
- 2025-03-01: sells all of it.
- 2025-12-02: buys `5 HOOL {90.00 EUR}`.
- Prices: `2026-01-01 price HOOL 120 USD` and `2026-01-01 price USD 0.90 EUR`.

```sh
bea -f /abs/p2.bean balance -t 2025-12 --allow-errors
```

Actual: `HOOL → EUR has no price before 2026-01-01; earlier rows shown in
HOOL`. The `--json report balance-sheet` summary and the exit-1 details say
the same (hunter's run). The only HOOL held in December is the
`{90.00 EUR}` lot, which is never valued through USD. A USD-cost lot bought
later, in another account, has the same effect (hunter's run).

Control: `p0.bean` (the same without the 2025 USD lot) says `HOOL has no EUR
price at any date; shown in units`, which is w5/557's acceptance wording.

Expected: the undated wording, because no unvalued lot is held at USD cost.

Cause: `_price_summary_lines` (`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:699`) takes
`_cost_currencies(ledger.all_entries)` (`:713`): every posting ever made,
whatever the period, the account filter, or whether the lot is still held.

Fix: take the cost currencies from the lots actually left unvalued.

Dedupe: w5/557 and 126 (done) are the parents.

Acceptance: `p2.bean` gets the `p0.bean` wording.
