# In `bea ask`, Ctrl-C between the approval panels of a multi-write reply returns to the prompt, but the queued writes keep running: the next panel draws over the prompt, an approved write lands after the turn was cancelled, and Ctrl-D then hangs (incomplete fix of w5/544)

**Major · cli · 20–30m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `eea79d702`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Simulation: a local AI-proxy stub on 127.0.0.1 (fake token), driven through a PTY. Follow-up to w5/544 (done, `2857e778e`).
USAGE:1798-1804 says Ctrl-C "abandons the turn in flight, and returns to the
prompt", and that a cancelled turn "names the directives it already wrote".
w5/544's acceptance says Ctrl-D never hangs. No product fix implemented.

The stub's first reply holds three `write_directive` calls (Alpha, Bravo,
Charlie, each a small Food/Cash transaction); later replies are text. The
ledger holds 30,000 small transactions so each dry run takes about 3 s, which
only widens the window.

In `bea -f /abs/books/main.bean ask` on a PTY:
1. Type `add three`.
2. Answer `y` to the first "Confirm AI write" panel.
3. Press Ctrl-C about 1 s later, then answer any panel that appears.
4. Press Ctrl-D.

Actual: my run, matching the hunter's 3 of 3:
- `(cancelled)` prints and the prompt returns.
- A few seconds later Bravo's panel draws over the prompt, and answering it
  writes Bravo after the turn was cancelled.
- Ctrl-D prints `Goodbye.`, then Charlie's panel appears, and the process
  never exits (killed at the harness timeout).
- The ledger ends with Alpha and Bravo, and the message never named Alpha.

Control: Ctrl-C during a single write's dry run gives `(cancelled)`, no
panel, and a clean exit. Every y/n/a/d/Enter answer, and Ctrl-C at a panel,
behaves correctly, and denied writes are never written.

Expected: once the turn is cancelled, no further panel appears and no further
write happens. Writes that already landed are named, and Ctrl-D exits.

Cause: the `write_directive` worker threads wait on `write_lock`
(`cli/src/cli/ask/agent.py:438`, `:449`) and outlive the interrupted run. The
REPL's `except KeyboardInterrupt` (`cli/src/cli/ask/repl.py:326`) cannot cancel
them, so each later runs its dry run, its own prompt_toolkit confirm app and
its write. Two apps then read one terminal, and a blocked thread keeps the
process alive.

Fix: keep a per-turn cancel flag. Any `write_directive` that has not shown its
panel returns "Write cancelled" once the turn is interrupted. Record each
completed write in `deps.writes` before the interrupt is reported.

Dedupe: w5/544 (done) is the parent; w3/449, w5/547 and 550 cover other ask
interrupt paths.

Acceptance: in the scenario above, only Alpha is written and named, no panel
appears after `(cancelled)`, and Ctrl-D exits 0. A PTY test pins it.
