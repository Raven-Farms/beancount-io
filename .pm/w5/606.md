# A stop that lands just after the atomic replace makes bea die silently (SIGTERM/SIGINT/SIGHUP/SIGKILL) although the write committed; a retry then records the entry twice

**Minor · cli · 30–45m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-16, HEAD `c210da9b`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
USAGE says "a stopped write never commits" and "a retry never records the
entry twice", and gives exit 4 for "a write whose outcome is unknown". In a
window of about 30 ms after the replace, a stop is forwarded and the
frontend re-raises the signal on itself. The engine's success envelope (if
any) is discarded, and the process dies with no output. Supervisors and
agents that time out and retry then duplicate the write. No product fix
implemented.

A 40-transaction ledger. A watcher script polls `main.bean`'s (inode, size,
mtime) and sends SIGTERM to bea the moment it changes:

```sh
python3 oncommit.py <dir> main.bean TERM 0 -- add transaction Coffee --date 2026-01-05 -p "Expenses:Food 3 USD" -p Assets:Checking
bea -f <dir>/main.bean add transaction Coffee --date 2026-01-05 -p "Expenses:Food 3 USD" -p Assets:Checking   # the retry
```

Actual: bea exits by SIGTERM (`rc=-15`) with empty stdout and stderr, and the
transaction is in the ledger; the retry exits 0, and the ledger has two
`"Coffee"` entries (`check` still clean). SIGINT, SIGHUP and SIGKILL give the
same; `import --apply`, `init`, `format -i` and `add transactions` too. Only
from ~60 ms after the replace does bea reliably report "Added".

Control: stops before the replace leave the ledger unchanged (dozens of
signal × delay cases for add, import and format).

Expected: once the commit has happened, bea reports it (the normal success
output, or an exit-4 "the write may have completed; check before retrying").
It never dies silently after writing.

Cause: `_forwarding_teardown.forward` (`cli/src/cli/engine/launch.py:534-546`)
re-raises the forwarded signal on the frontend after the child exits; the
engine's `_terminate` (`cli/src/bea_engine/stopping.py:120-129`) raises
`SystemExit` even after `append` committed (`write.py:2750-2751` has no
committed state).

Fix: in the engine, defer termination from the commit until the envelope is
written; in the frontend, after forwarding a stop, render a received success
envelope (or exit 4 if the child died after committing) instead of dying.
Narrow USAGE's claim for SIGKILL, which cannot be deferred.

Dedupe: w5/300 and w1/074 (done) cover the pre-commit window; w5/437 covers
captured natives.

Acceptance: a SIGTERM delivered right after the replace yields the success
output or exit 4, never a silent death. A test with an injected delay pins it.
