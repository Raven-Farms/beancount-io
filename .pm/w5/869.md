# `import --apply` raced by a write to an include in another directory still exits 4 instead of queueing (remainder of w5/718)

**Minor · cli · 2–4h · A1.** Split from w5/718 (done, `fe80c7096`), 2026-10-18. The 718 fix covers every writer that decides under `locked_graph` (all of `add`, raw `bea-engine append`); `import --apply` still decides lock-free and keeps its safe exit-4 refusal. No product fix implemented.

`books/main.bean` holds `option "operating_currency" "USD"`, opens, `include "2024/jan.bean"`, and ~20k transactions; `imp.csv` holds one ordinary row.

```sh
bea -f /abs/books/main.bean import imp.csv --csv date=Date,amount=Amount,narration=Description --account Assets:Checking --apply &
sleep 0.5; bea format -i /abs/books/2024/jan.bean; wait
```

Actual: `format -i` exits 0. The import exits 4 with `Error: The ledger changed during the operation: …/books/2024/jan.bean. Nothing was written; retry.` Nothing is written and nothing corrupts — the refusal is safe, just not queued.

Expected: the import waits for `format -i` (or the other writer) and then lands, as USAGE promises writers queue. Exit 4 stays for external edits and for preview-vs-apply staleness (`expected`).

Cause: `importing.answer` (`cli/src/bea_engine/importing.py:131-817`) captures its snapshot at `:177`, extracts, dedupes against that load, validates at `:668`, and appends at `:805` — all with no lock held. The 718 `append` widening cannot help: it re-verifies the handed-in snapshot under the wide locks, but with `validated` already computed it must refuse rather than re-validate, because re-validation alone would not re-run the dedupe decisions the raced commit invalidated.

Fix: restructure the apply path to extract lock-free, then take `locked_graph` and re-capture, re-load, re-dedupe, re-validate, and append under it. Previews (`apply=False`) must stay lock-free. Do NOT paper over with validate-only retry: a dedupe decision invalidated mid-apply must be re-decided, never skipped, or rows double-book.

Dedupe: w5/718 (done) is the parent; w5/183 (done) the deciding-lock pattern.

Acceptance: the race above lands both writes repeatedly; a test pins the import-vs-format case; no duplicate rows under the race.
