# A `-t` period in which no account holds the foreign currency is refused with "Missing prices for USD → EUR" because of an earlier, settled currency trade: the period cut's own equity entries are checked one by one

**Minor · cli · 15m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `d657cff90`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
No product fix implemented.

`usdtrip.bean` (operating currency EUR):
- `Assets:Bank` opens with 1000.00 EUR.
- 2026-01-10: buys `100.00 USD @ 0.92 EUR` into `Assets:Wallet`.
- 2026-01-12: spends the 100.00 USD on `Expenses:Food`.
- 2026-02-10: a 5.00 EUR coffee.

```sh
bea --json -f /abs/usdtrip.bean report balance-sheet -t 2026-02
bea -f /abs/usdtrip.bean balance -t 2026-02
```

Actual:
- Both exit 1: `Missing prices for USD → EUR. … Add prices covering the dates
  below or pass --allow-errors for partial balances.` and `USD has no EUR price
  at any date; shown in units`. `report trial-balance -t 2026-02` fails the same
  way.
- With `--allow-errors`, the result is a "Partial valuation" with no
  adjustment or total-equity line. `Earnings:Previous` reads `100.00 USD`, and
  `Opening-Balances` reads `-908.00 EUR -100.00 USD`. The USD nets out.
- Adding any made-up USD price (0.50 or 2.00 EUR) makes every total identical
  to the cent. Only the split between the two equity lines moves (hunter's
  runs).

Control: `report income-statement -t 2026-02` and `balance Bank -t 2026-02` exit 0.

Expected: nothing holds USD at 2026-02-28, and every total can be valued. The
report should be complete and reconciled, and exit 0. USAGE ties the refusal
to amounts the report cannot value, and the "Add prices covering
2026-02-28" advice misleads.

Cause: the period cut moves January's USD dinner into `Earnings:Previous` and
leaves the trade's −100 USD in `Opening-Balances`. The balance sheet, the
trial balance and `_balances` check every equity node's own balance for
missing prices (`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py`, around `:174`, `:321-325`,
`:367-373`), and `_valuation` (around `:731-757`) raises.

Fix: judge the cut-created equity lines by their combined balance (or value
them at the booked amounts), so a period with no foreign holdings needs no
foreign price.

Dedupe: w5/127 and w3/288 (done) cover other equity and missing-price cases.

Acceptance: both commands exit 0 with a reconciled report. A test pins it.
