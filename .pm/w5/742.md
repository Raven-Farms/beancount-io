# Import-id hashing gaps: a Python importer's second buy at a different compound cost (`{100 # 10 USD}` after `{100 # 5 USD}`) is skipped as an exact duplicate, and dedup.md says source postings are joined "in sorted order" while bea sorts them numerically (incomplete fix of w5/349 and w5/555)

**Minor · cli + skills · 15m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `15b5e52f4`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Follow-up to w5/349 and w5/555 (done).
IMPORTING.md:464-467 says two same-day buys at different prices are two rows.
dedup.md is the grammar importer authors compute ids from. No product fix
implemented.

The importer is a minimal beangulp `Importer` that reads JSON rows and builds
`CostSpec(number_per, number_total, currency, …)` postings on
`Assets:Broker:AAPL`. The ledger opens `Assets:Broker:AAPL` and
`Assets:Broker:Cash`, with cash.

1. **Compound cost.**
   - `a.rows` is one 2026-05-07 "BUY AAPL" row: `5 AAPL {100 # 5 USD}`, cash
     −505. `b.rows` is the same with `{100 # 10 USD}`, cash −510.
   - `bea --json --no-input --file /abs/main.bean import a.rows --config
     importers.py --apply` writes `import-id: "csv:sha256:f24c082abb6572b0"`
     and `5 AAPL {100 # 5 USD}`.
   - The same for `b.rows` exits 0 with row status `duplicate`
     (`import-id csv:sha256:f24c082abb6572b0 is already in the ledger.`) and
     `written: 0`. The preview shows `5 AAPL {100 USD}`, which is neither
     lot's real cost (101 or 102 per unit).
   - Control: `b` with a plain `{102 USD}` is new (hunter's run).
2. **Sort order.**
   - One row posting `10 AAPL {120 USD}` then `2 AAPL {100 USD}` is written
     as `csv:sha256:6648ad67a6d94d97`. That is the hash of
     `2026-05-07|2 AAPL {100 USD}+10 AAPL {120 USD}|BUY AAPL|Assets:Broker:AAPL`,
     sorted by number.
   - Sorting the strings, as dedup.md's "in sorted order" reads, gives
     `10 AAPL {120 USD}+2 AAPL {100 USD}` → `2d4e9f6e65e25e79`.
   - `-1 BTC` / `-0.5 ETH` differ the same way (hunter's run).
   - The doc's only example, `-1 BTC+-1 ETH`, sorts the same either way.

Expected: (1) different compound costs give different ids. (2) dedup.md
states the order bea uses, with an example where the two orders differ.

Cause:
- `_lot` (`cli/src/bea_engine/importing.py:876-889`) uses `number_total` only
  when `number_per` is missing.
- The legs are sorted by number, currency and lot text before the `+` join
  (`:1411-1428`).
- `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-import/references/dedup.md:29` says
  "sorted order".

Fix: give a compound cost the per-unit cost `number_per + number_total/|units|`
(or render the `#` form), in both the hash and the preview. Say "sorted by
numeric amount, then commodity, then lot" in dedup.md with a differing
example.

Dedupe: w5/349 and 555 (done) are the parents; w5/384 and 538 cover input
refusals.

Acceptance: `b.rows` imports as new, and dedup.md's grammar reproduces
`6648ad67a6d94d97`. Tests pin the compound case.
