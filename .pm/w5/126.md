# The missing-price notice says "no price at any date; shown in units" for a holding the same report values through its cost currency

**Minor · cli · 30–40m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-11, HEAD `a8b75aed` (CLI unchanged since `97600b74`),
checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS.
A foreign-listed stock held in a home-currency ledger is valued through its
cost currency (ACME → USD → EUR). When only an early row lacks a rate, the
notice claims ACME "has no EUR price at any date; shown in units" — directly
above a report that shows the holding in EUR. Under `--json` that sentence
is the error detail of a run that exits 1. No amounts are wrong. No product
fix implemented.

Create disposable `/absolute/books/main.bean` (`bea check` exits 0):

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "EUR"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Bank
2026-01-01 open Assets:Broker
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening

2026-01-02 * "opening"
  Assets:Bank  5000.00 EUR
  Equity:Opening

2026-01-15 * "buy a US stock"
  Assets:Broker  10 ACME {100.00 USD}
  Assets:Bank  -800.00 EUR @@ 1000.00 USD

2026-02-27 price ACME 110.00 USD
2026-02-27 price USD 0.90 EUR
2026-03-31 price ACME 120.00 USD
2026-03-31 price USD 0.95 EUR
```

Use isolated BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1, no
inherited BEA_FILE/BEA_TOKEN, closed stdin, no TTY.

```sh
bea -f /absolute/books/main.bean report balance-sheet --allow-errors
bea --json -f /absolute/books/main.bean report balance-sheet
```

Actual: the first exits 0 showing `Broker 1,140.00 EUR` and net worth
`Unavailable EUR` for 2026-01-31, `5,190.00 EUR` for 02-28 and
`5,340.00 EUR` for 03-31, with stderr `ACME has no EUR price at any date;
shown in units`. The second exits 1 with that sentence as `details`, while
`missing_price_dates` lists only `2026-01-31`.

Independent arithmetic: 10 × 120 × 0.95 = 1,140 EUR on 03-31 and
10 × 110 × 0.90 = 990 EUR on 02-28; a worker's BQL double `convert` agrees.
Only January lacks a rate.

Control: on a single-hop ledger (ACME bought and priced in USD, operating
currency USD, first price 2026-02-27) the notice reads `ACME → USD has no
price before 2026-02-27; earlier rows shown in ACME`.

Expected: `docs/USAGE.md` distinguishes "no … price at any date; shown in
units" from "has no price before <date>; earlier rows shown in …". Here the
second applies: `ACME → EUR has no price before 2026-02-27; earlier rows
shown in ACME`.

Cause: `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:453-455` (`_price_summary_lines`)
looks only at the direct pair of commodity and target currency, while the
valuation also goes through the lot's cost currency
(`cli/src/fava/core/conversion.py:104-125`). A commodity priced only in its
cost currency therefore always gets the "at any date" wording.

Fix: derive the line from the dates actually missing (already in
`missing_price_dates`) or from the earliest date either route resolves, and
say "at any date; shown in units" only when no report date converts. No
dependency or API change.

Blast radius: all reports and `bea balance`, in the stderr notice, JSON
`missing_price_summary` and strict-mode `error.details`.

Dedupe: board search for the two wordings and `_price_summary_lines` finds
w1/m13/t002 (which introduced them), w3/427 (conversion-name validation) and
w1/125 (headline nulling); none covers the cost-currency route.

Acceptance: on the fixture the notice names 2026-02-27 as the first priced
date in text, JSON summary and error details; a commodity with no route at
any date keeps today's wording; the single-hop control is unchanged. Only
macOS and the checkout were exercised.
