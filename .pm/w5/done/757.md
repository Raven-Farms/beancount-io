# `£` and `₩` are treated as GBP and KRW only, so a GIP, FKP, SHP or EGP (or KPW) account's export is refused, and the suggested fix books the money as GBP or KRW

**Minor · cli · 5–10m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `0e9729be5`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
IMPORTING.md:112-115 applies the refusal only to "a symbol that names exactly
one commodity", and says `$` and `¥` "name several commodities each and are
accepted". `£` also names GIP, FKP, SHP and EGP, and `₩` also names KPW. No
product fix implemented.

`main.bean`: `option "operating_currency" "GIP"`, `open Assets:Gib GIP`, `open
Expenses:Uncategorized`. `gip.csv` is `Date,Description,Amount` /
`2026-01-02,Coffee,-£12.50`.

```sh
bea --file /abs/main.bean import gip.csv --account Assets:Gib --apply
```

Actual: exit 2, `Row 1 (line 2): column 'Amount' carries '£' (GBP), but the
row would post GIP. Name the commodity with --csv currency=GBP … or open the
source account for GBP.`. Both suggestions relabel the money. A KPW account
with `-₩1200` is refused the same way, naming KRW (hunter's run).

Control: `€` on a EUR account and `$` on any account are accepted.

Expected: `£` is accepted on an account whose currency is one of the
commodities it names (GBP, GIP, FKP, SHP, EGP), and likewise `₩` for KRW and
KPW.

Cause: `_UNAMBIGUOUS_SYMBOLS` (`cli/src/bea_engine/csv_mapper.py:60`) maps `£`
and `₩` to one currency each, and `_check_cell_symbols` (`:1128`) applies it.

Fix: map those symbols to every commodity they name, and accept the cell when
the resolved currency is among them.

Dedupe: w3/421 (done) introduced the check.

Acceptance: the GIP fixture imports `-12.50 GIP`. A test pins it.

---
**Resolved 2026-10-19** — `£`/`₩` map to every commodity they name (GBP/GIP/FKP/SHP/EGP; KRW/KPW); rows post when their currency is among them, genuine mismatches list the whole set; shipped in `dabfdb4ce` (`cli`)
