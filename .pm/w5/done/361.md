# The `beancount-migrate` skill never says to negate a credit card's stated balance; with a derived opening the endpoint assertion is a tautology, so "Amex 129.24" migrates as a +129.24 credit balance and every check passes

**Minor · skills · 15–20m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-14, HEAD `bce2c992`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1, engine Python 3.12.
This is the [359](./359.md) gap, in the migrate skill, where it is worse.
The skill asks for each account's **current balance** "from the old app's
account screen or the real bank". Apps and banks show a card's balance as
the positive amount owed. The skill gives liability sign rules only for
charges and payments, never for that balance.

With no stated opening, the skill derives `opening = current −
Σ(rows)`. The endpoint assertion then holds by construction, whatever
sign was used. A positive card balance therefore migrates as money the
bank owes the user:

- `check` passes and the report says "ties to";
- net worth is overstated by twice the card balance.

No product fix implemented.

Use the skill's own `evals/files/eval1_mint.csv` (Chase Checking plus Amex
Card; Amex flows in ledger sign are +370.76). Create the ledger with `bea
--no-input init . --currency USD --date 2026-01-01`. Use isolated
BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1, no inherited
BEA_FILE/BEA_TOKEN, and closed stdin. Follow `SKILL.md` §§2–5, with the
user stating the card balance as the app shows it, "Amex 129.24":

1. Map Amex to `Liabilities:CreditCard:Amex`.
2. Derive the opening, 129.24 − 370.76 = −241.52.
3. Write the batch with `bea --file "$L" --json --no-input add
   transactions --from batch.json --into main.bean`.
4. Run `bea --file "$L" add balance --date 2026-01-23 --account
   Liabilities:CreditCard:Amex --amount '129.24 USD'`.
5. Run `bea --file "$L" check`.

Actual:

- The batch, the assertion and `check` all exit 0.
- `bea balance Amex` shows `+129.24 USD`.
- The ledger claims the bank owes the user 129.24, so net worth is 258.48
  too high.

With a stated opening given unnegated ("owed 500 at start, owes 129.24
now"), the assertion fails with `expected 129.24 USD != accumulated
870.76 USD (741.52 too much)`. The skill then directs the agent to look
for "rows missing from the export, pending transactions, wrong stated
balance". Reproduced twice by the hunter. I checked the skill text
myself.

Control: stated as `-129.24`, the opening is −500.00 and the assertion
passes, matching eval1's expected values.

Expected: next to the liability sign rule, the skill should say that a
liability's current or opening balance as an app or bank shows it (the
amount owed) is negated into ledger sign before the opening math and the
assertion. A card in credit becomes positive. Because a derived opening
cannot catch this error, the skill should confirm the sign with the
user, for example "Amex shows 129.24 owed → ledger −129.24, right?".

Cause:

- `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-migrate/references/mapping.md:5`
  covers lines only.
- `mapping.md:37-38` asks for the balance with no sign rule.
- `SKILL.md:36`, `:57` and `:67` give no sign rule.
- `references/qbo.md:18` and `references/monarch.md:19` send the agent
  to app screens that show the amount owed as positive.
- Evals 1, 4, 5 and 6 word the prompt "Amex **owes** 129.24", which steers
  the model, so CI never sees an unsigned balance.

Fix:

- Add the balance rule beside `mapping.md:5`, and refer to it from `:37`
  and from `SKILL.md`.
- In the derived-opening note, say that a sign error is exactly what the
  tautological assertion cannot detect, and require the confirmation.
- Add an eval whose prompt says "Amex balance 129.24" and expects
  −129.24 or a confirmation question.

Blast radius: every `beancount-migrate` run that includes a credit card,
loan or line of credit. With a derived opening, the error is silent and
lasting.

Dedupe: searched `.pm/**`, including `done/`, for migrate together with
liability, credit card, current balance and sign. 359 covers the
reconcile skill only. w5/m10 covers asset-side transfer dedupe. Nothing
else matches.

Acceptance: following the skill with "Amex balance 129.24" either writes
−129.24 or asks for confirmation first, and `bea balance` shows
`-129.24 USD`. A CI-run eval covers the unsigned wording.

**Resolved.** `beancount-migrate/references/mapping.md` changes in three places:

- Next to the account sign rule, it now says a liability's balance flips too. "Amex 129.24" owed is `−129.24`, and a card in credit is positive.
- Step 1 now requires confirming a liability balance that comes without a sign or an "owed"/"credit" word ("Amex shows 129.24 owed → ledger −129.24, right?"). It shows the eval1 arithmetic as a marked `liability-opening` example: current −129.24, rows +370.76, opening −500.00.
- Step 2 now explains why the confirmation is needed: a derived opening makes the assertion pass whatever sign was used.

`SKILL.md` Identify/Convert/Verify, `qbo.md` and `monarch.md` say the same.

New eval8 (`liability-balance-unsigned`) prompts with "Amex balance 129.24". It expects −129.24, or a sign confirmation, and an opening of −500.00, not −241.52.

`test_migrate_negates_a_stated_card_balance_before_the_opening_math` checks the example against an independent sum of the eval1 Amex rows. It then writes the −500.00 opening and those rows and asserts `-129.24 USD` through the balance recipe, and `check` passes. The test fails without the rule.
