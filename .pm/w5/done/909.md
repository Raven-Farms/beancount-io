# Refuse an unforced close when offsetting units still leave a cost-lot balance

**Minor · cli · 30–45m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-21, checkout
`bea 0.3.1`, HEAD `e9c4c0d1d` (CLI unchanged through `eb03e9b7a`), Darwin
25.5.0 arm64, frontend Python 3.13.3. Two remaining lots net to zero shares
but −100 USD at cost. `add close` treats that as drained and closes without
`--force`, stranding the lots. No product fix implemented.

Create `/abs/books/main.bean`:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2024-01-01 open Assets:Stock HOOL "NONE"
2024-01-01 open Assets:Cash USD
2024-02-01 * "Long"
  Assets:Stock 1 HOOL {100 USD}
  Assets:Cash -100 USD
2024-02-02 * "Short"
  Assets:Stock -1 HOOL {200 USD}
  Assets:Cash 200 USD
```

`NONE` is an explicitly supported booking method here; both cost lots are
intentional. The fixture passes the real CLI check before and after closing.
From `/abs/books`, closed stdin/no TTY, isolated absolute `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`
and `XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, with inherited `BEA_FILE`
and `BEA_TOKEN` removed:

```sh
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean query "SELECT sum(position), sum(cost(position)) WHERE account = 'Assets:Stock'"
bea --json --no-input -f /abs/books/main.bean add close --date 2024-02-03 --account Assets:Stock
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean check
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean balance Assets:Stock -x at_cost -t 2024-02
```

Actual: query exits 0 and returns two positions, **+1 HOOL {100 USD,
2024-02-01}** and **−1 HOOL {200 USD, 2024-02-02}**, with total cost
**−100 USD**. Close exits 0, `written: 1`, `warnings: []`, appending
`2024-02-03 close Assets:Stock`. Check passes. A fresh query retains the
same two positions and −100 USD cost; the filtered book-value balance still
shows `assets.balance_children: {"USD":"-100"}`. No holding was cleared.
Human close also succeeds without warning. Both modes reproduced twice
from pristine fixtures.

A subsequent attempt to clear the lots is refused at exit 1 for an inactive
account, preserving the closed ledger bytes:

```sh
bea --json --no-input -f /abs/books/main.bean add transaction 'Clear lots' --date 2024-02-04 -p 'Assets:Stock -1 HOOL {100 USD, 2024-02-01}' -p 'Assets:Stock 1 HOOL {200 USD, 2024-02-02}' -p 'Assets:Cash -100 USD'
```

Expected: the existing funded-close policy refuses the close at exit 1 and
leaves the file unchanged, naming the remaining cost lots and requiring a
clearing transaction on/before the close date or an explicit `--force`.
A zero total share count does not establish that this account has no book
balance. The policy introduced by w2/092 and USAGE's `add close` example
requires an explicit force when a balance remains.

Twice-fresh controls:

- Add `--force` to the affected fixture: closes successfully, deliberately
  retaining the same positions/cost balance.
- Keep just the +1 share purchase: unforced close exits 1, names `still
  holds 1 HOOL`, writes nothing, and no close lists afterward.
- Give the negative share the same 100 USD cost and explicit acquisition
  date `2024-02-01`: the actual position and cost inventories are both
  empty, and unforced close correctly succeeds.
- Costless +1/−1 HOOL postings also genuinely cancel and correctly close.
- Run the lot-clearing transaction above on February 3 before closing:
  both position/cost inventories become empty, and an unforced same-day
  close succeeds and passes check.
- Add `plugin "beancount.plugins.check_drained"` to the affected fixture:
  the unforced close still succeeds with −100 USD cost remaining. Its
  synthesized units assertion is zero and passes; it cannot rescue this
  cost-lot case. All 12 initial fixture journeys pass check; before/after
  BQL confirms that closing itself clears none of their holdings.

Cause: `_held_through` in `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/adding.py:305–321`
adds only `posting.units.number` under a commodity key, discarding costs,
lot dates and labels, then removes zero sums. `_close` (:324–350) gets an
empty dictionary for these remaining positions and skips its refusal.
With `check_drained`, it bypasses even that check at :339. Ordinary ledger
validation permits a nonempty close, so `_appended` accepts it. History
`d70e58002` added this helper/guard for w2/092.

Fix: assess the close from the booked inventory through its date, retaining
cost-lot information until deciding whether a balance remains. Name those
positions accurately in the refusal; advising a bare USD posting to this
HOOL account would not clear its lots. Do not rely on a units-only plugin
assertion for this case. `_held_through` also feeds same-day bulk ordering
at :1990, tracked by [884](./884.md); preserve that caller's required unit
view rather than changing its return meaning incidentally. No dependency
change is needed.

Dedupe: whole-board searches for closing offset/net-zero lots and
`_held_through`, plus current milestone review, found no open coverage.
Completed w2/092 is the parent. Open [888](./888.md) concerns the plugin
shortcut hiding a nonzero cash balance beside a same-day assertion; its
proposed unit-sum check would still miss this no-plugin cost-lot fixture.
Hosted funded-close parity remains separately tracked by blocked w2/095;
no hosted behavior was tested or claimed here.

Acceptance: both affected plugin/no-plugin fixtures refuse unforced close
without writing, preserve the two lots and −100 USD cost, and offer explicit
force or a lot-clearing remedy. Keep forced, genuinely drained and ordinary
funded controls correct, including same-day postings. Pin real CLI coverage.
Evidence is under ignored `.tmp/qa-2026-10-20-cli-loop-pass8-80df309f/`:
`repro_close_offset_cost_lots.py`, `close-offset-cost-results.json` and
`captures/close-offset-*.json`.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — `add close` now also nets the account's booked postings per lot (cost number, currency, date, label) and, where a commodity's units cancel but its lots do not, refuses an unforced close naming each remaining lot and the remedy (a clearing transaction on or before the close date, or `--force`); a units balance keeps its existing refusal and `check_drained` cannot rescue the lot case since its assertion sees units only. Same-cost offsets, costless offsets, a clearing transaction and `--force` still close. `cli/tests/test_add_close_offsetting_lots.py` fails for the two refusal cases without the fix; shipped in the commit that moves this note (`cli`). The human-mode close output and same-day bulk ordering (which keeps `_held_through`'s unit view) were not rerun.
