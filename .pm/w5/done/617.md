# Interval series judge clipped boundary fragments by the wrong entries: a plugin-generated transaction there is dropped (weekly rows sum to 960, headline 965), while a lone balance assertion keeps an empty one-day row (gap in w5/086/365)

**Minor · cli · 20–30m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-16, HEAD `0c520fd1`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
USAGE: flow series "drop such a fragment when no entries fall in it, so a
clipped boundary does not read as one more quiet week", and "a fragment
holding entries … stays". The quiet-fragment test treats every entry from a
`<…>` filename as synthesized, though only the clamp's `<summarize>`,
`<transfer_balances>` and `<conversions>` are. Plugins conventionally use
`<name>` too. It also counts any declared directive (balance, price, open,
note), not postings to the series' accounts. No product fix implemented.

1. **Plugin transaction dropped.** `accrue.py` adds `2026-03-31 * "interest
   accrual"` (`Assets:Cash 5.00 USD`, `Income:Interest -5.00 USD`) with
   `data.new_metadata("<accrue>", 0)`. The ledger has a 40.00 food expense on
   03-05 and 1000.00 salary on 03-20.
   `bea --json -f /abs/main.bean report income-statement -t 2026-03 -i
   weekly` gives the headline `net_profit` 965.00, but the rows are
   03-08…03-29 and sum to 960.00. The overview's 03-31 row shows assets
   rising while income reads "—".
2. **Empty row kept.** A ledger with `2026-03-01 balance Assets:Cash -5 USD`
   and `2026-03-31 balance Assets:Cash 955 USD` around the same March
   activity: the weekly rows are `2026-03-01, 03-08, 03-15, 03-22, 03-29,
   03-31`, with the first and last all zero. Month-start and month-end
   statement assertions are what the close workflow writes, so every closed
   month shows these rows.

Control: (1) with `-i monthly` the single row is 965.00; (2) without the two
`balance` lines the rows are 03-08…03-29.

Expected: a fragment is quiet unless a transaction in it posts to the
series' in-scope accounts; then (1) keeps the 03-31 row (rows sum to the
headline) and (2) drops both empty rows.

Cause: `cli/src/fava/modules/chart.py:62-66`: `declared` excludes every entry
whose filename starts with `<`, and `quiet_fragment` counts any declared
directive via `slice_entry_dates`.

Fix: test fragments for transactions posting to the flow accounts,
whatever their source; exclude only the clamp's three synthesized markers.

Dedupe: w5/086, w5/365 and w3/375 (done) handled the clamp entries and the
leading fragment.

Acceptance: both fixtures behave as above. Tests pin them.

**Resolved 2026-10-17** — Shipped in `98178483` (`cli`).
- `_flow_ranges` (`cli/src/fava/modules/chart.py`, marked as a Beancount.io change) now counts a clipped fragment as busy only when a transaction in it posts to an income or expense account in scope.
- Only the clamp's `<summarize>`, `<transfer_balances>` and `<conversions>` entries are excluded, so a plugin's `<accrue>` transaction counts. Balance, price and open directives no longer keep a row.
- The flow roots are the statement's, so the income, expense and net-profit series keep the same rows.
- Fixture 1 (plugin accrual on 03-31) now lists 03-08 to 03-31, and the rows sum to the 965.00 headline. Fixture 2 (month-start and month-end balance assertions) lists 03-08 to 03-29, without the empty 03-01 and 03-31 rows.
- USAGE documents the rule.
- `tests/test_interval_fragments_by_flow_postings.py` pins both; both fail without the fix. `make check-all` passed.
