# `doctor linked`/`region` precision after w5/571: a currency carried only by interpolated legs now prints at the ledger's maximum precision (`-2451.2000 USD`), and `--conversion cost|value` still prints 26 decimals (regression and incomplete fix of w5/571)

**Minor · cli · 10–15m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `b9427aca2`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Follow-up to w5/571 (done, `1ddc2c9be`).
No product fix implemented.

1. **Max precision (new with the fix).** The ledger opens `Assets:Broker`,
   `Assets:Cash USD` and `Expenses:Food USD`, and holds:
   - a typed lunch, `12.50 USD`;
   - `2024-01-03 * "Buy VTI" ^b`: `Assets:Broker  10 VTI {245.12 USD}` and an
     interpolated `Assets:Cash` leg;
   - `2024-01-31 price VTI 251.3378 USD`.

   `bea doctor linked /abs/main.bean ^b` exits 0. The entry listing shows
   `Assets:Cash -2451.20 USD`, but the tree shows `` `-- Cash -2451.2000 USD ``.
   Upstream `bean-doctor`, the pre-fix source and `bea balance` show 2 places
   (hunter's runs).
2. **`--conversion`.** A ledger with `2024-01-03 * "Buy" ^x`, `Assets:Broker  3
   HOOL {{100.00 USD}}`, an interpolated `Assets:Cash`, and `2024-01-05 price
   HOOL 40.00 USD`. `bea doctor region /abs/total.bean 4:6 --conversion cost`
   prints `Broker 99.99999999999999999999999999 USD` and `Cash
   -99.99999999999999999999999999 USD`. With `--conversion value` it prints
   `120.00000000000000000000000000` and `-20.00000000000000000000000001`. Upstream
   prints `100.00`/`-100.00` and `120.00`/`-20.00` (hunter's run).

Expected: tree amounts at the ledger's usual precision (2 places here) in
every mode.

Cause:
- `_ExactInScope` (`cli/src/bea_engine/scoped.py:155-166`) forces
  `Precision.MAXIMUM` on a copy of the ledger's context, whose
  `fractional_max` comes from price and cost numbers.
- The converted realized balances, including the interpolated legs' 26-digit
  cost, are learned as display precision (around `:181-190`).

Fix: for inferred-only currencies, use the ledger's most-common precision.
Quantize converted balances to the learned precision before learning them.

Dedupe: w5/571 and 498 (done) are the parents; w5/668 (open) is the
report-side analogue.

Acceptance: fixture 1 prints `-2451.20`; fixture 2 prints `100.00` /
`-100.00` and `120.00` / `-20.00`. Tests pin both.

---
**Resolved 2026-10-19** — inferred-only currencies render at the ledger's most-common precision, and converted balances using all 28 division digits are learned rounded to it (honest conversions still learn raw, keeping w5/498); shipped in `fb52c3881` (`cli`)
