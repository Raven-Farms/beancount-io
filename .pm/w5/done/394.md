# `add close` for a never-opened account, or dated before the account's `open`, gets only upstream's bare "Unopened account X is being closed", which is wrong for an opened account and gives no remedy

**Trivial · cli · 10–15m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-14, HEAD `bce2c992`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1, engine Python 3.12.
Every other directive kind on a bad account gets bea's remedies:

- "Did you mean …" and an `add open` line for an unknown account;
- the open's date and location for an inactive one.

A `close` gets upstream's sentence with nothing added. When the account
exists but opens after the close date, the sentence is also misleading.
`Assets:Cash` *is* opened, just later. No product fix implemented.

Use a ledger that opens `Assets:Cash` and `Equity:Opening` (USD) on
2026-01-01 and has one seed transaction. Use isolated
BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1, no inherited
BEA_FILE/BEA_TOKEN, and closed stdin.

```sh
bea -f /absolute/books/main.bean add close --account Assets:Cash --date 2025-12-01
bea -f /absolute/books/main.bean add close --account Assets:Nope --date 2026-02-01
```

Actual: both exit 1 with `Proposed append (not written): Unopened account
Assets:Cash is being closed` and `… Assets:Nope …`. JSON gives the same.
There is no open location and no "Did you mean". Reproduced twice by the
hunter (text and JSON) and once by me.

Control: an `add balance` or `add note` on `Assets:Nope` gets `Did you
mean Assets:Cash? To create it, use bea add open …`. One dated before
the open gets `Account opened 2026-01-01 at main.bean:2 …`.

Expected: give `close` the same remedies.

- If the account was never opened: "Did you mean …", without advice to
  open an account only to close it.
- If the close predates the open: name the open's date and location and
  say to move the close date.

Cause: upstream `beancount/ops/validation.py` (`validate_open_close`)
reports "Unopened account … is being closed". The enrichment in
`cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/write.py` (around `:1225-1291`) matches only
`Invalid reference to …` messages.

Fix: in the same loop, handle a `Close` entry whose message contains "is
being closed". Look up the account's `Open` and add the location remedy
or the difflib suggestion. No dependency change.

Blast radius: `add close` and bulk close rows.

Dedupe: searched `.pm/**`, including `done/`, for "Unopened account" and
"being closed". There are no hits. w5/076, w5/222 and 393 cover other
messages.

Acceptance: the first command names `Assets:Cash`'s open at `main.bean:2`
(2026-01-01). The second suggests `Assets:Cash`. A test pins both.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-15** — Shipped in `987229e7`. The write error loop in `validate_candidate` (`cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/write.py`) now enriches upstream's "Unopened account X is being closed" on a `Close`:
- When the account opens later: "Account opened <date> at <file:line>; date the close on or after <date>."
- When it is never opened, the difflib suggestion: "Did you mean Assets:Cash?"
- Otherwise: "The ledger never opens X; check the account name."

None of these advise opening an account only to close it. The per-account open/close index the inactive-account and posting-currency hints build inline is now one `_lifecycles` helper, shared with this branch.

`tests/test_add_close_unopened_hint.py` pins:
- the note's two commands (open at `main.bean:2`, and `Assets:Nope` → `Assets:Cash`);
- an unrelated name.

All three fail at the prior HEAD.
