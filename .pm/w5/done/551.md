with `--time "$month"`, and beancount-init's `references/bea-cli.md` lists
them as the shared reads. Under `--json`, a report that must value an
unpriced commodity exits 1 unless `--allow-errors` is passed (USAGE,

**Resolved 2026-10-17** — Shipped in `d24b8bdb` (`skills`).
- **Close skill:** Phase 6 and its preamble (`beancount-close/SKILL.md`) name the valuation as book value. The new `close-income-statement` and `close-balance-sheet` recipes in `references/close-checklist.md` run both reports with `-x at_cost`, matching the checklist's `cost(position)` period-end balances. A cost-basis lot counts at its cost, and an unpriced commodity (an `@`-priced foreign charge, an option lot) appears in its own units instead of stopping the close. USD expense totals come from beancount-ask's `weight` recipes (Travel 330 USD for `300 EUR @ 1.10 USD`). A `missing_prices` exit is listed in the close report and never treated as an engine failure.
- **`bea-cli.md`:** engine repair is scoped to actual engine failures. A JSON error envelope is bea's answer about the ledger or the request, and a paragraph explains the `missing_prices` exit.
- **beancount-ask:** says the same for its `balance`/`report` reads.

Tests: eval 7 (`foreign-charge-report-at-book-value`, fixture `evals/files/eval7_ledger.beancount`) adds a foreign-charge close. `skills/scripts/test_beancount_skills.py::test_close_reports_run_at_book_value_beside_an_unpriced_foreign_charge` runs both recipes on that shape plus an AAPL lot, then checks complete valuation and the `weight` answer. It fails without the recipes, and fails again when they drop `-x at_cost` (`Missing prices for EUR → USD`).

Checks: `ci-check.py`, `test_ci_check.py` and `check-agent-guidance.py` pass. `test_beancount_skills.py` passes 84/84 against the checkout's `bea`. This machine's PATH `bea` is an old Homebrew 0.1.0, which fails an unrelated 0.3.1-only migration test.
