# beancount-migrate derives the opening from the user's *current* balance but asserts it the day after the export's last row, so any activity after the export is silently absorbed into the opening; the bank import then lands the ledger off by exactly that activity, with `check` clean

**Major · skills · 15–20m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `0a4036ed5`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
No product fix implemented; bea behaves correctly, and the cause is the skill
text.

The skill text involved:
- `skills/beancount-migrate/SKILL.md:39` and
  `references/mapping.md:39` ask for each account's **current balance** "from
  the old app or the real bank", with no date.
- `SKILL.md:60` and `mapping.md:47` derive `opening = stated current balance −
  Σ(converted rows)`, and `mapping.md:56` dates the endpoint assertion the day
  after the last row.
- `references/mint.md:3` notes that Mint exports stopped at the 2024 shutdown,
  and `references/monarch.md:20` covers a lapsed subscription. In both cases
  the only balance available is today's.

Fixture (synthetic):
- A Mint export with two Chase rows: 2026-01-10 payroll +2500.00 and
  2026-01-12 Trader Joes −54.20.
- The bank's CSV has those two rows plus 2026-01-20 Trader Joes −33.10.
- The user states Chase is 3412.70 today, so the skill derives an opening of
  966.90 (dated 2026-01-09).

Following the skill: `bea --no-input init . --currency USD --date 2026-01-09`,
`add open`, `add transactions --from batch.json` (opening plus the two rows),
`add balance --date 2026-01-13 … "3412.70 USD"`, `check`, then `bea import
bank.csv --csv 'date=Date,narration=Description,amount=Amount,sign=bank'
--account Assets:Bank:ChaseChecking --date-format %m/%d/%Y --apply` and
`check`.

Actual: every step exits 0, and `check` reports valid both times. The import
writes 1 row (2 exact duplicates), and Chase ends at **3379.60 USD**.

Expected: 3412.70. The true opening is 1000.00, and 1000 + 2500 − 54.20 − 33.10
= 3412.70. The derived opening is wrong by exactly the post-export activity,
and no check or report can reveal it.

Control: a balance stated as of the export's last day, or a stated opening,
gives a correct ledger or a visible refusal.

Fix: ask for the date each stated balance applies to. When it is after the
export ends, import the gap first, or use the balance only as a later check
rather than to derive the opening. Correct `monarch.md:20` and `mint.md`, and
add an eval with post-export activity.

Dedupe: w5/361 (done) is the liability-sign variant; w5/600–602 (open) cover
post-migration double-booking.

Acceptance: following the skill on this fixture ends at 3412.70, or asks for
the gap.
