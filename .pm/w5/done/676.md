# `bea init` and `bea price export` report an engine killed after writing as "produced no result" (exit 1 `validation`), though the files exist; the retry is then refused as "Already exists"

**Minor · cli · 5–10m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `6d74be10`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Simulated: a wrapper engine that runs the real one, then `kill -KILL $$`.
The exit-code table gives 4 for "a write whose outcome is unknown", and `add`
does this when its engine dies. `init` and `price export` call the engine
without marking the call as a write, so an engine killed after writing (an
out-of-memory kill, a supervisor timeout) is reported as a plain failure.
No product fix implemented.

`BEA_ENGINE_PYTHON` points at a wrapper that runs
`~/.local/share/bea/engine/0.3.1/bin/python "$@"` and then kills itself with
SIGKILL (the `-c` probe passes through).

```sh
BEA_ENGINE_PYTHON=/abs/fakeenv/bin/python bea --json init books --currency USD
bea init books --currency USD
BEA_ENGINE_PYTHON=/abs/fakeenv/bin/python bea -f /abs/main.bean price export -o exp
bea -f /abs/main.bean price export -o exp
```

Actual: `init` exits 1 (`validation`): `The Beancount engine was killed by
SIGKILL answering 'bea-engine init …' and produced no result.`, yet
`books/main.bean` exists; the retry exits 4 with `Already exists`. `price
export` likewise exits 1 with `exp/main.bean` written, and the retry exits 2
asking for `--force`.

Control: `add open` under the same wrapper exits 4 with "the outcome is
unknown" (and the entry landed).

Expected: exit 4 with the unknown-outcome wording for both.

Cause: `cli/src/cli/commands/init.py:182` and `cli/src/cli/commands/price.py:85`
call `launch.helper_json(argv)` without `writes=True`.

Fix: pass `writes=True` for `init` and `price export`.

Dedupe: w5/606 (open) is about frontend signals after a commit; w5/252 and
309 (done) are different.

Acceptance: both commands exit 4 under the wrapper. A test pins them.

Addendum (CLI QA 2026-10-17, HEAD `2f69b1461`): the same missing `writes=True` also
defeats w5/606's stop handling. SIGTERM sent as soon as `main.bean` appears,
during `bea init main.bean --currency USD --date 2026-01-01`, ends with rc −15
and no output while `main.bean` exists. The retry then exits 4 "Already
exists". SIGINT and SIGHUP behave the same (hunter's runs). Cause:
`cli/src/cli/commands/init.py` (around `:198`) calls `launch.helper_json(argv)`
without `writes=True`, so the committed check in `_run_helper` is always false.
The fix here (marking init and price export as writes) covers this too.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-18** — Shipped in `8e8afe844536f8867d27fe9ee69652b521463152` (`cli`). Init and portable price export now mark their helper calls as writes, so a lost answer after files are created reports conflict exit 4 with inspection-before-retry advice. The shared price-answer function keeps status/refresh as reads. Real signal tests also exposed two post-commit gaps: export now guards each final replacement with `stopping.committing()`, and the frontend evaluates the answer under its forwarding handlers, handing directly to post-commit handlers without restoring defaults in between. Original handlers still return when the command closes, and a late stop is reported once. Twenty-one real-command cases cover text/JSON lost and retained answers, ordinary exit 9, SIGTERM/SIGINT/SIGHUP after init/export commits, a deterministic frontend answer-handoff stop, healthy commands, and read controls. All pass. Reverting all four production files gives 13 failures and eight passing controls; reverting only the handoff reproduces its failure, and reverting only the export guard reproduces all three export signal failures while the three init controls pass. An existing delay-sweep test initially caught an add write followed by silent SIGTERM; the handoff fix resolves that same gap. Final `make check-all`: 7,891 passed, 2 skipped; 101 expanded checks passed; tracked-content secret scan clean.
