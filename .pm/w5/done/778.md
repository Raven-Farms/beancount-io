# beancount-migrate's per-account endpoint assertion fails on a correct migration when a card payment posts on different days in the two accounts (the ±3-day pairing dates the merged transfer on the outflow side), and the skill then blames missing rows; transfer and residual equity accounts are never opened

**Minor · skills · 10m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `0a4036ed5`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
No product fix implemented; the cause is the skill text.

The skill text involved: `SKILL.md:58` and `references/mapping.md:23` merge
transfer rows within ≤3 days into one transaction. `mapping.md:33` takes the
payee from the outflow side and gives no rule for the merged date.
`SKILL.md:60`/`mapping.md:56` date each account's assertion the day after
*its* last row. Evals 1 and 8 pin that.

1. **Lagged transfer.** Amex opens at −500.00, has a −63.75 Whole Foods
   charge on 2026-01-08, and a payment credited on 2026-01-14. Chase debits
   the payment on 2026-01-15, so the merged transfer is dated 2026-01-15. The
   stated Amex balance is −113.75, and the skill's arithmetic ties.
   - The assertion dated the day after Amex's last row, `bea --file
     /abs/main.bean --json --no-input add balance --date 2026-01-15 --account
     Liabilities:CreditCard:Amex --amount "-113.75 USD" --into main.bean`, exits
     1: `Balance failed … expected -113.75 USD != accumulated -563.75 USD (450.00
     too little)`.
   - `SKILL.md:73` then has the agent report missing rows, pending
     transactions or a wrong stated balance. None of those is the cause.
   - Control: the same assertion dated 2026-01-16 exits 0.
2. **Unopened equity accounts (trivial).** The skill books unpaired transfers
   to `Equity:Transfers-Review` and accepted residuals to
   `Equity:Migration-Residual` (`SKILL.md:58`, `:73`). Its open step (`SKILL.md:61`)
   covers only "mapped accounts", so the batch is refused until the agent
   follows bea's `add open` hint (hunter's run).

Expected: endpoint assertions dated after each account's last *converted*
posting (or merged pairs dated on the earlier row), and the review and
residual accounts opened with the rest.

Fix: update `SKILL.md` and `mapping.md`, and add a lagged-transfer fixture to
the evals.

Dedupe: searched "merged pair", "outflow side", "lagged"; no match.

Acceptance: following the skill on the lagged fixture writes a passing
