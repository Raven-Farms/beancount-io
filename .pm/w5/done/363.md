# `bea init` limits all 14 starter accounts, every expense and income category included, to the one operating currency, so an ordinary foreign card charge is refused with a bare `Invalid currency EUR for account 'Expenses:Dining'` and no remedy, and no `bea` command can widen the open

**Minor · cli · 20–30m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-14, HEAD `bce2c992`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1, engine Python 3.12.
`bea init` writes each starter account as `open <account> <CURRENCY>`.
That includes the categories (`Expenses:Dining`, `Expenses:Groceries`,
`Income:*`, `Expenses:Uncategorized`) as well as the card. Any posting in
another currency, such as a restaurant charge abroad written `45 EUR @
1.10 USD`, is then refused by `add` with Beancount's bare message:

- the message names neither the `open` line nor a fix;
- `bea add open` cannot widen the existing open, because it is refused as
  a duplicate;
- the only way out is to hand-edit the open line, and nothing says so.

No product fix implemented.

Use isolated BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1, no
inherited BEA_FILE/BEA_TOKEN, and closed stdin.

```sh
bea --json --no-input init a --currency USD --date 2026-01-01
bea --file /absolute/a/main.bean add transaction --date 2026-03-02 --payee CAFE --narration lunch -p 'Expenses:Dining 45 EUR @ 1.10 USD' -p Liabilities:CreditCard
bea --file /absolute/a/main.bean add open --account Expenses:Dining --date 2026-01-01 -c USD -c EUR
```

Actual:

- `init` writes 14 opens, each ending in ` USD`.
- The `add transaction` exits 1 with `Proposed append (not written):
  Invalid currency EUR for account 'Expenses:Dining'` and nothing more.
  The ledger hash is unchanged.
- The `add open` is refused with `Duplicate open directive for
  Expenses:Dining`.
- The bulk JSON form gives the same refusal. On an EUR ledger, putting the
  conversion on the card leg (`Liabilities:CreditCard -100 USD @@ 90
  EUR`) is refused the same way for the card.

Reproduced by the hunter on fresh USD and EUR ledgers, and by me on a
third.

Control: an open with no currency (`2026-01-01 open Expenses:Travel`)
plus the same command exits 0.

Expected:

- `docs/USAGE.md` "Creating a ledger" never says that `init` restricts
  accounts to one currency.
- The shared skill contract (`beancount-init/references/bea-cli.md:51`)
  says "Only restrict currencies when that matches the account's intended
  holdings". Expense and income categories are not holdings.
- `bea import` already offers a remedy for disallowed currencies, but
  `add` offers none.

Either change would fix this:

- `init` leaves the category accounts unrestricted, restricting at most
  the asset and liability accounts;
- the `add` refusal names the open's location and says to widen it
  (`USD EUR`) or to put an `@` price on an allowed leg.

Both would be best.

Cause:

- `cli/src/bea_engine/initiating.py:84` (`"".join(f"{day} open {account}
  {currency}\n" for account in ACCOUNTS)`), unchanged since the first
  engine commit.
- The no-bea template in
  `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-init/references/compatibility.md:35-48`
  has the same lines.
- The write-path hints in `cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/write.py` cover
  unknown accounts and balances, but not a transaction's currency
  violation.

Fix:

- Drop the currency from the `Income:*`, `Expenses:*` and
  `Equity:OpeningBalances` opens in both templates. Keeping it on assets
  and the card is a product choice, given w3/421's wrong-commodity
  protection.
- Add an `Invalid currency X for account 'A'` hint on `add` that names
  `file:line` of the open and the two remedies.
- Document whichever restriction remains under "Creating a ledger".
- Have the init skill ask whether the user spends in other currencies.
- No dependency change.

Blast radius: every ledger created by `bea init` (and by the init skill
without bea), on the first foreign-currency expense or income. That is
common for travellers and online purchases.

Dedupe: searched `.pm/**`, including `done/`, for `Invalid currency`,
widen, and init together with restrict or foreign currency. Related notes:

- w3/276, w3/323 and w5/074: import blocking and validation.
- 350: the balance-directive message.
- w3/421: CSV commodity resolution.
- 358: its fixture used an unrestricted account, so it never met this.

None covers `init`'s blanket restriction or the missing `add` remedy.

Acceptance: on a fresh `init` ledger, the EUR dining charge with an `@
USD` price is written, or the refusal names `main.bean:N` and says how to
widen it. `check` and `format --check` still pass on a fresh `init`. A
real-command test covers the charge and the hint.

**Resolved (both changes).**

- **Init template.** `bea init` (`initiating.py`) now opens only the `Assets:*` and `Liabilities:*` starter accounts for the chosen currency. The income, expense and equity categories take any currency, which keeps w3/421's wrong-commodity protection on the accounts that hold money. The init skill's no-bea template in `compatibility.md` matches.
- **Refusal hint.** A refused posting currency now gets a hint from `ledger/write.py` (`_currency_widen_hint`). It names the open's `file:line`, or the plugin behind it, and the allowed currencies. It gives the exact widened line to write, for example `'2026-01-01 open Liabilities:CreditCard USD,EUR'`, says that `bea add open` cannot change an existing open, and offers recording the posting in the allowed currency instead.
- **Docs.** `docs/USAGE.md` "Creating a ledger" documents the restriction.

`tests/test_init_category_currencies.py` covers three cases on a fresh init:

- the EUR dining charge is written, and `check` and `format --check` pass;
- a EUR card leg is refused, leaves the ledger unchanged, and the hint names `main.bean:9` and the remedies;
- with the fix stashed, both tests fail.

The init skill was not changed to ask about other currencies: with the categories unrestricted, that question is no longer needed.
