# `import --date-format '%d/%m'` (no year directive) books every row in 1900, and the blocked-row advice says to re-date the account's open to 1900

**Minor · cli · 5–10m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `46d7394e9`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
Many bank exports print dates without a year (`05/03`). No product fix
implemented.

`real.beancount` opens `Assets:Checking USD` and `Expenses:Uncategorized USD` on
2020-01-01. `noyear.csv` is `Date,Amount,Description` / `05/03,-10.00,A`.

```sh
bea --file /abs/real.beancount import noyear.csv --csv date=Date,amount=Amount,narration=Description --account Assets:Checking --date-format '%d/%m'
```

Actual:
- Exit 0. The preview shows `1 blocked 1900-03-05 A -10.00 USD` with `Row 1:
  Account 'Assets:Checking' opens 2020-01-01 …, after this row's 1900-03-05.
  Re-date that open to 1900-03-05 or earlier.`, and the diff adds
  `1900-03-05 ! "A"`.
- On a ledger whose opens predate 1900, the rows are `new` and `--apply` would
  write them.
- A `29/02` row fails as "cannot parse date" without saying why (1900 is not
  a leap year).

Control: `--date-format '%d/%m/%Y'` on dated rows parses correctly. With no
`--date-format`, the yearless file is refused.

Expected: exit 2 for a `--date-format` without `%Y`, `%y` or `%G`, saying rows
would be read as 1900.

Cause: `datetime.strptime` defaults the year to 1900
(`cli/src/bea_engine/csv_mapper.py:1230`), and `--date-format` is never checked
for a year directive.

Fix: refuse a date format with no year directive (and optionally none for
month or day).

Dedupe: searched `1900`, "no year", `%d/%m`; no match.

Acceptance: the command exits 2 naming the missing year.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-19** — a `--date-format` with no `%Y`, `%y` or `%G` exits 2 before any row is read instead of booking every row in 1900, shipped in `551b6e93c` (`cli`)
