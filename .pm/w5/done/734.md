# In `bea ask`, Ctrl-C about a second after submitting a question can end the whole session with exit 130, losing the conversation, instead of cancelling the turn (gap in w3/449)

**Trivial · cli · 5m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `eea79d702`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Simulation: a local AI-proxy stub that sleeps 4 s, driven through a PTY. Timing-dependent.
No product fix implemented.

In `bea --debug -f /abs/books/main.bean ask`, type `first`, press Enter, then
press Ctrl-C about 1.0 s later.

Actual: in 1 of 2 runs (the hunter saw 3 of 3), the process exits 130, the
stub receives no request, and the session is gone. The hunter's wrapper
caught the `KeyboardInterrupt` escaping from `status.start()`. The PTY sends
no cursor-position replies, which may widen the window; this was not tried
in a real terminal.

Control: Ctrl-C 2 s in prints `^C`, cancels the turn, and the next question
is answered.

Expected: USAGE:1798-1800 says Ctrl-C "abandons the turn in flight, and
returns to the prompt with the conversation intact".

Cause: `status.start()` and the lines before it
(`cli/src/cli/ask/repl.py`, around `:300-306`) sit outside the `try` whose
`except KeyboardInterrupt` cancels the turn.

Fix: move them inside the `try`.

Dedupe: w3/449 and w1/066 (done) cover the broader interrupt handling;
w5/731 is the multi-write case.

Acceptance: Ctrl-C at any point after Enter returns to the prompt.

**Resolved 2026-10-19** — Shipped in `040d5c5ce` (`cli`). The spinner startup moved inside the turn's `try`, with `status`/`turn` pre-bound and the spinner stopped in the arm, so Ctrl-C there cancels the turn instead of escaping to exit 130. Turn state resets via a new `begin_turn` helper at turn start (also called by `translated_failures`, keeping `--print`), so a pre-run cancel never names the previous turn's writes. Probed the live window first: no blocking gap on this machine's PTY (Ctrl-C at 0.3–1.5s always landed in the covered run), so the tests mock `status.start()` raising — pre-fix the KI escapes and kills the run, post-fix both pass. 2 new tests. Full suite 9106 passed / 2 skipped; `spec-check` still fails identically on clean HEAD (pre-existing backend drift). No doc change: USAGE already promised turn-cancel.
