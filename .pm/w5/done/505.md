# `report balance-sheet -x at_cost` (and `at_value`) claims `equity_reconciled: true` with a "valuation adjustment" of `-100.00 EUR 110.00 USD` across unlike commodities

**Minor · cli · 15–20m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-16, HEAD `e03cf71e`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1.
USAGE: "Unconverted `units` reports do not claim an equity reconciliation or
invent a valuation adjustment across unlike commodities." `at_cost` leaves
cost-less holdings (foreign cash) in their own units, so it is unconverted
in exactly that sense. It still reports a reconciled balance sheet whose
"adjustment" is simply the FX trade, in two currencies. No product fix
implemented.

`fxcost.bean`:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"

2024-01-01 open Assets:USD USD
2024-01-01 open Assets:EUR EUR
2024-01-01 open Equity:Opening-Balances

2024-01-01 * "Opening"
  Assets:USD 1000.00 USD
  Equity:Opening-Balances

2024-01-10 * "Buy EUR"
  Assets:EUR 100.00 EUR @ 1.10 USD
  Assets:USD -110.00 USD

2024-01-31 price EUR 1.20 USD
```

```sh
bea -f /abs/fxcost.bean --json report balance-sheet -x units
bea -f /abs/fxcost.bean --json report balance-sheet -x at_cost
```

Actual: `units` gives `equity_reconciled: false`, adjustment `null`. `at_cost`
gives the same net worth (`100.00 EUR, 890.00 USD`) but `equity_reconciled:
true`, `valuation_adjustment {"EUR": "-100.00", "USD": "110.00"}`; text prints
`Valuation/translation adjustment (credit): -100.00 EUR 110.00 USD`.
`at_value` on a ledger with lots in a foreign cost currency does the same.

Control: `-x USD` gives a single-currency adjustment, `-10 USD`, and
reconciles.

Expected: when the converted balances span more than one commodity, no
reconciliation is claimed and the adjustment is `null`, as for `units`.

Cause: `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:333` withholds the reconciliation only
for `conversion == "units"`.

Fix: withhold it whenever the converted net worth (or adjustment) has more
than one commodity; update USAGE's sentence to say so.

Dedupe: searched `equity_reconciled`, `unlike commodities`. w3/395 and w5/127
(done) are other reconciliation notes.

Acceptance: `at_cost` on the fixture reports `equity_reconciled: false` and no
adjustment; `-x USD` is unchanged. A test pins it.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-17** — Shipped in `7083b0a9` (`cli`). The balance sheet (`cli/src/bea_engine/report.py`) now withholds the equity reconciliation whenever the converted net worth or the valuation adjustment spans more than one commodity, as it already did for `units`. `-x at_cost` on the fixture reports `equity_reconciled: false` and `valuation_adjustment: null`. `-x USD` and `-x at_value` convert to one currency, so they still reconcile with a single-currency adjustment. `cli/docs/USAGE.md` states the rule. `tests/test_balance_sheet_reconciliation_single_commodity.py` pins both pairs; the `at_cost` case fails without the fix.
