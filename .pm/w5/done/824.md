# A CSV row with a payee that meets a payee-less ledger entry on its narration is told "Date, payee and source amount match" (gap in w5/602)

**Trivial · cli · 5m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `5b305b485`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Follow-up to w5/602 (done); not covered by w5/615.
No product fix implemented.

The ledger holds `2026-09-28 * "CARD PURCHASE 1234"` (no payee), Checking −4.50.
`bank.csv` is `Date,Payee,Description,Amount` / `2026-09-28,Starbucks,CARD
PURCHASE 1234,-4.50`.

```sh
bea --json --file /abs/main.bean import bank.csv --csv date=Date,payee=Payee,narration=Description,amount=Amount --account Assets:Checking
```

Actual: `possible_duplicate` with `Date, payee and source amount match; neither
has a bank ID.` The ledger entry has no payee; the two met on their
narrations, which is IMPORTING's own wording.

Expected: "Date, narration and source amount match…".

Cause: `_twin_reason` (`cli/src/bea_engine/importing.py:1175`) picks "payee"
from the row alone. `_possible_twin` (`:1911`) admitted the twin through the
narration key.

Fix: say "payee" only when both sides have one.

Dedupe: w5/602 and 615 (done) are related.

Acceptance: the reason says "narration".

Addendum (CLI QA 2026-10-17, HEAD `d6c32f470`): the mirror case. The ledger holds
`2026-10-04 * "Netflix" "monthly plan"`, and a narration-only row `10/04/2026,Netflix,-15.49`
is told `Date, narration and source amount match`. The row's narration met
the ledger's *payee*; the ledger's narration is "monthly plan". The fix should
name the fields that actually met on both sides.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-20** — `_twin_reason` now compares both sides. It says "Date, payee and source amount match" only when the two payees met, and "Date, narration and source amount match" when the narrations did. Otherwise it names the crossing: "Date and source amount match the ledger entry, and this row's narration is its payee" for the addendum's Netflix row, and "… this row's payee is its narration" for the reverse; an earlier row of the import is named as `row N of this import`. The note's Starbucks row now reads "Date, narration and source amount match; neither has a bank ID." `tests/test_import_twin_reason_names_met_fields.py` covers the note, its addendum, the reverse crossing and the payee control (three fail at HEAD).
