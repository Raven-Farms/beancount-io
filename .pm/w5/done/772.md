# A close dated before an account's later postings (or an open dated after its first posting) is reported as an error in the existing entry, citing the unwritten directive at a line past the end of the file and saying "Correct the transaction date"; the open variant is framed as a pre-existing error and `--allow-errors` then writes the misdated open

**Minor · cli · 15m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `955c0274e`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Sibling of w5/394 (done) and w5/666 (open, item 4).
No product fix implemented.

1. **Backdated close.** A 13-line ledger opens `Assets:Bank`, funds it, and
   has a 2026-02-10 "Lunch" posting on line 11. `bea -f /abs/main.bean add
   close --date 2026-02-01 -a Assets:Bank` exits 1, with the file unchanged:
   `…/main.bean:11: Invalid reference to inactive account 'Assets:Bank'
   Account opened 2026-01-01 at …/main.bean:3. Account closed 2026-02-01 at
   …/main.bean:15. Correct the transaction date or edit that directive…`.
   - Line 15 does not exist (it is the proposed close), and there is no
     "Proposed append" label.
   - The remedy points at the valid Lunch entry.
   - `close_tree`, an account opened in an include, and an importer `Close`
     row behave the same. The importer row previews as ready, then
     `--apply` exits 1 (hunter's runs).
2. **Late open.** `Expenses:Food` is posted on 2026-01-10 and never opened.
   `add open --date 2026-02-01 -a Expenses:Food` exits 1 with `The ledger
   already has 1 error(s); nothing was written. Pass --allow-errors to write
   anyway.`, and its detail cites the unwritten open's line. Following that
   advice writes the misdated open (exit 0), and `check` still fails (hunter's
   run).

Control: `add close --date 2026-02-10` and `add open --date 2026-01-10` write.

Expected: say the proposed close predates the account's last posting
(2026-02-10 at main.bean:11) and must be dated on or after it. For the open,
say it must be dated on or before 2026-01-10. This is the w5/394 wording for
a close before its open.

Cause: the inactive-account hint (`cli/src/bea_engine/ledger/write.py`, around
`:1762-1800`) prints the location of every open and close from `_lifecycles`
(`:2283`), including this write's appended one, and takes its noun from the
failing existing entry. `_preexisting_errors` (`:2334`) treats the reworded
error on an existing line as pre-existing.

Fix: when the named open or close is the write's own append, label it as
proposed, and give the date bound from the stranded postings.

Dedupe: w5/394 (done) covers a close before its open; w5/666 (open) item 4 is
the `auto_accounts` variant.

Acceptance: both refusals name the date bound and the existing posting, and
neither suggests `--allow-errors`. Tests pin both.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-19** — the backdated close now names the last stranded posting and its date bound, and an inactive-account error naming an account the write itself opens/closes is the write's own misdating (never pre-existing, never `--allow-errors`), shipped in `d6da791f5` (`cli`)
