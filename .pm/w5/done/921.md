# Classify report series by account root components, not text prefixes

**Major · cli · 35–50m · A1.** Valid renamed roots that share a text prefix
make report breakdowns classify equity as income or net worth. A monthly
profit of 20 USD becomes 125 USD in its breakdown; net-worth series can erase
an entire opening balance. Headlines and native BQL remain correct. No product
fix implemented.

Real CLI QA, 2026-10-21, HEAD `162bce75a` (CLI unchanged from
`73e13a673`), checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1 arm64, frontend Python
3.13.3, existing managed Python 3.12 engine. Fresh subprocesses, closed
stdin/no TTY, isolated absolute `BEA_CONFIG_DIR`/`XDG_CACHE_HOME`,
`BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1`, no inherited `BEA_FILE`/`BEA_TOKEN`. Reproduced
twice from pristine synthetic fixtures; all checks pass and reads preserve bytes.

Create disposable `/abs/books/main.bean`:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
option "name_income" "Earnings"
option "name_equity" "EarningsCapital"
2020-01-01 open Assets:Cash USD
2020-01-01 open Earnings:Salary USD
2020-01-01 open EarningsCapital:Opening USD
2020-03-01 * "Opening"
  Assets:Cash 100 USD
  EarningsCapital:Opening -100 USD
2020-03-02 * "Salary"
  Assets:Cash 20 USD
  Earnings:Salary -20 USD
2020-03-30 * "Capital"
  Assets:Cash 5 USD
  EarningsCapital:Opening -5 USD
2020-03-31 price HOOL 10 USD
```

From `/abs/books`, with the isolated child environment above:

```sh
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean check
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean report income-statement --time 2020-03 --interval monthly
bea -f /abs/books/main.bean report income-statement --time 2020-03 --interval monthly
bea --json -f /abs/books/main.bean query "SELECT account,sum(number) GROUP BY account ORDER BY account"
```

All exit 0. JSON's income tree is correctly **−20 USD** and headline net
profit is **20 USD**; its one period instead reports income **−125 USD** and
net profit **125 USD**. Human output prints both contradictory profits in the
same report. BQL gives Cash **125**, Salary **−20**, Opening **−105**. The
105 USD capital contributions are equity, not earned income. Overview's income
series is likewise −125 while its income total is −20.

With `--interval weekly`, the same misclassification retains March 1 and
March 30–31 as busy income fragments and reports 100/5 USD profit there.
Those fragments contain only capital and a price directive, so flow series
should omit them; stock series must still reach March 31. With
`--account EarningsCapital`, the income headline is zero but the monthly
profit breakdown is **105 USD**. `--account Earnings` correctly isolates the
salary and gives 20 in both places.

The same root-prefix test also affects net worth. Create a separate fresh
`/abs/books/worth.bean`:

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
option "name_assets" "Capital"
option "name_equity" "CapitalEquity"
2024-01-01 open Capital:Cash USD
2024-01-01 open CapitalEquity:Opening USD
2024-01-02 * "Opening"
  Capital:Cash 100 USD
  CapitalEquity:Opening -100 USD
```

```sh
bea --json -f /abs/books/worth.bean check
bea --json -f /abs/books/worth.bean report balance-sheet --time 2024-01 --interval monthly
```

Expected net worth and its January 31 series point: **100 USD**. Actual:
headline 100, series **0**; it incorrectly includes `CapitalEquity` as an
asset root. Source account components still unambiguously identify equity.

Controls and blast radius:

- Eight initial fixtures (two repetitions, four root layouts) compare overview,
  income statement, balance sheet and trial balance. Default roots agree
  everywhere. Income `Earnings` plus expense `EarningsTax` contaminates the
  income breakdown (−12 instead of −20); reversing which flow root prefixes
  the other contaminates expenses (−12 instead of 8). Asset `Capital`, liability
  `CapitalDebt`, equity `CapitalEquity` give net-worth series **12 instead of
  112 USD**. All four reports' section totals/headlines remain correct.
- Four additional fixtures compare the public income/equity layout and a
  nonoverlapping `Capital` equity control, twice each. Across **240** JSON
  reports, daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly intervals and all/income/
  equity/cash account scopes reproduce the classification gap. The nonoverlapping
  control and income-only filter keep correct profit figures. Fresh BQL and
  human reports confirm the source facts and visible contradiction.

Cause: `cli/src/fava/modules/chart.py` tests
`posting.account.startswith(flows)` in `_flow_ranges` (:82),
`startswith(accounts)` in `ChartModule.interval_totals` (:222), and
`startswith(types)` in `net_worth` (:391). These are raw configured root
strings, without an account separator. `EarningsCapital` thus passes the
`Earnings` test, and `CapitalEquity` passes `Capital`. Tree-based headlines
select the actual root nodes, while `account_balance` already uses the
component-aware `account_tester`; that explains the working section/headline
controls. The statement module shares these chart helpers across overview,
income statement and balance sheet. Trial balance is a working control.

Fix: use actual root-component membership, or the existing component-aware
account helpers, at all three classification sites. Distinct valid roots may
share text prefixes; do not reject or rename them to avoid the report bug.
Keep `--account` anchoring, legitimate child-account inclusion, currency/sign
rules, conversion and the clipped-flow/stock-series policies unchanged.

Dedupe: searched all board states for overlapping/prefixed renamed roots and
these chart helpers, and scanned active milestone scopes. Completed w5/616
anchors the user-supplied account filter; that filter works here. Completed
w5/617 and 365 repair which clipped fragments survive, but their new flow-root
check still uses raw prefixes. The totals/net-worth prefix checks predate the
Fava vendoring commit `41a2e6299`; 617's classifier is from `981784838`.
No open component-classification repair or later fix was found. This is a CLI
reporting follow-up, not a backend/API or native ledger-semantics change.

Acceptance: public income figures are −20/20 in both headline and period;
capital-only weekly fragments disappear from flow series, while balance series
retain the as-of date. Public net-worth series shows 100. All four initial root
layouts, filters and five intervals use the correct account types; default and
nonoverlapping roots remain correct. Pin real report/query/check journeys and
preserve source bytes. Only macOS/checkout tested; repaired behavior unverified.

**Resolved 2026-10-22** — the chart series helpers (`_flow_ranges`' busy-week test, `interval_totals`, `net_worth`) now classify a posting by its account's root component (`_under_roots`) instead of a text prefix, so valid renamed roots that share a prefix are no longer each other's accounts: the monthly income breakdown agrees with the headline (−20 / 20, not −125 / 125), capital-only weekly fragments drop out of the flow series, and the net-worth series keeps an opening under a `Capital`/`CapitalEquity` pair. `--account` anchoring, child accounts, conversion and the clipped-fragment policies are unchanged. `cli/tests/test_report_overlapping_roots.py` fails for all three journeys without the fix; shipped in the commit that moves this note (`cli`). The expense-root variant, the other intervals and the account-scope matrix were not run separately.
