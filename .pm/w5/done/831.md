# Pending-row handling after w5/614: a pending row with a blank or "Pending" date cell still refuses the whole import (no `status=` hint, or inference reads the left-out row), and statuses like "Temporary Hold", "Pre-authorized", "Declined" and "Reverted" are imported as posted (incomplete fix of w5/614)

**Minor · cli · 15–20m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `d6c32f470`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Follow-up to w5/614 (done).
IMPORTING.md:268-272 says a column that marks rows pending is named in a note
suggesting `status=`, and that a row whose status reads `Pending`, `Authorized`
or a hold is left out. Card exports often give pending charges no posted
date. No product fix implemented.

The ledger opens `Liabilities:CreditCard`, `Expenses:Dining` and
`Expenses:Uncategorized` (USD).

1. **Refusal without a hint.** `blankdate.csv` has `Posted
   Date,Description,Amount,Status`, then `09/28/2026,BLUE BOTTLE,-4.50,Posted`
   and `,BISTRO 9,-50.00,Pending`. `import blankdate.csv --csv 'date=Posted
   Date,narration=Description,amount=Amount' --account Liabilities:CreditCard
   --apply` exits 2 with `Row 2 (line 3): cannot parse date '' in column
   'Posted Date' with format '%m/%d/%Y'.`. Nothing mentions pending rows or
   `status=Status`. A remembered mapping from before the fix lands on the same
   path (hunter's run).
2. **Inference reads left-out rows.** With `status=Status` mapped (or inferred)
   and a pending row whose date cell reads `Pending`, the import exits 2 with
   `Row 2 (line 3): cannot parse date 'Pending' … pass --date-format …`. The
   row is the one the import leaves out, and preview numbering skips it.
3. **Unrecognised statuses.** Rows with `Temporary Hold`, `On-Hold`,
   `Pre-authorized`, `Declined`, `Reverted`, `Posted` and `Pending` statuses
   import with `written: 6` and `skipped_pending: 1`. Only `Pending` is left out,
   with no note about the others, and the card ends at −294.50 instead of
   −4.50. `Held`, `Authorization`, `Processing`, `Not Posted`, `Failed` and
   `Cancelled` are also imported (hunter's sweep). Revolut's and PayPal's
   exports use several of these.

Expected: (1) the refusal suggests `status=COLUMN`. (2) Rows whose status
reads pending are skipped for date inference and numbering. (3) Hold and
authorization variants and never-posted states are left out, or at least
named in a note when `status=` is mapped.

Cause:
- The date error is raised before the pending note is built
  (`cli/src/bea_engine/importing.py`, around `:256-278`).
- `inspect_date_column` (`cli/src/cli/csv_mapper.py:748`) reads every row.
- `is_pending` (`cli/src/bea_engine/csv_mapper.py:520`) accepts only `pending*`
  and four exact words.

Fix: add the hint to parse refusals on pending-marked rows, skip pending rows
in inference, and widen `is_pending` (noting unknown status values).

Dedupe: w5/614 (done) is the parent.

Acceptance: each fixture behaves as expected. Tests pin all three.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-20** — (1) a date that will not parse on a row an unmapped column marks pending now adds `Column 'Status' marks this row 'Pending': add status=Status to the --csv mapping …`; (2) the frontend's date inference skips rows whose mapped status is not posted, so a `Pending` date cell no longer refuses the export and numbering matches the import's; (3) the status vocabulary covers authorizations (`Pre-authorized`, `Authorization`), holds (`Held`, `On-Hold`, `Temporary Hold`), `Processing` and `Not Posted` as pending, and `Declined`, `Reverted`, `Failed`, `Cancelled`, `Void` and `Rejected` as never posted, all left out and counted in `skipped_pending`, never-posted ones with their own note. The frontend keeps a twin of the words and a test pins that the two agree. `tests/test_import_unposted_rows.py` covers the three fixtures; IMPORTING lists the words.
