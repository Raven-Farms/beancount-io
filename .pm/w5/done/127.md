# A price gap in an earlier interval row removes the balance sheet's valuation adjustment and total equity

**Minor · cli · 30–45m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-11, HEAD `a8b75aed` (CLI unchanged since `97600b74`),
checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS.
A holding bought before its first quote leaves one early net-worth row
unvalued. The headline figures at the as-of date are all valued, yet the
balance sheet drops its "Valuation/translation adjustment" and "Total equity"
lines, so the printed sheet does not balance (assets 5,200, equity −5,000) and
JSON reports `equity_reconciled: false`. The same ledger with a quarterly
interval reconciles. Owner call on the doc sentence quoted below. No product
fix implemented.

Create disposable `/absolute/books/main.bean` (`bea check` exits 0):

```beancount
option "operating_currency" "USD"
2026-01-01 open Assets:Bank
2026-01-01 open Assets:Broker
2026-01-01 open Equity:Opening

2026-01-02 * "opening"
  Assets:Bank  5000.00 USD
  Equity:Opening

2026-01-15 * "buy"
  Assets:Broker  10 ACME {100.00 USD}
  Assets:Bank  -1000.00 USD

2026-02-27 price ACME 110.00 USD
2026-03-31 price ACME 120.00 USD
```

Use isolated BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1, no
inherited BEA_FILE/BEA_TOKEN, closed stdin, no TTY.

```sh
bea -f /absolute/books/main.bean report balance-sheet --allow-errors
bea --json -f /absolute/books/main.bean report balance-sheet --allow-errors
bea --json -f /absolute/books/main.bean report balance-sheet --allow-errors -i quarterly
```

Actual: the text report exits 0 with Assets `5,200.00 USD`, Equity
`-5,000.00 USD`, current-period earnings `0.00 USD`, Net Worth
`5,200.00 USD`, and no adjustment or total-equity line. Monthly JSON has
`"valuation_adjustment": null`, `"equity_total": null`,
`"equity_reconciled": false`, `"net_worth": {"USD": "5200.00"}`.

Control: the quarterly run, same as-of date, has `"valuation_adjustment":
{"USD": "-200.00"}`, `"equity_total": {"USD": "-5200.00"}`,
`"equity_reconciled": true`.

Independent arithmetic: assets 4,000 + 10 × 120 = 5,200; equity −5,000;
earnings 0; adjustment −(5,200 − 5,000) = −200; total equity −5,200.

Expected: `docs/USAGE.md` says balance sheets include the adjustment and
total "so converted assets, liabilities, and total equity reconcile", and
that "A gap only in an earlier row (a holding bought before its first quote)
leaves the headline valued". The equity lines are as-of figures like the
headline, so they should be present, and the interval chosen should not
decide whether the sheet reconciles. The same paragraph ends "It also
withholds the derived equity adjustment and equity total", which can be read
as covering this; if that is the intent, say so explicitly for row-only gaps
and explain it in the text report.

Cause: `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py:284` sets `incomplete` from any
missing price in any series row and `:289` feeds it into the reconciliation
decision. w1/125 moved the headline totals to an as-of judgement but left
this line on the any-row rule.

Fix: judge reconciliation on the as-of balances being fully in the target
currency; keep `valuation: "partial"` and `missing_price_dates` for the
rows. No dependency change; JSON gains values where it had nulls.

Blast radius: balance sheet only, text and JSON, at the default monthly
interval for any holding bought before its first quote.

Dedupe: board search for `equity_reconciled`, `valuation_adjustment` and
total equity finds w1/125 (net worth and net profit only), w3/374 (the
opposite series case) and w3/395 (account-filter reconciliation); none
covers the equity lines under a row-only gap.

Acceptance: on the fixture the monthly balance sheet prints the −200.00
adjustment and −5,200.00 total equity and JSON reports
`equity_reconciled: true`, matching the quarterly run; a commodity unvalued
at the as-of date still withholds them. Only macOS and the checkout were
exercised.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-13** — The balance sheet in `cli/src/bea_engine/report.py` now decides equity reconciliation from the as-of balances instead of from any interval row that lacked a price, matching the headline rule from w1/125. A holding bought before its first quote keeps its valuation adjustment and total equity at every interval, while `valuation: "partial"` and `missing_price_dates` still describe the unvalued row; a commodity still unvalued at the as-of date withholds the lines as before. Two regression cases fail with the fix reverted and a third pins the still-withheld control. The note flagged the matching `docs/USAGE.md` sentence as an owner call; it was rewritten to state this rule. `make check-all` passed. Only macOS was exercised. Shipped in `c98a2067` (`cli`).
