# `query --format csv` and `-o x.csv` drop a lot's cost date and label, so distinct lots become identical cells (`10 HOOL {100 USD}, 10 HOOL {100 USD}`); `--json` keeps both

**Trivial · cli · 10m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-14, HEAD `bce2c992`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1, engine Python 3.12.
CSV is the format USAGE points programs at for reading results back. Its
position rendering keeps the cost number and currency but discards the
cost's date and label. Two lots that the ledger and the JSON output keep
apart therefore merge into indistinguishable text. A spreadsheet or
script cannot tell which lot is which. No product fix implemented.

Create disposable `lots.bean`, a USD ledger with `Assets:Brk` and
`Assets:Cash` and two buys at the same cost:

```beancount
2026-01-03 * "Buy"
  Assets:Brk 10 HOOL {100 USD, 2026-01-03, "lotA"}
  Assets:Cash -1000 USD
2026-01-06 * "Buy B"
  Assets:Brk 10 HOOL {100 USD, 2026-01-06, "lotB"}
  Assets:Cash -1000 USD
```

Use isolated BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1, and
closed stdin.

```sh
bea -f /absolute/books/lots.bean query --format csv "SELECT account, sum(position) AS s WHERE account = 'Assets:Brk' GROUP BY account"
bea -f /absolute/books/lots.bean query -o /absolute/out/lots.csv "SELECT date, position WHERE account = 'Assets:Brk'"
```

Actual: the first prints `Assets:Brk,"10 HOOL {100 USD}, 10 HOOL {100
USD}"`. The second writes two rows of `10 HOOL {100 USD}`. `--json`
keeps `cost: {…, date: "2026-01-03", label: "lotA"}` and the lotB
equivalent. Reproduced twice by the hunter and once by me.

Control: `--json` is exact. Text and `--source` also omit the date and
label, which is upstream beanquery's native rendering.

Expected: CSV renders the full cost spec as Beancount writes it, `10 HOOL
{100 USD, 2026-01-03, "lotA"}`, so distinct lots stay distinct.

Cause: `_csv_from_jsonable` (`cli/src/bea_engine/query.py:1310-1330`,
about `:1318-1324`) formats the position's cost dict through the
`"number" in value and "currency" in value` branch, which returns only
`number currency`.

Fix: when formatting a cost, append `, <date>` and `, "<label>"` when
present. No dependency change.

Blast radius: CSV exports of positions or inventories with dated or
labelled lots.

Dedupe: no `.pm` hit for lot, label or cost date together with csv.
w5/294 and 383 are other rendering notes.

Acceptance: both commands keep each lot's date and label, so the two lots
are distinguishable. A test pins it.

Addendum (CLI QA r110, 2026-10-14): as a stopgap, selecting `cost_date`,
`cost_label`, `cost_number` and `cost_currency` as separate columns keeps
the lot detail in CSV. The fix should still render positions in full,
and USAGE could mention the columns. Every other machine-readable surface
(`list --json`, `--details`, `add` echo, `format`, `price export`,
`query --json`) keeps the lot date and label.

---

**Resolved 2026-10-15** — `_csv_cost` (`cli/src/bea_engine/query.py`) renders a position's cost in CSV the way Beancount writes it: number and currency, then the lot date and the quoted label, with quotes and backslashes escaped. So `10 HOOL {100 USD, 2026-01-03, "lotA"}` and its lotB twin stay distinct in `--format csv` and `-o x.csv`, for single positions and summed inventories alike. Text and `--source` keep upstream's native table. `cli/tests/test_query_csv_lot_cost.py` parses the CSV for both statements, including a label with escaped quotes; both cases fail with the fix reverted. USAGE was not changed (the addendum's separate-columns stopgap still works). Only macOS was exercised. `make check-all` passed (5970 tests). Shipped in `3feb465e` (`cli`).
