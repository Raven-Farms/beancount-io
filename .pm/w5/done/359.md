# The `beancount-reconcile` skill gives liability sign rules only for lines, never for the statement ending balance, so a credit-card reconcile followed as written gets a residual of twice the balance and withholds the assertion

**Minor · skills · 15–20m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-14, HEAD `bce2c992`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1, engine Python 3.12.
A card statement prints what you owe as a positive number ("New Balance
342.20", or a running `Balance` column ending at `342.20`). The reconcile
skill carefully flips each transaction line into ledger signs: a charge
becomes −, a payment +. It then puts the statement's ending balance into
the tie-out, the sanity walk and `bea add balance` with no sign rule. For
a liability, that number must be negated. Followed as written:

- the residual is exactly twice the balance, so the skill must withhold
  the assertion and report it as "unexplained";
- the sanity walk fails, and the doc blames "your sign mapping (or a
  missing/extra line)", although both are correct.

The only liability eval avoids the question, because its user supplies
`-138.00` directly. No product fix implemented.

Set up with `bea --no-input init . --currency USD --date 2026-01-01`,
adding `open Liabilities:CreditCard USD`. Use isolated
BEA_CONFIG_DIR/XDG_CACHE_HOME, BEA_NO_UPDATE_NOTIFIER=1, no inherited
BEA_FILE/BEA_TOKEN, and closed stdin. Create `card-may.csv`:

```text
Transaction Date,Description,Amount,Balance
05/03/2026,TRADER JOES #123,54.20,54.20
05/10/2026,SHELL OIL 5544,40.00,94.20
05/15/2026,ONLINE PAYMENT THANK YOU,-50.00,44.20
05/21/2026,BLUE BOTTLE COFFEE,8.00,52.20
05/28/2026,UNITED AIRLINES,290.00,342.20
```

Save the first four data rows of `card-may.csv` (with its header), that is everything except the UNITED AIRLINES row, as `card-apr.csv`. Import them with the skill's csv-apply recipe: `bea
--file "$L" import card-apr.csv --csv 'date=Transaction
Date,narration=Description,amount=Amount,sign=ledger' --account
Liabilities:CreditCard --date-format '%m/%d/%Y' --into main.bean
--apply`. The ledger card balance is then −52.20, with charges negative
as the skill requires. Reconcile against the full file: United (−290.00)
is missing from the ledger. The ending balance is "the last row's
balance" (`statement-formats.md:21`), 342.20. Run the tie-out as written:

```sh
bea --file "$L" --json --no-input query "SELECT sum(number) AS ledger, sum(number) + -290.00 AS projected, 342.20 - (sum(number) + -290.00) AS residual WHERE account = 'Liabilities:CreditCard' AND currency = 'USD' AND date < 2026-06-01"
bea --file "$L" add balance --date 2026-06-01 --account Liabilities:CreditCard --amount '342.20 USD'
```

Actual:

- The query returns `[-52.20, -342.20, 684.40]`. Under `SKILL.md:105`,
  a non-zero residual means the skill withholds the assertion.
- The literal `add balance` exits 1 with `Balance failed … expected 342.20
  USD != accumulated -52.20 USD`.
- The sanity walk at `statement-formats.md:63` (`opening + sum(normalized)
  == ending`) also fails, giving −342.20 vs 342.20.

Reproduced twice by the hunter in fresh directories. I checked the
skill text myself.

Control: with `-342.20`, the residual is `0.00`. The asset (checking)
journey ties out with residual 0.00 and passes `check`.

Expected: the skill should say, next to the liability line rules, that a
liability statement's ending balance (and its running-balance column) is
the amount owed, and is negated into ledger sign before the tie-out, the
sanity walk and the assertion. For example, statement "New Balance 342.20"
becomes `2026-06-01 balance Liabilities:CreditCard -342.20 USD`, and a
credit balance on the card becomes positive.

Cause:

- In `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-reconcile/references/statement-formats.md`,
  `:21` and `:52-63` give line rules only.
- `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-reconcile/SKILL.md:70-76` and
  `:103-121` take "the ending balance" without a sign rule.
- `skills/.claude/skills/beancount-init/references/bea-cli.md:140` says
  "the signed statement ending amount" without saying which sign.
- `evals/evals.json` (the liability case) supplies a pre-negated
  balance.

Fix:

- Add the ending-balance rule to the liability section of
  `statement-formats.md`, and point `SKILL.md` and `bea-cli.md` at it.
- Make the sanity walk compare against the ledger-signed ending balance.
- Add a CI-run liability eval whose statement prints a positive "New
  Balance" and expects the negative assertion.
- beancount-migrate's "ask for each source account's current balance"
  (`references/mapping.md:37`) has the same gap. That one is suspected,
  not run.

Blast radius: every `beancount-reconcile` run on a credit card or line of
credit, the most common reconcile target after checking.

Dedupe: searched `.pm/**`, including `done/`, for liability or credit
card together with ending balance, statement balance and reconcile. The
only hits are mobile running-balance notes (w4/036, w4/053, w3/189). None
of w5/292–358 covers this.

Acceptance: following the skill on the fixture gives a residual of
`0.00` and writes `balance Liabilities:CreditCard -342.20 USD`, and the
root `check` passes. A CI-run eval covers a positive printed card balance.

**Resolved.** The liability section of `statement-formats.md` now has a "balances flip too" rule. A card or credit-line statement's opening, ending and running-balance figures are the amount owed, so they are negated before the sanity walk, the tie-out's `$statement_ending` and the `balance` assertion, and a credit balance (negative or `CR`) becomes positive. A marked `liability-ending` example shows "New Balance 342.20" as `2026-06-01 balance Liabilities:CreditCard -342.20 USD`, and the sanity walk now says both balances are in ledger sign.

The same rule now appears in:

- the liability bullet in `beancount-reconcile/SKILL.md`, and a new `$statement_ending` line under the tie-out recipe;
- the assertion step in `beancount-init/references/bea-cli.md`;
- the current/opening balance step in `beancount-migrate/references/mapping.md`.

New eval14 (`liability-printed-positive-balance`) uses the note's fixture: four matched lines, United missing, and a running `Balance` that ends at 342.20. It expects residual 0.00 and the −342.20 assertion. Both of its fixture files are `bea check`ed in CI.

`test_liability_tie_out_negates_the_printed_ending_balance` follows the documented rule on that fixture:

- the tie-out returns `[-52.20, -342.20, 0.00]`;
- the unflipped printed figure gives the reported 684.40 residual;
- the balance recipe writes −342.20 USD and `check` passes.

The test fails without the rule.
