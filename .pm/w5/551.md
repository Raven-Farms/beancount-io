**Major · skills · 30–45m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-16, HEAD `66aeb02e`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Skill-text drift; the CLI behaves as documented.
Close Phase 6 requires `report income-statement` and `report balance-sheet`
with `--time "$month"`, and beancount-init's `references/bea-cli.md` lists
them as the shared reads. Under `--json`, a report that must value an
unpriced commodity exits 1 unless `--allow-errors` is passed (USAGE,
"Missing prices"). A single hotel bill in EUR paid by a USD card, or the
options skill's own short-put example, therefore stops the month-end close.

repair or retry its managed engine, which is the wrong remedy for a
valuation refusal. w5/358 fixed the BQL recipes for `@`-priced charges, not
the report path.
