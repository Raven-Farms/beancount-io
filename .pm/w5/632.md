# Hosted ledger: shadowing a managed feed point whose metadata follows an indented comment leaves orphaned indented lines, and the effective feed fails to parse (follow-up of w5/477)

**Minor · backend-cluster/ledger · 15–20m · A1.** Follow-up from w5/477 (CLI fix `e76b9d38`). Reproduced at library level (compiled `dist/` module with `@rustledger/wasm` 0.24.0), not against a deployed hosted ledger.
`applyLedgerPricePrecedence`
(`backend-cluster/ledger/src/foundation/managed-prices/managed-price-overlay.ts:125-142`)
comments out a shadowed point's metadata only while `METADATA_LINE_RE`
matches. That is the loop the CLI dropped in w5/477. An indented comment
between the price and its metadata ends the walk, and the metadata line is
left indented under a comment. w5/476 and w5/478 got hosted follow-ups
(w5/625, w5/629); w5/477 did not.

Feed `K2`:

```text
; alias: K2
; commodity: XC
; quote: USD
2026-10-15 price XC 10 USD
    ; provider note
  observed-at: "2026-10-16T12:00:00Z"
```

Ledger: `2026-01-01 open Assets:Cash USD` and `2026-10-15 price XC 12 USD`.

Actual (Node script against the compiled module, after `initSync`): the
validator accepts the feed (`ok: true`); in the effective text the price line
is commented out but `    ; provider note` and `  observed-at: …` remain;
`validateMultiFile` fails with `{"code": "P0012", "message": "feed.bean: parse
error: unexpected input", "line": 6}`.

Control: the same feed without the ledger's price validates (`valid: true`).

Expected: the shadowed point and every following indented line up to the next
column-0 line are commented out; the effective feed parses.

Fix: port the CLI's walk (comment out every non-blank indented line until the
next line at column 0); add the K2 case to `managed-price-overlay.test.ts`.

Dedupe: searched `applyLedgerPricePrecedence`, `METADATA_LINE_RE`, hosted with
`shadow`; w5/625 and 629 are the sibling hosted follow-ups.

Acceptance: a unit test shadows the K2 point and the multi-file validation
passes.
