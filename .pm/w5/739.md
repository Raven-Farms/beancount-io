# `bea ask` answers turn character references such as `&#x202E;` into raw bidi override characters on the terminal (`00.001` can display as `100.00`), and Markdown link reference definitions and link titles vanish (gap in w5/548)

**Minor · cli · 10m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `15b5e52f4`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Simulation: a local AI-proxy stub on 127.0.0.1 with a fake token. Follow-up to w5/548 (done, `0706393f1`).
USAGE:1044-1046 says bidi characters are escaped as `\uNNNN`, and the
`answer_markdown` docstring says "no control character reaches the screen
raw". No product fix implemented.

1. **Character references.** The stub answers `Paid &#x202E;00.001&#x202C; USD
   to Bob; raw \u202e is escaped` (the second one a literal U+202E). `bea -f
   /abs/main.bean ask q --print` exits 0 with stdout `Paid \u202e00.001\u202c
   USD to Bob; raw \\u202e is escaped`, which is printed with a raw U+202E and
   U+202C. The literal U+202E later in the same answer is escaped. On a
   bidi-aware terminal the amount reads backwards. `&#x2066;`, `&#x2069;` and
   `&#8238;` do the same in `--print` and the session (hunter's runs).
   Control: a raw U+202E, ESC and OSC 8 are escaped; `&#27;` becomes U+FFFD.
2. **Reference definitions and titles.** The answer `Sources:\n\n[1]:
   https://beancount.io/docs/a\n[2]: https://example.com/b "Fee 12.00
   USD"\n\nEnd.` prints only `Sources:` and `End.`, so both URLs and the fee
   are gone. An inline link's title is dropped too (hunter's run). The
   docstring promises "every character of it shown".

Cause: `cli/src/cli/ask/render.py:24-25` runs `inert_text` on the Markdown
source. markdown-it then decodes the character references back into the
characters just escaped. It also emits no tokens for reference definitions,
and Rich ignores link titles.

Fix: apply `inert_text` to the parsed token text (or disable markdown-it's
`entity` rule), and render unused reference definitions and link titles as
literal text.

Dedupe: w5/548 (done) is the parent; w5/148, 331 and 542 cover other
surfaces.

Acceptance: the entity answer prints `\u202e` escaped, and the
reference-definition answer shows both URLs and the title. Tests pin both.
