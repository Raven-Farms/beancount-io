`bank.csv` is `Date,Payee,Description,Amount` / `2026-09-28,Starbucks,CARD
PURCHASE 1234,-4.50`.

```sh
bea --json --file /abs/main.bean import bank.csv --csv date=Date,payee=Payee,narration=Description,amount=Amount --account Assets:Checking
```

narrations, which is IMPORTING's own wording.

Cause: `_twin_reason` (`cli/src/bea_engine/importing.py:1175`) picks "payee"
from the row alone. `_possible_twin` (`:1911`) admitted the twin through the
narration key.
