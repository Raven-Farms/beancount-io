# A stopped `price export` leaves a partial snapshot (`main.bean` plus a few of its includes) and dies silently; the retry then demands `--force` to overwrite the half-written tree (engine-side sibling of w5/608)

**Minor · cli · 10–15m · A1.** Real CLI QA, 2026-10-17, HEAD `2f69b1461`, checkout `bea 0.3.1`, macOS 26.5.1. Sibling of w5/608 (done).
w5/608 made a stopped frontend export leave nothing behind. USAGE says an
export into a directory that already holds such files is refused, "writing
nothing". No product fix implemented.

`main.bean` (USD; `Assets:Checking` and `Expenses:Food`) has 30 `include
"inc/partNN.bean"` lines, each part holding 2,500 transactions.

```sh
bea --json -f /abs/px/main.bean price export -o /abs/px-out    # SIGTERM when px-out/inc/part03.bean appears
bea -f /abs/px/main.bean price export -o /abs/px-out
```

Actual:
- The stopped export ends with rc −15 and no output. `px-out` holds `main.bean`
  and `inc/part00.bean` through `part03.bean`, but no `.bea-*` files.
- The retry exits 2: `Cannot export into …/px-out: it already holds
  …/inc/part00.bean, which the export would overwrite … pass --force …`
  (hunter's run).
- SIGINT and SIGHUP behave the same.

Control: a disk that fills mid-tree reports `17 of 31 files were already
written (…)`, exit 1 (hunter's run).

Expected: a stopped export removes what it wrote (or builds the tree in a
temporary sibling and renames it once), and at least says what it wrote.

Cause: `_write_export` (`cli/src/bea_engine/managed_load.py:1836`) stages and
replaces one file at a time. On a stop, only the current staged copy is
discarded.

Fix: track created files and remove them on a stop or failure, or rename a
completed temporary tree.

Dedupe: w5/608 (done) covers frontend exports; w5/056, 117 and 191 (done)
cover planning failures; w5/676 (open) covers the unknown outcome on a kill.

Acceptance: a stopped export leaves `px-out` absent (or unchanged), and the
retry writes without `--force`.
